Chinese phrase of the day · January 1 · Season

新年快乐！

xīn nián kuài lè！

Happy New Year!

A New Year greeting you can use both on January 1 and at Spring Festival (Chinese New Year), though in China Spring Festival is celebrated on a much bigger scale. You can simply reply with 「新年快乐！」 too.

Example

新年快乐！今年也请大家多多关照！
xīn nián kuài lè！ jīn nián yě qǐng dà jiā duō duō guān zhào！
Happy New Year! I hope everyone will keep looking out for me this year too!
