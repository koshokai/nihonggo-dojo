Chinese phrase of the day · January 2 · Season

新年新气象！

xīn nián xīn qì xiàng！

New year, fresh start!

A set phrase for the "turn over a new leaf" feeling at the start of the year, often heard in company New Year greetings. You can also use it jokingly after tidying your room or getting a haircut.

Example

剪了个新发型，新年新气象嘛！
jiǎn le ge xīn fà xíng， xīn nián xīn qì xiàng ma！
I got a new haircut, new year, fresh start!
