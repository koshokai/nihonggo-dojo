Chinese phrase of the day · January 3 · Idiom

千里之行，始于足下

qiān lǐ zhī xíng， shǐ yú zú xià

A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step

Even a big goal starts with one step from where you stand. It comes from the Laozi (and is the source of the English saying), and it's a perfect encouragement for someone starting something new.

Example

别怕难，千里之行，始于足下，先从每天十个单词开始吧。
bié pà nán， qiān lǐ zhī xíng， shǐ yú zú xià， xiān cóng měi tiān shí ge dān cí kāi shǐ ba。
Don't worry about how hard it is, a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. Start with ten words a day.
