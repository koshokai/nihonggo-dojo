Chinese phrase of the day · January 4 · Internet slang

立个flag

lì ge flag

to publicly declare a goal ("raise a flag")

Declaring a goal, such as a New Year's resolution. It has nothing to do with a warning sign; the core meaning is announcing your goal to everyone, though it's often used with a self-deprecating punchline about a "flag" you won't be able to keep.

Example

我先立个flag：今年一定要瘦十斤！
wǒ xiān lì ge flag： jīn nián yí dìng yào shòu shí jīn！
Let me raise a flag first: this year I'm definitely losing ten jin (5 kg)!
