Chinese phrase of the day · January 5 · Greeting

好久不见！

hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn！

Long time no see!

For someone you haven't seen in a while, and it works with friends and superiors alike. You can reply with 「好久不见！」 too; it's said to be the origin of the English "long time no see."

Example

好久不见！你一点儿都没变啊！
hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn！ nǐ yì diǎnr dōu méi biàn a！
Long time no see! You haven't changed a bit!
