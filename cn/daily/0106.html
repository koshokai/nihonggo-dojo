Chinese phrase of the day · January 6 · Work

合作愉快！

hé zuò yú kuài！

Here's to a good partnership!

The standard line said with a handshake once a contract or partnership is agreed. Literally "let's cooperate happily," it's like saying "I look forward to working with you" in English.

Example

合同签好了，祝我们合作愉快！
hé tong qiān hǎo le， zhù wǒ men hé zuò yú kuài！
The contract's signed. Here's to a great partnership!
