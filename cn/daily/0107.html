Chinese phrase of the day · January 7 · Idiom

万事开头难

wàn shì kāi tóu nán

Every beginning is hard

A saying meaning the start is always the hardest part of anything new. Saying 「万事开头难，慢慢来」 to someone who's just started and is struggling is a gentle encouragement.

Example

刚学发音肯定难，万事开头难嘛。
gāng xué fā yīn kěn dìng nán， wàn shì kāi tóu nán ma。
Pronunciation is bound to be hard when you first start learning. Every beginning is hard, after all.
