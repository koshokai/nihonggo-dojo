Chinese phrase of the day · January 8 · Season

冷死了！

lěng sǐ le！

It's freezing!

「〜死了」 means "to death," an intensifier that doesn't mean anyone is literally dying; you can attach it to almost anything, as in 「热死了」 「累死了」 「饿死了」. In the north, indoor heating in winter is so warm that the contrast with outside is a shock.

Example

今天零下十度，冷死了！
jīn tiān líng xià shí dù， lěng sǐ le！
It's minus ten degrees today. It's freezing!
