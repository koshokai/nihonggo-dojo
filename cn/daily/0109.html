Chinese phrase of the day · January 9 · Food

少放点辣。

shǎo fàng diǎn là。

Go easy on the chili, please.

A handy line in Sichuan or Hunan restaurants. Even 「中辣」 (medium) is often very hot for many foreign visitors, so be careful; if you want no heat at all ask for 「不要辣」, or 「微辣」 for just a little.

Example

服务员，这道菜少放点辣，我不太能吃辣。
fú wù yuán， zhè dào cài shǎo fàng diǎn là， wǒ bú tài néng chī là。
Excuse me, could you go easy on the chili in this dish? I can't really handle spicy food.
