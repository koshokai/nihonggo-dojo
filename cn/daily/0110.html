Chinese phrase of the day · January 10 · Friends

够意思！

gòu yì si！

You're a true friend!

Praise for a friend who has done something loyal and generous for you, meaning "loyal" or "reliable." The opposite, 「不够意思」, means "that's not very friendly of you" or "cold."

Example

你特意来机场接我，真够意思！
nǐ tè yì lái jī chǎng jiē wǒ， zhēn gòu yì si！
You came all the way to the airport to pick me up? You're a true friend!
