Chinese phrase of the day · January 11 · Work

年终奖发了吗？

nián zhōng jiǎng fā le ma？

Did you get your year-end bonus?

「年终奖」 is the year-end bonus paid between the end of the year and Spring Festival. It's a common topic among coworkers and friends, but asking the amount directly can be rude, so a light 「发了吗？」 is the safe way to ask.

Example

年终奖发了吗？听说咱们部门下周发。
nián zhōng jiǎng fā le ma？ tīng shuō zán men bù mén xià zhōu fā。
Did you get your year-end bonus? I heard our department gets it next week.
