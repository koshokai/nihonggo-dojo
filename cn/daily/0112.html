Chinese phrase of the day · January 12 · Internet slang

666

liù liù liù

Awesome! So good!

It sounds the same as 「溜（liù）」, meaning "skilled, awesome." It started in comments on game livestreams and is now used in everyday chat, much like typing "insane!" or "so good lol" in English.

Example

你一分钟就修好了电脑？666！
nǐ yì fēn zhōng jiù xiū hǎo le diàn nǎo？ liù liù liù！
You fixed the computer in one minute? Awesome!
