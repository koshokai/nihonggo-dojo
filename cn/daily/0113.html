Chinese phrase of the day · January 13 · Season

下雪了！

xià xuě le！

It's snowing!

What you say when snow starts falling; 「了」 marks a change of situation, giving it a "hey, it's started snowing!" feeling of discovery. People in the north routinely post photos of the first snow on social media, and for people in the south snow is especially exciting news.

Example

快看窗外，外面下雪了！
kuài kàn chuāng wài， wài miàn xià xuě le！
Look out the window, it's snowing outside!
