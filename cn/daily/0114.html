Chinese phrase of the day · January 14 · Travel

洗手间在哪儿？

xǐ shǒu jiān zài nǎr？

Where's the restroom?

A line you'll definitely need when traveling. 「厕所」 is also understood, but 「洗手间」 is more polite, and in shops you'll often see signs saying 「卫生间」.

Example

请问，洗手间在哪儿？在二楼吗？
qǐng wèn， xǐ shǒu jiān zài nǎr？ zài èr lóu ma？
Excuse me, where's the restroom? Is it on the second floor?
