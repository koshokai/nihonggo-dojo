Chinese phrase of the day · January 15 · Feelings

太好了！

tài hǎo le！

That's great! / Yay!

For when you hear good news or feel relieved. 「太〜了」 is the basic "so ..." pattern, and you can extend it to 「太棒了」 「太可惜了」 and so on.

Example

你考过了？太好了，恭喜你！
nǐ kǎo guo le？ tài hǎo le， gōng xǐ nǐ！
You passed the exam? That's great, congratulations!
