Chinese phrase of the day · January 16 · Food

趁热吃！

chèn rè chī！

Eat it while it's hot!

Said when food arrives, urging others to start eating right away. Hot food matters a lot in China, so you'll hear this again and again at the table; when someone says it to you, don't be too reserved, say 「好，我不客气了」 and dig in.

Example

饺子刚出锅，快趁热吃！
jiǎo zi gāng chū guō， kuài chèn rè chī！
The dumplings just came out of the pot, eat them while they're hot!
