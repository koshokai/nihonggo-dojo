Chinese phrase of the day · January 17 · Travel

抢到票了吗？

qiǎng dào piào le ma？

Did you manage to get tickets?

The standard question during 「春运」, the Spring Festival travel rush. Train tickets are "fought over" the moment they go on sale, hence 「抢」; if you got one say 「抢到了！」, and if not, something like 「没抢到，只能站票了」.

Example

回家的票抢到了吗？我只抢到了站票。
huí jiā de piào qiǎng dào le ma？ wǒ zhǐ qiǎng dào le zhàn piào。
Did you get a ticket home? I could only get a standing ticket.
