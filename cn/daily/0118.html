Chinese phrase of the day · January 18 · Idiom

温故知新

wēn gù zhī xīn

Review the old to learn the new

Gaining new knowledge by going back over what you've learned. A four-character idiom from the Analects, it's used in Chinese when talking about the importance of studying and reviewing.

Example

考试前温故知新，把错题再做一遍吧。
kǎo shì qián wēn gù zhī xīn， bǎ cuò tí zài zuò yí biàn ba。
Review before the exam: go over the questions you got wrong once more.
