Chinese phrase of the day · January 19 · Season

过了腊八就是年

guò le là bā jiù shì nián

Once Laba has passed, New Year is here

On 「腊八」, the 8th day of the 12th lunar month, people eat 「腊八粥」, a porridge of mixed grains, and preparations for Spring Festival are said to begin that day. The saying captures the end-of-year mood and is good for year-end small talk.

Example

今天喝了腊八粥，过了腊八就是年啦。
jīn tiān hē le là bā zhōu， guò le là bā jiù shì nián la。
I had Laba porridge today. Once Laba has passed, New Year is practically here!
