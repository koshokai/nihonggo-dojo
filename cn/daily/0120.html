Chinese phrase of the day · January 20 · Greeting

请多关照。

qǐng duō guān zhào。

Nice to meet you. / I look forward to working with you.

A greeting for first meetings or a new workplace. It isn't something you say every time; it suits formal moments like the end of a self-introduction, and friends don't need to say it at all.

Example

我叫田中，刚来公司，请多关照。
wǒ jiào tián zhōng， gāng lái gōng sī， qǐng duō guān zhào。
My name is Tanaka. I've just joined the company, and I look forward to working with you.
