Chinese phrase of the day · January 21 · Feelings

无聊死了。

wú liáo sǐ le。

I'm so bored...

A grumble when you have nothing to do. 「无聊」 means "bored, boring," and said of a person or a conversation it becomes a criticism, "pointless, silly." It's often heard on the last day of a long weekend or while waiting around.

Example

下雨出不去，在家无聊死了。
xià yǔ chū bu qù， zài jiā wú liáo sǐ le。
It's raining so I can't go out. I'm bored to death at home.
