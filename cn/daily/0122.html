Chinese phrase of the day · January 22 · Internet slang

打卡

dǎ kǎ

Been there! / Checked in!

Originally it meant punching a time card at work. Now it's used for visiting a popular spot or shop and posting photos, and also for logging that you kept up a study or exercise habit; 「网红打卡地」 means an Instagrammable spot.

Example

今天学了三十分钟中文，打卡！
jīn tiān xué le sān shí fēn zhōng zhōng wén， dǎ kǎ！
Studied Chinese for 30 minutes today. Checked in!
