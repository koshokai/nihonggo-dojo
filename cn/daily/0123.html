Chinese phrase of the day · January 23 · Season

你回家过年吗？

nǐ huí jiā guò nián ma？

Are you going home for New Year?

A conversation you're bound to have with coworkers and friends as Spring Festival approaches, since going back to your hometown to spend it with family is the most important custom in China. Natural answers are 「回，后天走」 or 「今年不回了」.

Example

你回家过年吗？我下周五就走了。
nǐ huí jiā guò nián ma？ wǒ xià zhōu wǔ jiù zǒu le。
Are you going home for New Year? I'm leaving next Friday.
