Chinese phrase of the day · January 24 · Shopping

包邮吗？

bāo yóu ma？

Is shipping free?

One of the most common questions in online shopping; 「包邮」 means "shipping included." Many people check this first in the chat with the seller.

Example

亲，这件衣服包邮吗？
qīn， zhè jiàn yī fu bāo yóu ma？
Hi dear, does this top come with free shipping?
