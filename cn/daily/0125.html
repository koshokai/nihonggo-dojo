Chinese phrase of the day · January 25 · Friends

老地方见！

lǎo dì fang jiàn！

See you at the usual place!

What friends say when arranging to meet at "the usual place or restaurant." It shows a closeness where nothing more needs to be said, just like "see you at the usual spot" in English.

Example

今晚七点，咱们老地方见！
jīn wǎn qī diǎn， zán men lǎo dì fang jiàn！
Tonight at seven, see you at the usual place!
