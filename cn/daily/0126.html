Chinese phrase of the day · January 26 · Season

年货买齐了吗？

nián huò mǎi qí le ma？

Have you done your New Year shopping?

「年货」 means the food, decorations and other things bought for Spring Festival. Before the holiday, supermarkets and markets are packed with people buying 「年货」, and more and more people buy it all online; 「办年货」 means "to do the New Year shopping."

Example

年货买齐了吗？我就差春联还没买。
nián huò mǎi qí le ma？ wǒ jiù chà chūn lián hái méi mǎi。
Have you finished your New Year shopping? All I still need are the couplets.
