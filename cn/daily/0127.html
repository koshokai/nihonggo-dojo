Chinese phrase of the day · January 27 · Greeting

晚安！

wǎn ān！

Good night!

A goodnight greeting, often sent at the end of a chat. With people you're close to, adding 「晚安，好梦！」 ("Good night, sweet dreams!") comes across as warm.

Example

我先睡了，晚安，明天见！
wǒ xiān shuì le， wǎn ān， míng tiān jiàn！
I'm going to bed. Good night, see you tomorrow!
