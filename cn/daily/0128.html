Chinese phrase of the day · January 28 · Travel

有空房吗？

yǒu kòng fáng ma？

Do you have any rooms available?

The basic phrase when you walk into a hotel or guesthouse without a reservation; adding the date, as in 「今晚还有空房吗？」, makes it clearer. During holidays places are often 「客满」 (fully booked), so booking early is the safe choice.

Example

你好，今晚还有空房吗？两个人。
nǐ hǎo， jīn wǎn hái yǒu kòng fáng ma？ liǎng ge rén。
Hello, do you still have a room available tonight? For two.
