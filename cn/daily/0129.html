Chinese phrase of the day · January 29 · Season

回家吃年夜饭

huí jiā chī nián yè fàn

to go home for the New Year's Eve dinner

「年夜饭」 is the feast the whole family eats together on New Year's Eve. It's the most important meal of the year, and many people want to come home for it even if they live far away; more and more families now book it at a restaurant.

Example

不管多忙，除夕都要回家吃年夜饭。
bù guǎn duō máng， chú xī dōu yào huí jiā chī nián yè fàn。
No matter how busy I am, I have to go home for the New Year's Eve dinner.
