Chinese phrase of the day · January 30 · Season

过年好！

guò nián hǎo！

Happy New Year!

A spoken greeting for people you meet around Spring Festival. It's common in the north and sounds warm and friendly, and you can use it with anyone in the days before and after the holiday.

Example

王阿姨，过年好！给您带了点水果。
wáng ā yí， guò nián hǎo！ gěi nín dài le diǎn shuǐ guǒ。
Happy New Year, Auntie Wang! I brought you some fruit.
