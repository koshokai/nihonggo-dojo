Chinese phrase of the day · January 31 · Season

春节快乐！

chūn jié kuài lè！

Happy Spring Festival!

The most common greeting for Lunar New Year, also fine for messages and cards. It's almost the same as 「新年快乐」 but makes it clear you mean Spring Festival; reply with 「春节快乐！」 or 「你也是！」.

Example

祝你和家人春节快乐，身体健康！
zhù nǐ hé jiā rén chūn jié kuài lè， shēn tǐ jiàn kāng！
Wishing you and your family a happy Spring Festival and good health!
