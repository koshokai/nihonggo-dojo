Chinese phrase of the day · February 1 · Season

给您拜年了！

gěi nín bài nián le！

Happy New Year to you! (a New Year visit)

The set phrase for paying New Year respects to relatives and elders at Spring Festival; 「拜年」 means making New Year visits. Many people now do their 「拜年」 by video call or message, and saying it might even earn you a red envelope!

Example

爷爷奶奶，给您拜年了！祝您健康长寿！
yé ye nǎi nai， gěi nín bài nián le！ zhù nín jiàn kāng cháng shòu！
Grandpa, Grandma, Happy New Year! Wishing you good health and a long life!
