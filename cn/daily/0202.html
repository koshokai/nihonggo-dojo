Chinese phrase of the day · February 2 · Season

恭喜发财！

gōng xǐ fā cái！

May you prosper!

The classic Spring Festival blessing, wishing for good business and wealth. It's a running joke for kids to add 「恭喜发财，红包拿来！」 ("now hand over the red envelope!").

Example

恭喜发财！祝你今年生意越做越好！
gōng xǐ fā cái！ zhù nǐ jīn nián shēng yi yuè zuò yuè hǎo！
May you prosper! I hope your business does better and better this year!
