Chinese phrase of the day · February 3 · Internet slang

抢到红包了！

qiǎng dào hóng bāo le！

I grabbed a red envelope!

Grabbing a digital red envelope 「红包」 handed out in a group chat, first come, first served. The amounts are random, family and friend groups go wild over it at Spring Festival, and even a small amount gets celebrated as 「手气好」 (lucky).

Example

群里有人发红包，我抢到了五块钱！
qún lǐ yǒu rén fā hóng bāo， wǒ qiǎng dào le wǔ kuài qián！
Someone in the group sent red envelopes, and I grabbed five yuan!
