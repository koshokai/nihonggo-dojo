Chinese phrase of the day · February 4 · Season

年年有余！

nián nián yǒu yú！

May you have surplus every year!

Because 「余」 (surplus) sounds the same as 「鱼」 (fish), fish is a must on the Spring Festival table. Leaving some of the fish uneaten instead of finishing it is also done for good luck.

Example

除夕的鱼留一点，年年有余嘛。
chú xī de yú liú yì diǎn， nián nián yǒu yú ma。
Let's leave a bit of the New Year's Eve fish, for surplus every year.
