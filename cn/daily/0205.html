Chinese phrase of the day · February 5 · Season

万事如意！

wàn shì rú yì！

May everything go as you wish!

A standard blessing at Spring Festival and other celebrations; pairing it as 「身体健康，万事如意！」 makes it sound like a proper greeting. It works in New Year cards and messages and is safe to use with superiors.

Example

祝您新的一年身体健康，万事如意！
zhù nín xīn de yì nián shēn tǐ jiàn kāng， wàn shì rú yì！
Wishing you good health and that everything goes your way in the new year!
