Chinese phrase of the day · February 6 · Feelings

哇塞！

wā sāi！

Wow! Amazing!

An exclamation of surprise or delight, for beautiful scenery, a lavish meal or a surprising story. It sounds young and casual, close to "Whoa!" or "No way, that's amazing!" in English.

Example

哇塞，这烟花也太漂亮了吧！
wā sāi， zhè yān huā yě tài piào liang le ba！
Wow, these fireworks are way too beautiful!
