Chinese phrase of the day · February 7 · Season

心想事成！

xīn xiǎng shì chéng！

May all your wishes come true!

A blessing meaning "may what you wish for in your heart come true." It's common in New Year and birthday messages, works with friends and superiors alike, and is a perfect encouragement for someone taking exams or job hunting.

Example

新年快乐！祝你心想事成！
xīn nián kuài lè！ zhù nǐ xīn xiǎng shì chéng！
Happy New Year! May all your wishes come true!
