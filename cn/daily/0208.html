Chinese phrase of the day · February 8 · Travel

玩得开心！

wán de kāi xīn！

Have fun!

A send-off for someone going on a trip or an outing, the equivalent of the English "Have fun!" A natural reply is something like 「谢谢！给你带礼物」 ("I'll bring you a souvenir").

Example

明天就去海南了？真羡慕，玩得开心！
míng tiān jiù qù hǎi nán le？ zhēn xiàn mù， wán de kāi xīn！
You're off to Hainan tomorrow? I'm so jealous. Have fun!
