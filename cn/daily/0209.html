Chinese phrase of the day · February 9 · Season

越来越没年味儿了。

yuè lái yuè méi nián wèir le。

New Year feels less and less festive.

「年味儿」 means the festive Spring Festival atmosphere. People who feel the celebrations have become simpler than in the past often say this, in nostalgic small talk usually followed by 「小时候的年才有意思」.

Example

现在大家都在看手机，越来越没年味儿了。
xiàn zài dà jiā dōu zài kàn shǒu jī， yuè lái yuè méi nián wèir le。
Now everyone's just looking at their phones. New Year feels less and less festive.
