Chinese phrase of the day · February 10 · Shopping

便宜一点吧。

pián yi yì diǎn ba。

Can you make it a bit cheaper?

The standard haggling line; making it a question, 「能便宜点吗？」, sounds softer. At markets and small shops, just asking can get you a little off.

Example

老板，买两个，便宜一点吧？
lǎo bǎn， mǎi liǎng ge， pián yi yì diǎn ba？
Boss, I'm buying two, can you make it a bit cheaper?
