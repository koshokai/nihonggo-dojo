Chinese phrase of the day · February 11 · Work

开工大吉！

kāi gōng dà jí！

Here's to a great start back at work!

A greeting exchanged on the first working day after Spring Festival. At some companies the boss hands out a 「开工红包」 (back-to-work red envelope) to staff, and sending coworkers 「新年开工大吉！」 is a standard message.

Example

大家新年开工大吉，今年也一起加油！
dà jiā xīn nián kāi gōng dà jí， jīn nián yě yì qǐ jiā yóu！
Everyone, here's to a great start back at work. Let's give it our all together again this year!
