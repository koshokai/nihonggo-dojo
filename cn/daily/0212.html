Chinese phrase of the day · February 12 · Friends

你真行！

nǐ zhēn xíng！

You're amazing! Nice one!

Praise for a friend who has pulled something off. Depending on tone it can mean "you're unbelievable" in an exasperated way, so say it clearly with a smile.

Example

你一个人做了这么大一桌菜？你真行！
nǐ yí ge rén zuò le zhè me dà yì zhuō cài？ nǐ zhēn xíng！
You made this whole table of food by yourself? You're amazing!
