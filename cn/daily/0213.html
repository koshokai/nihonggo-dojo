Chinese phrase of the day · February 13 · Feelings

吓我一跳！

xià wǒ yí tiào！

You scared me!

For when someone suddenly speaks to you or startles you. 「一跳」 means "one jump," jumping out of your skin in surprise, and it's a very common everyday expression.

Example

你怎么突然在我后面？吓我一跳！
nǐ zěn me tū rán zài wǒ hòu mian？ xià wǒ yí tiào！
Why are you suddenly behind me? You scared me!
