Chinese phrase of the day · February 14 · Season

情人节快乐！

qíng rén jié kuài lè！

Happy Valentine's Day!

In China, Valentine's Day usually means men giving women flowers or gifts, and there's almost no custom of obligatory chocolate for coworkers. Qixi is also called "Chinese Valentine's Day."

Example

亲爱的，情人节快乐！这束花送给你。
qīn ài de， qíng rén jié kuài lè！ zhè shù huā sòng gěi nǐ。
Happy Valentine's Day, darling! These flowers are for you.
