Chinese phrase of the day · February 15 · Idiom

一见钟情

yí jiàn zhōng qíng

love at first sight

Falling in love at first glance, and it can be used for things and places as well as people. It comes up often in conversation, as in 「我对这件衣服一见钟情」 ("I fell in love with this top at first sight").

Example

我在店里看到这个杯子，一见钟情，马上买了。
wǒ zài diàn lǐ kàn dào zhè ge bēi zi， yí jiàn zhōng qíng， mǎ shàng mǎi le。
I saw this mug in the shop, fell in love with it at first sight and bought it right away.
