Chinese phrase of the day · February 16 · Internet slang

真香

zhēn xiāng

(In the end) it's actually amazing!

Internet slang that laughs at someone who swore they'd never do something and ends up loving it, and it isn't limited to food. Used about yourself, it's a self-deprecating punchline: "I take it back, I'm hooked."

Example

说好不吃夜宵的，结果一口下去，真香！
shuō hǎo bù chī yè xiāo de， jié guǒ yì kǒu xià qu， zhēn xiāng！
I said I wouldn't eat a late-night snack, but after one bite... so good!
