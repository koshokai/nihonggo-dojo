Chinese phrase of the day · February 17 · Food

有什么推荐的吗？

yǒu shén me tuī jiàn de ma？

Do you have any recommendations?

A lifesaver when you can't read the menu or can't decide; asking 「招牌菜（看板料理）是什么？」 ("what's your signature dish?") is also a good idea. It can lead you to local flavors when traveling.

Example

我第一次来，有什么推荐的吗？
wǒ dì yī cì lái， yǒu shén me tuī jiàn de ma？
It's my first time here. Do you have any recommendations?
