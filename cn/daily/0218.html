Chinese phrase of the day · February 18 · Work

我想请个假。

wǒ xiǎng qǐng ge jià。

I'd like to take some time off.

The basic phrase for asking your boss for leave; 「请假」 means "to take time off." You can add the number of days, as in 「请一天假」, or give a reason such as 「病假」 (sick leave) or 「事假」 (personal leave).

Example

经理，我明天想请个假，去医院看看。
jīng lǐ， wǒ míng tiān xiǎng qǐng ge jià， qù yī yuàn kàn kan。
Manager, I'd like to take tomorrow off to go to the hospital.
