Chinese phrase of the day · February 19 · Travel

几点出发？

jǐ diǎn chū fā？

What time are we leaving?

For checking plans with travel companions; answer with something like 「八点出发」. To ask where to meet, say 「在哪儿集合？」, and since you'll hear these often on tours, learn to catch them.

Example

明天几点出发？要在酒店门口集合吗？
míng tiān jǐ diǎn chū fā？ yào zài jiǔ diàn mén kǒu jí hé ma？
What time are we leaving tomorrow? Are we meeting at the hotel entrance?
