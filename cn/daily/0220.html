Chinese phrase of the day · February 20 · Idiom

一言为定！

yì yán wéi dìng！

It's a deal!

Confirming a promise you won't go back on; when making plans with a friend, you seal it with 「那就一言为定！」. It's close to English "It's a deal!" or "You have my word," and as casual as a pinky promise.

Example

下周六一起去爬山，一言为定！
xià zhōu liù yì qǐ qù pá shān， yì yán wéi dìng！
Hiking together next Saturday, it's a deal!
