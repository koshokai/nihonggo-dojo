Chinese phrase of the day · February 21 · Work

期待您的回复。

qī dài nín de huí fù。

I look forward to your reply.

A standard closing line for business emails, with 「您」 adding politeness. It works just like "I look forward to hearing from you" in English.

Example

资料已发送，期待您的回复。
zī liào yǐ fā sòng， qī dài nín de huí fù。
I've sent the materials. I look forward to your reply.
