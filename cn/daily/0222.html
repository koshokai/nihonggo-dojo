Chinese phrase of the day · February 22 · Food

点外卖吧。

diǎn wài mài ba。

Let's order delivery.

The go-to suggestion when you can't be bothered to cook. In China, 「外卖」, ordering food on your phone and getting it delivered fast, is an essential part of life, and ordering lunch together at the office is called 「拼外卖」.

Example

今天太累了，不想做饭，点外卖吧。
jīn tiān tài lèi le， bù xiǎng zuò fàn， diǎn wài mài ba。
I'm too tired today, I don't feel like cooking. Let's order delivery.
