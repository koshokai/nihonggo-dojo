Chinese phrase of the day · February 23 · Season

元宵节快乐！

yuán xiāo jié kuài lè！

Happy Lantern Festival!

The festival on the 15th day of the first lunar month that closes the Spring Festival season. People eat the sweet rice balls 「元宵」 or 「汤圆」 and enjoy lanterns, a warm occasion symbolizing family togetherness.

Example

元宵节快乐！晚上一起去看花灯吧。
yuán xiāo jié kuài lè！ wǎn shang yì qǐ qù kàn huā dēng ba。
Happy Lantern Festival! Let's go see the lanterns together tonight.
