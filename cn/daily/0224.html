Chinese phrase of the day · February 24 · Internet slang

我是i人。

wǒ shì i rén。

I'm an introvert.

From the I (introvert) and E (extrovert) of personality tests: 「i人」 and 「e人」 jokingly describe your type. It's hugely popular with young people in self-introductions and small talk.

Example

我是i人，人多的聚会有点紧张。
wǒ shì i rén， rén duō de jù huì yǒu diǎn jǐn zhāng。
I'm an introvert, so big parties make me a bit nervous.
