Chinese phrase of the day · February 25 · Travel

手机没电了。

shǒu jī méi diàn le。

My phone's dead.

A common crisis when traveling or out and about, and a serious one in China, where people rely on their phones for payments and maps. It helps to follow up with 「能借个充电宝吗？」 ("can I borrow a power bank?"), and many shops rent them.

Example

糟了，手机没电了，没法付钱了！
zāo le， shǒu jī méi diàn le， méi fǎ fù qián le！
Oh no, my phone's dead, I can't pay!
