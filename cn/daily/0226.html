Chinese phrase of the day · February 26 · Shopping

有优惠吗？

yǒu yōu huì ma？

Are there any discounts?

A line for checking for discounts or promotions in a shop. 「优惠」 covers discounts and perks in general, and asking this sounds nicer than haggling; 「会员有优惠吗？」 ("is there a member discount?") and 「有优惠券吗？」 ("do you have coupons?") are handy too.

Example

这个套餐现在有优惠吗？
zhè ge tào cān xiàn zài yǒu yōu huì ma？
Is there any discount on this set meal right now?
