Chinese phrase of the day · February 27 · Internet slang

佛系

fó xì

laid-back, not pushy

Internet slang for being as unattached to things as the Buddha, used in phrases like 「佛系青年」 and 「佛系养生」. It's lighter than 「躺平」, perfect for jokingly describing a relaxed attitude of being content with "good enough."

Example

我现在很佛系，考多少分都行。
wǒ xiàn zài hěn fó xì， kǎo duō shao fēn dōu xíng。
I'm very laid-back now. Whatever score I get is fine.
