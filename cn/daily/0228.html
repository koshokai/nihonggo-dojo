Chinese phrase of the day · February 28 · Feelings

饿死我了！

è sǐ wǒ le！

I'm starving!

「饿」 means hungry, and 「〜死我了」 is a colloquial intensifier, "... to death." It's more dramatic than 「好饿啊」, perfect for grumbling before a meal, and similar forms like 「累死我了」 and 「笑死我了」 are common too.

Example

饿死我了，中午还没吃饭呢。
è sǐ wǒ le， zhōng wǔ hái méi chī fàn ne。
I'm starving, I haven't even had lunch yet.
