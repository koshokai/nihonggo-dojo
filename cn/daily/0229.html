Chinese phrase of the day · February 29 · Idiom

千载难逢

qiān zǎi nán féng

a once-in-a-lifetime chance

An opportunity so precious it comes along only once in a thousand years, much like "a chance of a lifetime" in English. It's an exaggeration, so using it jokingly to praise a sale or a lucky break makes it fun.

Example

这么好的机会千载难逢，你一定要试试！
zhè me hǎo de jī huì qiān zǎi nán féng， nǐ yí dìng yào shì shi！
A chance this good comes once in a lifetime. You've got to give it a try!
