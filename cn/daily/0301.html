Chinese phrase of the day · March 1 · Work

人到齐了吗？

rén dào qí le ma？

Is everyone here?

Checking numbers before a meeting, a dinner or a tour sets off; 「到齐」 means "everyone has arrived." Once they have, continue with 「到齐了，开始吧！」, and it's handy to know when you're the organizer or host.

Example

人到齐了吗？到齐了我们就开会吧。
rén dào qí le ma？ dào qí le wǒ men jiù kāi huì ba。
Is everyone here? If so, let's start the meeting.
