Chinese phrase of the day · March 2 · Idiom

一年之计在于春

yì nián zhī jì zài yú chūn

The year's plans are made in spring

A saying that the year's plans should be made in spring. It's like making New Year's resolutions, except in China it's tied to the beginning of spring, so use it to encourage a friend starting a new habit.

Example

一年之计在于春，我们开始每天跑步吧！
yì nián zhī jì zài yú chūn， wǒ men kāi shǐ měi tiān pǎo bù ba！
The year's plans are made in spring. Let's start running every day!
