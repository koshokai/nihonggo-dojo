Chinese phrase of the day · March 3 · Greeting

吃了吗？

chī le ma？

Have you eaten?

A greeting closer to "How are you?" than a real question about food, often used by older people and neighbors. A light 「吃了，你呢？」 is a perfectly good reply.

Example

王大爷，吃了吗？今天天气真不错。
wáng dà ye， chī le ma？ jīn tiān tiān qì zhēn bú cuò。
Mr. Wang, have you eaten? The weather's really nice today.
