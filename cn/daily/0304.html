Chinese phrase of the day · March 4 · Greeting

打扰一下。

dǎ rǎo yí xià。

Sorry to bother you.

A polite lead-in when you speak to someone or interrupt someone at work, close to "Sorry to bother you" or "Excuse me for interrupting" in English. To ask for directions, 「请问」 is natural; for someone in the middle of a task, 「打扰一下」.

Example

打扰一下，这个会议室现在有人用吗？
dǎ rǎo yí xià， zhè ge huì yì shì xiàn zài yǒu rén yòng ma？
Sorry to bother you, is anyone using this meeting room right now?
