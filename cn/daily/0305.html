Chinese phrase of the day · March 5 · Food

好吃！

hǎo chī！

Delicious!

The basic way to praise the taste of food; the key point is that for drinks you say 「好喝」 instead. Strengthening it to 「太好吃了！」 or 「超好吃！」 will please whoever treated you.

Example

这家的红烧肉真好吃！
zhè jiā de hóng shāo ròu zhēn hǎo chī！
The red-braised pork here is really delicious!
