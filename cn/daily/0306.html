Chinese phrase of the day · March 6 · Season

倒春寒

dào chūn hán

a spring cold snap

A sudden return of cold after it has warmed up, a word often heard in weather forecasts and family conversations. There's also the saying 「春捂秋冻」 (dress warmly in spring, lightly in autumn), and people will warn you not to switch to light clothes too early.

Example

最近倒春寒，别急着把厚衣服收起来。
zuì jìn dào chūn hán， bié jí zhe bǎ hòu yī fu shōu qǐ lai。
There's been a cold snap lately, so don't rush to put your warm clothes away.
