Chinese phrase of the day · March 7 · Internet slang

种草

zhòng cǎo

to be tempted by a recommendation ("planting grass")

Being made to think "I want that!" by someone's post or review; 「被种草了」 means "now I want it." It's a word that symbolizes the culture of product word-of-mouth.

Example

看了你的推荐，我又被种草了！
kàn le nǐ de tuī jiàn， wǒ yòu bèi zhòng cǎo le！
After seeing your recommendation, I'm tempted all over again!
