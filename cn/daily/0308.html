Chinese phrase of the day · March 8 · Season

女神节快乐！

nǚ shén jié kuài lè！

Happy Women's Day!

An affectionate name used by younger people and shops for 「妇女节」, International Women's Day on March 8. At some companies female employees get half a day off, or flowers and gifts are handed out.

Example

今天三八，祝各位女士女神节快乐！
jīn tiān sān bā， zhù gè wèi nǚ shì nǚ shén jié kuài lè！
Today is March 8. Happy Women's Day to all the ladies!
