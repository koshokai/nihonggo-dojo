Chinese phrase of the day · March 9 · Travel

我迷路了。

wǒ mí lù le。

I'm lost.

A line for asking for help when you're in trouble; following it up by showing the address of your destination on your phone makes things smooth. People in China will often kindly help you.

Example

不好意思，我迷路了，这里是哪儿？
bù hǎo yì si， wǒ mí lù le， zhè lǐ shì nǎr？
Excuse me, I'm lost. Where is this?
