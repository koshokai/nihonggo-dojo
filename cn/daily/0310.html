Chinese phrase of the day · March 10 · Friends

包在我身上！

bāo zài wǒ shēn shàng！

Leave it to me!

A reassuring line for fully taking on a request. Literally "wrap it on my body," it means taking responsibility, and it's more proactive than 「放心吧」; reply with 「那就拜托你了！」 ("then I'm counting on you!").

Example

搬家的事包在我身上，你就放心吧！
bān jiā de shì bāo zài wǒ shēn shàng， nǐ jiù fàng xīn ba！
Leave the move to me, don't worry!
