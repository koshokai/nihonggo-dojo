Chinese phrase of the day · March 11 · Feelings

绝了！

jué le！

Amazing! / Incredible!

Praise for food, scenery, a song and so on that's "just too good." It's young people's slang, close to "epic!" or "insane (in a good way)" in English, and it can also be used sarcastically.

Example

这家的小笼包绝了，一定要尝尝！
zhè jiā de xiǎo lóng bāo jué le， yí dìng yào cháng chang！
The soup dumplings here are incredible, you have to try them!
