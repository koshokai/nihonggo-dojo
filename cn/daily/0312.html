Chinese phrase of the day · March 12 · Idiom

一分钱一分货

yì fēn qián yì fēn huò

You get what you pay for

It means quality matches price, and expensive things are expensive for a reason. It's very common in shopping conversations, and you can also say it to yourself as a lesson after a cheap choice goes wrong.

Example

这双鞋便宜，但一分钱一分货，穿了一个月就坏了。
zhè shuāng xié pián yi， dàn yì fēn qián yì fēn huò， chuān le yí ge yuè jiù huài le。
These shoes were cheap, but you get what you pay for: they fell apart after a month.
