Chinese phrase of the day · March 13 · Work

我先走了。

wǒ xiān zǒu le。

I'm heading out now.

A goodbye when leaving work. It isn't as fixed a ritual phrase as in some cultures, so 「我先走了，明天见」 is plenty with coworkers; the others reply 「慢走」 or 「拜拜」.

Example

今天我有点事，我先走了，大家辛苦了！
jīn tiān wǒ yǒu diǎn shì， wǒ xiān zǒu le， dà jiā xīn kǔ le！
I have something on today, so I'm heading out. Thanks for your hard work, everyone!
