Chinese phrase of the day · March 14 · Feelings

气死我了！

qì sǐ wǒ le！

That makes me so mad!

What slips out when you're angry; 「〜死我了」 is the intensifier pattern. It works for anything from real anger to a half-joking grumble, and to calm someone down you say 「别生气了」 ("don't be angry").

Example

他又迟到了一个小时，气死我了！
tā yòu chí dào le yí ge xiǎo shí， qì sǐ wǒ le！
He was an hour late again. That makes me so mad!
