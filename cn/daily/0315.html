Chinese phrase of the day · March 15 · Food

不要香菜。

bú yào xiāng cài。

No cilantro, please.

Noodles and small dishes often come with cilantro, so if you don't like it, be sure to say so when ordering. If you love it, ask for 「多放香菜」 and they'll add plenty.

Example

一碗牛肉面，不要香菜，谢谢。
yì wǎn niú ròu miàn， bú yào xiāng cài， xiè xie。
One bowl of beef noodles, no cilantro, thanks.
