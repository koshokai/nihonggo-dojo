Chinese phrase of the day · March 16 · Internet slang

上头

shàng tou

hooked, can't stop

Originally it meant alcohol going to your head. Now it's internet slang for being so absorbed in a drama, game or food that you can't stop, and the classic phrase is 「越看越上头」 ("the more I watch, the more hooked I get").

Example

这部剧越看越上头，一晚上看了八集。
zhè bù jù yuè kàn yuè shàng tou， yì wǎn shang kàn le bā jí。
The more I watch this show, the more hooked I get. I watched eight episodes in one night.
