Chinese phrase of the day · March 17 · Idiom

井底之蛙

jǐng dǐ zhī wā

a frog in a well

A metaphor for narrow-mindedness, like a frog at the bottom of a well, an image from an ancient Chinese story. Saying it about someone else is rude, so it sounds natural used modestly about yourself: 「我是井底之蛙」.

Example

出国以后才发现自己以前是井底之蛙。
chū guó yǐ hòu cái fā xiàn zì jǐ yǐ qián shì jǐng dǐ zhī wā。
It wasn't until I went abroad that I realized I'd been a frog in a well.
