Chinese phrase of the day · March 18 · Travel

在哪儿换乘？

zài nǎr huàn chéng？

Where do I transfer?

A way to ask about changing subway lines or buses; 「换乘」 means "to transfer." It's also written on station signs, so being able to read it helps, and if you ask 「在哪站换乘？」 people will answer with the station name.

Example

请问去机场的话，在哪儿换乘？
qǐng wèn qù jī chǎng de huà， zài nǎr huàn chéng？
Excuse me, where do I transfer to get to the airport?
