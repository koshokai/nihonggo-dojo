Chinese phrase of the day · March 19 · Greeting

最近怎么样？

zuì jìn zěn me yàng？

How have you been lately?

A line for asking a friend or acquaintance you haven't seen for a while how they're doing. Standard replies are 「还行」 (not bad), 「挺好的」 (pretty good) and 「有点忙」 (a bit busy).

Example

最近怎么样？工作还那么忙吗？
zuì jìn zěn me yàng？ gōng zuò hái nà me máng ma？
How have you been? Is work still as busy as ever?
