Chinese phrase of the day · March 20 · Shopping

我再看看。

wǒ zài kàn kan。

I'll keep looking.

For when a shop assistant makes a recommendation but you're not ready to decide; literally "I'll look a bit more," it turns them down without awkwardness. 「我就看看」 is for when you never intended to buy, while this one is for when you're undecided.

Example

谢谢，我再看看，先不买了。
xiè xie， wǒ zài kàn kan， xiān bù mǎi le。
Thanks, I'll think about it a bit more. I won't buy it for now.
