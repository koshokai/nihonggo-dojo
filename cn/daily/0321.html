Chinese phrase of the day · March 21 · Friends

他很靠谱。

tā hěn kào pǔ。

He's reliable.

「靠谱」 is colloquial for trustworthy, dependable, and it works for both people and plans. The opposite is 「不靠谱」 (unreliable), and it's a very frequently used word.

Example

这件事交给他吧，他很靠谱。
zhè jiàn shì jiāo gěi tā ba， tā hěn kào pǔ。
Let's leave this to him. He's reliable.
