Chinese phrase of the day · March 22 · Food

再来一碗！

zài lái yì wǎn！

Another bowl, please!

A line for asking for seconds of rice or noodles. 「再来一〜」 means "one more ...," so you can order more of anything, as in 「再来一瓶啤酒」 or 「再来一个菜」, and saying it in someone's home really pleases the cook.

Example

阿姨，您做的面太好吃了，再来一碗！
ā yí， nín zuò de miàn tài hǎo chī le， zài lái yì wǎn！
Auntie, your noodles are so good. Another bowl, please!
