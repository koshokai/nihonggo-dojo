Chinese phrase of the day · March 23 · Work

请多指教。

qǐng duō zhǐ jiào。

I look forward to learning from you.

A greeting to a team you've just joined or to senior colleagues, also said as 「多多指教」. It conveys humility and is a perfect way to end a self-introduction.

Example

我是新来的小李，请多指教！
wǒ shì xīn lái de xiǎo lǐ， qǐng duō zhǐ jiào！
I'm Xiao Li, the new one here. I look forward to learning from you!
