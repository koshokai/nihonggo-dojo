Chinese phrase of the day · March 24 · Season

春困秋乏

chūn kùn qiū fá

sleepy in spring, sluggish in autumn

A saying about feeling sleepy or sluggish when the seasons change, much like "spring fever" in English. Some people use it jokingly as an excuse for nodding off in a meeting.

Example

最近老想睡觉，真是春困秋乏啊。
zuì jìn lǎo xiǎng shuì jiào， zhēn shì chūn kùn qiū fá a。
I'm always sleepy lately. Spring drowsiness really is a thing.
