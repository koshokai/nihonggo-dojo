Chinese phrase of the day · March 25 · Internet slang

点个赞！

diǎn ge zàn！

Like! / Thumbs up!

Hitting "like" on social media, and by extension saying "nice!" out loud to praise someone. 「给你点赞」 expresses "I applaud you."

Example

你做的视频太有意思了，给你点个赞！
nǐ zuò de shì pín tài yǒu yì si le， gěi nǐ diǎn ge zàn！
Your video is so funny, I'm giving it a like!
