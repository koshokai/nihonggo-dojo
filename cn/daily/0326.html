Chinese phrase of the day · March 26 · Feelings

好紧张啊。

hǎo jǐn zhāng a。

I'm so nervous.

Before an exam, interview, presentation and the like. 「紧张」 means "nervous," but it is also used to mean "tight, pressed (for time or money)"; to encourage someone, say 「别紧张，放松点」 ("relax").

Example

马上要上台演讲了，好紧张啊。
mǎ shàng yào shàng tái yǎn jiǎng le， hǎo jǐn zhāng a。
I'm about to go on stage to give my speech. I'm so nervous.
