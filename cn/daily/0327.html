Chinese phrase of the day · March 27 · Shopping

可以扫码吗？

kě yǐ sǎo mǎ ma？

Can I pay by QR code?

Mobile payment is the norm in China, and some services let travelers link a foreign card. When the shop scans your code instead, check with 「我扫你还是你扫我？」.

Example

老板，可以扫码吗？我没带现金。
lǎo bǎn， kě yǐ sǎo mǎ ma？ wǒ méi dài xiàn jīn。
Excuse me, can I pay by scanning? I don't have cash on me.
