Chinese phrase of the day · March 28 · Work

已经发您邮箱了。

yǐ jīng fā nín yóu xiāng le。

I've sent it to your email.

A standard business phrase for saying you've sent documents or files; 「发您邮箱」 means "send to your email address." Adding 「请查收」 ("please check it") at the end is more polite.

Example

报价单已经发您邮箱了，请查收。
bào jià dān yǐ jīng fā nín yóu xiāng le， qǐng chá shōu。
I've emailed you the quotation. Please check it.
