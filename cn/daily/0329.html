Chinese phrase of the day · March 29 · Travel

风景真美！

fēng jǐng zhēn měi！

The scenery is so beautiful!

For when you come across a stunning view while traveling. 「美」 can praise a wide range of things, from scenery to people to photos, and sharing the moment with your companions makes the trip even more fun.

Example

山顶的风景真美，爬上来值了！
shān dǐng de fēng jǐng zhēn měi， pá shàng lai zhí le！
The view from the top is gorgeous. The climb was worth it!
