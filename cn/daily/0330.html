Chinese phrase of the day · March 30 · Season

一起去看樱花吧！

yì qǐ qù kàn yīng huā ba！

Let's go see the cherry blossoms!

In China too, famous cherry blossom spots are hugely popular in spring, and blossom festivals are held all over. Rather than picnicking and partying under the trees, though, most people stroll and take photos; 「赏花」 means "flower viewing."

Example

听说公园的樱花开了，周末一起去看樱花吧！
tīng shuō gōng yuán de yīng huā kāi le， zhōu mò yì qǐ qù kàn yīng huā ba！
I heard the cherry blossoms in the park are out. Let's go see them this weekend!
