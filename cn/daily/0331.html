Chinese phrase of the day · March 31 · Season

春暖花开

chūn nuǎn huā kāi

warm spring days and blooming flowers

A four-character phrase for the arrival of spring, handy for seasonal greetings in letters and on social media. You can also use it in conversation, as in 「春暖花开的时候，一起去旅游吧！」.

Example

春暖花开的时候，我们一起去杭州吧！
chūn nuǎn huā kāi de shí hou， wǒ men yì qǐ qù háng zhōu ba！
When spring comes and the flowers bloom, let's go to Hangzhou together!
