Chinese phrase of the day · April 1 · Feelings

别骗我了！

bié piàn wǒ le！

Stop pulling my leg!

A reply of "No way, really?" when you hear something unbelievable, and perfect for April Fools' Day, 「愚人节」. It isn't a strong denial and is usually said laughing; 「你骗人！」 sounds a bit childish.

Example

你中彩票了？今天愚人节，别骗我了！
nǐ zhòng cǎi piào le？ jīn tiān yú rén jié， bié piàn wǒ le！
You won the lottery? It's April Fools' Day today, stop pulling my leg!
