Chinese phrase of the day · April 2 · Idiom

画蛇添足

huà shé tiān zú

to overdo it ("drawing legs on a snake")

Ruining something by adding what isn't needed, from the story of someone who drew legs on a snake. It's often used when talking about polishing a presentation or a piece of writing.

Example

结尾这句话有点画蛇添足，删掉吧。
jié wěi zhè jù huà yǒu diǎn huà shé tiān zú， shān diào ba。
That last sentence is a bit unnecessary. Let's cut it.
