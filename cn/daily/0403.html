Chinese phrase of the day · April 3 · Internet slang

社牛

shè niú

a social butterfly

Short for 「社交牛人」, someone who can talk to strangers without any hesitation. It arose as the opposite of 「社恐」 (socially anxious), and it can be praise or gentle teasing.

Example

他真是社牛，第一天就跟全班同学聊熟了。
tā zhēn shì shè niú， dì yī tiān jiù gēn quán bān tóng xué liáo shú le。
He's a real social butterfly. He got to know the whole class on the first day.
