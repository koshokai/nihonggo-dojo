Chinese phrase of the day · April 4 · Season

清明安康

qīng míng ān kāng

Wishing you peace and health this Qingming

Qingming in early April is a day for visiting graves and remembering ancestors, so instead of 「快乐」 people usually say 「安康」. Remember it's not a celebration but a quiet greeting wishing someone well.

Example

清明回老家扫墓，祝大家清明安康。
qīng míng huí lǎo jiā sǎo mù， zhù dà jiā qīng míng ān kāng。
I'm going back to my hometown to sweep the graves for Qingming. Wishing you all peace and health.
