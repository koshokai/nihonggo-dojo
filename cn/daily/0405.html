Chinese phrase of the day · April 5 · Friends

她是我闺蜜。

tā shì wǒ guī mì。

She's my best friend.

「闺蜜」 is a woman's best girlfriend; best friends between men are 「哥们儿」 or 「好兄弟」. It's a warm word for introducing your best friend.

Example

这是我闺蜜小林，我们从小一起长大。
zhè shì wǒ guī mì xiǎo lín， wǒ men cóng xiǎo yì qǐ zhǎng dà。
This is my best friend Xiao Lin. We grew up together.
