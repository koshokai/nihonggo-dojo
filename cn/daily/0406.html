Chinese phrase of the day · April 6 · Season

周末去踏青吧！

zhōu mò qù tà qīng ba！

Let's go on a spring outing this weekend!

「踏青」 means heading out to the countryside in spring to enjoy the fresh greenery, the classic outing around Qingming. It's a springlike word for inviting someone on a picnic or hike, and 「春游」 means something similar, often referring to a school outing.

Example

天气这么好，周末去郊外踏青吧！
tiān qì zhè me hǎo， zhōu mò qù jiāo wài tà qīng ba！
The weather's so nice, let's go for a spring outing in the countryside this weekend!
