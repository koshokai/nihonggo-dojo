Chinese phrase of the day · April 7 · Food

要一杯珍珠奶茶。

yào yì bēi zhēn zhū nǎi chá。

One bubble tea, please.

An ordering phrase at drink shops. Part of the fun in China is specifying details afterwards: 「少糖」 (less sugar), 「去冰」 (no ice), 「热的」 (hot).

Example

要一杯珍珠奶茶，少糖去冰。
yào yì bēi zhēn zhū nǎi chá， shǎo táng qù bīng。
One pearl milk tea, less sugar, no ice.
