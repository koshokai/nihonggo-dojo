Chinese phrase of the day · April 8 · Friends

我来接你。

wǒ lái jiē nǐ。

I'll pick you up.

Offering to pick someone up at the station, airport or home. Picking up friends and family is an important part of hospitality in China; to decline, say 「不用了，我自己过去就行」 ("no need, I can get there myself").

Example

你几点到车站？我开车来接你。
nǐ jǐ diǎn dào chē zhàn？ wǒ kāi chē lái jiē nǐ。
What time do you get to the station? I'll drive over and pick you up.
