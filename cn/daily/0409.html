Chinese phrase of the day · April 9 · Feelings

好可惜啊。

hǎo kě xī a。

What a shame. / What a waste.

For when a chance slips away or plans get canceled. 「可惜」 covers both "too bad" and "what a waste," which makes it a very handy word.

Example

演唱会取消了？好可惜啊。
yǎn chàng huì qǔ xiāo le？ hǎo kě xī a。
The concert was canceled? What a shame.
