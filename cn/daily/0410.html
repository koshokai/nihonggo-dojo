Chinese phrase of the day · April 10 · Food

你有什么忌口吗？

nǐ yǒu shén me jì kǒu ma？

Is there anything you don't eat?

A considerate question before inviting someone to eat or ordering, asking what they can't eat. 「忌口」 means foods avoided for health, religious or personal reasons; answers include 「我不吃辣」 or 「我对海鲜过敏」 ("I'm allergic to seafood").

Example

点菜之前问一下，你有什么忌口吗？
diǎn cài zhī qián wèn yí xià， nǐ yǒu shén me jì kǒu ma？
Before we order, is there anything you don't eat?
