Chinese phrase of the day · April 11 · Internet slang

安利一下！

ān lì yí xià！

You've got to try this!

Eagerly recommending something you love to others, as in 「给你安利一部剧」 ("let me recommend a drama to you"). It's close to English "evangelizing" or "pushing" something you're a fan of.

Example

给你安利一家超好吃的面馆！
gěi nǐ ān lì yì jiā chāo hǎo chī de miàn guǎn！
Let me recommend a super tasty noodle shop to you!
