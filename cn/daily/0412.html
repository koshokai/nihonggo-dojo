Chinese phrase of the day · April 12 · Greeting

到家了说一声。

dào jiā le shuō yì shēng。

Let me know when you get home.

A caring line when seeing off a friend or family member heading home late; you may also be asked 「到家了吗？」 in a chat. When you arrive, it's good manners to send a quick 「到家了，放心吧」, a gesture familiar in English too ("text me when you get home").

Example

天黑了，路上小心，到家了说一声。
tiān hēi le， lù shàng xiǎo xīn， dào jiā le shuō yì shēng。
It's dark now, be careful on the way. Let me know when you get home.
