Chinese phrase of the day · April 13 · Shopping

可以退吗？

kě yǐ tuì ma？

Can I return it?

Good to check before you buy. Online shops commonly offer 「七天无理由退货」, returns within seven days with no reason needed.

Example

这个尺码不合适的话，可以退吗？
zhè ge chǐ mǎ bù hé shì de huà， kě yǐ tuì ma？
If this size doesn't fit, can I return it?
