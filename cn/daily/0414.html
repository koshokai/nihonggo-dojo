Chinese phrase of the day · April 14 · Idiom

活到老，学到老

huó dào lǎo， xué dào lǎo

Never too old to learn

A saying meaning keep learning even as you grow old, like "you're never too old to learn" in English. Use it to praise older learners or to encourage yourself; 「学无止境」 ("learning has no end") means the same.

Example

我爸六十岁开始学英语，真是活到老，学到老。
wǒ bà liù shí suì kāi shǐ xué yīng yǔ， zhēn shì huó dào lǎo， xué dào lǎo。
My dad started learning English at sixty. Truly, you're never too old to learn.
