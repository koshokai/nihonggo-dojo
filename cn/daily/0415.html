Chinese phrase of the day · April 15 · Greeting

你先忙吧。

nǐ xiān máng ba。

I'll let you get back to it.

A considerate line for wrapping up a call or conversation, implying "you must be busy, so go ahead." When someone says it to you, reply 「好，回头聊」.

Example

你还有工作吧？那你先忙吧，回头聊！
nǐ hái yǒu gōng zuò ba？ nà nǐ xiān máng ba， huí tóu liáo！
You still have work, right? I'll let you get back to it. Talk later!
