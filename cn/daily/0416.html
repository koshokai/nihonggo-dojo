Chinese phrase of the day · April 16 · Feelings

太离谱了！

tài lí pǔ le！

That's ridiculous! / That's outrageous!

「离谱」 means far beyond common sense: prices way too high, a story that's total nonsense and so on, said when you're appalled. Online, 「离谱」 alone is a hugely popular retort, mixing surprise and exasperation.

Example

一杯咖啡要一百块？太离谱了吧！
yì bēi kā fēi yào yì bǎi kuài？ tài lí pǔ le ba！
100 yuan for a cup of coffee? That's ridiculous!
