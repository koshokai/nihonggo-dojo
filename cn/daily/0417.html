Chinese phrase of the day · April 17 · Friends

走起！

zǒu qǐ！

Let's go!

An upbeat rallying cry when setting off to hang out or eat, used a lot by young people. It has more energy than 「走吧」, close to "Let's roll!" or "Let's do this!" in English.

Example

人都到齐了吧？出发，走起！
rén dōu dào qí le ba？ chū fā， zǒu qǐ！
Is everyone here? Off we go, let's roll!
