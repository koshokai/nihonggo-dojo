Chinese phrase of the day · April 18 · Travel

我晕车。

wǒ yùn chē。

I get carsick.

Telling someone you get motion sickness; switch to 「晕船」 for boats and 「晕机」 for planes. It's useful on tour buses on mountain roads, and people will often kindly suggest 「坐前面吧」 ("sit up front").

Example

我晕车，能坐前排吗？
wǒ yùn chē， néng zuò qián pái ma？
I get carsick. Can I sit in the front row?
