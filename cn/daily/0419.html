Chinese phrase of the day · April 19 · Idiom

七上八下

qī shàng bā xià

on pins and needles

Feeling anxious and unable to settle down, used in the form 「心里七上八下的」, for example while waiting for exam results. It's close to "on pins and needles" or "a bundle of nerves" in English.

Example

等面试结果的时候，我心里七上八下的。
děng miàn shì jié guǒ de shí hou， wǒ xīn lǐ qī shàng bā xià de。
While waiting for the interview results, I was on pins and needles.
