Chinese phrase of the day · April 20 · Food

尝尝这个！

cháng chang zhè ge！

Try this!

A line for offering someone something tasty. Doubling the verb as 「尝尝」 gives a casual "have a little taste" feel, and saying it while serving food onto someone's plate is classic Chinese hospitality; 「尝一下」 means the same.

Example

这是我妈做的点心，你尝尝这个！
zhè shì wǒ mā zuò de diǎn xin， nǐ cháng chang zhè ge！
These are snacks my mom made. Try this!
