Chinese phrase of the day · April 21 · Season

带伞了吗？

dài sǎn le ma？

Did you bring an umbrella?

A caring question for family and friends in the rainy season; 「带」 means "to take along." It's a standard line as someone heads out the door, and it's especially useful in the south, where spring brings long spells of rain and changeable weather.

Example

外面要下雨了，你带伞了吗？
wài miàn yào xià yǔ le， nǐ dài sǎn le ma？
It's about to rain outside. Did you bring an umbrella?
