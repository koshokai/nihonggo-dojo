Chinese phrase of the day · April 22 · Shopping

有别的颜色吗？

yǒu bié de yán sè ma？

Do you have any other colors?

When choosing clothes or goods; you can follow up by naming a specific color, as in 「有黑色的吗？」. Learn it together with 「有大一号的吗？」 for asking about sizes, and shopping gets much smoother.

Example

这件衣服挺好看的，有别的颜色吗？
zhè jiàn yī fu tǐng hǎo kàn de， yǒu bié de yán sè ma？
This top looks really nice. Do you have it in other colors?
