Chinese phrase of the day · April 23 · Friends

别见外！

bié jiàn wài！

Don't treat me like a stranger!

Said when a friend is being overly polite or formal. 「见外」 means "to treat someone as an outsider"; it's used between closer friends than 「别客气」 and carries the warmth of "come on, it's us."

Example

别见外，咱们是老朋友了，有事就说！
bié jiàn wài， zán men shì lǎo péng you le， yǒu shì jiù shuō！
Don't be so formal, we're old friends. If you need anything, just say so!
