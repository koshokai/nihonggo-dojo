Chinese phrase of the day · April 24 · Internet slang

拿捏

ná niē

to have it nailed / to have someone wrapped around your finger

Originally "to get a feel for the right amount." Online it's popular for controlling a person or situation exactly as you like, or doing something perfectly, often as 「拿捏了」 or 「被拿捏了」 with a joking tone.

Example

这道题我已经拿捏了，考试稳了！
zhè dào tí wǒ yǐ jīng ná niē le， kǎo shì wěn le！
I've totally nailed this type of question. The exam's in the bag!
