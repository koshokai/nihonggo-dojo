Chinese phrase of the day · April 25 · Work

我这边没问题。

wǒ zhè biān méi wèn tí。

No problem on my end.

Saying there's no problem on your side in a meeting or when scheduling. 「我这边」 means "on my end" and is used constantly in business; replying 「您那边呢？」 ("how about on your end?") keeps the conversation going.

Example

时间改到周四吗？我这边没问题。
shí jiān gǎi dào zhōu sì ma？ wǒ zhè biān méi wèn tí。
Moving it to Thursday? That works for me.
