Chinese phrase of the day · April 26 · Greeting

好久没联系了。

hǎo jiǔ méi lián xì le。

It's been a while since we were in touch.

An opening line for a message to someone you haven't contacted in a while. 「好久不见」 is for meeting in person, while this one naturally fits getting in touch; follow up with 「最近好吗？」 to ask how they are.

Example

老师，好久没联系了，您最近身体好吗？
lǎo shī， hǎo jiǔ méi lián xì le， nín zuì jìn shēn tǐ hǎo ma？
Professor, it's been a while since we were in touch. Have you been well?
