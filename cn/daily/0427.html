Chinese phrase of the day · April 27 · Travel

附近有什么好玩的？

fù jìn yǒu shén me hǎo wán de？

Is there anything fun to do around here?

Asking locals or hotel staff for recommendations while traveling; 「好玩」 means "fun to visit or do." For food, switch to 「附近有什么好吃的？」, and you may get tips on places that aren't in the guidebooks.

Example

你好，附近有什么好玩的地方吗？
nǐ hǎo， fù jìn yǒu shén me hǎo wán de dì fang ma？
Hi, are there any fun places around here?
