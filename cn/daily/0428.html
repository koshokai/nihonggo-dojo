Chinese phrase of the day · April 28 · Work

又要调休了。

yòu yào tiáo xiū le。

Another make-up workday again...

In China, to make holidays longer, people work on a nearby weekend in exchange, a system called 「调休」. It can mean seven workdays in a row around a holiday, so it's a classic thing to grumble about, often heard around May Day and National Day.

Example

五一放五天，但是又要调休了，周日得上班。
wǔ yī fàng wǔ tiān， dàn shì yòu yào tiáo xiū le， zhōu rì děi shàng bān。
We get five days off for May Day, but there's another make-up workday, so I have to work Sunday.
