Chinese phrase of the day · April 29 · Greeting

幸会幸会！

xìng huì xìng huì！

A pleasure to meet you!

A greeting said with a handshake when meeting someone for the first time. It sounds a little formal, suited to business or being introduced, like "It's an honor to meet you"; between friends, the more casual 「认识你很高兴」 is natural.

Example

张总，幸会幸会！久闻您的大名。
zhāng zǒng， xìng huì xìng huì！ jiǔ wén nín de dà míng。
Mr. Zhang, a pleasure to meet you! I've long heard of you.
