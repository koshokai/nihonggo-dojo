Chinese phrase of the day · April 30 · Idiom

吃一堑，长一智

chī yí qiàn， zhǎng yí zhì

Learn from your mistakes

A saying that every stumble makes you wiser, used to encourage someone who failed or as a lesson to yourself. It's a positive expression, like "every failure is a lesson" in English.

Example

这次被骗了，吃一堑，长一智，下次小心点。
zhè cì bèi piàn le， chī yí qiàn， zhǎng yí zhì， xià cì xiǎo xīn diǎn。
I got scammed this time, but you learn from your mistakes. I'll be more careful next time.
