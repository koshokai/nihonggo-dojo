Chinese phrase of the day · May 1 · Season

五一劳动节快乐！

wǔ yī láo dòng jié kuài lè！

Happy May Day!

A greeting for Labor Day on May 1. It's a major holiday in China, and many people go traveling; the short 「五一快乐！」 is common, and asking 「五一去哪儿玩？」 about holiday plans is a standard question.

Example

祝大家五一劳动节快乐，假期愉快！
zhù dà jiā wǔ yī láo dòng jié kuài lè， jià qī yú kuài！
Happy May Day, everyone, and enjoy the holiday!
