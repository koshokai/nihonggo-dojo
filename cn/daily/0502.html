Chinese phrase of the day · May 2 · Work

有问题随时联系我。

yǒu wèn tí suí shí lián xì wǒ。

Contact me anytime if you have questions.

A line for closing an email or meeting. It's a standard business phrase that puts the other person at ease; 「随时」 means "anytime."

Example

这是我的电话，有问题随时联系我。
zhè shì wǒ de diàn huà， yǒu wèn tí suí shí lián xì wǒ。
Here's my phone number. Contact me anytime if you have any questions.
