Chinese phrase of the day · May 3 · Greeting

路上小心！

lù shàng xiǎo xīn！

Get home safe! / Take care!

A caring line when seeing off someone going out or heading home. 「慢走」 is for seeing off guests, while this one shows concern for their safety on the way, natural at night, in the rain or before a long trip; reply 「好，你也是！」.

Example

下雨了，路滑，路上小心！
xià yǔ le， lù huá， lù shàng xiǎo xīn！
It's raining and the roads are slippery. Take care on the way!
