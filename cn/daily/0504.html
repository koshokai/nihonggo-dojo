Chinese phrase of the day · May 4 · Feelings

好开心！

hǎo kāi xīn！

I'm so happy!

A word that expresses happiness and fun directly; 「开心」 covers both "happy" and "having fun." It's perfect for social media posts too.

Example

今天跟老朋友聚会，好开心！
jīn tiān gēn lǎo péng you jù huì， hǎo kāi xīn！
I got together with old friends today. I had so much fun!
