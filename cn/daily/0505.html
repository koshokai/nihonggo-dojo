Chinese phrase of the day · May 5 · Food

几位？

jǐ wèi？

How many?

The question a server is sure to ask when you walk into a restaurant. Answer using 「位」, as in 「两位」 or 「三位」, or just show the number with your fingers; 「一个人」 works too, and if you've booked, say 「我订了位子」.

Example

欢迎光临，请问几位？这边请。
huān yíng guāng lín， qǐng wèn jǐ wèi？ zhè biān qǐng。
Welcome! How many of you? This way, please.
