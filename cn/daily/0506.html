Chinese phrase of the day · May 6 · Internet slang

躺平

tǎng píng

lying flat (not overdoing it)

Literally "to lie flat." It describes stepping back from excessive competition and living without pushing yourself, often used by young people self-mockingly or as a joke, and opinions of it vary.

Example

周末我哪儿也不去，就在家躺平。
zhōu mò wǒ nǎr yě bú qù， jiù zài jiā tǎng píng。
I'm not going anywhere this weekend, just lying flat at home.
