Chinese phrase of the day · May 7 · Travel

能寄存行李吗？

néng jì cún xíng li ma？

Can I leave my luggage here?

For when you want to store luggage before check-in or after check-out at a hotel. 「寄存」 means "to leave something temporarily," stations and tourist sites have a 「寄存处」 (luggage storage), and picking it up is 「取行李」.

Example

我下午才走，能寄存一下行李吗？
wǒ xià wǔ cái zǒu， néng jì cún yí xià xíng li ma？
I'm not leaving until the afternoon. Could I leave my luggage with you?
