Chinese phrase of the day · May 8 · Idiom

对牛弹琴

duì niú tán qín

talking to a brick wall ("playing the zither to a cow")

Playing music to a cow, which won't understand it: a metaphor for talking to someone who doesn't get it. Saying it directly to someone is rude, so use it as a joke or self-mockingly.

Example

跟他讲道理简直是对牛弹琴。
gēn tā jiǎng dào li jiǎn zhí shì duì niú tán qín。
Reasoning with him is like talking to a brick wall.
