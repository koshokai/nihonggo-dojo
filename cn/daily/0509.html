Chinese phrase of the day · May 9 · Feelings

舍不得

shě bu de

can't bear to part with

Expresses finding it hard to say goodbye, or not being able to bring yourself to use something. It's used in phrases like 「舍不得你走」 ("I don't want you to go") and 「舍不得花钱」 ("I hate to spend the money"), covering more than "missing" someone.

Example

明天你就要回国了，真舍不得你。
míng tiān nǐ jiù yào huí guó le， zhēn shě bu de nǐ。
You're going back to your country tomorrow. I'm really going to miss you.
