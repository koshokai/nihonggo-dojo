Chinese phrase of the day · May 10 · Shopping

打八折。

dǎ bā zhé。

20% off

Chinese discounts are expressed as the portion you pay: 「八折」 means you pay 80% of the price, which is 20% off, and 「五折」 is half price. Be careful not to read it as 80% off!

Example

这件外套打八折，比上周便宜多了。
zhè jiàn wài tào dǎ bā zhé， bǐ shàng zhōu pián yi duō le。
This coat is 20% off. It's much cheaper than last week.
