Chinese phrase of the day · May 11 · Season

母亲节快乐！

mǔ qīn jié kuài lè！

Happy Mother's Day!

A greeting for Mother's Day, the second Sunday in May. In China too, many people give carnations or have a family meal, and those too shy to say it in person typically send 「妈，母亲节快乐！」 as a message.

Example

妈，母亲节快乐！您辛苦了！
mā， mǔ qīn jié kuài lè！ nín xīn kǔ le！
Mom, happy Mother's Day! Thank you for everything!
