Chinese phrase of the day · May 12 · Friends

有空吗？

yǒu kòng ma？

Are you free?

A lead-in before inviting someone; adding a time, as in 「今天晚上有空吗？」, makes it specific. To decline, say it gently: 「不好意思，今天有事」.

Example

今天晚上有空吗？一起去看电影吧。
jīn tiān wǎn shang yǒu kòng ma？ yì qǐ qù kàn diàn yǐng ba。
Are you free tonight? Let's go see a movie together.
