Chinese phrase of the day · May 13 · Work

跳槽

tiào cáo

to change jobs

Colloquial for changing jobs, from "jumping to another trough (feeding trough)." It comes up often in chats with coworkers, used as 「他跳槽了」 or 「我在考虑跳槽」, with the image of moving to a company with better conditions.

Example

他去年跳槽到了一家外企，工资涨了不少。
tā qù nián tiào cáo dào le yì jiā wài qǐ， gōng zī zhǎng le bù shǎo。
Last year he jumped ship to a foreign company, and his salary went up quite a bit.
