Chinese phrase of the day · May 14 · Food

打包。

dǎ bāo。

To go, please. / Could you pack this up?

For taking leftovers home or ordering takeout. In China, having leftover dishes packed up to take home is completely normal, and 「帮我打包一下」 is the polite way to ask.

Example

这些菜没吃完，帮我们打包一下吧。
zhè xiē cài méi chī wán， bāng wǒ men dǎ bāo yí xià ba。
We couldn't finish these dishes. Could you pack them up for us?
