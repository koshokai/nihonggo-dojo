Chinese phrase of the day · May 15 · Internet slang

搭子

dā zi

a buddy (for a particular activity)

A casual companion you do just one particular activity with, such as a 「饭搭子」 (meal buddy), 「旅游搭子」 (travel buddy) or 「健身搭子」 (gym buddy). It's a word popular with young people for an easygoing relationship that doesn't need to be a deep friendship.

Example

我找到了一个健身搭子，每天一起去健身房。
wǒ zhǎo dào le yí ge jiàn shēn dā zi， měi tiān yì qǐ qù jiàn shēn fáng。
I found a gym buddy, and we go to the gym together every day.
