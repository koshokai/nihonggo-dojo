Chinese phrase of the day · May 16 · Travel

能帮我拍张照吗？

néng bāng wǒ pāi zhāng zhào ma？

Could you take a photo for me?

For asking someone to take a souvenir photo at a tourist spot; afterwards, thank them with 「拍得真好！谢谢！」. People may ask you too, so it's handy to be able to catch it.

Example

不好意思，能帮我拍张照吗？把后面的塔拍进去。
bù hǎo yì si， néng bāng wǒ pāi zhāng zhào ma？ bǎ hòu mian de tǎ pāi jìn qu。
Excuse me, could you take a photo of me? Get the pagoda behind me in it.
