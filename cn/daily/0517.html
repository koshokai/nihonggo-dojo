Chinese phrase of the day · May 17 · Feelings

原来如此。

yuán lái rú cǐ。

I see, so that's why.

A response when you understand the reason or situation; 「原来是这样啊」 means the same and is more colloquial. It's used like "I see" in English, but repeating it while a superior explains something can sound a bit condescending.

Example

原来如此，难怪他今天没来上班。
yuán lái rú cǐ， nán guài tā jīn tiān méi lái shàng bān。
I see, no wonder he didn't come to work today.
