Chinese phrase of the day · May 18 · Greeting

欢迎欢迎！

huān yíng huān yíng！

Welcome!

A cheerful greeting when welcoming guests to your home or office. Saying it twice is the key, making the welcome much warmer, and it's friendlier than shops' 「欢迎光临」.

Example

欢迎欢迎！快请进，路上辛苦了！
huān yíng huān yíng！ kuài qǐng jìn， lù shàng xīn kǔ le！
Welcome, welcome! Come on in, it must have been a long trip!
