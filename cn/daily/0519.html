Chinese phrase of the day · May 19 · Shopping

买一送一

mǎi yí sòng yī

buy one, get one free

"Buy one, give one," the classic two-for-one sale often seen at drink shops and supermarkets. Since you can share with a friend, people invite each other with 「我们买一送一吧」.

Example

这家奶茶今天买一送一，我请你！
zhè jiā nǎi chá jīn tiān mǎi yí sòng yī， wǒ qǐng nǐ！
This milk tea is buy one get one free today. My treat!
