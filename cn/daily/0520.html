Chinese phrase of the day · May 20 · Internet slang

520快乐！

wǔ èr líng kuài lè！

Happy 520! (I love you)

Because "520" sounds similar to 「我爱你」, May 20 has become a day for confessing love and for couples, an anniversary born on the internet. Some people use it to thank family and friends too, not just partners.

Example

今天是五月二十号，520快乐！
jīn tiān shì wǔ yuè èr shí hào， wǔ èr líng kuài lè！
Today is May 20th. Happy 520!
