Chinese phrase of the day · May 21 · Idiom

三天打鱼，两天晒网

sān tiān dǎ yú， liǎng tiān shài wǎng

to never stick with anything

Fishing for three days and drying the nets for two, that is, never keeping anything up for long. It's often used self-mockingly when talking about studying or dieting.

Example

我学吉他总是三天打鱼，两天晒网。
wǒ xué jí tā zǒng shì sān tiān dǎ yú， liǎng tiān shài wǎng。
I never stick with guitar practice for long.
