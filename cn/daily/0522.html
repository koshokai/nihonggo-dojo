Chinese phrase of the day · May 22 · Friends

带我一个！

dài wǒ yí ge！

Count me in!

For when you hear friends planning something and want to join; 「算我一个」 means the same. It sounds casual and works for outings and meals alike, just like "Count me in!" or "I'm in!" in English.

Example

你们周末去露营？带我一个！
nǐ men zhōu mò qù lù yíng？ dài wǒ yí ge！
You're going camping this weekend? Count me in!
