Chinese phrase of the day · May 23 · Work

您贵姓？

nín guì xìng？

May I ask your surname?

A polite way to ask a new acquaintance's surname. In reply you use 「姓」, as in 「我姓田中」, without 「贵」, and it's an essential expression in business.

Example

初次见面，请问您贵姓？我姓田中。
chū cì jiàn miàn， qǐng wèn nín guì xìng？ wǒ xìng tián zhōng。
Nice to meet you. May I ask your surname? Mine is Tanaka.
