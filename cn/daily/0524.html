Chinese phrase of the day · May 24 · Food

好香啊！

hǎo xiāng a！

Smells so good!

What slips out at the smell of cooking or bread. Besides the aroma of food, 「香」 is also used for sleeping soundly, 「睡得香」, and eating with gusto, 「吃得香」, and saying it as you walk into the kitchen will please the cook.

Example

好香啊！你在做什么好吃的？
hǎo xiāng a！ nǐ zài zuò shén me hǎo chī de？
Smells so good! What are you making?
