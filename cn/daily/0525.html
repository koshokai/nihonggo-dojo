Chinese phrase of the day · May 25 · Season

出门记得涂防晒。

chū mén jì de tú fáng shài。

Don't forget your sunscreen when you go out.

A caring line for early summer, when the sun gets strong; 「防晒」 means sunscreen or sun protection in general. Chinese women are especially careful about tanning, and in summer parasols and long-sleeved sun-protective clothing, 「防晒衣」, are hugely popular.

Example

今天紫外线很强，出门记得涂防晒。
jīn tiān zǐ wài xiàn hěn qiáng， chū mén jì de tú fáng shài。
The UV is strong today. Don't forget to put on sunscreen when you go out.
