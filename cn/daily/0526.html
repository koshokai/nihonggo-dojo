Chinese phrase of the day · May 26 · Feelings

随便。

suí biàn。

Whatever. / Anything's fine.

A reply to questions like "What do you want to eat?" Depending on how you say it, though, it can sound dismissive, so 「都可以」 ("either is fine") often sounds nicer.

Example

你想吃什么？我都行，随便。
nǐ xiǎng chī shén me？ wǒ dōu xíng， suí biàn。
What do you want to eat? Anything works for me, whatever.
