Chinese phrase of the day · May 27 · Travel

到站了。

dào zhàn le。

This is our stop.

Said when your train or bus reaches your destination: you might tell people around you as you get off, or send 「到站了，马上出来」 in a chat. If you miss your stop, say 「坐过站了」.

Example

到站了，快下车，别忘了东西！
dào zhàn le， kuài xià chē， bié wàng le dōng xi！
This is our stop, hurry and get off, don't forget your things!
