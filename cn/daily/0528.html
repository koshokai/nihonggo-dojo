Chinese phrase of the day · May 28 · Internet slang

绝绝子

jué jué zi

Totally amazing! (a bit dated)

Internet slang that intensifies 「绝了」 even further, hugely popular for a while. It's now a little dated and sometimes used teasingly, so it's useful to recognize but use it sparingly.

Example

这家店的甜品绝绝子，下次还来！
zhè jiā diàn de tián pǐn jué jué zi， xià cì hái lái！
The desserts here are totally amazing. Let's come again!
