Chinese phrase of the day · May 29 · Greeting

哪里哪里。

nǎ lǐ nǎ lǐ。

Oh, not at all.

A modest reply when you're praised, literally "where? (what is there to praise?)." It sounds a little old-fashioned, and more and more people now simply accept praise with 「谢谢」; it reflects a culture where deflecting compliments is polite.

Example

哪里哪里，我的中文还差得远呢。
nǎ lǐ nǎ lǐ， wǒ de zhōng wén hái chà de yuǎn ne。
Oh no, not at all. My Chinese still has a long way to go.
