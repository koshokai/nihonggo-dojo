Chinese phrase of the day · May 30 · Idiom

一举两得

yì jǔ liǎng dé

to kill two birds with one stone

Getting two benefits from one action, just like "killing two birds with one stone" in English. Chinese also has 「一石二鸟」, but in everyday speech 「一举两得」 is more common.

Example

骑车上班又省钱又锻炼身体，一举两得。
qí chē shàng bān yòu shěng qián yòu duàn liàn shēn tǐ， yì jǔ liǎng dé。
Cycling to work saves money and gives you exercise. Two birds with one stone.
