Chinese phrase of the day · May 31 · Work

开个短会。

kāi ge duǎn huì。

Let's have a quick meeting.

Literally "hold a short meeting," a line for proposing a brief huddle. Saying how long, as in 「我们开个短会，十分钟就好」, makes it clearer, a bit like "Got a minute? Just five minutes" in English.

Example

大家过来一下，我们开个短会。
dà jiā guò lai yí xià， wǒ men kāi ge duǎn huì。
Everyone, come over here for a moment. Let's have a quick meeting.
