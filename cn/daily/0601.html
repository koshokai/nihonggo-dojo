Chinese phrase of the day · June 1 · Season

六一儿童节快乐！

liù yī ér tóng jié kuài lè！

Happy Children's Day!

June 1 is Children's Day in China. It's a running joke for adults to send each other 「祝大朋友儿童节快乐」 ("happy Children's Day to the big kids too"). Note that the date differs from other countries' Children's Days.

Example

祝大朋友小朋友们，六一儿童节快乐！
zhù dà péng you xiǎo péng you men， liù yī ér tóng jié kuài lè！
Happy June 1 Children's Day to all the big kids and little kids!
