Chinese phrase of the day · June 2 · Food

AA吧。

AA ba。

Let's split the bill.

「AA制」 means going Dutch, and it's common among younger people and coworkers. These days people usually settle up by sending their share through a payment app.

Example

今天这顿AA吧，我先付，回头你们转我。
jīn tiān zhè dùn AA ba， wǒ xiān fù， huí tóu nǐ men zhuǎn wǒ。
Let's split today's meal. I'll pay first, and you can transfer me your share later.
