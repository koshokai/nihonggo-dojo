Chinese phrase of the day · June 3 · Feelings

算了吧。

suàn le ba。

Forget it. / Let's not.

Used when giving up, calming someone down or dropping a suggestion; 「算了」 alone also works. Depending on tone it can sound dismissive, so softening it with 「算了，没关系」 comes across better.

Example

雨这么大，算了吧，我们改天再去。
yǔ zhè me dà， suàn le ba， wǒ men gǎi tiān zài qù。
It's raining so hard, let's forget it and go another day.
