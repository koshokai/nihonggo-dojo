Chinese phrase of the day · June 4 · Travel

我是第一次来。

wǒ shì dì yī cì lái。

It's my first time here.

Works as a kind of self-introduction when traveling or in a shop. Letting people know it's your first time often gets you recommendations and extra kindness.

Example

我是第一次来上海，有什么好玩的地方吗？
wǒ shì dì yī cì lái shàng hǎi， yǒu shén me hǎo wán de dì fang ma？
It's my first time in Shanghai. Are there any fun places to go?
