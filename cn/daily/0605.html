Chinese phrase of the day · June 5 · Friends

不见不散！

bú jiàn bú sàn！

Be there or be square! / I'll wait for you!

Meaning "we won't leave until we've met," a line that firms up a plan to meet, a cheerful phrase between friends. It's close to "See you there, don't be late!" or "I'll be waiting" in English, and often ends a chat confirming plans.

Example

明天下午三点，咖啡店门口，不见不散！
míng tiān xià wǔ sān diǎn， kā fēi diàn mén kǒu， bú jiàn bú sàn！
Tomorrow at 3 p.m., in front of the café. See you there!
