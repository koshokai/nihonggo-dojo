Chinese phrase of the day · June 6 · Greeting

麻烦你了。

má fan nǐ le。

Sorry for the trouble. / Thanks a lot.

Thanks and apology in one, used when asking for something or after someone has helped. It fits "Sorry to trouble you, thanks" perfectly, and with superiors 「麻烦您了」 using 「您」 is more polite.

Example

这么晚还让你帮忙，麻烦你了。
zhè me wǎn hái ràng nǐ bāng máng， má fan nǐ le。
Sorry for making you help so late. Thanks so much.
