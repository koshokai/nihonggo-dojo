Chinese phrase of the day · June 7 · Season

高考加油！

gāo kǎo jiā yóu！

Good luck on the gaokao!

Encouragement for students taking 「高考」, the national college entrance exam in early June. It's a huge event the whole country pays attention to, with people even keeping quiet around exam sites, and you can say it to the students' families too.

Example

明天就考试了，高考加油，你一定行！
míng tiān jiù kǎo shì le， gāo kǎo jiā yóu， nǐ yí dìng xíng！
The exam is tomorrow. Good luck on the gaokao, you've got this!
