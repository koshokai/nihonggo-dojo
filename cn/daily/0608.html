Chinese phrase of the day · June 8 · Food

开吃吧！

kāi chī ba！

Let's dig in!

Chinese has no fixed phrase like a pre-meal grace or "bon appétit," so people start eating with a casual line like this. When elders are at the table, it's good manners to wait for them to pick up their chopsticks first.

Example

菜都上齐了，大家开吃吧！
cài dōu shàng qí le， dà jiā kāi chī ba！
All the dishes are here. Let's dig in, everyone!
