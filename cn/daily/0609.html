Chinese phrase of the day · June 9 · Season

甜粽子还是咸粽子？

tián zòng zi hái shì xián zòng zi？

Sweet zongzi or savory zongzi?

The 「粽子」 eaten at the Dragon Boat Festival are mostly sweet in the north and savory with meat in the south, and which one people prefer is debated online every year. As a foreigner, answering "I like both" keeps the peace.

Example

端午节快到了，你喜欢甜粽子还是咸粽子？
duān wǔ jié kuài dào le， nǐ xǐ huan tián zòng zi hái shì xián zòng zi？
The Dragon Boat Festival is almost here. Do you prefer sweet or savory zongzi?
