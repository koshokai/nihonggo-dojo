Chinese phrase of the day · June 10 · Internet slang

情绪价值

qíng xù jià zhí

emotional value

The emotional value of making someone feel happy or reassured, used as in 「他很会提供情绪价值」 ("he's great at making people feel good"). It's a new term often heard in talk about relationships and friendships.

Example

跟她聊天特别开心，情绪价值拉满了。
gēn tā liáo tiān tè bié kāi xīn， qíng xù jià zhí lā mǎn le。
Talking with her is so much fun. She maxes out the emotional value.
