Chinese phrase of the day · June 11 · Season

端午安康！

duān wǔ ān kāng！

Wishing you good health this Dragon Boat Festival!

A greeting for the Dragon Boat Festival on the 5th day of the 5th lunar month, the day people eat zongzi. Since it was originally about warding off evil, some say 「安康」 is more fitting than 「快乐」, but 「端午节快乐」 is also widely used.

Example

端午安康！记得吃粽子哦。
duān wǔ ān kāng！ jì de chī zòng zi ó。
Wishing you good health this Dragon Boat Festival! Don't forget to eat zongzi.
