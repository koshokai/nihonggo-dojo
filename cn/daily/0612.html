Chinese phrase of the day · June 12 · Shopping

试衣间在哪儿？

shì yī jiān zài nǎr？

Where's the fitting room?

For trying on clothes in a shop; 「试衣间」 is the fitting room. Some shops limit how many items you can take in or give you a numbered tag, so learn it together with 「可以试一下吗？」.

Example

你好，我想试试这条裤子，试衣间在哪儿？
nǐ hǎo， wǒ xiǎng shì shi zhè tiáo kù zi， shì yī jiān zài nǎr？
Hi, I'd like to try on these pants. Where's the fitting room?
