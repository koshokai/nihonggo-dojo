Chinese phrase of the day · June 13 · Friends

咱们一起拍个照吧！

zán men yì qǐ pāi ge zhào ba！

Let's take a photo together!

The classic line when out with friends. 「咱们」 is "we" including the listener and sounds friendly, and the standard thing to say when snapping is 「一、二、三，茄子！」, the Chinese "say cheese."

Example

今天天气这么好，咱们一起拍个照吧！
jīn tiān tiān qì zhè me hǎo， zán men yì qǐ pāi ge zhào ba！
The weather's so nice today, let's take a photo together!
