Chinese phrase of the day · June 14 · Idiom

饭后百步走

fàn hòu bǎi bù zǒu

Take a stroll after meals

The first half of the saying "walk a hundred steps after a meal and you'll live to ninety-nine," used to recommend a walk for your health. Many people in China stroll in parks and residential compounds after dinner, so the saying is part of daily life.

Example

吃完饭别马上躺下，饭后百步走嘛。
chī wán fàn bié mǎ shàng tǎng xià， fàn hòu bǎi bù zǒu ma。
Don't lie down right after eating. Take a walk after meals, you know.
