Chinese phrase of the day · June 15 · Greeting

慢走！

màn zǒu！

Take care! (seeing someone off)

The standard line for seeing off guests or friends. Literally "walk slowly," it's like "take care" or "get home safely" in English; reply with 「好，再见！」.

Example

您慢走，欢迎下次再来我们店！
nín màn zǒu， huān yíng xià cì zài lái wǒ men diàn！
Take care, and please visit our shop again!
