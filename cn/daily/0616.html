Chinese phrase of the day · June 16 · Season

黄梅天

huáng méi tiān

plum rain season

The long rainy spell in the lower Yangtze region around when plums turn yellow. Like the rainy season in East Asia generally, it's humid and laundry won't dry; in Shanghai and nearby, people also say 「入梅了」 ("the rainy season has started").

Example

一到黄梅天，衣服怎么晒都不干。
yí dào huáng méi tiān， yī fu zěn me shài dōu bù gān。
Once the plum rains arrive, clothes never dry no matter how you hang them.
