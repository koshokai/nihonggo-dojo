Chinese phrase of the day · June 17 · Friends

改天一起吃饭吧！

gǎi tiān yì qǐ chī fàn ba！

Let's get a meal sometime!

Said when parting from a friend or to someone you've just met. As with "let's grab lunch sometime" in English, it may just be a polite formula; if you mean it, suggest a specific day, like 「周六怎么样？」.

Example

好久没见了，改天一起吃饭吧！
hǎo jiǔ méi jiàn le， gǎi tiān yì qǐ chī fàn ba！
It's been ages. Let's get a meal together sometime!
