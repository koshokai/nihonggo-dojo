Chinese phrase of the day · June 18 · Shopping

618你囤了什么？

liù yāo bā nǐ dùn le shén me？

What did you stock up on for 618?

A question often exchanged around 「618」, the big online sale around June 18; 「囤」 means "to stock up." It's a shopping event on par with Singles' Day (Double 11), and many people buy household goods in bulk.

Example

618你囤了什么？我买了一大箱纸巾。
liù yāo bā nǐ dùn le shén me？ wǒ mǎi le yí dà xiāng zhǐ jīn。
What did you stock up on for 618? I bought a huge box of tissues.
