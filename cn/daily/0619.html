Chinese phrase of the day · June 19 · Idiom

车到山前必有路

chē dào shān qián bì yǒu lù

Things will work out

When the cart reaches the mountain there will always be a road, meaning difficulties can be overcome somehow. It's a positive saying, close to "cross that bridge when you come to it" in English.

Example

别太担心了，车到山前必有路。
bié tài dān xīn le， chē dào shān qián bì yǒu lù。
Don't worry too much. Things will work out.
