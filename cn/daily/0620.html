Chinese phrase of the day · June 20 · Season

父亲节快乐！

fù qīn jié kuài lè！

Happy Father's Day!

A greeting for Father's Day, the third Sunday in June. It's not as big as Mother's Day, but more and more people send messages or gifts; if you feel shy, showing it through actions, like cooking your dad's favorite dish, is the Chinese way.

Example

爸，父亲节快乐！周末请您吃饭。
bà， fù qīn jié kuài lè！ zhōu mò qǐng nín chī fàn。
Dad, happy Father's Day! I'll treat you to dinner this weekend.
