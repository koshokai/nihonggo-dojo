Chinese phrase of the day · June 21 · Shopping

我就看看。

wǒ jiù kàn kan。

I'm just looking.

A line for when a shop assistant approaches you; 「随便看看」 means the same. They'll usually leave you alone after you say it, so it's handy when you want to browse at your own pace.

Example

谢谢，我就看看，需要的话再叫你。
xiè xie， wǒ jiù kàn kan， xū yào de huà zài jiào nǐ。
Thanks, I'm just looking. I'll call you if I need anything.
