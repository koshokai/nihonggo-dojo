Chinese phrase of the day · June 22 · Internet slang

笑死我了！

xiào sǐ wǒ le！

LOL! I'm dying of laughter!

An exaggerated reaction to something hilarious. In chat, people often type just 「笑死」 or a string of 「哈哈哈」; it's like "LOL" or "I'm dead" in English, used casually among friends.

Example

你发的那个视频笑死我了！
nǐ fā de nà ge shì pín xiào sǐ wǒ le！
That video you sent me had me dying of laughter!
