Chinese phrase of the day · June 24 · Season

毕业快乐！

bì yè kuài lè！

Happy graduation!

Chinese universities hold graduation around June, and tossing mortarboards for a photo is a classic. Adding 「祝你前程似锦」 ("wishing you a bright future") conveys your feelings even better, and graduates say it to each other too.

Example

毕业快乐！祝你工作顺利，前程似锦！
bì yè kuài lè！ zhù nǐ gōng zuò shùn lì， qián chéng sì jǐn！
Happy graduation! Wishing you success at work and a bright future!
