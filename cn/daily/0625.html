Chinese phrase of the day · June 25 · Friends

有事找我。

yǒu shì zhǎo wǒ。

Come to me if you need anything.

A reassuring line telling someone to rely on you when in trouble, fine with friends and coworkers alike. Making it 「有事随时找我」 sounds even more supportive, and it's more colloquial and friendly than 「有问题随时联系我」.

Example

你刚来不熟悉，有事找我就行。
nǐ gāng lái bù shú xī， yǒu shì zhǎo wǒ jiù xíng。
You're new here and don't know your way around yet. Just come to me if you need anything.
