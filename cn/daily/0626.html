Chinese phrase of the day · June 26 · Travel

我想要靠窗的座位。

wǒ xiǎng yào kào chuāng de zuò wèi。

I'd like a window seat.

For requesting a seat on a plane or train or in a restaurant; an aisle seat is 「靠过道的座位」. On high-speed trains the window seats are lettered 「A」 and 「F」, so you can also ask by seat letter.

Example

你好，我想要靠窗的座位，可以吗？
nǐ hǎo， wǒ xiǎng yào kào chuāng de zuò wèi， kě yǐ ma？
Hi, could I have a window seat?
