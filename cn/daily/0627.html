Chinese phrase of the day · June 27 · Idiom

不打不相识

bù dǎ bù xiāng shí

No discord, no concord

Becoming closer precisely because you clashed at first, said with a laugh when a conflict turned into a friendship. It's a positive saying, close to "a fight can clear the air" in English.

Example

我们俩是吵架认识的，真是不打不相识。
wǒ men liǎ shì chǎo jià rèn shi de， zhēn shì bù dǎ bù xiāng shí。
We met through an argument. Truly, no discord, no concord.
