Chinese phrase of the day · June 28 · Greeting

替我向叔叔阿姨问好。

tì wǒ xiàng shū shu ā yí wèn hǎo。

Say hi to your parents for me.

A line for sending regards to a friend's family; in China you call a friend's parents 「叔叔阿姨」 ("uncle and auntie"). It works just like "say hi to your folks for me," when parting or to a friend heading home.

Example

你回老家的话，替我向叔叔阿姨问好。
nǐ huí lǎo jiā de huà， tì wǒ xiàng shū shu ā yí wèn hǎo。
If you go back home, say hello to your mom and dad for me.
