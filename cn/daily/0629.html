Chinese phrase of the day · June 29 · Season

今天好闷热啊。

jīn tiān hǎo mēn rè a。

It's so muggy today.

Describes the humid, sticky heat from the rainy season into summer; 「闷」 means "stuffy, closed in." Dry heat is 「热」 and muggy heat is 「闷热」, and it's handy for everyday weather small talk.

Example

今天好闷热啊，开空调吧。
jīn tiān hǎo mēn rè a， kāi kōng tiáo ba。
It's so muggy today. Let's turn on the AC.
