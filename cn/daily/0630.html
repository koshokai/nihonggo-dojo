Chinese phrase of the day · June 30 · Internet slang

下次一定

xià cì yí dìng

Definitely next time (but probably not)

An internet in-joke reply of "definitely next time" to streamers' requests like "please support me," jokingly implying you have no real intention. It's like "I'll try to make it" in English, used as a casual way of saying no.

Example

这次的聚会我去不了，下次一定！
zhè cì de jù huì wǒ qù bu liǎo， xià cì yí dìng！
I can't make it to this get-together. Definitely next time!
