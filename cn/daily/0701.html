Chinese phrase of the day · July 1 · Idiom

货比三家

huò bǐ sān jiā

Shop around before you buy

The advice to compare several shops before deciding. It's often said before big purchases, and online too, as in 「先货比三家再下单」, expressing careful shopping habits.

Example

买手机别着急，货比三家再决定吧。
mǎi shǒu jī bié zháo jí， huò bǐ sān jiā zài jué dìng ba。
Don't rush into buying a phone. Shop around before you decide.
