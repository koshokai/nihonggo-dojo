Chinese phrase of the day · July 2 · Greeting

辛苦了！

xīn kǔ le！

Thanks for your hard work!

After work or when someone has helped you. It's not used as constantly as a casual "good job," though; it's natural as appreciation for someone who really did work hard, and a typical reply is 「不辛苦！」.

Example

项目终于完成了，大家辛苦了！
xiàng mù zhōng yú wán chéng le， dà jiā xīn kǔ le！
The project is finally finished. Thank you all for your hard work!
