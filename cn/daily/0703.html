Chinese phrase of the day · July 3 · Season

热死了！

rè sǐ le！

It's boiling!

The classic summer grumble, with 「〜死了」 as the intensifier. Summers in China can approach 40°C in some areas, and southern cities such as Chongqing and Wuhan are famous for their heat and called 「火炉」 ("furnaces").

Example

今天三十八度，热死了！
jīn tiān sān shí bā dù， rè sǐ le！
It's 38 degrees today. It's boiling!
