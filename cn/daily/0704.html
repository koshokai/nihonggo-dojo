Chinese phrase of the day · July 4 · Internet slang

破防了

pò fáng le

That hit me hard / That got me

From gaming, "your defense has been broken." It's used both when you're so moved you start crying and when someone's remark hits a nerve, and you'll often see it in comments on touching videos.

Example

看到妈妈给我写的信，我直接破防了。
kàn dào mā ma gěi wǒ xiě de xìn， wǒ zhí jiē pò fáng le。
When I read the letter my mom wrote me, I just broke down.
