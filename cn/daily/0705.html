Chinese phrase of the day · July 5 · Feelings

我也是。

wǒ yě shì。

Me too.

The basic way to say you share someone's feelings or situation; you can also continue, as in 「我也很喜欢」 or 「我也觉得」. In the negative it becomes 「我也不〜」, so be careful not to say 「我也是不喜欢」.

Example

你也喜欢吃辣？我也是！
nǐ yě xǐ huan chī là？ wǒ yě shì！
You like spicy food too? Me too!
