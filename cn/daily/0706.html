Chinese phrase of the day · July 6 · Food

这个怎么吃？

zhè ge zěn me chī？

How do you eat this?

For when you come across an unfamiliar dish or ingredient. Chinese people are often happy to show you how to eat it, so it's a great conversation starter that makes eating on your travels more fun.

Example

这个螃蟹好大，这个怎么吃？
zhè ge páng xiè hǎo dà， zhè ge zěn me chī？
This crab is huge. How do you eat it?
