Chinese phrase of the day · July 7 · Season

放暑假了！

fàng shǔ jià le！

Summer vacation is here!

What students say when the long-awaited summer vacation starts. In China it often runs from mid-July to the end of August, and a common joke is that kids' summer vacation is the parents' season of headaches.

Example

终于放暑假了，我要睡到中午！
zhōng yú fàng shǔ jià le， wǒ yào shuì dào zhōng wǔ！
Summer vacation at last! I'm going to sleep till noon!
