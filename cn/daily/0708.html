Chinese phrase of the day · July 8 · Travel

师傅，去这个地方。

shī fu， qù zhè ge dì fang。

Driver, please take me here.

For telling a taxi driver where to go. 「师傅」 is a respectful way to address drivers and skilled workers, showing an address or map as you say it makes sure you get there, and ride-hailing apps are common too.

Example

师傅，去这个地方，大概要多久？
shī fu， qù zhè ge dì fang， dà gài yào duō jiǔ？
Driver, please take me here. About how long will it take?
