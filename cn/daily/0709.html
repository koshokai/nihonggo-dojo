Chinese phrase of the day · July 9 · Work

画大饼

huà dà bǐng

to make empty promises ("drawing a big pancake")

Literally "drawing a big pancake," motivating people with promises unlikely to come true. Employees often say it sarcastically when a boss verbally promises promotions or raises; it's like "pie in the sky" in English.

Example

老板又在给我们画大饼，说明年一定加薪。
lǎo bǎn yòu zài gěi wǒ men huà dà bǐng， shuō míng nián yí dìng jiā xīn。
The boss is making empty promises again, saying we'll definitely get a raise next year.
