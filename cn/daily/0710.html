Chinese phrase of the day · July 10 · Feelings

有道理。

yǒu dào li。

That makes sense.

A response when you're convinced by someone's view. It's slightly more reserved agreement than 「说得对」 ("that's right"), fine in meetings or casual talk, and 「很有道理」 strengthens it.

Example

你说得有道理，我们就按这个方案做吧。
nǐ shuō de yǒu dào li， wǒ men jiù àn zhè ge fāng àn zuò ba。
You've got a point. Let's go with this plan.
