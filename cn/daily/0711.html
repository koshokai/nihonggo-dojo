Chinese phrase of the day · July 11 · Season

吃根雪糕吧！

chī gēn xuě gāo ba！

Let's get a popsicle!

Suggesting something cold on a hot day. 「雪糕」 is an ice pop or ice cream bar and 「冰淇淋」 is soft-serve or ice cream in a cup; 「根」 is the measure word for long thin things, so you say 「一根」 for an ice pop.

Example

太热了，去便利店吃根雪糕吧！
tài rè le， qù biàn lì diàn chī gēn xuě gāo ba！
It's too hot. Let's go to the convenience store and get a popsicle!
