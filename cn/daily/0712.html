Chinese phrase of the day · July 12 · Idiom

入乡随俗

rù xiāng suí sú

When in Rome, do as the Romans do

When you go somewhere, follow local customs, the same idea as the English saying. You can use it in talk about travel or studying abroad: 「入乡随俗嘛！」.

Example

在中国吃饭大家一起分着吃，入乡随俗嘛。
zài zhōng guó chī fàn dà jiā yì qǐ fēn zhe chī， rù xiāng suí sú ma。
In China everyone shares the dishes at a meal. When in Rome, right?
