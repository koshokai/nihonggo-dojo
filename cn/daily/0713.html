Chinese phrase of the day · July 13 · Friends

铁哥们儿

tiě gē menr

best buddy (between men)

A male friend bonded as firmly as iron. 「哥们儿」 alone means "buddy, pal" and is often used by men to address each other, while a woman's best girlfriend is 「闺蜜」.

Example

他是我大学时的铁哥们儿，什么话都能说。
tā shì wǒ dà xué shí de tiě gē menr， shén me huà dōu néng shuō。
He's been my best buddy since college. I can tell him anything.
