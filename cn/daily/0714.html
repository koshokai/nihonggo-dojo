Chinese phrase of the day · July 14 · Shopping

有大一号的吗？

yǒu dà yí hào de ma？

Do you have one size up?

After trying on clothes or shoes; for a size down, say 「小一号」. Chinese sizes may differ from what you're used to, so it's best to try things on.

Example

这双鞋有点挤，有大一号的吗？
zhè shuāng xié yǒu diǎn jǐ， yǒu dà yí hào de ma？
These shoes are a bit tight. Do you have them one size up?
