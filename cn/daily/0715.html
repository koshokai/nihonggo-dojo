Chinese phrase of the day · July 15 · Season

下暴雨了！

xià bào yǔ le！

It's pouring!

A sudden heavy summer downpour: 「暴雨」 is a rainstorm, while a passing shower is 「阵雨」. When a 「暴雨预警」 (heavy rain warning) is issued, school and work plans may change, so check before heading out.

Example

突然下暴雨了，我们先在店里躲一下吧。
tū rán xià bào yǔ le， wǒ men xiān zài diàn lǐ duǒ yí xià ba。
It's suddenly pouring. Let's wait it out in the shop for a bit.
