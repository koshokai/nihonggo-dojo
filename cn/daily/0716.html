Chinese phrase of the day · July 16 · Work

收到。

shōu dào。

Got it.

The standard reply to messages and emails. In chat, 「收到！」 alone means "received, understood," and 「好的，收到」 is more polite.

Example

收到，我下午就把文件发过去。
shōu dào， wǒ xià wǔ jiù bǎ wén jiàn fā guò qù。
Got it. I'll send the documents over this afternoon.
