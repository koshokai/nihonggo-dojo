Chinese phrase of the day · July 17 · Feelings

好尴尬啊。

hǎo gān gà a。

How awkward...

For moments that are awkward or embarrassing; 「尴尬」 is used constantly in daily life and online, for example when you get someone's name wrong or a conversation falls silent. It covers both "awkward" and "embarrassing."

Example

我叫错了他的名字，好尴尬啊。
wǒ jiào cuò le tā de míng zi， hǎo gān gà a。
I called him by the wrong name. So awkward...
