Chinese phrase of the day · July 18 · Food

我请客！

wǒ qǐng kè！

It's on me!

What you say when you'll pay for the meal. In China, the person who invited or who has something to celebrate often pays; the smart reply is 「下次我请」 ("next time's on me").

Example

今天是我生日，我请客，大家随便点！
jīn tiān shì wǒ shēng rì， wǒ qǐng kè， dà jiā suí biàn diǎn！
It's my birthday today, so it's on me. Order whatever you like!
