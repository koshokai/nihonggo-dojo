Chinese phrase of the day · July 19 · Idiom

冬吃萝卜夏吃姜

dōng chī luó bo xià chī jiāng

Radishes in winter, ginger in summer

A food-as-medicine saying about balancing the body with seasonal foods. The idea is that in summer, when you have too many cold things, ginger warms you up, and it comes up often at Chinese family tables and in talk about health.

Example

冬吃萝卜夏吃姜，夏天也别老喝冰水。
dōng chī luó bo xià chī jiāng， xià tiān yě bié lǎo hē bīng shuǐ。
They say radishes in winter, ginger in summer, so don't keep drinking ice water just because it's summer.
