Chinese phrase of the day · July 20 · Internet slang

emo了

emo le

feeling down / in my feels

From the English "emotional," used lightly when you feel down or sentimental. 「深夜emo」, suddenly feeling low late at night, is also a common phrase.

Example

下雨天一个人在家，突然有点emo了。
xià yǔ tiān yí ge rén zài jiā， tū rán yǒu diǎn emo le。
Alone at home on a rainy day, I suddenly feel a bit down.
