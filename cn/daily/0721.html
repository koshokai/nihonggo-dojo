Chinese phrase of the day · July 21 · Travel

坐过站了！

zuò guò zhàn le！

I missed my stop!

For when you've gone past your stop on a train or bus, also 「坐过了」. It's an easy mistake when you're glued to your phone; follow up with 「下一站下车，再坐回去」 ("get off at the next stop and go back").

Example

光顾着看手机，坐过站了！
guāng gù zhe kàn shǒu jī， zuò guò zhàn le！
I was so busy looking at my phone that I missed my stop!
