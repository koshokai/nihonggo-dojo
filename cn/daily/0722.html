Chinese phrase of the day · July 22 · Greeting

不客气。

bú kè qi。

You're welcome.

The basic reply when someone says 「谢谢」; between friends 「没事儿」 or 「小事儿」 ("no big deal") are natural too. There's no need to be overly modest.

Example

不客气，这点小事儿，不用谢！
bú kè qi， zhè diǎn xiǎo shìr， bú yòng xiè！
You're welcome, it's nothing. No need to thank me!
