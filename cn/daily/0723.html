Chinese phrase of the day · July 23 · Season

入伏了

rù fú le

The dog days have begun

Says the hottest part of summer, 「三伏天」, has started; it begins around mid-July and lasts about a month. In the north some people eat noodles on the first day, and there's also the saying 「头伏饺子二伏面」.

Example

今天入伏了，接下来一个月最热。
jīn tiān rù fú le， jiē xià lai yí ge yuè zuì rè。
The dog days start today. The next month will be the hottest.
