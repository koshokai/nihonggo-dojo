Chinese phrase of the day · July 24 · Friends

我们加个微信吧。

wǒ men jiā ge wēi xìn ba。

Let's add each other on WeChat.

The standard phrase for exchanging contacts in China. WeChat is essential infrastructure, handling messaging, social media and payments in one, and 「我扫你」 means you'll scan their QR code.

Example

认识你很高兴，我们加个微信吧。
rèn shi nǐ hěn gāo xìng， wǒ men jiā ge wēi xìn ba。
Great to meet you. Let's add each other on WeChat.
