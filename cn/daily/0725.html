Chinese phrase of the day · July 25 · Food

吃货

chī huò

foodie

Someone who can't resist good food. It used to be an insult, but now it's established as a fun way to introduce your love of food, as in 「我是个吃货」, perfect for people who travel to eat.

Example

我是个吃货，去旅游主要是为了吃。
wǒ shì ge chī huò， qù lǚ yóu zhǔ yào shì wèi le chī。
I'm a foodie, so I travel mainly to eat.
