Chinese phrase of the day · July 26 · Idiom

欲速则不达

yù sù zé bù dá

More haste, less speed

Rushing too much keeps you from reaching your goal, a line from the Analects. It's perfect for advising someone who's in a hurry: 「别急，欲速则不达」.

Example

别急着交，欲速则不达，再检查一遍。
bié jí zhe jiāo， yù sù zé bù dá， zài jiǎn chá yí biàn。
Don't rush to hand it in. More haste, less speed. Check it once more.
