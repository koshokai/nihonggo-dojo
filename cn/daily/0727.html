Chinese phrase of the day · July 27 · Shopping

快递到了！

kuài dì dào le！

My package is here!

What you say when something you ordered online arrives. In China, deliveries are called 「快递」, and people often pick them up from parcel lockers in their building or pickup points; the excitement of waiting for a package is universal.

Example

快递到了，我下楼去拿一下。
kuài dì dào le， wǒ xià lóu qù ná yí xià。
My package is here. I'll pop downstairs to get it.
