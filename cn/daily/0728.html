Chinese phrase of the day · July 28 · Feelings

加油！

jiā yóu！

Go for it! / You can do it!

The go-to cheer for any situation: exams, games, work. Said to yourself it means "let's do this," and good replies are 「谢谢！」 or 「一起加油！」 ("let's both do our best!").

Example

明天就比赛了，加油！我们都支持你！
míng tiān jiù bǐ sài le， jiā yóu！ wǒ men dōu zhī chí nǐ！
The game is tomorrow. Go for it! We're all behind you!
