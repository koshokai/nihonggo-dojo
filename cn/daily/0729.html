Chinese phrase of the day · July 29 · Season

夏天就得吃西瓜！

xià tiān jiù děi chī xī guā！

Summer means watermelon!

Watermelon is the quintessential Chinese summer treat, and you'll often see big watermelons sold by the roadside. 「得（děi）」 means "have to," and cutting up a watermelon together as a family is a summer memory for many people.

Example

这么热的天，夏天就得吃西瓜！
zhè me rè de tiān， xià tiān jiù děi chī xī guā！
On a day this hot, summer means watermelon!
