Chinese phrase of the day · July 30 · Work

我马上处理。

wǒ mǎ shàng chǔ lǐ。

I'll take care of it right away.

A line for responding quickly to a request or problem; 「马上」 means "right away," and it makes you sound dependable. In chat, people sometimes just reply 「马上」.

Example

您说的问题我马上处理，请稍等。
nín shuō de wèn tí wǒ mǎ shàng chǔ lǐ， qǐng shāo děng。
I'll deal with the issue you mentioned right away. Please wait a moment.
