Chinese phrase of the day · July 31 · Internet slang

秒回

miǎo huí

instant reply

Replying to a message within "seconds," used to praise or tease someone who replies super fast. Reading a message but not replying is 「已读不回」, and a common joke is that you can't help replying instantly to someone you like.

Example

每次给他发消息，他都秒回。
měi cì gěi tā fā xiāo xi， tā dōu miǎo huí。
Every time I message him, he replies instantly.
