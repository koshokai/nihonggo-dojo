Chinese phrase of the day · August 1 · Feelings

我好累啊。

wǒ hǎo lèi a。

I'm so tired.

A complaint after work or a trip; 「好」 is a colloquial way to say "very." A kind reply is 「辛苦了，早点休息」 ("you've worked hard, get some rest").

Example

爬了一天山，我好累啊，想早点睡。
pá le yì tiān shān， wǒ hǎo lèi a， xiǎng zǎo diǎn shuì。
I hiked all day. I'm so tired, I want to go to bed early.
