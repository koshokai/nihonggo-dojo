Chinese phrase of the day · August 2 · Idiom

心静自然凉

xīn jìng zì rán liáng

A calm mind keeps you cool

A saying that if you calm your mind, you'll feel the heat less, often used to soothe someone complaining about the heat. It's close to the English advice "keep your cool."

Example

别老喊热了，心静自然凉嘛。
bié lǎo hǎn rè le， xīn jìng zì rán liáng ma。
Stop going on about the heat. A calm mind keeps you cool.
