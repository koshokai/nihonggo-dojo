Chinese phrase of the day · August 3 · Travel

我们打车去吧。

wǒ men dǎ chē qù ba。

Let's take a taxi.

「打车」 means to catch or call a taxi. Nowadays most people call one with a ride-hailing app, which is also 「打车」, and 「打个车」 sounds more casual.

Example

太晚了，地铁停了，我们打车去吧。
tài wǎn le， dì tiě tíng le， wǒ men dǎ chē qù ba。
It's too late, the subway's stopped. Let's take a taxi.
