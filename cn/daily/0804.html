Chinese phrase of the day · August 4 · Greeting

保重身体。

bǎo zhòng shēn tǐ。

Take care of yourself.

Words of concern for someone's health when parting or at the end of a letter, especially for someone going far away, older people or busy people. It's also said 「多保重」, and it has a polite tone like "please take good care of yourself."

Example

工作别太累了，一定要保重身体。
gōng zuò bié tài lèi le， yí dìng yào bǎo zhòng shēn tǐ。
Don't overwork yourself. Be sure to take care of your health.
