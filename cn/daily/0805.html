Chinese phrase of the day · August 5 · Season

小心中暑。

xiǎo xīn zhòng shǔ。

Watch out for heatstroke.

A caring line before going out or exercising in midsummer; 「中暑」 means heatstroke or heat exhaustion. In China, mung bean soup, 「绿豆汤」, is the classic way to beat the heat and is often made at home.

Example

中午太阳太毒了，多喝水，小心中暑。
zhōng wǔ tài yang tài dú le， duō hē shuǐ， xiǎo xīn zhòng shǔ。
The midday sun is brutal. Drink lots of water and watch out for heatstroke.
