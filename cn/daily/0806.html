Chinese phrase of the day · August 6 · Shopping

性价比很高。

xìng jià bǐ hěn gāo。

It's great value for money.

Literally "the ratio of performance to price," it means value for money and is common in shopping reviews and word of mouth. The opposite is 「性价比不高」, and it's also handy to learn 「物美价廉」 ("good and cheap").

Example

这款耳机才一百块，音质这么好，性价比很高。
zhè kuǎn ěr jī cái yì bǎi kuài， yīn zhì zhè me hǎo， xìng jià bǐ hěn gāo。
These earphones are only 100 yuan and the sound is great. Excellent value for money.
