Chinese phrase of the day · August 7 · Season

贴秋膘

tiē qiū biāo

to fatten up for autumn

A northern custom of eating meat dishes at the Start of Autumn (around August 7) to rebuild strength lost over the summer; 「膘」 means fat. These days it's also often used jokingly to justify overeating: "it's the Start of Autumn, so I have to eat!"

Example

今天立秋，晚上去吃烤肉贴秋膘吧！
jīn tiān lì qiū， wǎn shang qù chī kǎo ròu tiē qiū biāo ba！
It's the Start of Autumn today. Let's go have barbecue tonight and fatten up for autumn!
