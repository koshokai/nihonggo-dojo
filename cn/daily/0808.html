Chinese phrase of the day · August 8 · Work

打工人

dǎ gōng rén

worker bee, wage slave (self-mocking)

Originally it meant a part-time worker, but now it's established as a self-mocking way for office workers to refer to themselves. The pep-talk line 「打工人，打工魂！」 ("worker by day, worker at heart!") also became a trend.

Example

周一早上的地铁里全是打工人。
zhōu yī zǎo shang de dì tiě lǐ quán shì dǎ gōng rén。
The subway on Monday morning is packed with wage slaves.
