Chinese phrase of the day · August 9 · Friends

想你了。

xiǎng nǐ le。

I miss you.

Words that tell someone close that you miss them, used not only with partners but with family and friends too. Sending 「想你了」 in a chat conveys warmth, and the standard reply is 「我也想你」.

Example

好久没见了，想你了，什么时候回来？
hǎo jiǔ méi jiàn le， xiǎng nǐ le， shén me shí hou huí lai？
We haven't seen each other in ages. I miss you. When are you coming back?
