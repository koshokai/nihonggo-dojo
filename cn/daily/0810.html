Chinese phrase of the day · August 10 · Food

我吃饱了。

wǒ chī bǎo le。

I'm full.

At the end of a meal. There's no set phrase for thanking for a meal, so this line or 「太好吃了」 conveys your thanks, and you can also use it to decline when offered more.

Example

我吃饱了，菜太好吃了，谢谢款待！
wǒ chī bǎo le， cài tài hǎo chī le， xiè xie kuǎn dài！
I'm full. The food was delicious, thank you for having me!
