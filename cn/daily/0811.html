Chinese phrase of the day · August 11 · Travel

说走就走

shuō zǒu jiù zǒu

to just up and go

Setting off on a whim without a plan. The phrase 「说走就走的旅行」 ("a spur-of-the-moment trip") in particular expresses longing for carefree travel, like "spontaneous road trip" in English.

Example

周末没事，来一场说走就走的旅行吧！
zhōu mò méi shì， lái yì chǎng shuō zǒu jiù zǒu de lǚ xíng ba！
I've got nothing on this weekend. Let's take a spur-of-the-moment trip!
