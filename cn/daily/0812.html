Chinese phrase of the day · August 12 · Idiom

说曹操，曹操到

shuō cáo cāo， cáo cāo dào

Speak of the devil

From the idea that you're talking about Cao Cao of the Three Kingdoms and the man himself appears. It's used exactly like "Speak of the devil" in English, the moment the person being talked about shows up.

Example

说曹操，曹操到，我们正说你呢！
shuō cáo cāo， cáo cāo dào， wǒ men zhèng shuō nǐ ne！
Speak of the devil! We were just talking about you!
