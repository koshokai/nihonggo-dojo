Chinese phrase of the day · August 13 · Work

甩锅

shuǎi guō

to pass the buck

Literally "to fling the wok," pushing responsibility or blame onto someone else; taking the blame for someone else is 「背锅」. It's a slightly sarcastic colloquialism used all the time in office chat and online.

Example

项目出了问题，他马上开始甩锅。
xiàng mù chū le wèn tí， tā mǎ shàng kāi shǐ shuǎi guō。
As soon as the project ran into trouble, he started passing the buck.
