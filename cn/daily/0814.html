Chinese phrase of the day · August 14 · Season

去海边玩吧！

qù hǎi biān wán ba！

Let's go to the beach!

The classic summer invitation. Seaside places like Hainan, Qingdao and Xiamen are popular summer destinations in China, and many people enjoy strolling on the beach and taking photos more than swimming.

Example

放假了，我们一起去海边玩吧！
fàng jià le， wǒ men yì qǐ qù hǎi biān wán ba！
We're on vacation, let's go to the beach together!
