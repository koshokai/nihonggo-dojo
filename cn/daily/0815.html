Chinese phrase of the day · August 15 · Friends

等等我！

děng deng wǒ！

Wait for me!

When you're about to be left behind or need time to get ready. 「等我一下」 means "wait a sec," and in chats 「等我五分钟」 ("give me five minutes") is standard.

Example

等等我！我马上换好衣服。
děng deng wǒ！ wǒ mǎ shàng huàn hǎo yī fu。
Wait for me! I'll be changed in a second.
