Chinese phrase of the day · August 16 · Feelings

羡慕死了！

xiàn mù sǐ le！

I'm so jealous!

A jokey way of expressing strong envy, a standard comment on friends' travel photos or posts of tasty food. Online, jealousy is likened to a 「柠檬」 (lemon), and people also say 「我酸了」 ("I've gone sour").

Example

你下个月去冰岛？羡慕死了！
nǐ xià ge yuè qù bīng dǎo？ xiàn mù sǐ le！
You're going to Iceland next month? I'm so jealous!
