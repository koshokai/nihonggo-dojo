Chinese phrase of the day · August 17 · Internet slang

显眼包

xiǎn yǎn bāo

attention-seeker, class clown

Someone who does attention-grabbing things on purpose to make people laugh. It's a bit teasing but also affectionate toward a charming person, and it can refer to the person who lightens the mood at work or school.

Example

他在年会上跳舞，真是个显眼包。
tā zài nián huì shàng tiào wǔ， zhēn shì ge xiǎn yǎn bāo。
He danced at the company year-end party. What a show-off.
