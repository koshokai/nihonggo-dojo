Chinese phrase of the day · August 18 · Season

七夕快乐！

qī xī kuài lè！

Happy Qixi!

China's Qixi (the 7th day of the 7th lunar month) is called "Chinese Valentine's Day," when couples exchange gifts. It has a different feel from the Tanabata festival as celebrated in Japan, where people write wishes on strips of paper.

Example

七夕快乐！今晚想吃什么，我请你。
qī xī kuài lè！ jīn wǎn xiǎng chī shén me， wǒ qǐng nǐ。
Happy Qixi! What do you want to eat tonight? My treat.
