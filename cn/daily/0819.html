Chinese phrase of the day · August 19 · Food

好撑啊。

hǎo chēng a。

I'm stuffed.

What you say when you've eaten so much you could burst; 「撑」 means "stretched, bloated." It's even fuller than 「我吃饱了」, and since Chinese dinners come with plenty of food, you'll have lots of chances to use it.

Example

自助餐吃太多了，好撑啊。
zì zhù cān chī tài duō le， hǎo chēng a。
I ate too much at the buffet. I'm stuffed.
