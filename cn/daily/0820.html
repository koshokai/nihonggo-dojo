Chinese phrase of the day · August 20 · Travel

这儿能拍照吗？

zhèr néng pāi zhào ma？

Can I take photos here?

For checking whether photography is allowed in museums, temples, shops and so on. Flash is banned in many places, so 「能用闪光灯吗？」 is handy to know too; a no-photography sign reads 「禁止拍照」.

Example

请问，这儿能拍照吗？不用闪光灯。
qǐng wèn， zhèr néng pāi zhào ma？ bú yòng shǎn guāng dēng。
Excuse me, can I take photos here? Without flash.
