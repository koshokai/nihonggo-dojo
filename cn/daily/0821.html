Chinese phrase of the day · August 21 · Friends

我挺你！

wǒ tǐng nǐ！

I've got your back!

「挺」 means "to support," used to fully back a friend's decision or action. You can strengthen it to 「无论如何我都挺你」 ("I've got your back no matter what"), and it's a standard comment online too.

Example

你想辞职创业？我挺你！
nǐ xiǎng cí zhí chuàng yè？ wǒ tǐng nǐ！
You want to quit and start your own business? I'm behind you!
