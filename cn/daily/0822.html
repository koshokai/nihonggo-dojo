Chinese phrase of the day · August 22 · Work

下班了！

xià bān le！

Done with work!

Expresses the relief of finishing work. In chats people invite friends with 「下班了，去吃饭吗？」; 「上班」 is going to work and 「下班」 is getting off work, and on days you get out on time you celebrate with 「今天准时下班」 ("left on time today").

Example

终于下班了，今晚去吃火锅吧！
zhōng yú xià bān le， jīn wǎn qù chī huǒ guō ba！
Finally off work. Let's have hotpot tonight!
