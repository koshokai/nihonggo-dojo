Chinese phrase of the day · August 23 · Feelings

我就知道。

wǒ jiù zhī dao。

I knew it.

For when your prediction comes true, like "See?" or "Just as I thought." Depending on tone it can become a sarcastic "I told you so," and to praise someone you can say 「我就知道你能行！」.

Example

我就知道你能考上，恭喜你！
wǒ jiù zhī dao nǐ néng kǎo shàng， gōng xǐ nǐ！
I knew you'd get in. Congratulations!
