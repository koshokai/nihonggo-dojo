Chinese phrase of the day · August 24 · Food

去吃夜宵吧！

qù chī yè xiāo ba！

Let's go get a late-night snack!

A line for inviting friends out late at night. China has a late-night snack culture you can enjoy into the small hours, like street barbecue skewers, 「烧烤」, and crayfish, and eating outdoors on a summer night is special; it's also written 「宵夜」.

Example

加班到十点，饿了，去吃夜宵吧！
jiā bān dào shí diǎn， è le， qù chī yè xiāo ba！
I worked overtime until ten and I'm hungry. Let's go get a late-night snack!
