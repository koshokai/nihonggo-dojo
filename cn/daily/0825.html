Chinese phrase of the day · August 25 · Internet slang

特种兵旅游

tè zhǒng bīng lǚ yóu

a whirlwind trip ("special forces travel")

A grueling travel style that crams lots of sights into a short time, named after how tough military special forces training is. It spread as internet slang for the weekend trips of energetic young people.

Example

我们两天去了三个城市，真是特种兵旅游。
wǒ men liǎng tiān qù le sān ge chéng shì， zhēn shì tè zhǒng bīng lǚ yóu。
We visited three cities in two days. Total special forces travel.
