Chinese phrase of the day · August 26 · Feelings

心累

xīn lèi

emotionally drained

A colloquialism, used online and in daily life, for being tired in the heart rather than the body. It's for when you're worn down by relationships or tedious paperwork, typically said with a sigh as 「好心累啊」.

Example

跟他解释了半天还是不懂，好心累。
gēn tā jiě shì le bàn tiān hái shì bù dǒng， hǎo xīn lèi。
I explained it to him for ages and he still doesn't get it. I'm so drained.
