Chinese phrase of the day · August 27 · Shopping

这个保修吗？

zhè ge bǎo xiū ma？

Does this come with a warranty?

A line for checking the warranty when buying appliances or phones; learn 「保修多久？」 ("how long is the warranty?") with it. Note that without an official receipt, 「发票」, you may not be able to use the warranty.

Example

这个电饭锅保修吗？保修多久？
zhè ge diàn fàn guō bǎo xiū ma？ bǎo xiū duō jiǔ？
Does this rice cooker come with a warranty? For how long?
