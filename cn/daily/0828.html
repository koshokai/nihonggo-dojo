Chinese phrase of the day · August 28 · Food

我在减肥。

wǒ zài jiǎn féi。

I'm on a diet.

The standard line for turning down food you're offered; 「减肥」 means losing weight. In China, though, saying 「我在减肥」 and then eating a late-night snack is a running joke, and you might hear 「明天再减」 ("diet starts tomorrow") in reply.

Example

谢谢，蛋糕我就不吃了，我在减肥。
xiè xie， dàn gāo wǒ jiù bù chī le， wǒ zài jiǎn féi。
Thanks, I'll pass on the cake. I'm on a diet.
