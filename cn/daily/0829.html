Chinese phrase of the day · August 29 · Greeting

好的好的。

hǎo de hǎo de。

Sure, sure. / Okay, got it.

Repeating the "okay" shows you're happy to take something on, a pleasant reply common in chats and on the phone. Depending on the tone it can sound like an irritated "yeah, yeah," though, so say it brightly.

Example

好的好的，我明天上午给您回电话。
hǎo de hǎo de， wǒ míng tiān shàng wǔ gěi nín huí diàn huà。
Okay, sure. I'll call you back tomorrow morning.
