Chinese phrase of the day · August 30 · Idiom

远亲不如近邻

yuǎn qīn bù rú jìn lín

A near neighbor is better than a distant relative

In a pinch, neighbors are more help than relatives far away. Use it when a neighbor has helped you or when talking about the importance of being on good terms with the neighbors.

Example

邻居帮我照顾了猫一周，真是远亲不如近邻。
lín jū bāng wǒ zhào gu le māo yì zhōu， zhēn shì yuǎn qīn bù rú jìn lín。
My neighbor looked after my cat for a week. A near neighbor really is better than a distant relative.
