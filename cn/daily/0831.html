Chinese phrase of the day · August 31 · Season

秋老虎

qiū lǎo hǔ

an Indian summer ("autumn tiger")

A word likening the fierce heat that lingers after the Start of Autumn to a tiger, often heard in weather forecasts from late August into September. It's like "Indian summer" in English, used as in 「秋老虎来了」 or 「秋老虎还没走」.

Example

都立秋了还这么热，秋老虎真厉害。
dōu lì qiū le hái zhè me rè， qiū lǎo hǔ zhēn lì hai。
It's already past the Start of Autumn and still so hot. This lingering heat is brutal.
