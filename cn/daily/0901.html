Chinese phrase of the day · September 1 · Season

开学了！

kāi xué le！

School's back!

For the start of the new term around September 1; in China the school year starts in September. The classic picture is parents relieved, 「终于开学了」 ("school's finally started"), and kids sad the vacation is over.

Example

开学了，又要早起了。
kāi xué le， yòu yào zǎo qǐ le。
School's started again, so it's back to getting up early.
