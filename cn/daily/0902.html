Chinese phrase of the day · September 2 · Greeting

回头见！

huí tóu jiàn！

See you later!

Literally "see you when I turn around," a light goodbye to someone you'll see again soon. It's a casual phrase for friends and coworkers, and 「明天见」 means "see you tomorrow."

Example

我先去开会，回头见！
wǒ xiān qù kāi huì， huí tóu jiàn！
I'm off to a meeting first. See you later!
