Chinese phrase of the day · September 3 · Internet slang

摸鱼

mō yú

to slack off (at work)

From "catching fish by hand," it means sneaking a break at work. It's used casually as an office joke, about as light as "just slacking off a bit" in English.

Example

上班摸鱼被老板看到了，好尴尬。
shàng bān mō yú bèi lǎo bǎn kàn dào le， hǎo gān gà。
The boss caught me slacking off at work. So awkward.
