Chinese phrase of the day · September 4 · Feelings

怪不得

guài bu de

no wonder

Said when you find out the reason and it all makes sense, as in 「怪不得他这么高兴」 ("no wonder he's so happy"). 「难怪」 means almost the same, and compared with 「原来如此」 it mixes surprise with understanding.

Example

他是北京人啊，怪不得普通话这么标准。
tā shì běi jīng rén a， guài bu de pǔ tōng huà zhè me biāo zhǔn。
He's from Beijing? No wonder his Mandarin is so standard.
