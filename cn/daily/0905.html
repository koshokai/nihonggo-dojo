Chinese phrase of the day · September 5 · Food

服务员，点菜！

fú wù yuán， diǎn cài！

Excuse me, we'd like to order!

For calling a server over to order in a restaurant; 「服务员」 is the normal way to address them. These days many restaurants also let you order on your phone by scanning a QR code.

Example

服务员，点菜！先来一个宫保鸡丁。
fú wù yuán， diǎn cài！ xiān lái yí ge gōng bǎo jī dīng。
Excuse me, we'd like to order! First, one kung pao chicken.
