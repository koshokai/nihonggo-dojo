Chinese phrase of the day · September 6 · Shopping

缺货了

quē huò le

out of stock

When an item has no stock left. In online shops you'll often see notices like 「暂时缺货」 (temporarily out of stock) and 「到货通知」 (restock alert), and when a popular item sells out people also say 「卖光了」.

Example

这个颜色缺货了，下周才能到。
zhè ge yán sè quē huò le， xià zhōu cái néng dào。
This color is out of stock. It won't arrive until next week.
