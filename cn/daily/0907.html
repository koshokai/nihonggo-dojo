Chinese phrase of the day · September 7 · Idiom

塞翁失马

sài wēng shī mǎ

a blessing in disguise

The lesson that what looks like bad luck can lead to good fortune; the full form is 「塞翁失马，焉知非福」. Use it to cheer up a friend who's feeling down.

Example

别难过了，塞翁失马，焉知非福，说不定是好事。
bié nán guò le， sài wēng shī mǎ， yān zhī fēi fú， shuō bu dìng shì hǎo shì。
Don't be sad. It could be a blessing in disguise, maybe it's a good thing.
