Chinese phrase of the day · September 8 · Greeting

来了来了！

lái le lái le！

Coming, coming!

A reply when someone calls you or the doorbell rings; doubling it gives a hurried "I'll be right there." Servers also say it when called to take an order, an everyday expression just like "Coming!" in English.

Example

来了来了！别敲了，我马上开门。
lái le lái le！ bié qiāo le， wǒ mǎ shàng kāi mén。
Coming, coming! Stop knocking, I'm opening the door.
