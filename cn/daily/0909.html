Chinese phrase of the day · September 9 · Travel

我要办入住。

wǒ yào bàn rù zhù。

I'd like to check in.

At a hotel front desk; say it while handing over your passport and you'll be understood. Checking out is 「退房」, so 「我要退房」 does the job.

Example

您好，我要办入住，我在网上订了房间。
nín hǎo， wǒ yào bàn rù zhù， wǒ zài wǎng shàng dìng le fáng jiān。
Hello, I'd like to check in. I booked a room online.
