Chinese phrase of the day · September 10 · Season

教师节快乐！

jiào shī jié kuài lè！

Happy Teachers' Day!

September 10 is Teachers' Day in China, when students give teachers flowers and cards to say thank you. Many people still message their former teachers after graduating, and it's standard to add 「老师辛苦了！」.

Example

王老师，教师节快乐！谢谢您的教导！
wáng lǎo shī， jiào shī jié kuài lè！ xiè xie nín de jiào dǎo！
Happy Teachers' Day, Professor Wang! Thank you for your guidance!
