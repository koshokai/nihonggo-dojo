Chinese phrase of the day · September 11 · Feelings

没事儿。

méi shìr。

It's fine, no problem.

A super handy phrase for when someone apologizes or worries about you, and it can even replace "you're welcome." Adding 「儿」 is northern speech and gives it a friendly ring.

Example

没事儿，我自己能回去，你别送了。
méi shìr， wǒ zì jǐ néng huí qu， nǐ bié sòng le。
It's fine, I can get back on my own. You don't need to see me off.
