Chinese phrase of the day · September 12 · Internet slang

干饭人

gān fàn rén

someone who lives to eat

「干饭」 means eating a hearty meal. Phrases like 「干饭人，干饭魂」 poke fun at yourself as someone who looks forward to meals above all, and inviting people at lunch break with 「走，干饭去！」 is classic.

Example

十二点到了，干饭人准备出发！
shí èr diǎn dào le， gān fàn rén zhǔn bèi chū fā！
It's twelve o'clock. Foodies, prepare to move out!
