Chinese phrase of the day · September 13 · Work

请稍等。

qǐng shāo děng。

Just a moment, please.

For every work situation: answering calls, serving customers, meetings. 「稍等一下」 means the same, and between friends 「等一下」 or 「等会儿」 is fine.

Example

请稍等，我帮您查一下。
qǐng shāo děng， wǒ bāng nín chá yí xià。
One moment, please. Let me check that for you.
