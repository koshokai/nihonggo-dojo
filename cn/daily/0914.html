Chinese phrase of the day · September 14 · Friends

你还是老样子。

nǐ hái shì lǎo yàng zi。

You haven't changed a bit.

Said to a friend you haven't seen in a long time, about either looks or personality. It can be praise or teasing, so say it with a smile; the standard reply is 「你也一样！」.

Example

十年没见，你还是老样子，一点没变。
shí nián méi jiàn， nǐ hái shì lǎo yàng zi， yì diǎn méi biàn。
Ten years and you're the same as ever, you haven't changed a bit.
