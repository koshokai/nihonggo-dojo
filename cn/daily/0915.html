Chinese phrase of the day · September 15 · Shopping

多少钱？

duō shao qián？

How much is it?

The phrase you'll use most when shopping; point and say 「这个多少钱？」 and you're set. 「块」 in the answer is the spoken form of 「元」 on price tags and means the same thing.

Example

这个苹果多少钱一斤？
zhè ge píng guǒ duō shao qián yì jīn？
How much are these apples per jin (500 g)?
