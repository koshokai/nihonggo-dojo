Chinese phrase of the day · September 16 · Feelings

我服了。

wǒ fú le。

I give up. / You win.

Used both when you're in awe of someone's ability and when you're too exasperated for words. 「我服了你」 ("you've got me beat") makes the admiration or exasperation toward the person clear, and the meaning depends on your delivery and tone.

Example

他一个人吃了五碗饭，我服了。
tā yí ge rén chī le wǔ wǎn fàn， wǒ fú le。
He ate five bowls of rice by himself. I give up.
