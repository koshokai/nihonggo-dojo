Chinese phrase of the day · September 17 · Travel

能说慢一点吗？

néng shuō màn yì diǎn ma？

Could you speak a bit more slowly?

A lifesaver when you can't catch what's being said; adding 「我的中文不太好」 will get people to slow down. It may be the very first line a learner should memorize.

Example

不好意思，我听不太懂，能说慢一点吗？
bù hǎo yì si， wǒ tīng bú tài dǒng， néng shuō màn yì diǎn ma？
Sorry, I don't quite understand. Could you speak a bit more slowly?
