Chinese phrase of the day · September 18 · Food

这个辣吗？

zhè ge là ma？

Is this spicy?

An essential question if you can't handle spicy food. Note that when Chinese people say 「不太辣」 ("not very spicy") it can still be plenty hot for many visitors, so if you're worried, say clearly 「一点儿辣都不要」 ("no chili at all").

Example

服务员，这个水煮鱼辣吗？
fú wù yuán， zhè ge shuǐ zhǔ yú là ma？
Excuse me, is this boiled fish in chili oil spicy?
