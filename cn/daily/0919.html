Chinese phrase of the day · September 19 · Season

国庆去哪儿玩？

guó qìng qù nǎr wán？

Where are you going for National Day?

Standard small talk about plans for the big early-October holiday (Golden Week). Every tourist spot gets mobbed, sometimes 「人山人海」 (a sea of people), so travel talk gets lively.

Example

国庆去哪儿玩？要不要一起去成都？
guó qìng qù nǎr wán？ yào bu yào yì qǐ qù chéng dū？
Where are you going for National Day? Want to go to Chengdu together?
