Chinese phrase of the day · September 20 · Work

麻烦你跟进一下。

má fan nǐ gēn jìn yí xià。

Could you follow up on this?

A standard office expression for asking a coworker to check on or handle an ongoing matter. 「跟进」 means "to follow up and keep things moving," in the sense of tracking progress; if your boss says it to you, reply 「好的，我跟进」.

Example

客户那边还没回复，麻烦你跟进一下。
kè hù nà biān hái méi huí fù， má fan nǐ gēn jìn yí xià。
The client still hasn't replied. Could you follow up on it?
