Chinese phrase of the day · September 21 · Feelings

别担心。

bié dān xīn。

Don't worry.

A line to encourage someone who looks anxious; adding 「有我呢」 ("I'm here") makes you sound dependable. 「放心吧」 works in the same situations.

Example

别担心，有我呢，一定没问题。
bié dān xīn， yǒu wǒ ne， yí dìng méi wèn tí。
Don't worry, I'm here. It'll definitely be fine.
