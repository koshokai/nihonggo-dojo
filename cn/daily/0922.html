Chinese phrase of the day · September 22 · Idiom

有缘千里来相会

yǒu yuán qiān lǐ lái xiāng huì

Fate brings people together from afar

If fate wills it, you'll meet even a thousand li apart. Use it when you make friends with someone from a faraway country or to celebrate a chance meeting; it's like saying "it was meant to be."

Example

我们一个在东京，一个在上海，真是有缘千里来相会。
wǒ men yí ge zài dōng jīng， yí ge zài shàng hǎi， zhēn shì yǒu yuán qiān lǐ lái xiāng huì。
One of us in Tokyo and one in Shanghai, we were truly destined to meet.
