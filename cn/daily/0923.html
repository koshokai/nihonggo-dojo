Chinese phrase of the day · September 23 · Season

花好月圆

huā hǎo yuè yuán

blooming flowers and a full moon

From beautiful flowers in bloom and a full moon, a phrase wishing a family harmony and happiness. It's often used in Mid-Autumn Festival and wedding messages, and as a four-character phrase it looks elegant written in a card.

Example

中秋佳节，祝你们一家花好月圆！
zhōng qiū jiā jié， zhù nǐ men yì jiā huā hǎo yuè yuán！
Wishing your whole family happiness and togetherness this Mid-Autumn Festival!
