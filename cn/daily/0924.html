Chinese phrase of the day · September 24 · Season

中秋节快乐！

zhōng qiū jié kuài lè！

Happy Mid-Autumn Festival!

The 15th day of the 8th lunar month, an important holiday when families gather to admire the moon and eat mooncakes; it's also a day for thinking of family far away. Asking 「月饼你喜欢什么馅儿的？」 ("which mooncake filling do you like?") gets a conversation going.

Example

中秋节快乐！今年你家吃什么馅儿的月饼？
zhōng qiū jié kuài lè！ jīn nián nǐ jiā chī shén me xiànr de yuè bǐng？
Happy Mid-Autumn Festival! What filling of mooncakes is your family having this year?
