Chinese phrase of the day · September 25 · Season

今晚的月亮好圆啊！

jīn wǎn de yuè liang hǎo yuán a！

The moon is so round tonight!

Said while gazing at the moon with family or friends on Mid-Autumn night. In China the full moon symbolizes 「团圆」 (family reunion), and some people message family living far away that they're looking at the same moon.

Example

快出来看，今晚的月亮好圆啊！
kuài chū lai kàn， jīn wǎn de yuè liang hǎo yuán a！
Come out and look, the moon is so round tonight!
