Chinese phrase of the day · September 26 · Internet slang

我有点社恐。

wǒ yǒu diǎn shè kǒng。

I'm a bit socially awkward.

Short for 「社交恐惧症」 (social anxiety disorder), a light way of saying you're not great with people. It's much lighter than the original medical term and used casually in self-introductions and small talk.

Example

我有点社恐，第一次见面不太会聊天。
wǒ yǒu diǎn shè kǒng， dì yī cì jiàn miàn bú tài huì liáo tiān。
I'm a bit socially awkward, so I'm not good at chatting with people I've just met.
