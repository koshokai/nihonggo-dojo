Chinese phrase of the day · September 27 · Travel

一路平安！

yí lù píng ān！

Have a safe trip!

A send-off wishing a traveler a safe journey; some people prefer it for those flying. On arrival, it's good manners to send 「平安到达」 ("arrived safely"), and learn it together with 「一路顺风」.

Example

祝你一路平安，到了给我发个消息！
zhù nǐ yí lù píng ān， dào le gěi wǒ fā ge xiāo xi！
Have a safe trip, and send me a message when you get there!
