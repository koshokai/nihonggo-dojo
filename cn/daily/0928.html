Chinese phrase of the day · September 28 · Shopping

可以试一下吗？

kě yǐ shì yí xià ma？

Can I try it?

A handy phrase for trying on clothes, testing cosmetics or sampling food. 「试」 means "to try," and it combines into words like 「试吃」 and 「试穿」.

Example

这件衬衫可以试一下吗？
zhè jiàn chèn shān kě yǐ shì yí xià ma？
Can I try on this shirt?
