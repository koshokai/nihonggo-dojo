Chinese phrase of the day · September 29 · Greeting

让你破费了。

ràng nǐ pò fèi le。

You shouldn't have spent so much.

A line of appreciation and embarrassment when someone treats you or gives you a gift; 「破费」 means making someone spend money. It's like "Oh, you shouldn't have!" in English, and the other person replies 「哪里，应该的」.

Example

这么贵的礼物，让你破费了。
zhè me guì de lǐ wù， ràng nǐ pò fèi le。
Such an expensive gift, you really shouldn't have.
