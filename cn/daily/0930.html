Chinese phrase of the day · September 30 · Idiom

好事多磨

hǎo shì duō mó

Good things take time

It means good things usually come with many obstacles along the way. In Chinese it's used as encouragement, "good things come after hardship," a bit like "the course of true love never did run smooth."

Example

你们终于要结婚了，真是好事多磨！
nǐ men zhōng yú yào jié hūn le， zhēn shì hǎo shì duō mó！
You two are finally getting married. Good things really do take time!
