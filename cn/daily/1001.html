Chinese phrase of the day · October 1 · Season

国庆节快乐！

guó qìng jié kuài lè！

Happy National Day!

A greeting for 「国庆节」, the National Day on October 1. It kicks off the roughly week-long holiday 「黄金周」 (Golden Week), when travel and trips home fill the whole country; for people enjoying the holiday, adding 「假期愉快！」 is natural.

Example

国庆节快乐！祝大家假期愉快！
guó qìng jié kuài lè！ zhù dà jiā jià qī yú kuài！
Happy National Day! Enjoy the holiday, everyone!
