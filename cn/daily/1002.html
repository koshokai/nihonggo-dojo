Chinese phrase of the day · October 2 · Friends

生日快乐！

shēng rì kuài lè！

Happy birthday!

The standard birthday greeting. It's often sent by chat, and some people include a red envelope of gift money; replying 「谢谢！」 with a smiley emoji is perfect.

Example

生日快乐！这是给你的礼物，打开看看吧。
shēng rì kuài lè！ zhè shì gěi nǐ de lǐ wù， dǎ kāi kàn kan ba。
Happy birthday! Here's your present, open it!
