Chinese phrase of the day · October 3 · Idiom

人山人海

rén shān rén hǎi

a sea of people

Crowds of people like mountains and seas. It's perfect for describing crowds at tourist spots during holidays or at popular events, a four-character idiom used all the time in everyday conversation.

Example

国庆的长城人山人海，根本走不动。
guó qìng de cháng chéng rén shān rén hǎi， gēn běn zǒu bu dòng。
The Great Wall during National Day was a sea of people. You could barely move.
