Chinese phrase of the day · October 4 · Travel

高速堵成停车场了。

gāo sù dǔ chéng tíng chē chǎng le。

The highway's turned into a parking lot.

A jokey way to describe heavy holiday traffic jams. In China, highways are sometimes toll-free on public holidays, so car travel all happens at once; 「堵车」 means a traffic jam, and it's the same scene as holiday-weekend gridlock anywhere.

Example

国庆第一天，高速堵成停车场了。
guó qìng dì yī tiān， gāo sù dǔ chéng tíng chē chǎng le。
First day of the National Day holiday, and the highway's a parking lot.
