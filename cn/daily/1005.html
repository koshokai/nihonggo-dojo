Chinese phrase of the day · October 5 · Greeting

没关系。

méi guān xi。

Never mind. / It's okay.

The reply when someone apologizes with 「对不起」 or 「不好意思」, a soft way to put them at ease. More casually, 「没事儿」 is common.

Example

没关系，我们也刚到，快坐吧！
méi guān xi， wǒ men yě gāng dào， kuài zuò ba！
It's okay, we just got here too. Have a seat!
