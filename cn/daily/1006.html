Chinese phrase of the day · October 6 · Internet slang

爷青回

yé qīng huí

My childhood is back!

Short for 「我的青春回来了」 ("my youth is back"), internet slang for when cartoons, songs or snacks you loved as a kid make a comeback. 「爷」 ("grandpa," i.e. "yours truly") is a playful way to say "I," and it's a standard comment on nostalgic things.

Example

小时候看的动画片出新版了，爷青回！
xiǎo shí hou kàn de dòng huà piàn chū xīn bǎn le， yé qīng huí！
A new version of the cartoon I watched as a kid is out. My childhood is back!
