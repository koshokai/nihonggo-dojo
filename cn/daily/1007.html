Chinese phrase of the day · October 7 · Work

今天又要加班了。

jīn tiān yòu yào jiā bān le。

Overtime again today...

「加班」 means working overtime, good for grumbling with coworkers; if someone says it to you, show appreciation with 「辛苦了！」. Working on a weekend is also called 「加班」.

Example

今天又要加班了，晚饭只能点外卖。
jīn tiān yòu yào jiā bān le， wǎn fàn zhǐ néng diǎn wài mài。
Overtime again today. Dinner will have to be delivery.
