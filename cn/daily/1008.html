Chinese phrase of the day · October 8 · Work

节后综合征

jié hòu zōng hé zhēng

post-holiday blues

Feeling sluggish after a long holiday because you can't get back into the rhythm of work or school. It's often talked about after National Day or Spring Festival, like "post-vacation blues" in English, and used half-jokingly.

Example

假期结束了，今天上班节后综合征发作。
jià qī jié shù le， jīn tiān shàng bān jié hòu zōng hé zhēng fā zuò。
The holiday's over, and today at work the post-holiday blues hit hard.
