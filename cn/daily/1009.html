Chinese phrase of the day · October 9 · Food

干杯！

gān bēi！

Cheers!

The toast when clinking glasses. It originally means "dry the cup," and at banquets you may be pressed to drink it all in one go; if you can't, saying 「随意」 ("as you like") is fine.

Example

为了我们的友谊，干杯！
wèi le wǒ men de yǒu yì， gān bēi！
To our friendship, cheers!
