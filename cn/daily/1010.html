Chinese phrase of the day · October 10 · Feelings

太可爱了！

tài kě ài le！

So cute!

Said when you see a baby, an animal or a cute little thing. 「可爱」 means "cute" but is rarely used for adult men, and online people sometimes double it as 「可可爱爱」.

Example

你家的小狗太可爱了，可以摸摸吗？
nǐ jiā de xiǎo gǒu tài kě ài le， kě yǐ mō mo ma？
Your puppy is so cute! Can I pet it?
