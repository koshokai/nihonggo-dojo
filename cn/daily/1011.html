Chinese phrase of the day · October 11 · Work

方便的时候回复我就行。

fāng biàn de shí hou huí fù wǒ jiù xíng。

Just reply whenever it's convenient.

A soft request that's considerate of the other person's schedule; 「方便」 means "convenient." It's handy for saying something isn't urgent, and it leaves a much better impression.

Example

不着急，方便的时候回复我就行。
bù zháo jí， fāng biàn de shí hou huí fù wǒ jiù xíng。
No rush, just reply whenever it's convenient.
