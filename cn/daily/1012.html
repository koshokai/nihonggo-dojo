Chinese phrase of the day · October 12 · Season

秋高气爽

qiū gāo qì shuǎng

crisp, clear autumn weather

A four-character phrase for the high, clear autumn sky and refreshing air, perfect for praising autumn weather or inviting people on an outing or hike. Autumn is said to be Beijing's most pleasant season.

Example

秋高气爽，正是爬山的好时候。
qiū gāo qì shuǎng， zhèng shì pá shān de hǎo shí hou。
The autumn air is crisp and clear, just the right time for hiking.
