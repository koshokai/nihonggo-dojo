Chinese phrase of the day · October 13 · Feelings

马马虎虎。

mǎ mǎ hǔ hǔ。

So-so.

The standard modest answer when asked "How are you?" or "How's your Chinese?" 「马马虎虎」 also originally means "careless, sloppy," so using it about someone else's work is a criticism.

Example

我的中文马马虎虎，还在努力学习。
wǒ de zhōng wén mǎ mǎ hǔ hǔ， hái zài nǔ lì xué xí。
My Chinese is so-so. I'm still working hard on it.
