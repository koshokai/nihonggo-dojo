Chinese phrase of the day · October 14 · Shopping

拼单

pīn dān

to group-buy, to order together

Several people ordering together online to meet the conditions for a discount or free shipping; you ask friends or coworkers 「一起拼单吗？」. 「拼」 is also used for sharing deliveries or taxi rides.

Example

满两百包邮，我们一起拼单吧！
mǎn liǎng bǎi bāo yóu， wǒ men yì qǐ pīn dān ba！
Free shipping over 200 yuan, so let's order together!
