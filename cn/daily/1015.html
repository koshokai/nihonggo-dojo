Chinese phrase of the day · October 15 · Food

螃蟹肥了

páng xiè féi le

Crab season is here

Autumn is the season for the hairy crab, 「大闸蟹」; females are said to be best in the 9th lunar month and males in the 10th, and talk of crab signals autumn's arrival. It's also often used as an excuse to invite friends out for a meal.

Example

秋天到了，螃蟹肥了，周末去吃螃蟹吧！
qiū tiān dào le， páng xiè féi le， zhōu mò qù chī páng xiè ba！
Autumn's here, and so is crab season. Let's go eat crab this weekend!
