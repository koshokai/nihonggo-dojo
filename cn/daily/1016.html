Chinese phrase of the day · October 16 · Friends

你说呢？

nǐ shuō ne？

What do you think?

For asking someone's opinion or leaving the decision to them; 「你觉得呢？」 means the same. Said a bit forcefully, it becomes a rhetorical "isn't it obvious?"

Example

这件事该怎么办？你说呢？
zhè jiàn shì gāi zěn me bàn？ nǐ shuō ne？
What should we do about this? What do you think?
