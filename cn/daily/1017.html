Chinese phrase of the day · October 17 · Season

重阳节快乐！

chóng yáng jié kuài lè！

Happy Double Ninth Festival!

The Double Ninth Festival on the 9th day of the 9th lunar month is also 「敬老节」, a day to honor the elderly. People call grandparents and older relatives or climb a mountain together with them, and adding 「身体健康」 to messages for older people is appreciated.

Example

爷爷，重阳节快乐！祝您身体健康！
yé ye， chóng yáng jié kuài lè！ zhù nín shēn tǐ jiàn kāng！
Grandpa, happy Double Ninth Festival! Wishing you good health!
