Chinese phrase of the day · October 18 · Idiom

失败是成功之母

shī bài shì chéng gōng zhī mǔ

Failure is the mother of success

Encouragement that failure lays the foundation for success, the same idea as the English saying. It's a positive thing to say to a friend whose exam or work didn't go well.

Example

这次没考好也没关系，失败是成功之母。
zhè cì méi kǎo hǎo yě méi guān xi， shī bài shì chéng gōng zhī mǔ。
It's okay that this exam didn't go well. Failure is the mother of success.
