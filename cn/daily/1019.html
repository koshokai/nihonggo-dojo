Chinese phrase of the day · October 19 · Feelings

不至于吧。

bú zhì yú ba。

Isn't that a bit much?

For when you feel someone's reaction or worry is exaggerated; it means "it's not worth going that far." You can be specific, as in 「不至于这么生气吧」 ("no need to get that angry").

Example

就迟到五分钟，不至于吧，别生气了。
jiù chí dào wǔ fēn zhōng， bú zhì yú ba， bié shēng qì le。
I was only five minutes late. No need to get so angry, come on.
