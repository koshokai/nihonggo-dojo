Chinese phrase of the day · October 20 · Shopping

能开发票吗？

néng kāi fā piào ma？

Can I get an official receipt?

For when you need one for a business trip or expense claim; a Chinese 「发票」 is an official receipt for tax purposes. You may be asked for your company name and tax ID, so having them ready makes it smooth.

Example

你好，能开发票吗？公司要报销。
nǐ hǎo， néng kāi fā piào ma？ gōng sī yào bào xiāo。
Hi, could I get an official receipt? I need to claim it from my company.
