Chinese phrase of the day · October 21 · Travel

无线网密码是多少？

wú xiàn wǎng mì mǎ shì duō shao？

What's the Wi-Fi password?

Useful in cafés, hotels and friends' homes. In Chinese, Wi-Fi is called 「无线网」 or simply "WiFi," and many places print the password on the receipt or post it on the wall.

Example

你好，请问这里的无线网密码是多少？
nǐ hǎo， qǐng wèn zhè lǐ de wú xiàn wǎng mì mǎ shì duō shao？
Excuse me, what's the Wi-Fi password here?
