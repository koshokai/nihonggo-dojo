Chinese phrase of the day · October 22 · Idiom

一场秋雨一场寒

yì cháng qiū yǔ yì cháng hán

Each autumn rain brings more cold

A seasonal saying that every autumn rain brings the temperature down another notch, used in weather small talk or when telling family to dress warmly. It captures exactly the feeling of autumn getting colder with each rainfall.

Example

一场秋雨一场寒，明天记得多穿件外套。
yì cháng qiū yǔ yì cháng hán， míng tiān jì de duō chuān jiàn wài tào。
Each autumn rain brings more cold, so remember to wear an extra jacket tomorrow.
