Chinese phrase of the day · October 23 · Work

团建

tuán jiàn

team-building event

Short for 「团队建设」, a company event where coworkers eat, travel or play sports together. It's meant to bring people closer, but when it's held on a day off some people grumble, "team building again?"; think company trips and social gatherings.

Example

这周六公司团建，去郊外烧烤。
zhè zhōu liù gōng sī tuán jiàn， qù jiāo wài shāo kǎo。
The company has a team-building event this Saturday, a barbecue in the countryside.
