Chinese phrase of the day · October 24 · Feelings

后悔死了！

hòu huǐ sǐ le！

I regret it so much!

An exaggerated way of expressing strong regret, typically followed by 「早知道就不〜了」 ("if I'd known, I wouldn't have ..."). It's common in casual situations, from shopping mistakes to oversleeping.

Example

昨天没买那件打折的大衣，现在后悔死了！
zuó tiān méi mǎi nà jiàn dǎ zhé de dà yī， xiàn zài hòu huǐ sǐ le！
I didn't buy that discounted coat yesterday, and now I regret it so much!
