Chinese phrase of the day · October 25 · Internet slang

冲！

chōng！

Charge! / Let's go!

From "to charge forward," it's a rallying cry when taking on a challenge or diving into a sale. Online, doubling it up as 「冲冲冲！」 for momentum is standard, and you can cheer a friend on with 「冲啊！」 too.

Example

演唱会门票十点开抢，冲！
yǎn chàng huì mén piào shí diǎn kāi qiǎng， chōng！
Concert tickets go on sale at ten. Let's go!
