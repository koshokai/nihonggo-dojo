Chinese phrase of the day · October 26 · Shopping

物美价廉

wù měi jià lián

good quality at a low price

A four-character phrase for goods that are good and cheap at the same time, often seen in shop slogans and reviews. It's similar to 「性价比高」 but a bit more literary, and it can also praise a market or an eatery.

Example

这家小店的东西物美价廉，学生都爱来。
zhè jiā xiǎo diàn de dōng xi wù měi jià lián， xué sheng dōu ài lái。
This little shop's stuff is good and cheap, so students love coming here.
