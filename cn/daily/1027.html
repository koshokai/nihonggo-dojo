Chinese phrase of the day · October 27 · Season

降温了，注意保暖。

jiàng wēn le， zhù yì bǎo nuǎn。

It's gotten colder, keep warm.

A caring line for family and friends on a day that suddenly turns cold. 「降温」 means the temperature dropping and 「保暖」 means keeping your body warm, and the phrase is common in weather app notifications too.

Example

明天大降温，出门注意保暖。
míng tiān dà jiàng wēn， chū mén zhù yì bǎo nuǎn。
There's a big temperature drop tomorrow, so keep warm when you go out.
