Chinese phrase of the day · October 28 · Work

在家办公

zài jiā bàn gōng

working from home

Doing your job at home, also called 「远程办公」 (remote work). You might tell coworkers 「今天在家办公」, or the company may order it on days with bad weather; it's the same as "WFH" in English.

Example

今天下大雨，公司让大家在家办公。
jīn tiān xià dà yǔ， gōng sī ràng dà jiā zài jiā bàn gōng。
It's pouring today, so the company told everyone to work from home.
