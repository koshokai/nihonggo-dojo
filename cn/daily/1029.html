Chinese phrase of the day · October 29 · Idiom

民以食为天

mín yǐ shí wéi tiān

Food is the people's heaven

An old saying that eating matters more than anything to people. Foodies also use it jokingly as an excuse, "food comes first, right?", and it shows the passion of Chinese food culture.

Example

民以食为天，先吃饭再说工作吧。
mín yǐ shí wéi tiān， xiān chī fàn zài shuō gōng zuò ba。
Food comes first. Let's eat, then talk about work.
