Chinese phrase of the day · October 30 · Idiom

患难见真情

huàn nàn jiàn zhēn qíng

A friend in need is a friend indeed

Hard times show people's true feelings and friendships, close to "A friend in need is a friend indeed" in English. Using it to thank a friend who helped you really gets your feelings across.

Example

我住院的时候你天天来看我，真是患难见真情。
wǒ zhù yuàn de shí hou nǐ tiān tiān lái kàn wǒ， zhēn shì huàn nàn jiàn zhēn qíng。
You came to see me every day when I was in the hospital. A friend in need is a friend indeed.
