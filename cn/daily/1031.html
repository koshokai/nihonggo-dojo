Chinese phrase of the day · October 31 · Greeting

改天再聊！

gǎi tiān zài liáo！

Talk to you later!

A casual line for wrapping up a conversation, often at the end of a call or chat; 「回头再聊」 means the same. It's handy when you're truly busy, but if you're cutting someone off, adding 「不好意思」 is more polite.

Example

我要上车了，改天再聊！拜拜！
wǒ yào shàng chē le， gǎi tiān zài liáo！ bài bài！
I'm getting in the car now. Talk to you later! Bye!
