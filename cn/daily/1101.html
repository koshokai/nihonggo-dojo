Chinese phrase of the day · November 1 · Idiom

百闻不如一见

bǎi wén bù rú yí jiàn

Seeing is believing

Seeing something once tells you more than hearing about it a hundred times, like "seeing is believing" or "a picture is worth a thousand words." Try saying 「真是百闻不如一见！」 when you're traveling.

Example

这里的风景真是百闻不如一见，比照片还美。
zhè lǐ de fēng jǐng zhēn shì bǎi wén bù rú yí jiàn， bǐ zhào piàn hái měi。
The scenery here really proves seeing is believing. It's even more beautiful than in the photos.
