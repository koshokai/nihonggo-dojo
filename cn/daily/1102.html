Chinese phrase of the day · November 2 · Season

该穿秋裤了。

gāi chuān qiū kù le。

Time to put on long johns.

「秋裤」 are long underpants worn for warmth. Chinese parents telling their kids 「穿秋裤！」 as it gets cold is such a cliché it's an internet joke, and the word symbolizes family concern as the seasons change.

Example

天越来越冷了，该穿秋裤了。
tiān yuè lái yuè lěng le， gāi chuān qiū kù le。
It's getting colder and colder. Time to put on long johns.
