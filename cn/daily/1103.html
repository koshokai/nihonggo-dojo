Chinese phrase of the day · November 3 · Season

天冷了，多穿点。

tiān lěng le， duō chuān diǎn。

It's getting cold, bundle up.

A caring line for family and friends as the seasons change, and a line Chinese parents often say to their kids. When someone says it to you, reply 「知道了，你也是！」.

Example

天冷了，多穿点，别感冒了。
tiān lěng le， duō chuān diǎn， bié gǎn mào le。
It's getting cold, so bundle up and don't catch a cold.
