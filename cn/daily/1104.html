Chinese phrase of the day · November 4 · Greeting

早点休息。

zǎo diǎn xiū xi。

Get some rest.

A caring line for someone who's tired or working late, often used to close a chat. 「早点睡」 means the same and is more colloquial; a natural reply is 「好，你也是，晚安」.

Example

今天辛苦了，早点休息吧。
jīn tiān xīn kǔ le， zǎo diǎn xiū xi ba。
You worked hard today. Get some rest early.
