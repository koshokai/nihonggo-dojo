Chinese phrase of the day · November 5 · Food

多吃点！

duō chī diǎn！

Eat more!

A hospitable phrase you'll hear again and again at Chinese tables, and people will often serve food onto your plate. If you're full, reply with a smile: 「够了够了，谢谢」.

Example

多吃点，这些菜都是专门给你做的！
duō chī diǎn， zhè xiē cài dōu shì zhuān mén gěi nǐ zuò de！
Eat more, I made all these dishes especially for you!
