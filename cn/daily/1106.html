Chinese phrase of the day · November 6 · Internet slang

摆烂

bǎi làn

to give up and let it all go

Deliberately ceasing to try because things won't work out anyway. It originally comes from sports, and online it's used self-mockingly, as in 「今天摆烂了」 ("zero motivation today").

Example

作业太多了，我今天先摆烂一天。
zuò yè tài duō le， wǒ jīn tiān xiān bǎi làn yì tiān。
There's too much homework. I'm just giving up for today.
