Chinese phrase of the day · November 7 · Travel

离这儿远吗？

lí zhèr yuǎn ma？

Is it far from here?

For checking the distance to where you're going; it helps to follow up with 「走路要多久？」 ("how long on foot?"). Even if they say 「不远」, it can turn out to be surprisingly far...

Example

请问，博物馆离这儿远吗？走路要多久？
qǐng wèn， bó wù guǎn lí zhèr yuǎn ma？ zǒu lù yào duō jiǔ？
Excuse me, is the museum far from here? How long does it take on foot?
