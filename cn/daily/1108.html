Chinese phrase of the day · November 8 · Feelings

真倒霉！

zhēn dǎo méi！

Just my luck!

A grumble when bad luck keeps coming; 「倒霉」 means bad luck. It's used as in 「今天真倒霉」 or 「倒霉透了」 ("the worst"), much like "Just my luck!" or "What a day!" in English, and you'll hear it often.

Example

出门忘带伞，又赶上下雨，真倒霉！
chū mén wàng dài sǎn， yòu gǎn shàng xià yǔ， zhēn dǎo méi！
I forgot my umbrella and got caught in the rain again. Just my luck!
