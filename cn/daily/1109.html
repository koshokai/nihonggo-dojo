Chinese phrase of the day · November 9 · Shopping

凑满减

còu mǎn jiǎn

to add items to hit a discount threshold

Adding other items to meet the conditions of a 「满减」 deal, "X yuan off when you spend Y." It's a classic headache during sales, and friends sometimes club together with 「一起凑满减」.

Example

为了凑满减，我又加了一包零食。
wèi le còu mǎn jiǎn， wǒ yòu jiā le yì bāo líng shí。
To hit the discount threshold, I added another bag of snacks.
