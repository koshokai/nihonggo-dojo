Chinese phrase of the day · November 10 · Internet slang

清空购物车

qīng kōng gòu wù chē

to empty the shopping cart (buy it all)

Buying everything you've saved in an online shopping cart in one go. People declare it as a sale-season goal, as in 「今年双十一要清空购物车！」, and it often goes together with the self-mocking 「剁手」 (overspending).

Example

等了一个月，今晚终于可以清空购物车了。
děng le yí ge yuè， jīn wǎn zhōng yú kě yǐ qīng kōng gòu wù chē le。
After waiting a month, tonight I can finally empty my shopping cart.
