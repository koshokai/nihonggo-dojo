Chinese phrase of the day · November 11 · Season

双十一买了啥？

shuāng shí yī mǎi le shá？

What did you buy on Double 11?

A standard conversation around 「双十一」, the giant online sale on November 11. It started as Singles' Day, 「光棍节」, but is now the biggest shopping event of the year; common answers are like 「买了一堆日用品」.

Example

双十一买了啥？我买了一个新的电饭锅。
shuāng shí yī mǎi le shá？ wǒ mǎi le yí ge xīn de diàn fàn guō。
What did you buy on Double 11? I bought a new rice cooker.
