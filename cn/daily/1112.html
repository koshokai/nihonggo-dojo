Chinese phrase of the day · November 12 · Shopping

剁手了。

duò shǒu le。

I've overspent again.

A self-mocking internet expression meaning "chop off my hands." It's used when laughing at your own impulse buys or shopping sprees while regretting them, often during sales like 「双十一」.

Example

双十一又剁手了，下个月只能吃土了。
shuāng shí yī yòu duò shǒu le， xià ge yuè zhǐ néng chī tǔ le。
I overspent again on Double 11. Next month I'll be eating dirt.
