Chinese phrase of the day · November 13 · Feelings

放心吧！

fàng xīn ba！

Don't worry! Leave it to me!

Literally "put your heart down." Use it when taking on a request or reassuring someone; it has a dependable ring, close to "Leave it to me" in English.

Example

这件事交给我，你就放心吧！
zhè jiàn shì jiāo gěi wǒ， nǐ jiù fàng xīn ba！
Leave this to me, don't worry!
