Chinese phrase of the day · November 14 · Work

我在开会，稍后回电。

wǒ zài kāi huì， shāo hòu huí diàn。

I'm in a meeting, I'll call back later.

The standard message to send when you can't take a call; 「稍后」 means "a little later," and it's also used in phones' auto-replies. In formal situations, 「我在开会，稍后给您回电话」 with 「您」 is more polite.

Example

不好意思，我在开会，稍后回电。
bù hǎo yì si， wǒ zài kāi huì， shāo hòu huí diàn。
Sorry, I'm in a meeting. I'll call you back later.
