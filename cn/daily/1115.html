Chinese phrase of the day · November 15 · Season

来暖气了！

lái nuǎn qì le！

The heating's on!

In the north, district-wide central heating, 「暖气」, starts around mid-November. Once it's on, it's warm enough indoors for short sleeves, and it's a seasonal topic people have been eagerly waiting for; the south basically doesn't have it.

Example

终于来暖气了，屋里暖和多了！
zhōng yú lái nuǎn qì le， wū lǐ nuǎn huo duō le！
The heating's finally on. The room is so much warmer!
