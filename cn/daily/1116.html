Chinese phrase of the day · November 16 · Internet slang

yyds

yyds

GOAT! The best! (a bit dated)

The pinyin initials of 「永远的神」 ("forever a god"). It was hugely popular for a while and now feels a bit dated; knowing it helps you read online posts, but don't overuse it.

Example

这家的红烧牛肉面真是yyds！
zhè jiā de hóng shāo niú ròu miàn zhēn shì yyds！
This place's braised beef noodles are the GOAT!
