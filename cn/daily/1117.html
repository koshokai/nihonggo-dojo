Chinese phrase of the day · November 17 · Friends

你怎么来了？

nǐ zěn me lái le？

What are you doing here? (pleasant surprise)

A line of surprise when someone turns up unexpectedly. Depending on tone it can sound like an annoyed "what are you here for?", so say it with a smile; it's often used where English would say "Oh, you came!"

Example

你怎么来了？不是说今天加班吗？
nǐ zěn me lái le？ bú shì shuō jīn tiān jiā bān ma？
What are you doing here? Didn't you say you had to work late today?
