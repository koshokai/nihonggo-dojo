Chinese phrase of the day · November 18 · Shopping

太贵了！

tài guì le！

That's too expensive!

The opening line in haggling. It works at markets and stalls at tourist sites, but department stores and chains normally don't give discounts, and the trick to negotiating is to say it with a smile.

Example

一件T恤三百块？太贵了！
yí jiàn T xù sān bǎi kuài？ tài guì le！
300 yuan for a T-shirt? That's too expensive!
