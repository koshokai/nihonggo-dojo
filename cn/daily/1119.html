Chinese phrase of the day · November 19 · Idiom

知足常乐

zhī zú cháng lè

Contentment brings happiness

People who are satisfied with what they have are always happy, a philosophy of enjoying the present without being greedy. It's close to "contentment is true wealth," and older people often say it.

Example

工作稳定，家人健康，知足常乐吧。
gōng zuò wěn dìng， jiā rén jiàn kāng， zhī zú cháng lè ba。
My job is stable and my family is healthy. Contentment brings happiness, right?
