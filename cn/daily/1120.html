Chinese phrase of the day · November 20 · Work

我确认一下。

wǒ què rèn yí xià。

Let me check.

A grown-up phrase for when you can't answer on the spot. Saying 「我确认一下再回复您」 ("I'll check and get back to you") sounds polite and reassuring, even in business.

Example

这个日期我确认一下，再回复您。
zhè ge rì qī wǒ què rèn yí xià， zài huí fù nín。
Let me check this date and get back to you.
