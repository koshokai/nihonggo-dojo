Chinese phrase of the day · November 21 · Food

冬天就该吃火锅！

dōng tiān jiù gāi chī huǒ guō！

Winter means hotpot!

A line for inviting someone out for hotpot on a cold day; 「该」 means "should, has to." Chinese hotpot comes in many regional varieties, from fiery Sichuan to Beijing's mutton hotpot, and a 「鸳鸯锅」 is a split pot with spicy and non-spicy broths.

Example

外面这么冷，冬天就该吃火锅！
wài miàn zhè me lěng， dōng tiān jiù gāi chī huǒ guō！
It's so cold outside. Winter means hotpot!
