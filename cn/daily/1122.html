Chinese phrase of the day · November 22 · Greeting

不好意思。

bù hǎo yì si。

Excuse me. / Sorry.

Used widely for light apologies, getting someone's attention or feeling embarrassed. It's lighter than 「对不起」, a very handy word that covers both "excuse me" and "sorry."

Example

不好意思，借过一下。
bù hǎo yì si， jiè guò yí xià。
Excuse me, could I get past?
