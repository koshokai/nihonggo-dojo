Chinese phrase of the day · November 23 · Internet slang

嘴替

zuǐ tì

someone who says what you're thinking

A person who says what you wanted to say for you, as in 「你就是我的嘴替」 ("you said exactly what I wanted to say"). It's often seen in replies to comments people relate to.

Example

这条评论说得太对了，简直是我的嘴替！
zhè tiáo píng lùn shuō de tài duì le， jiǎn zhí shì wǒ de zuǐ tì！
This comment is so right. It says exactly what I was thinking!
