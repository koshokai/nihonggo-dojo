Chinese phrase of the day · November 24 · Travel

一路顺风！

yí lù shùn fēng！

Bon voyage! Have a good trip!

The standard send-off for someone setting out, with the image of "a favorable wind." Some people, however, feel 「一路平安」 is better for someone taking a flight.

Example

祝你一路顺风，到了那边多保重！
zhù nǐ yí lù shùn fēng， dào le nà biān duō bǎo zhòng！
Have a good trip, and take care of yourself over there!
