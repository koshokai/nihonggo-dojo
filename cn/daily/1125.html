Chinese phrase of the day · November 25 · Feelings

天哪！

tiān nǎ！

Oh my God!

An exclamation for when you're shocked or something unbelievable happens, often strengthened to 「我的天哪！」. It works for good and bad things alike, like "Oh my God!" or "No way!" in English.

Example

天哪！我把钥匙锁在屋里了！
tiān nǎ！ wǒ bǎ yào shi suǒ zài wū lǐ le！
Oh my God! I locked my keys in the room!
