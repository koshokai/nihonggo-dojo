Chinese phrase of the day · November 26 · Food

买单！

mǎi dān！

Check, please!

For when you've finished eating and want to pay; just raise your hand and say this. 「结账」 means the same, and you'll often see friends "fighting" over who pays.

Example

服务员，买单！可以扫码吗？
fú wù yuán， mǎi dān！ kě yǐ sǎo mǎ ma？
Excuse me, check, please! Can I pay by QR code?
