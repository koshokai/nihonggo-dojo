Chinese phrase of the day · November 27 · Shopping

正品吗？

zhèng pǐn ma？

Is this genuine?

A line for making sure brand goods or cosmetics aren't fakes; 「正品」 means genuine goods. Online shops often display 「保证正品」 (authenticity guaranteed), and at markets it's fine to ask straight out.

Example

这个包这么便宜，是正品吗？
zhè ge bāo zhè me pián yi， shì zhèng pǐn ma？
This bag is so cheap. Is it genuine?
