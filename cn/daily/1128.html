Chinese phrase of the day · November 28 · Idiom

一日不见，如隔三秋

yí rì bú jiàn， rú gé sān qiū

One day apart feels like three years

Not seeing someone for just one day feels like being apart for three years, expressing longing for a partner or someone close. It's also fun to say jokingly to a friend you haven't seen in a while.

Example

才分开一天就想你了，一日不见，如隔三秋啊。
cái fēn kāi yì tiān jiù xiǎng nǐ le， yí rì bú jiàn， rú gé sān qiū a。
We've only been apart a day and I miss you already. One day apart feels like three years.
