Chinese phrase of the day · November 29 · Work

喂，你好。

wèi， nǐ hǎo。

Hello? (on the phone)

The basic greeting when answering the phone. 「喂」 is the phone "hello" and is often pronounced in the second tone, 「wéi」; in business, you identify yourself with 「喂，您好，这里是〜」 ("Hello, this is ...").

Example

喂，你好，请问是王经理吗？
wèi， nǐ hǎo， qǐng wèn shì wáng jīng lǐ ma？
Hello, is this Manager Wang?
