Chinese phrase of the day · November 30 · Feelings

真的假的？

zhēn de jiǎ de？

For real?

The standard reaction to surprising news, literally "is it true or false?" It's casual and for friends, so with superiors 「真的吗？」 ("really?") is safer.

Example

你下个月要结婚了？真的假的？
nǐ xià ge yuè yào jié hūn le？ zhēn de jiǎ de？
You're getting married next month? For real?
