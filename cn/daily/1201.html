Chinese phrase of the day · December 1 · Friends

约饭

yuē fàn

to make plans to eat together

Making a date to eat with friends, or inviting someone to eat, as in 「约个饭吧」 or 「周末约饭吗？」. It's a casual expression used a lot in young people's chats, like "let's grab food" in English.

Example

好久没见了，这周末约饭吗？
hǎo jiǔ méi jiàn le， zhè zhōu mò yuē fàn ma？
We haven't seen each other for ages. Want to grab a meal this weekend?
