Chinese phrase of the day · December 2 · Internet slang

内卷

nèi juǎn

rat race, cutthroat competition

A state where everyone competes harder than necessary until they're all worn out, often used about school and work. People also say 「别卷了」 ("stop competing so hard") jokingly.

Example

大家都在报补习班，太内卷了。
dà jiā dōu zài bào bǔ xí bān， tài nèi juǎn le。
Everyone's signing up for cram classes. The competition is insane.
