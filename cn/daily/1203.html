Chinese phrase of the day · December 3 · Food

排队排了一个小时。

pái duì pái le yí ge xiǎo shí。

I waited in line for an hour.

A line for when you've queued a long time at a popular restaurant or attraction. Queuing, 「排队」, is a given at popular places in China, and some use apps to get a place in line; pair it with 「值了」 ("worth it") afterwards.

Example

这家店排队排了一个小时，不过真好吃，值了！
zhè jiā diàn pái duì pái le yí ge xiǎo shí， bú guò zhēn hǎo chī， zhí le！
I waited in line an hour for this place, but it was really delicious. Totally worth it!
