Chinese phrase of the day · December 4 · Work

这是我的名片。

zhè shì wǒ de míng piàn。

Here's my business card.

For exchanging business cards at a first meeting. Handing it over with both hands is considered polite in China too, but these days people often exchange WeChat contacts and skip the cards.

Example

您好，这是我的名片，请多关照。
nín hǎo， zhè shì wǒ de míng piàn， qǐng duō guān zhào。
Hello, here's my business card. I look forward to working with you.
