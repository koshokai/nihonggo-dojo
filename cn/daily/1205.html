Chinese phrase of the day · December 5 · Feelings

好感动！

hǎo gǎn dòng！

I'm so touched!

An honest reaction when something moves you, such as a movie, a book or a friend's thoughtfulness. 「感动」 is used much more casually than "deeply moved" in English, and people say 「好感动」 even for small kindnesses.

Example

你还记得我的生日，好感动！
nǐ hái jì de wǒ de shēng rì， hǎo gǎn dòng！
You remembered my birthday? I'm so touched!
