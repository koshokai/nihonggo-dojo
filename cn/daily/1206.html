Chinese phrase of the day · December 6 · Idiom

熟能生巧

shú néng shēng qiǎo

Practice makes perfect

Getting used to something naturally makes you better at it, perfect for language learning. Saying 「多练习，熟能生巧！」 makes for encouragement like "Keep at it, practice makes perfect!"

Example

写汉字一开始很难，熟能生巧，多写就好了。
xiě hàn zì yì kāi shǐ hěn nán， shú néng shēng qiǎo， duō xiě jiù hǎo le。
Writing Chinese characters is hard at first, but practice makes perfect. Just write a lot.
