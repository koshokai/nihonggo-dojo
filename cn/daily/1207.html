Chinese phrase of the day · December 7 · Travel

上车睡觉，下车拍照

shàng chē shuì jiào， xià chē pāi zhào

Sleep on the bus, snap photos off the bus

A familiar line poking fun at group tours: you sleep while traveling and just take photos at the sights, so you remember nothing. It's perfect for getting a laugh when sharing travel stories.

Example

跟团游就是上车睡觉，下车拍照。
gēn tuán yóu jiù shì shàng chē shuì jiào， xià chē pāi zhào。
Group tours are all about sleeping on the bus and taking photos off the bus.
