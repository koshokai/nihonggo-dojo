Chinese phrase of the day · December 8 · Friends

祝你好运！

zhù nǐ hǎo yùn！

Good luck!

Before important moments like exams, interviews or games, the equivalent of the English "Good luck!" Combining it with 「加油」 as 「加油，祝你好运！」 really gets your feelings across.

Example

明天面试吧？祝你好运！
míng tiān miàn shì ba？ zhù nǐ hǎo yùn！
Your interview's tomorrow, right? Good luck!
