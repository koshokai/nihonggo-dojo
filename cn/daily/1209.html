Chinese phrase of the day · December 9 · Work

背锅

bèi guō

to take the blame (for someone else)

Literally "to carry the wok on your back," taking the blame for someone else's mistake, the opposite of 「甩锅」 (passing the buck). It's used when grumbling at work, as in 「我又背锅了」 ("I got blamed again").

Example

明明不是我的错，最后却让我背锅。
míng míng bú shì wǒ de cuò， zuì hòu què ràng wǒ bèi guō。
It clearly wasn't my fault, but in the end I took the blame.
