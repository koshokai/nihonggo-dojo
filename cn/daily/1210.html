Chinese phrase of the day · December 10 · Greeting

早上好！

zǎo shang hǎo！

Good morning!

The morning greeting. At work people often just say 「早！」, which actually sounds more natural, and you can reply 「早！」 too; the evening 「晚上好」 sounds a bit formal.

Example

早上好！今天天气不错啊。
zǎo shang hǎo！ jīn tiān tiān qì bú cuò a。
Good morning! Nice weather today.
