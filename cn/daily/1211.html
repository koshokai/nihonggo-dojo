Chinese phrase of the day · December 11 · Greeting

我回来了！

wǒ huí lai le！

I'm home!

The greeting when you get home. Chinese has no fixed pair of phrases for "I'm home" and "welcome back," so this line is often used, and the natural response is 「回来啦！」; some families don't say it every time.

Example

妈，我回来了！今天晚饭吃什么？
mā， wǒ huí lai le！ jīn tiān wǎn fàn chī shén me？
Mom, I'm home! What's for dinner tonight?
