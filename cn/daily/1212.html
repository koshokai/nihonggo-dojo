Chinese phrase of the day · December 12 · Internet slang

吃瓜

chī guā

to watch the drama (as a bystander)

Watching a hot topic from the sidelines and enjoying it, literally "eating melon"; 「吃瓜群众」 (the melon-eating crowd) is common too. It's a self-mocking way to describe yourself watching gossip or a scandal unfold.

Example

这事跟我没关系，我就在旁边吃瓜。
zhè shì gēn wǒ méi guān xi， wǒ jiù zài páng biān chī guā。
This has nothing to do with me. I'm just watching from the sidelines with my popcorn.
