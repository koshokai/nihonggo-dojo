Chinese phrase of the day · December 13 · Food

加个菜吧。

jiā ge cài ba。

Let's order one more dish.

A suggestion when there isn't enough food or you want a bit more. Eating out in China usually means sharing big plates, so ordering more to match the number of people is normal, and it's often used when treating guests.

Example

人有点多，菜不够，我们再加个菜吧。
rén yǒu diǎn duō， cài bú gòu， wǒ men zài jiā ge cài ba。
There are a lot of us and not enough food. Let's order another dish.
