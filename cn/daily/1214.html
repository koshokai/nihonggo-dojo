Chinese phrase of the day · December 14 · Feelings

我太难了。

wǒ tài nán le。

Life is so hard for me.

From a viral catchphrase, a joking complaint when troubles pile up. It's not serious, more a grumble with a laugh in it, and suits chats between friends.

Example

工作一大堆，孩子又生病了，我太难了。
gōng zuò yí dà duī， hái zi yòu shēng bìng le， wǒ tài nán le。
Work is piling up and now my kid is sick too. Life is so hard.
