Chinese phrase of the day · December 15 · Shopping

退款到账了。

tuì kuǎn dào zhàng le。

The refund has come through.

Saying the money has come back after returning something to an online shop; 「到账」 means arriving in your account. People also often ask 「钱到账了吗？」 to confirm a transfer, an expression typical of China, where mobile payment dominates.

Example

衣服退了，退款已经到账了。
yī fu tuì le， tuì kuǎn yǐ jīng dào zhàng le。
I returned the clothes, and the refund has already come through.
