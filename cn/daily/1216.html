Chinese phrase of the day · December 16 · Work

年终总结

nián zhōng zǒng jié

year-end review

The annual end-of-year report or meeting summing up a year's work results, a December headache for office workers. These days personal posts looking back on the year are also called 「年终总结」, often seen on social media.

Example

又到年底了，我还没开始写年终总结。
yòu dào nián dǐ le， wǒ hái méi kāi shǐ xiě nián zhōng zǒng jié。
It's the end of the year again, and I haven't even started my year-end review.
