Chinese phrase of the day · December 17 · Friends

别客气！

bié kè qi！

Don't be shy! / Make yourself at home!

For when a friend is thanking you too much or holding back. In China, too much thanking between close friends can feel distant; you can also say 「咱俩谁跟谁啊」 ("come on, it's us").

Example

别客气，想吃什么自己夹，就当在自己家！
bié kè qi， xiǎng chī shén me zì jǐ jiā， jiù dàng zài zì jǐ jiā！
Don't be shy, help yourself to whatever you want. Make yourself at home!
