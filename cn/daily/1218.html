Chinese phrase of the day · December 18 · Internet slang

电子榨菜

diàn zǐ zhà cài

a show to watch while eating

Videos or dramas you watch while eating. Internet slang meaning it's as essential to a meal as 「榨菜」, the pickled mustard greens eaten with rice; 「下饭剧」 means almost the same and is often discussed as a way to enjoy eating alone.

Example

这部综艺是我的电子榨菜，每次吃饭都看。
zhè bù zōng yì shì wǒ de diàn zǐ zhà cài， měi cì chī fàn dōu kàn。
This variety show is my mealtime viewing. I watch it every time I eat.
