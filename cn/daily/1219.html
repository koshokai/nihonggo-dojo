Chinese phrase of the day · December 19 · Shopping

就要这个了。

jiù yào zhè ge le。

I'll take this one.

A line for when you've finally decided; 「了」 adds a feeling of "decided!" It's also handy when settling on your order in a restaurant.

Example

这两件我都喜欢，算了，就要这个了！
zhè liǎng jiàn wǒ dōu xǐ huan， suàn le， jiù yào zhè ge le！
I like both of these, but... okay, I'll take this one!
