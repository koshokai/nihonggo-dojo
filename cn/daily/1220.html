Chinese phrase of the day · December 20 · Season

考研加油！

kǎo yán jiā yóu！

Good luck on your grad school exams!

Encouragement for candidates taking 「考研」, the national graduate school entrance exam held in late December. It's an important exam many young people take every year, so it's a perfect pep talk for those studying in libraries toward year-end.

Example

还有一周就考试了，考研加油，你一定能上岸！
hái yǒu yì zhōu jiù kǎo shì le， kǎo yán jiā yóu， nǐ yí dìng néng shàng àn！
One more week until the exam. Good luck on your grad school exam, I'm sure you'll make it!
