Chinese phrase of the day · December 21 · Season

冬至吃饺子了吗？

dōng zhì chī jiǎo zi le ma？

Did you eat dumplings for the winter solstice?

In the north people eat dumplings on the winter solstice, and there's a saying that if you don't, your ears will freeze. In many parts of the south people eat 「汤圆」 (sweet rice balls) instead, which makes the regional differences a fun topic.

Example

今天冬至，你吃饺子了吗？
jīn tiān dōng zhì， nǐ chī jiǎo zi le ma？
It's the winter solstice today. Did you eat dumplings?
