Chinese phrase of the day · December 22 · Feelings

早知道就好了。

zǎo zhī dao jiù hǎo le。

If only I'd known.

A line for regretting something you found out too late, often in the pattern 「早知道〜就〜了」 ("if I'd known ..., I would have ..."). It's an everyday expression, close to "If only I'd known!" or "You should've told me!" in English.

Example

早知道今天这么冷就好了，我穿太少了。
zǎo zhī dao jīn tiān zhè me lěng jiù hǎo le， wǒ chuān tài shǎo le。
If only I'd known it would be this cold today. I'm way underdressed.
