Chinese phrase of the day · December 23 · Travel

我的护照丢了。

wǒ de hù zhào diū le。

I've lost my passport.

For a serious emergency while traveling. First go to the police to file a report, 「报案」, and get a certificate, then go through the procedures at your embassy or consulate; 「丢了」 means "lost" and works for wallets and phones too.

Example

警察同志，我的护照丢了，该怎么办？
jǐng chá tóng zhì， wǒ de hù zhào diū le， gāi zěn me bàn？
Officer, I've lost my passport. What should I do?
