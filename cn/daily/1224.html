Chinese phrase of the day · December 24 · Season

平安夜快乐！

píng ān yè kuài lè！

Merry Christmas Eve!

A greeting for Christmas Eve, December 24. In China there's a custom of giving a 「平安果」 (an apple) on Christmas Eve, because 「平」 and 「苹」 are pronounced the same, and beautifully wrapped apples line shop shelves.

Example

平安夜快乐！送你一个苹果，平平安安！
píng ān yè kuài lè！ sòng nǐ yí ge píng guǒ， píng píng ān ān！
Merry Christmas Eve! Here's an apple for you, stay safe and well!
