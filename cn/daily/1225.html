Chinese phrase of the day · December 25 · Season

圣诞快乐！

shèng dàn kuài lè！

Merry Christmas!

In Chinese cities, too, Christmas is popular as an event. There's a custom of giving apples on Christmas Eve, apparently because 「平安夜」 (Christmas Eve) and 「苹果」 sound alike.

Example

圣诞快乐！今晚一起去看圣诞树吧！
shèng dàn kuài lè！ jīn wǎn yì qǐ qù kàn shèng dàn shù ba！
Merry Christmas! Let's go see the Christmas tree together tonight!
