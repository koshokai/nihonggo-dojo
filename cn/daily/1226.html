Chinese phrase of the day · December 26 · Travel

请问，地铁站怎么走？

qǐng wèn， dì tiě zhàn zěn me zǒu？

Excuse me, how do I get to the subway station?

The basic form for asking directions; adding 「请问」 makes it polite. Swap in any destination before 「〜怎么走」 and you can ask your way anywhere, and using a map app alongside is reassuring.

Example

请问，地铁站怎么走？是往前走吗？
qǐng wèn， dì tiě zhàn zěn me zǒu？ shì wǎng qián zǒu ma？
Excuse me, how do I get to the subway station? Do I go straight ahead?
