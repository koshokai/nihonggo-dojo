Chinese phrase of the day · December 27 · Friends

有你真好。

yǒu nǐ zhēn hǎo。

I'm so glad I have you.

Words of gratitude for someone who has helped you or is always there for you, fine for friends, family or a partner. It's a little embarrassing to say, but it's a warm expression perfect for thanking someone for the year at year's end.

Example

这一年谢谢你一直陪着我，有你真好。
zhè yì nián xiè xie nǐ yì zhí péi zhe wǒ， yǒu nǐ zhēn hǎo。
Thank you for being by my side all year. I'm so glad I have you.
