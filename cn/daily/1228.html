Chinese phrase of the day · December 28 · Idiom

笑一笑，十年少

xiào yi xiào， shí nián shào

Smile and you'll look ten years younger

A folk saying that smiling makes you younger, so keep smiling. It's a positive saying, close to "laughter is the best medicine," and saying it jokingly to someone who's down or frowning lightens the mood.

Example

别愁眉苦脸的，笑一笑，十年少嘛！
bié chóu méi kǔ liǎn de， xiào yi xiào， shí nián shào ma！
Don't look so glum. Smile, and you'll look ten years younger!
