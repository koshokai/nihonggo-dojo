Chinese phrase of the day · December 29 · Season

辞旧迎新

cí jiù yíng xīn

to see out the old and ring in the new

A four-character phrase for year-end and New Year greetings and cards, perfect for a speech at a company year-end party or a card. It's a bit literary, but you'll often hear it on TV New Year's Eve shows.

Example

辞旧迎新之际，祝大家新年快乐！
cí jiù yíng xīn zhī jì， zhù dà jiā xīn nián kuài lè！
As we see out the old year and ring in the new, Happy New Year, everyone!
