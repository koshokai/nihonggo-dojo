Chinese phrase of the day · December 30 · Season

一起跨年吧！

yì qǐ kuà nián ba！

Let's ring in the New Year together!

An invitation to spend New Year's Eve together and do the countdown; 「跨年」 means crossing into the new year. Young people often go out to events and countdowns with friends, and 「跨年夜」 is New Year's Eve.

Example

今年的最后一天，我们一起跨年吧！
jīn nián de zuì hòu yì tiān， wǒ men yì qǐ kuà nián ba！
It's the last day of the year. Let's ring in the New Year together!
