Chinese phrase of the day · December 31 · Season

明年见！

míng nián jiàn！

See you next year!

A jokey goodbye when parting on New Year's Eve; the fun is saying "see you next year" when you'll actually meet the next day. You can use it in place of "Happy New Year" as a parting greeting. Here's to the new year!

Example

今年就聊到这儿吧，明年见！
jīn nián jiù liáo dào zhèr ba， míng nián jiàn！
That's enough chatting for this year. See you next year!
