「是」: A is B

HSK 1

A + 是 + B ／ A + 不是 + B

A verb that links two nouns: "A is B." To negate it, put 「不」 in front: 「不是」. Chinese verbs don't change form depending on the subject, so 「我是」, 「他是」 and 「他们是」 all use the same form. However, 「是」 is not used in sentences with an adjective, such as "I'm busy" (see the entry on 「很」).

×「我是很忙。」→ No 「是」 is needed before an adjective; 「我很忙。」 is correct. Think of 「是」 as an "=" sign that links a noun to a noun.

Example sentences

我是日本人。
wǒ shì rì běn rén。
I'm Japanese.
她是我的老师。
tā shì wǒ de lǎo shī。
She's my teacher.
他不是学生。
tā bú shì xué sheng。
He isn't a student.
这是什么？
zhè shì shén me？
What is this?
