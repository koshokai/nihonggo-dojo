Adjective predicates and 「很」

HSK 1

主語 + 很 + 形容詞 ／ 主語 + 不 + 形容詞

To say "... is (adjective)," put the adjective directly after the subject. However, a lone adjective in an affirmative sentence sounds contrastive ("... is [adjective], compared with something else"), so you normally add 「很」. Unless it is stressed, this 「很」 carries almost none of its meaning "very." The negative is 「不」 + adjective.

「很」 is just a light filler, so don't overtranslate it as "very." Questions like 「汉语难吗？」 and negatives like 「不难」 don't need 「很」.

Example sentences

今天很热。
jīn tiān hěn rè。
It's hot today.
我很好，谢谢。
wǒ hěn hǎo， xiè xie。
I'm fine, thanks.
这个菜不贵。
zhè ge cài bú guì。
This dish isn't expensive.
汉语难吗？
hàn yǔ nán ma？
Is Chinese difficult?
