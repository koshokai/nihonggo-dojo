「有」: to have; there is/are

HSK 1

主語 + 有 + 名詞 ／ 場所 + 有 + 人・物

「有」 has two meanings: "(someone) has" and "there is/are ... (somewhere)." The second uses the word order place + 「有」 + person/thing, with the place first, as in "On the desk (there) is a book." The negative is 「没有」; you never say 「不有」.

×「我不有车。」→ 「有」 is always negated with 「没有」. In conversation, 「没」 alone is also fine: 「我没车。」

Example sentences

我有一个妹妹。
wǒ yǒu yí ge mèi mei。
I have a younger sister.
桌子上有一本书。
zhuō zi shàng yǒu yì běn shū。
There's a book on the desk.
你有时间吗？
nǐ yǒu shí jiān ma？
Do you have time?
我没有钱。
wǒ méi yǒu qián。
I don't have any money.
