「在」: to be (located) somewhere

HSK 1

人・物（特定）+ 在 + 場所

To say where a specific person or thing is, use person/thing + 「在」 + place. The difference from 「有」 lies in the subject: 「在」 gives the location of something specific that both speakers know about (「我的手机」, 「妈妈」), while 「有」 states the existence of something unspecified (「一本书」). The negative is 「不在」.

Specific things take 「在」, unspecified things take 「有」. "My book is on the desk" is 「我的书在桌子上。」, which is more natural than 「桌子上有我的书」.

Example sentences

我的手机在桌子上。
wǒ de shǒu jī zài zhuō zi shàng。
My phone is on the desk.
妈妈在家。
mā ma zài jiā。
Mom is at home.
洗手间在哪儿？
xǐ shǒu jiān zài nǎr？
Where's the restroom?
老师不在办公室。
lǎo shī bú zài bàn gōng shì。
The teacher isn't in the office.
