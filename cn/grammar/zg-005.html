「的」: possession and modification ('s, of)

HSK 1

名詞／代名詞／形容詞／動詞句 + 的 + 名詞

「的」 is similar to English "'s" or "of": the word before it describes the noun after it. Not only nouns but also verb phrases can be linked with 「的」 (「我买的书」 = the book I bought). However, for family members and groups you belong to, as in 「我妈妈」 and 「我们学校」, 「的」 is usually left out.

You don't need 「的」 every time English uses "'s" or "of." Closely bound terms like 「中国人」 and 「汉语书」, and family or affiliation (「我爸爸」), sound natural without 「的」.

Example sentences

这是我的书。
zhè shì wǒ de shū。
This is my book.
我妈妈是医生。
wǒ mā ma shì yī shēng。
My mom is a doctor.
我买的苹果很好吃。
wǒ mǎi de píng guǒ hěn hǎo chī。
The apples I bought are delicious.
他是我们班的学生。
tā shì wǒ men bān de xué sheng。
He's a student in our class.
