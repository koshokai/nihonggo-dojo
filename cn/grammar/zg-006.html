「吗」: yes-no questions

HSK 1

平叙文 + 吗？ ／ 動詞 + 不 + 動詞？

Just add 「吗」 to the end of a sentence to make a question that is answered with "yes" or "no." The word order stays the same. The basic way to answer is to repeat the verb or adjective from the question (「你忙吗？」—「忙。」/「不忙。」). Affirmative-negative questions, which put the positive and negative forms side by side as in 「你去不去？」, are also common.

×「你去不去吗？」→ Affirmative-negative questions don't take 「吗」. Sentences with a question word (「你去哪儿？」) don't need 「吗」 either.

Example sentences

你是中国人吗？
nǐ shì zhōng guó rén ma？
Are you Chinese?
你喝咖啡吗？―喝。
nǐ hē kā fēi ma？― hē。
Do you drink coffee? — Yes, I do.
明天你来不来？
míng tiān nǐ lái bu lái？
Are you coming tomorrow?
这个好吃吗？
zhè ge hǎo chī ma？
Is this good?
