Question-word questions (「什么」, 「谁」, 「哪儿」, 「几」...)

HSK 1

尋ねたい部分に疑問詞を置く（語順はそのまま）

Put the question word exactly where the answer would go. As in 「你去北京」 → 「你去哪儿？」, there is no need to change the word order. The main question words are 「什么」 (what), 「谁」 (who), 「哪儿」 (where), 「什么时候」 (when), 「几」 (how many, when you expect fewer than 10), 「多少」 (how much, how many), 「怎么」 (how) and 「为什么」 (why).

「几」 must be followed by a measure word (「几个人？」). When you expect a large number, or when asking about prices, use 「多少」. Question-word sentences don't take 「吗」.

Example sentences

你叫什么名字？
nǐ jiào shén me míng zi？
What's your name?
他是谁？
tā shì shéi？
Who is he?
你女儿几岁了？
nǐ nǚ ér jǐ suì le？
How old is your daughter?
这个多少钱？
zhè ge duō shao qián？
How much is this?
