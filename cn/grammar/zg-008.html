「呢」: "What about ...?" (elliptical questions)

HSK 1

名詞 + 呢？

Noun + 「呢？」 asks "What about ...? / Where is ...?", picking up on the previous topic. The classic example is 「我很好，你呢？」 (I'm fine, and you?). Said out of the blue, 「我的手机呢？」 means "Where's my phone?" Added to the end of a question-word question, 「呢」 softens the tone.

「呢」 can't replace 「吗」. ×「你是学生呢？」→ Yes-no questions take 「吗」. 「呢」 is for "What about ...?" when carrying on a topic.

Example sentences

我是日本人，你呢？
wǒ shì rì běn rén， nǐ ne？
I'm Japanese. What about you?
我的钥匙呢？
wǒ de yào shi ne？
Where are my keys?
你在想什么呢？
nǐ zài xiǎng shén me ne？
What are you thinking about?
我喝茶，你呢？
wǒ hē chá， nǐ ne？
I'll have tea. What about you?
