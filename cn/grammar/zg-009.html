「不」 vs. 「没（有）」

HSK 1

不 + 動詞・形容詞 ／ 没（有）+ 動詞

「不」 negates will, habits and qualities: "don't, won't, isn't." 「没（有）」 means "didn't" or "haven't ... yet," negating something that has not actually happened. Even in the past, habits take 「不」 (「以前不抽烟」 = (I) didn't smoke before). 「是」 is always negated as 「不是」, and 「有」 as 「没有」.

A common mistake is using 「不」 for "didn't." ×「昨天我不去。」→ 「昨天我没去。」 Also, sentences with 「没」 don't take 「了」 (×「我没去了」).

Example sentences

我不喝酒。
wǒ bù hē jiǔ。
I don't drink.
我昨天没去学校。
wǒ zuó tiān méi qù xué xiào。
I didn't go to school yesterday.
他还没来。
tā hái méi lái。
He hasn't come yet.
明天我不去。
míng tiān wǒ bú qù。
I'm not going tomorrow.
