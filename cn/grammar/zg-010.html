Measure words and 「这」, 「那」, 「哪」

HSK 1

（这／那／哪）+ 数詞 + 量詞 + 名詞

When giving a number, you must always put a measure word between the number and the noun (「一本书」, 「两个人」). "This" and "that" also need a measure word: 「这个人」, 「那本书」. Common measure words are 「个」 (people and things in general), 「本」 (books), 「张」 (flat things such as paper and tables), 「杯」 (cups and glasses) and 「口」 (number of people in a family). For "two," use 「两」, not 「二」.

×「两书」→ 「两本书」. Don't forget the measure word. "Two" is 「两个」; 「二个」 is wrong. If in doubt, the all-purpose 「个」 will still be understood.

Example sentences

我有两个哥哥。
wǒ yǒu liǎng ge gē ge。
I have two older brothers.
这本书很有意思。
zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yì si。
This book is interesting.
我要一杯咖啡。
wǒ yào yì bēi kā fēi。
I'd like a cup of coffee.
你家有几口人？
nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén？
How many people are there in your family?
