Time and place come before the verb

HSK 1

主語 + 時間 + 在+場所 + 動詞 + 目的語

"When" and "where" go before the verb. Time words (「今天」, 「晚上」, 「八点」) can come before or after the subject, but never after the verb. The basic word order is subject + time + place + verb + object. English usually puts time and place at the end of the sentence, but Chinese puts them before the verb; only the object follows the verb, as in English.

Don't follow English word order and say ×「我吃饭七点。」. Remember the order time → place → action: time and place go before the verb, and only the object comes after it.

Example sentences

我明天去北京。
wǒ míng tiān qù běi jīng。
I'm going to Beijing tomorrow.
他每天七点起床。
tā měi tiān qī diǎn qǐ chuáng。
He gets up at 7 every day.
今天晚上我们在家吃饭。
jīn tiān wǎn shang wǒ men zài jiā chī fàn。
We're eating at home tonight.
你几点下课？
nǐ jǐ diǎn xià kè？
What time does your class end?
