「在」 + place + verb (doing something somewhere)

HSK 1

主語 + 在 + 場所 + 動詞（+目的語）

Placed before a verb, 「在」 also works as a preposition marking where something happens ("at, in"). 「在家看电视」 means "watch TV at home." Unlike English, where "at home" comes after the verb, the 「在」 phrase goes before the verb. However, with verbs like 「住」, 「坐」 and 「放」, 「在」 + place comes after the verb, as in 「住在北京」, meaning "(live) in ..."

×「我学习在图书馆。」→ The place of an action goes before the verb. It comes after the verb only when it shows where someone or something ends up as a result of the action, as in 「住在」, 「坐在」 and 「放在」.

Example sentences

我在图书馆学习。
wǒ zài tú shū guǎn xué xí。
I study at the library.
他在医院工作。
tā zài yī yuàn gōng zuò。
He works at a hospital.
我住在东京。
wǒ zhù zài dōng jīng。
I live in Tokyo.
你在哪儿吃午饭？
nǐ zài nǎr chī wǔ fàn？
Where do you eat lunch?
