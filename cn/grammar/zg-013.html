「也」 and 「都」 (also, all)

HSK 1

主語 + 也／都 + 動詞・形容詞

「也」 (also) and 「都」 (all, both) are adverbs, so they always go before the verb or adjective. The trick is not to attach them to the noun, as English "me too" might tempt you to. When you use both, the order is 「也都」 (「我们也都是学生」 = We're all students too). 「都」 sums up the multiple people or things mentioned before it.

×「也我是学生。」 ×「我们是都学生。」→ The fixed position of 「也」 and 「都」 is after the subject and before the verb. Unlike English "too," they can't go at the end of the sentence either.

Example sentences

我也是日本人。
wǒ yě shì rì běn rén。
I'm Japanese too.
我们都喜欢音乐。
wǒ men dōu xǐ huan yīn yuè。
We all like music.
他们也都去。
tā men yě dōu qù。
They're all going too.
我也不知道。
wǒ yě bù zhī dao。
I don't know either.
