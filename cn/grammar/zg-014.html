「会」: can (learned skills)

HSK 1

主語 + 会 + 動詞（+目的語）

Use 「会」 to say you "can" do something you've learned through practice or study (「会说汉语」, 「会开车」, 「会游泳」). A noun can also follow it directly, as in 「会汉语」 (know Chinese). The negative is 「不会」. 「会」 also expresses likelihood, "will (probably)," as in 「明天会下雨」 (It will probably rain tomorrow).

"Can" isn't always 「会」. Skills you've learned take 「会」, while being able to under given conditions is 「能」 (see HSK2 「会」, 「能」 and 「可以」).

Example sentences

我会说一点儿汉语。
wǒ huì shuō yì diǎnr hàn yǔ。
I can speak a little Chinese.
你会游泳吗？
nǐ huì yóu yǒng ma？
Can you swim?
我不会做饭。
wǒ bú huì zuò fàn。
I can't cook.
明天会下雨。
míng tiān huì xià yǔ。
It will probably rain tomorrow.
