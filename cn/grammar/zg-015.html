「去」/「来」 + verb (serial verb sentences)

HSK 1

主語 + 去／来 + 場所 + 動詞（+目的語）

To say "go to ... and do ..." or "go (somewhere) to do ...," line up the verbs in the order the actions happen (「我去超市买东西」 = I'm going to the supermarket to buy some things). The going comes first and the purpose after it, as in English "go to the store to shop." The means of travel, however, is stated first, as in 「坐地铁去」 (go by subway, literally "ride subway go"), which follows the same logic of putting things in the order they happen.

×「我买东西去超市。」→ Chinese follows the order of events: first 「去超市」 (go to the supermarket), then 「买东西」 (buy things).

Example sentences

我去超市买东西。
wǒ qù chāo shì mǎi dōng xi。
I'm going to the supermarket to do some shopping.
他来日本学习日语。
tā lái rì běn xué xí rì yǔ。
He came to Japan to study Japanese.
我们坐地铁去吧。
wǒ men zuò dì tiě qù ba。
Let's take the subway.
周末你去哪儿玩儿？
zhōu mò nǐ qù nǎr wánr？
Where are you going for fun this weekend?
