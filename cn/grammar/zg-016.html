「了」: completed action

HSK 1

動詞 + 了 + 数量・修飾語 + 目的語

「了」 right after a verb shows that the action has been realized or completed. The object usually carries a number or modifier, as in 「两本书」 or 「很多水」; with a bare object, the sentence doesn't feel finished. 「了」 is not a past tense, so it isn't used for past habits or with 「是」. The negative is 「没」 + verb, which drops 「了」.

「了」 is not the past tense. ×「我以前常常去了。」→ Past habits don't take 「了」. The negative is likewise not ×「我没去了」 but 「我没去。」

Example sentences

我买了两本书。
wǒ mǎi le liǎng běn shū。
I bought two books.
他喝了很多水。
tā hē le hěn duō shuǐ。
He drank a lot of water.
我吃了饭就去。
wǒ chī le fàn jiù qù。
I'll go as soon as I've eaten.
我昨天没去。
wǒ zuó tiān méi qù。
I didn't go yesterday.
