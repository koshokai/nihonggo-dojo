Sentence-final 「了」: change of situation

HSK 2

文 + 了（…になった／…することにした）

「了」 at the end of a sentence shows that a new situation has come about (「下雨了」 = it's started raining, 「我饿了」 = I'm hungry now). Combined with a negative, it means "no longer ..." or "have stopped ..." (「我不喝酒了」). Using both the 「了」 right after the verb and the sentence-final 「了」 means "have been doing ... for (a period) up to now," with the action still continuing.

「我学了两年汉语」 means "I studied Chinese for two years" (whether I still do is unclear), while 「我学了两年汉语了」 means "I've been studying it for two years and am still at it." A single sentence-final 「了」 changes the meaning.

Example sentences

天冷了。
tiān lěng le。
It's getting cold.
我不吃肉了。
wǒ bù chī ròu le。
I don't eat meat anymore.
他已经是大学生了。
tā yǐ jīng shì dà xué sheng le。
He's already a college student now.
我学了两年汉语了。
wǒ xué le liǎng nián hàn yǔ le。
I've been studying Chinese for two years now.
