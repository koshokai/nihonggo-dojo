「过」: have done (experience)

HSK 2

動詞 + 过 + 目的語 ／ 没 + 動詞 + 过

「过」 right after a verb expresses experience: "have (ever) done ...." The negative is 「没（有）」 + verb + 「过」; unlike 「了」, 「过」 stays in the negative. Note the difference between 「去过中国」 (have been to China) and 「去了中国」 (went to China). The number of times goes after 「过」, as in 「去过两次」.

Don't drop 「过」 in the negative. ×「我没去。」 (= I didn't go) → "I've never been" is 「我没去过。」

Example sentences

我去过上海。
wǒ qù guo shàng hǎi。
I've been to Shanghai.
你吃过北京烤鸭吗？
nǐ chī guo běi jīng kǎo yā ma？
Have you ever had Peking duck?
我没看过这个电影。
wǒ méi kàn guo zhè ge diàn yǐng。
I've never seen this movie.
他来过日本两次。
tā lái guo rì běn liǎng cì。
He's been to Japan twice.
