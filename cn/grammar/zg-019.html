「在」/「正在…呢」: be doing (progressive)

HSK 2

主語 + （正）在 + 動詞（+目的語）+（呢）

To say "be doing ... right now," put 「在」 or 「正在」 before the verb. 「呢」 is often added at the end, and 「在…呢」, 「正在…呢」, or even 「…呢」 alone can express the progressive. The negative is 「没（在）」 + verb. Note that 「在」 marks only actions in progress; it is not used for ongoing states such as "be married."

Don't use 「在」 for states. ×「他在结婚。」→ "He is married" is 「他结婚了。」. Stative verbs such as 「知道」 don't take 「在」 either.

Example sentences

我在吃饭呢。
wǒ zài chī fàn ne。
I'm eating right now.
他正在开会。
tā zhèng zài kāi huì。
He's in a meeting.
你在干什么？
nǐ zài gàn shén me？
What are you doing?
我没在看电视，我在学习。
wǒ méi zài kàn diàn shì， wǒ zài xué xí。
I'm not watching TV, I'm studying.
