「着」: continuing state

HSK 2

動詞 + 着（+目的語）／ V1 + 着 + V2

「着」 shows that a state resulting from an action continues. As in 「门开着」 (the door is open) and 「他穿着红衣服」 (he's wearing red clothes), it describes a state that holds, rather than an action under way. 「V1着V2」 also means "do V2 while V1-ing" or "do V2 in the state of V1" (「站着吃」 = eat standing up).

「在」 marks an action in progress, 「着」 a continuing state. 「他在穿衣服」 means he is in the middle of getting dressed, while 「他穿着衣服」 means he has clothes on.

Example sentences

门开着。
mén kāi zhe。
The door is open.
她戴着一副眼镜。
tā dài zhe yí fù yǎn jìng。
She's wearing glasses.
他笑着说：“没问题。”
tā xiào zhe shuō： “méi wèn tí。”
He said with a smile, "No problem."
外面下着雨。
wài miàn xià zhe yǔ。
It's raining outside.
