「吧」: let's ..., ..., right?

HSK 2

文 + 吧

「吧」 is a modal particle added at the end of a sentence to soften the tone. It has three main uses: (1) suggestions and invitations, "let's ..." (「我们走吧」); (2) guesses and confirmation, "..., right?" (「你是日本人吧？」); (3) mild commands and requests, "go ahead and ..." (「你坐吧」). While 「吗」 is a pure question, 「吧」 is used when you already expect a certain answer.

For a simple "Is it ...?" use 「吗」; to check "It's ..., right?" use 「吧」. It helps to think of it as English "..., right?" or "I suppose ...."

Example sentences

我们一起去吧。
wǒ men yì qǐ qù ba。
Let's go together.
你是李老师吧？
nǐ shì lǐ lǎo shī ba？
You're Professor Li, right?
快点儿吃吧。
kuài diǎnr chī ba。
Hurry up and eat.
好吧，就这样吧。
hǎo ba， jiù zhè yàng ba。
OK, let's leave it at that.
