「想」, 「要」, 「打算」: want to, intend to

HSK 2

主語 + 想／要／打算 + 動詞

「想」 is a soft wish: "would like to." 「要」 means "want to, will," with stronger will, as if you've already decided. 「打算」 states a plan: "plan to." Watch the negatives: "don't want to" is 「不想」, while 「不要」 means "don't (do it)," a prohibition.

×「我不要去。」 (meant as "I don't want to go") → 「不要」 tends to sound like the prohibition "Don't go." "I don't want to go" is 「我不想去。」

Example sentences

我想喝水。
wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ。
I'd like some water.
我要去中国留学。
wǒ yào qù zhōng guó liú xué。
I'm going to study abroad in China.
你暑假打算做什么？
nǐ shǔ jià dǎ suan zuò shén me？
What are you planning to do over summer vacation?
我不想去。
wǒ bù xiǎng qù。
I don't want to go.
