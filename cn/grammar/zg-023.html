「会」, 「能」 and 「可以」

HSK 2

会（習得）／能（能力・条件）／可以（許可）+ 動詞

All three mean "can," but they're used differently. 「会」 is for skills learned through practice (「会游泳」). 「能」 is for the degree of an ability, or for something being possible because conditions allow it (「能游一千米」, 「明天能来」). 「可以」 is for permission, "may" (「这儿可以拍照吗？」). Prohibition, "must not," is expressed with 「不能」 or 「不可以」.

To ask "Can you swim?" about the skill, use 「会」; for "Can you swim today?" (health, conditions), use 「能」. ×「我会游一千米」→ For degree, such as distance or amount, use 「能」.

Example sentences

我会开车，可是今天喝酒了，不能开。
wǒ huì kāi chē， kě shì jīn tiān hē jiǔ le， bù néng kāi。
I can drive, but I've had a drink today, so I can't.
这儿可以拍照吗？
zhèr kě yǐ pāi zhào ma？
May I take pictures here?
他一分钟能打一百个字。
tā yì fēn zhōng néng dǎ yì bǎi ge zì。
He can type 100 characters a minute.
我明天有事，不能来。
wǒ míng tiān yǒu shì， bù néng lái。
I have something to do tomorrow, so I can't come.
