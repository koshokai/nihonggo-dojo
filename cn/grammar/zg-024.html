「比」: A is more ... than B

HSK 2

A + 比 + B + 形容詞（+差の量）

Comparisons use A + 「比」 + B + adjective. Unlike English, the adjective comes at the end, after B: literally "A compared-to B tall." The size of the difference goes after the adjective (「大三岁」 = three years older, 「贵一点儿」 = a little more expensive, 「好得多」 = much better). For emphasis you can put 「更」 or 「还」 before the adjective, but not 「很」 or 「非常」.

×「他比我很高。」→ 「很」 and 「非常」 are wrong in 「比」 sentences. 「他比我高得多」 and 「他比我更高」 are fine. The amount of difference goes after the adjective (×「他比我三岁大」).

Example sentences

今天比昨天热。
jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān rè。
Today is hotter than yesterday.
哥哥比我大三岁。
gē ge bǐ wǒ dà sān suì。
My older brother is three years older than me.
这个比那个便宜一点儿。
zhè ge bǐ nà ge pián yi yì diǎnr。
This one is a little cheaper than that one.
坐地铁比坐出租车快得多。
zuò dì tiě bǐ zuò chū zū chē kuài de duō。
Taking the subway is much faster than taking a taxi.
