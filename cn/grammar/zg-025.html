「跟…一样」: the same as ...

HSK 2

A + 跟 + B + 一样（+形容詞）

"A is the same as B" or "A is as ... as B" is A + 「跟」 + B + 「一样」 (+ adjective). 「跟」 can be replaced with 「和」. The negative is 「不一样」: 「A跟B不一样」 (A and B are different). Adding an adjective after 「一样」 gives "just as ...": 「我跟他一样高」 (I'm as tall as he is).

「不一样」 means "different." To say "A is not as ... as B," use 「没有」 (see HSK3 「没有…那么」).

Example sentences

我的手机跟你的一样。
wǒ de shǒu jī gēn nǐ de yí yàng。
My phone is the same as yours.
这儿的天气跟东京一样热。
zhèr de tiān qì gēn dōng jīng yí yàng rè。
The weather here is as hot as in Tokyo.
中国菜跟日本菜不一样。
zhōng guó cài gēn rì běn cài bù yí yàng。
Chinese food is different from Japanese food.
他跟我一样喜欢运动。
tā gēn wǒ yí yàng xǐ huan yùn dòng。
He likes sports as much as I do.
