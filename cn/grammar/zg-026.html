「是…的」: highlighting details of a past event

HSK 2

主語 + 是 + 時間／場所／方法など + 動詞 + 的

Used to single out and explain details such as when, where, how or with whom about something that has already happened. In these situations the fact itself (for example, that someone came) is already known, and the focus is on the detail, such as how they came. 「是」 can be omitted, but 「的」 cannot. The object sometimes goes after 「的」 (「我是昨天买的票」).

To ask "When?" or "Where?" about something already done, use 「是…的」. ×「你什么时候来了？」→ 「你（是）什么时候来的？」

Example sentences

你是什么时候来的？
nǐ shì shén me shí hou lái de？
When did you get here?
我是坐飞机来的。
wǒ shì zuò fēi jī lái de。
I came by plane.
这本书是在哪儿买的？
zhè běn shū shì zài nǎr mǎi de？
Where did you buy this book?
我是跟朋友一起去的。
wǒ shì gēn péng you yì qǐ qù de。
I went with a friend.
