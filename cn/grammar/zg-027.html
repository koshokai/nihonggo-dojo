Degree complement 「得」: how well an action is done

HSK 2

動詞 + 得 + 形容詞 ／（動詞）+ 目的語 + 動詞 + 得 + 形容詞

To evaluate how, or how well, an action is done, as in "speak well" or "get up early," use verb + 「得」 + adjective. When there is an object, either repeat the verb or move the object to the front (「他说汉语说得很好」 / 「他汉语说得很好」). To negate it, negate what comes after 「得」: 「说得不好」.

×「他说汉语得很好。」→ 「得」 can't come right after the object. Repeat the verb: 「（说）汉语说得很好」. Likewise for the negative: ×「他不说得好」→ 「他说得不好」.

Example sentences

他跑得很快。
tā pǎo de hěn kuài。
He runs fast.
你汉语说得真好！
nǐ hàn yǔ shuō de zhēn hǎo！
Your Chinese is really good!
我今天起得很早。
wǒ jīn tiān qǐ de hěn zǎo。
I got up early today.
她唱歌唱得不太好。
tā chàng gē chàng de bú tài hǎo。
She doesn't sing very well.
