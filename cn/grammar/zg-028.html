「因为…所以…」: because ..., so ...

HSK 2

因为 + 原因，所以 + 結果

The basic pairing for stating a cause or reason. You can also use just one of 「因为」 and 「所以」. The cause normally comes first, but you can also add the reason afterward with 「…，因为…」, much like English "..., because ..."; in that case 「所以」 is not used.

Just as English can say "..., because ...," Chinese can put the reason second with 「…，因为…」. In that case, however, 「所以」 is not added.

Example sentences

因为下雨，所以我没去。
yīn wèi xià yǔ， suǒ yǐ wǒ méi qù。
It was raining, so I didn't go.
因为他病了，所以今天没来上课。
yīn wèi tā bìng le， suǒ yǐ jīn tiān méi lái shàng kè。
He's sick, so he didn't come to class today.
我很累，因为昨天睡得太晚了。
wǒ hěn lèi， yīn wèi zuó tiān shuì de tài wǎn le。
I'm tired because I went to bed too late last night.
路上堵车，所以我迟到了。
lù shàng dǔ chē， suǒ yǐ wǒ chí dào le。
There was a traffic jam, so I was late.
