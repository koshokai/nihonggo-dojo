「虽然…但是…」: although ..., (but) ...

HSK 2

虽然 + A，但是／可是 + B

A contrast: "A, but B." English uses either "although" or "but," not both, but Chinese normally uses 「虽然」 and 「但是」 (or 「可是」) together as a set. 「虽然」 can be omitted, but it's more natural to keep 「但是」 in the second half. 「虽然」 can go before or after the subject.

Using 「虽然」 in the first half with nothing in the second half is unnatural. ×「虽然很累，我去。」→ 「虽然很累，但是我还是去。」 (「还是」 = still, even so)

Example sentences

虽然很贵，但是很好吃。
suī rán hěn guì， dàn shì hěn hǎo chī。
It's expensive, but it's delicious.
他虽然是美国人，但是汉语说得很好。
tā suī rán shì měi guó rén， dàn shì hàn yǔ shuō de hěn hǎo。
Although he's American, he speaks Chinese very well.
虽然下雨了，可是比赛没有停。
suī rán xià yǔ le， kě shì bǐ sài méi yǒu tíng。
Although it rained, the game didn't stop.
我很想去，但是没有时间。
wǒ hěn xiǎng qù， dàn shì méi yǒu shí jiān。
I really want to go, but I don't have time.
