「快…了」/「要…了」: about to ...

HSK 2

快／快要／就要／要 + 動詞・名詞 + 了

"Soon ..., about to ..." is expressed with 「要…了」, 「快要…了」, 「快…了」 or 「就要…了」; the final 「了」 is required. 「快」 can also be followed by a noun (「快十二点了」). When a specific time (「明天」, 「下个月」) comes before it, use 「就要…了」; 「快要」 can't be used.

×「明天快要考试了。」→ With a specific time, say 「明天就要考试了。」. Also, don't drop the final 「了」.

Example sentences

火车快要开了。
huǒ chē kuài yào kāi le。
The train is about to leave.
快十二点了，睡觉吧。
kuài shí èr diǎn le， shuì jiào ba。
It's almost twelve. Let's go to bed.
他下个月就要结婚了。
tā xià ge yuè jiù yào jié hūn le。
He's getting married next month.
天要下雨了。
tiān yào xià yǔ le。
It's about to rain.
