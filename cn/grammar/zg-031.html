「就」 and 「才」: early and late

HSK 2

時間・条件 + 就（もう）／才（やっと）+ 動詞

For the same point in time, the speaker uses 「就」 if it feels early and 「才」 if it feels late. 「他八点就来了」 means "He came as early as 8," while 「他八点才来」 means "He didn't come until 8." The key point is that 「就」 sentences tend to take 「了」, while 「才」 sentences don't.

×「他十点才来了。」→ 「才」 sentences don't take 「了」. Learn 「就…了」 and 「才…」 (no 「了」) as a pair.

Example sentences

我六点就起床了。
wǒ liù diǎn jiù qǐ chuáng le。
I was already up at six.
他十点才起床。
tā shí diǎn cái qǐ chuáng。
He didn't get up until ten.
我说了三遍他才懂。
wǒ shuō le sān biàn tā cái dǒng。
He didn't understand until I'd said it three times.
她十八岁就大学毕业了。
tā shí bā suì jiù dà xué bì yè le。
She graduated from college at just 18.
