「还是」 and 「或者」: or

HSK 2

A + 还是 + B？（疑問）／ A + 或者 + B（平叙）

Both mean "A or B," but in questions asking the listener to choose, use 「还是」, and in statements meaning "either A or B," use 「或者」. Choice questions with 「还是」 don't take 「吗」.

English "or" covers both uses, so the two are easy to confuse. ×「你喝茶或者咖啡？」→ Questions asking for a choice use 「还是」. 「还是…吗？」 is also wrong.

Example sentences

你喝茶还是喝咖啡？
nǐ hē chá hái shì hē kā fēi？
Would you like tea or coffee?
你今天去还是明天去？
nǐ jīn tiān qù hái shì míng tiān qù？
Are you going today or tomorrow?
我周末看书或者看电影。
wǒ zhōu mò kàn shū huò zhě kàn diàn yǐng。
On weekends I read or watch movies.
你可以坐地铁或者坐公共汽车去。
nǐ kě yǐ zuò dì tiě huò zhě zuò gōng gòng qì chē qù。
You can go by subway or by bus.
