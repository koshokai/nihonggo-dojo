Reduplicated verbs (do ... a bit, give ... a try)

HSK 2

動詞 + 動詞 ／ 動詞 + 一 + 動詞 ／ AB + AB

Doubling a verb gives a light feel: "do ... a bit, give ... a try." One-syllable verbs become 「看看」 or 「看一看」, and two-syllable verbs 「休息休息」, 「介绍介绍」 (ABAB). For something already done, insert 「了」: 「看了看」. Because it softens requests, it is common in conversation.

Two-syllable verbs don't become ×「休休息息」 (that's how adjectives reduplicate) → 「休息休息」. Verb + 「一下」 (「看一下」) also means "a bit" and is handy.

Example sentences

你看看这个。
nǐ kàn kan zhè ge。
Take a look at this.
我们休息休息吧。
wǒ men xiū xi xiū xi ba。
Let's take a little break.
请你介绍一下自己。
qǐng nǐ jiè shào yí xià zì jǐ。
Please introduce yourself.
他想了想，说：“好吧。”
tā xiǎng le xiǎng， shuō： “hǎo ba。”
He thought for a moment and said, "OK."
