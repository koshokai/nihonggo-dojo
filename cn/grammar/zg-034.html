Duration complements (how long an action lasts)

HSK 2

動詞 + (了) + 時間の長さ + (的) + 目的語

The length of time an action lasts, as in "study for two hours," goes after the verb (「学习两个小时」). This matches English, but be careful not to put it before the verb the way time points are. When there is an object, either put the duration before the object, as in 「看了一个小时（的）电视」, or repeat the verb, as in 「看电视看了一个小时」.

×「我两个小时学习。」→ Durations go after the verb. Distinguish time points (「八点」), which go before the verb, from durations (「八个小时」), which go after it.

Example sentences

我每天睡七个小时。
wǒ měi tiān shuì qī ge xiǎo shí。
I sleep seven hours a night.
我学了三年汉语。
wǒ xué le sān nián hàn yǔ。
I studied Chinese for three years.
他看电视看了两个小时。
tā kàn diàn shì kàn le liǎng ge xiǎo shí。
He watched TV for two hours.
我等了你半天。
wǒ děng le nǐ bàn tiān。
I waited for you for ages.
