Resultative complements (verb + result)

HSK 3

動詞 + 結果補語（完／好／到／懂／见／错…）

A characteristic of Chinese is that it states an action and its result separately. 「看」 (look) alone doesn't tell you whether you actually saw something, so the result is added after it: 「看见」 (see), 「看懂」 (look and understand), 「看完」 (finish reading or watching). The negative is 「没」 + verb + resultative complement, without 「了」.

「找」 means "look for," and 「找到」 means "find." "I looked but couldn't find it" is 「找了，可是没找到」. Stating the result as well is the Chinese way.

Example sentences

我听懂了。
wǒ tīng dǒng le。
I understood what I heard.
