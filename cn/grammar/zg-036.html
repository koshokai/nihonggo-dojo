Directional complements (simple: 「来」, 「去」)

HSK 3

動詞 + 来／去 ／ 動詞 + 場所 + 来／去

「来」 shows movement toward the speaker and 「去」 movement away from the speaker; they attach after the verb (「拿来」 = bring, 「拿去」 = take away). A place object always goes before 「来」/「去」 (「回家去」, 「进教室来」). An object that is a thing can go either before or after them (「带来一本书」 / 「带一本书来」).

×「他回去家了。」→ The place goes before 「来」/「去」: 「他回家去了」. The order is verb + place + 「来」/「去」.

Example sentences

他回家去了。
tā huí jiā qù le。
He went back home.
