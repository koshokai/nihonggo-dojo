Potential complements (can, can't)

HSK 3

動詞 + 得／不 + 結果補語・方向補語

Inserting 「得」 between a verb and its resultative or directional complement means "can," and inserting 「不」 means "can't" (「听得懂」 = can understand what one hears, 「听不懂」 = can't understand what one hears). The difference from 「能」 is that a potential complement expresses whether or not you can reach the result when you try. In conversation, the negative form is very common.

×「我不能听懂。」→ "I can't understand (what I hear)" is naturally 「我听不懂。」. 「不能」 expresses prohibition or impossibility due to circumstances, while a potential complement expresses failing to reach the result.

Example sentences

老师的话你听得懂吗？
lǎo shī de huà nǐ tīng de dǒng ma？
Can you understand what the teacher says?
