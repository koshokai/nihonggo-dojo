The 「把」 construction (doing something to an object)

HSK 3

主語 + 把 + 目的語（特定）+ 動詞 + 結果・方向・了など

「把」 brings the object in front of the verb to emphasize what was done to that object. The object must be something specific (「那本书」, 「我的手机」). The verb must be followed by a result, a direction, 「了」 or the like; the sentence can't end with the bare verb. Negatives and auxiliary verbs go before 「把」 (「没把…」, 「要把…」).

×「我把那本书看。」→ The sentence can't end with the bare verb. Add a result, such as 「看完了」. For the negative, ×「我把书没看完」→ 「我没把书看完」.

Example sentences

我把作业写完了。
wǒ bǎ zuò yè xiě wán le。
I've finished my homework.
