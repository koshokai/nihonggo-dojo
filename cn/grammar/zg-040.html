The 「被」 construction (passive)

HSK 3

主語（受け手）+ 被 +（行為者）+ 動詞 + 結果・了など

「被」 expresses the passive: "be ...-ed." The doer can be omitted (「我的自行车被偷了」), and in speech 「叫」 and 「让」 are also common (though the doer can't be omitted after 「叫」 or 「让」). As with 「把」, the verb needs a result or 「了」 after it. It was originally used mostly for unpleasant events, and Chinese uses the passive far less freely than English does.

Turning every English passive into 「被」 sounds unnatural. "This book is widely read" is often more natural in the active voice, as in 「很多人看这本书」 (many people read this book).

Example sentences

我的自行车被偷了。
wǒ de zì xíng chē bèi tōu le。
My bike was stolen.
