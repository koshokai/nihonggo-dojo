Intensifying complements (extremely ...)

HSK 3

形容詞・心理動詞 + 极了／死了／坏了／得很

These complements express a very high degree: 「好极了」 (wonderful), 「累死了」 (dead tired), 「高兴坏了」 (thrilled), 「冷得很」 (very cold) and so on. 「死了」 and 「坏了」 are colloquial and exaggerated. When there is an intensifying complement, 「很」 and similar words don't go before the adjective.

×「很累死了。」→ No 「很」 is needed with an intensifying complement. 「累死了」 doesn't mean you'll actually die, so feel free to use it in everyday life.

Example sentences

今天热死了！
jīn tiān rè sǐ le！
I'm dying of the heat today!
