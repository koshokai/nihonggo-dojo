Separable verbs (「见面」, 「睡觉」, etc.)

HSK 3

動詞部分 + 了／过／時間・回数 + 目的語部分

Words such as 「见面」, 「睡觉」, 「帮忙」, 「结婚」 and 「游泳」 are made up of verb + object and are called separable verbs. Since they already contain an object, they can't take another object after them (×「见面他」). The other person is brought to the front with a preposition (「跟他见面」), and 「了」, 「过」, durations and numbers of times go in the middle (「见过一次面」, 「睡了八个小时觉」).

×「我见面了朋友。」→ 「我跟朋友见面了。」 ×「帮忙我」→ 「帮我的忙」 / 「帮我一下」. Don't translate English "meet someone" or "help someone" word for word.

Example sentences

我明天跟她见面。
wǒ míng tiān gēn tā jiàn miàn。
I'm meeting her tomorrow.
