「没有…那么」: A is not as ... as B

HSK 3

A + 没有 + B +（那么／这么）+ 形容詞

The negative of a 「比」 comparison normally uses 「没有」 rather than 「不比」, expressing "A is not as ... as B." Inserting 「那么」 or 「这么」 before the adjective adds the nuance "that ..., so ...." Note that 「不比」 has a special meaning: "A is not more ... than B" (they are about the same).

×「我比他不高。」→ "A is not as ... as B" is A + 「没有」 + B + adjective. 「没有」 sentences can't take 「很」 or an amount of difference (such as 「三公分」).

Example sentences

我没有他那么高。
wǒ méi yǒu tā nà me gāo。
I'm not as tall as he is.
