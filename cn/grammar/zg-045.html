「越来越…」, 「越…越…」: more and more ...

HSK 3

越来越 + 形容詞／心理動詞 ／ 越 + A，越 + B

「越来越」 + adjective means "more and more ..." over time. 「越A越B」 means "the more A, the more B," expressing two linked changes (「越学越有意思」). In neither pattern can a degree adverb such as 「很」 or 「非常」 follow 「越」. 「了」 is often added at the end.

×「越来越很冷。」→ 「越来越冷」. No degree adverb is needed. 「越A越B」 can be used even when the subjects differ (「你越说，他越生气」).

Example sentences

天气越来越热了。
tiān qì yuè lái yuè rè le。
It's getting hotter and hotter.
