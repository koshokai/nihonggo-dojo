「一…就…」: as soon as ...

HSK 3

主語 + 一 + 動詞A，（主語）+ 就 + 動詞B

Expresses "as soon as A, B" or "whenever A, B." The first use is when B follows A immediately in time (「他一到家就睡了」); the second is a condition-and-result relationship (「我一喝酒就脸红」 = Whenever I drink, my face turns red). Both 「一」 and 「就」 go after the subject and before the verb.

With two subjects, the word order is 「老师一说，我就懂了」. ×「一老师说」→ Both 「一」 and 「就」 go after their subjects.

Example sentences

我一下课就回家。
wǒ yí xià kè jiù huí jiā。
I go home as soon as class is over.
