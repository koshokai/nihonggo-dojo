「如果…就…」: if ..., (then) ...

HSK 3

如果／要是 + 条件，（主語）+ 就 + 結果

Hypothetical conditions, "if ...," are expressed with 「如果…就…」. In speech 「要是」 is also common, and 「…的话」 is sometimes added at the end of the condition (「如果明天下雨的话」). The 「就」 in the second half is an adverb, so it goes after the subject and before the verb.

×「如果下雨，就我不去。」→ 「就」 goes after the subject: 「我就不去」. Remember that 「就」 is an adverb, so it always goes right before the verb.

Example sentences

如果明天下雨，我就不去了。
rú guǒ míng tiān xià yǔ， wǒ jiù bú qù le。
If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.
