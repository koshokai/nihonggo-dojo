「又」, 「再」, 「还」: again

HSK 3

又＋動詞（済んだ繰り返し）／再＋動詞（これからの繰り返し）／还＋動詞（継続・追加）

English uses "again" for all of these, but 「又」 is for a repetition that has already happened (「他又迟到了」), while 「再」 is for a repetition still to come (「明天再来」). 「还」 means "still, further," and placed before an auxiliary verb, as in 「还想去」, it also expresses "want to ... again (further)." Note the word order: 「再」 goes after the auxiliary verb (「想再去」), 「还」 before it (「还想去」).

×「请又说一遍。」→ For the future, use 「再」. ×「我再想去」→ 「我想再去」 / 「我还想去」. Past is 「又」, future is 「再」.

Example sentences

他今天又迟到了。
tā jīn tiān yòu chí dào le。
He was late again today.
