「为了」: in order to, for (purpose)

HSK 3

为了 + 目的，主語 + 動詞 ／ 主語 + 为了 + 目的 + 動詞

「为了」 expresses purpose: "in order to ..., for the sake of ...." It can go at the start of the sentence or after the subject. Distinguish it from 「因为」, which expresses cause: 「为了健康」 (for one's health = purpose) and 「因为生病」 (because of illness = cause) are different things.

English "for" can express both purpose ("for my health") and cause ("famous for its food"), but Chinese uses 「为了」 for purpose and 「因为」 for cause. ×「为了下雨，比赛取消了。」→ 「因为下雨…」

Example sentences

为了健康，我每天跑步。
wèi le jiàn kāng， wǒ měi tiān pǎo bù。
I run every day for my health.
