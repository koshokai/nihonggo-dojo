「应该」, 「得（děi）」: should, have to

HSK 3

主語 + 应该／得（děi）+ 動詞

「应该」 expresses what is right or a reasonable guess: "should, ought to, should be (probably)." 「得」, read 「děi」, expresses necessity in speech: "have to, gotta." Watch the negatives: the negative of 「应该」 is 「不应该」 (shouldn't), but the negative of 「得」 (「děi」) is 「不用」 (don't have to); you don't say 「不得」.

×「明天不得去。」→ "Don't have to go" is 「不用去」. Also note that 「得」 here is read 「děi」, not 「de」.

Example sentences

你应该多休息。
nǐ yīng gāi duō xiū xi。
You should rest more.
