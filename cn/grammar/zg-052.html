「有点儿」 and 「一点儿」

HSK 3

有点儿 + 形容詞（不満）／ 形容詞 + 一点儿（比較）

Both mean "a little," but their positions and meanings differ. 「有点儿」 + adjective goes before the adjective and expresses dissatisfaction: "a bit (too) ..." (「有点儿贵」). Adjective + 「一点儿」 goes after the adjective and means "a little more ... (by comparison)" (「便宜一点儿吧」 = Make it a little cheaper). 「一点儿」 can also go before a noun (「喝一点儿水」).

×「这个一点儿贵。」→ For dissatisfaction, say 「有点儿贵」. Also, 「有点儿」 is normally not used for positive things (×「有点儿便宜」).

Example sentences

这件衣服有点儿贵。
zhè jiàn yī fu yǒu diǎnr guì。
This piece of clothing is a bit expensive.
