「一边…一边…」: doing two things at once

HSK 3

主語 + 一边 + 動詞A + 一边 + 動詞B

Expresses doing two actions at the same time (「一边听音乐一边学习」 = study while listening to music). In speech it can be shortened to 「边…边…」. Unlike English "while ...," which marks one action as secondary, 「一边…一边…」 presents the two actions as equal partners.

"Do ... in a certain state" (eat standing up) uses 「着」, as in 「站着吃」. Use 「一边…一边…」 when consciously doing two actions at once.

Example sentences

我喜欢一边吃饭一边看电视。
wǒ xǐ huan yì biān chī fàn yì biān kàn diàn shì。
I like watching TV while I eat.
