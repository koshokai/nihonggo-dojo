「不但…而且…」: not only ... but also ...

HSK 3

不但 + A，而且 +（还／也）B

Used to pile up information: "not only A, but also B." 「还」 or 「也」 is often added in the second half. If there is one subject, 「不但」 goes after it (「他不但会说汉语，而且…」); if the two halves have different subjects, 「不但」 goes before the subject (「不但我去，而且他也去」).

With two subjects: ×「我不但去，而且他也去。」→ 「不但我去，而且他也去。」 The position of 「不但」 depends on the number of subjects.

Example sentences

这家饭馆不但便宜，而且好吃。
zhè jiā fàn guǎn bú dàn pián yi， ér qiě hǎo chī。
This restaurant is not only cheap but also good.
