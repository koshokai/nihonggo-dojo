Existential sentences (place + verb + person/thing)

HSK 3

場所・時間 + 動詞（着／了／補語）+ 人・物（不特定）

To say that something exists, appears or disappears somewhere, put the place at the start of the sentence and the person or thing after the verb (「桌子上放着一本书」 = There's a book lying on the desk). The person or thing after the verb is usually unspecified, such as 「一个人」. Something newly appearing also goes after the verb, as in 「来了一个客人」.

Following English "A guest came," it's easy to say ×「一个客人来了」. When an unspecified person or thing appears, put it after the verb: 「来了一个客人」.

Example sentences

墙上挂着一张地图。
qiáng shàng guà zhe yì zhāng dì tú。
There's a map hanging on the wall.
