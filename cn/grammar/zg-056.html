Question word + 「都」/「也」 (everyone, nothing, etc.)

HSK 3

疑問詞 + 都／也 + 動詞 ／ 一 + 量詞 + 名詞 + 也／都 + 否定

Adding 「都」 or 「也」 after a question word gives a total affirmative or total negative: "everyone, everything, everywhere" or "no one, nothing" (「谁都知道」, 「什么也没吃」). 「一」 + measure word (+ noun) + 「也」/「都」 + negative means "not a single ..." (「一个人也没有」). The key point is that even when it's the object, it goes before the verb.

×「我没吃什么也。」→ 「什么也」 goes before the verb: 「我什么也没吃」. Unlike English "I didn't eat anything," the "anything" part comes before the verb.

Example sentences

这件事谁都知道。
zhè jiàn shì shéi dōu zhī dao。
Everyone knows about this.
