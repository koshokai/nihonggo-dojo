Extended meanings of directional complements (「起来」, 「下去」, 「出来」, 「下来」)

HSK 4

動詞・形容詞 + 起来／下去／出来／下来

Directional complements are often used with abstract meanings too. 「起来」 = start to (「笑起来」), "when you ... it" (「看起来」 = it looks ...), recall (「想起来」). 「下去」 = keep on (「说下去」). 「出来」 = identifying, discovering (「听出来」 = tell by listening). 「下来」 = continuing up to the present, or stopping and fixing in place (「坚持下来」, 「停下来」, 「记下来」).

"Remember (recall)" is 「想起来」, while "come up with (an idea)" is 「想出来」. For a name you had forgotten, say 「我想起来他的名字了」.

Example sentences

她突然哭起来了。
tā tū rán kū qǐ lai le。
She suddenly started crying.
