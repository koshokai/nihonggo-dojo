「对」, 「对于」, 「关于」: toward, regarding, about

HSK 4

对／对于 + 対象 + 述語 ／ 关于 + 話題，…

「对」 means "toward, to, for," expressing attitudes toward people (「他对我很好」) or effects (「对身体不好」). 「对于」 is almost the same but more literary and awkward for attitudes toward people. 「关于」 means "about, concerning" and marks a topic; it goes at the start of the sentence or modifies a noun, as in 「关于…的书」. It can't go after the subject.

×「他关于这个问题很了解。」→ After the subject, use 「对」: 「他对这个问题很了解」. For attitudes toward people, use 「对」 and avoid 「对于」.

Example sentences

他对我很好。
tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo。
He's very good to me.
