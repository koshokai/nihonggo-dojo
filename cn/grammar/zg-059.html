「只要…就…」: as long as ...

HSK 4

只要 + 条件，（主語）+ 就 + 結果

Expresses a sufficient condition: "as long as this condition is met, the result follows." It carries the nuance that the bar is low and is often used for encouragement, as in 「只要努力，就能成功」 (As long as you work hard, you can succeed). It is easily confused with 「只有…才…」 (a necessary condition, the only way), so it's best to learn them as a pair.

「只要」 = as long as ..., it's fine; 「只有」 = without ..., it won't work. The pairings are fixed: 「只要…就」 and 「只有…才」.

Example sentences

只要你努力，就一定能学好汉语。
zhǐ yào nǐ nǔ lì， jiù yí dìng néng xué hǎo hàn yǔ。
As long as you work hard, you'll definitely get good at Chinese.
