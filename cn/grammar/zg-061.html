「连…都／也…」: even ...

HSK 4

连 + 強調したい語 + 都／也 + 動詞

Gives an extreme example for emphasis: "even ...." What follows 「连」 can be a subject, an object, a quantity and so on; even an object moves in front of the verb (「他连饭都没吃」). 「连一…都／也」 + negative is a strong negative, "not even one ..." (「连一句话也没说」).

×「他连没吃饭。」→ Don't forget 「都」/「也」. 「连」 singles out the item, and 「都」/「也」 completes the meaning "even"; they work as a pair.

Example sentences

这个问题连孩子都知道。
zhè ge wèn tí lián hái zi dōu zhī dao。
Even a child knows this.
