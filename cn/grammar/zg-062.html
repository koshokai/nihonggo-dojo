Question words used as indefinites (something, someone)

HSK 4

疑問詞を「何か・誰か・どこか」の意味で使う

Besides asking questions, question words can also mean "something, someone, somewhere." In 「你想吃点儿什么吗？」 (Do you want something to eat?), 「吗」 is added even though there is a question word, and 「什么」 means "something." In negative sentences they express "not particularly ...," as in 「没什么」 (it's nothing) and 「不怎么」 (not very ...).

Question word + 「吗」 looks like it breaks the rules, but here the question word means "something, someone." 「你要什么？」 (What do you want?) and 「你要什么吗？」 (Do you want anything?) are different.

Example sentences

你想吃点儿什么吗？
nǐ xiǎng chī diǎnr shén me ma？
Would you like something to eat?
