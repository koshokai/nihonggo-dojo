Rhetorical 「难道…吗？」: Surely not ...? Don't tell me ...!

HSK 4

难道 + 文 + 吗？

「难道…吗？」 is a rhetorical question, "Surely not ...? / Don't tell me ...!" (no, that can't be), which strongly asserts the negative of an affirmative sentence or the affirmative of a negative one. 「难道你不知道吗？」 means "Surely you know (you must know)." It is a strong expression of surprise or dissatisfaction, so be careful using it with superiors.

「难道你不知道吗？」 isn't a question seeking an answer but an assertion that "you must know." The final 「吗」 is sometimes omitted.

Example sentences

难道你忘了吗？
nán dào nǐ wàng le ma？
Don't tell me you forgot!
