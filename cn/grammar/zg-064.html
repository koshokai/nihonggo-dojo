「既然…就…」: since ..., (then) ...

HSK 4

既然 + 既定の事実，（主語）+ 就／也／还 + 判断・提案

「既然」 takes an established fact as a premise and draws a conclusion from it: "since ..., let's/you should ...." While 「如果」 is an uncertain hypothesis, 「既然」 means "since it's already the case that ...." The second half is often a suggestion, command or judgment, paired with 「就」, 「也」, 「还」 and so on.

It can't be used for things that haven't happened yet. ×「既然明天下雨…」 (not yet certain) → 「如果明天下雨…」. 「既然」 picks up on something that is already a fact.

Example sentences

既然你不舒服，就早点儿回去吧。
jì rán nǐ bù shū fu， jiù zǎo diǎnr huí qu ba。
Since you're not feeling well, go home early.
