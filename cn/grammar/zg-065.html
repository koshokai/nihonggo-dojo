「即使…也…」: even if ...

HSK 4

即使 + 仮定，（主語）+ 也 + 結果

Expresses a hypothetical concession: "even if ..., ..." (「即使明天下雨，我也要去」). While 「虽然」 is a contrast based on fact (it actually is raining), 「即使」 is a hypothetical contrast. In speech, 「就是…也」 and 「哪怕…也」 are also common. The 「也」 in the second half is required.

「虽然」 = fact ("even though it actually is ..."), 「即使」 = hypothesis ("even if it were ..."). Don't use 「但是」 in the second half: ×「即使…但是…」→ 「即使…也…」.

Example sentences

即使很忙，他也每天给家里打电话。
jí shǐ hěn máng， tā yě měi tiān gěi jiā lǐ dǎ diàn huà。
Even when he's busy, he calls home every day.
