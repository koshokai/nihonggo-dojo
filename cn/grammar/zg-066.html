「无论」/「不管…都…」: no matter ...

HSK 4

无论／不管 + 疑問詞・A还是B・反復形，（主語）+ 都／也 + …

Expresses that the result doesn't change whatever the conditions. 「无论」 and 「不管」 must be followed by a question word (「什么」, 「谁」, 「怎么」) or by alternatives such as 「A还是B」 or 「好不好」. A single plain condition can't follow them. 「不管」 is colloquial, and 「无论」 is somewhat more formal.

×「不管下雨，我都去。」→ 「不管下不下雨」 / 「不管下多大的雨」. 「无论」 and 「不管」 must be followed by alternatives or a question word.

Example sentences

无论遇到什么困难，我都不会放弃。
wú lùn yù dào shén me kùn nan， wǒ dōu bú huì fàng qì。
No matter what difficulties I face, I won't give up.
