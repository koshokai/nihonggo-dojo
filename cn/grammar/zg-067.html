「不得不」, 「只好」: have no choice but to

HSK 4

主語 + 不得不／只好 + 動詞

Both mean "do ... reluctantly, though you don't really want to." 「不得不」 has a strong sense of obligation, "can't avoid doing ... because of the circumstances," while 「只好」 has a strong sense of resignation, "there's no other way, so I'll just have to ...." Both go before the verb, but 「只好」 can also go before the subject.

「不得不」 is a double negative meaning "must." This 「得」 is read 「dé」. 「不得」 alone means "must not," so be careful.

Example sentences

因为下大雨，我们不得不取消了旅行。
yīn wèi xià dà yǔ， wǒ men bù dé bù qǔ xiāo le lǚ xíng。
Because of the heavy rain, we had to cancel our trip.
