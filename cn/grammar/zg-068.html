「甚至」: even, to the point of

HSK 4

A，甚至 + B ／ 甚至 + 極端な例 + 都／也

「甚至」 lines things up from lesser to greater and ends with an extreme example for emphasis: "even ..., to the point of ...." It can go before a word, a phrase or a clause. It is also combined with 「连…都」 as 「甚至连…都…」.

「甚至」 marks the peak of a buildup. The rule is to order things from weaker to stronger and always put the extreme example last.

Example sentences

他工作很忙，甚至周末也要加班。
tā gōng zuò hěn máng， shèn zhì zhōu mò yě yào jiā bān。
He's so busy at work that he even has to work overtime on weekends.
