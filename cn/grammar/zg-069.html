「否则」: otherwise

HSK 4

A（命令・条件），否则 + B（望ましくない結果）

「否则」 means "if not, otherwise," and is followed by the (usually bad) result of not meeting the condition in the first half. In speech, 「要不然」 and 「不然」 are also common. The first half often contains 「必须」, 「得」, 「要」 and the like.

What follows 「否则」 is the result of not doing what the first half says. It is used much like English "or else" or "otherwise."

Example sentences

快走吧，否则就要迟到了。
kuài zǒu ba， fǒu zé jiù yào chí dào le。
Let's hurry, or we'll be late.
