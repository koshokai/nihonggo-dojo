「却」: but, yet (contrary to expectation)

HSK 4

主語 + 却 + 動詞・形容詞

「却」 is an adverb meaning "but, yet" (contrary to expectations), and it goes after the subject and before the verb. 「但是」 is a conjunction and can go at the start of a clause, but 「却」 cannot. They are sometimes used together as 「但是…却…」.

×「却他没来。」→ 「却」 is an adverb, so it goes after the subject: 「他却没来」. The key point is that, unlike 「但是」, it can't start a clause.

Example sentences

我等了他一个小时，他却没来。
wǒ děng le tā yí ge xiǎo shí， tā què méi lái。
I waited an hour for him, but he never came.
