「按照」, 「根据」: according to, based on

HSK 4

按照 + 規則・計画 + 動詞 ／ 根据 + 資料・事実 + 判断

「按照」 means "do ... according to (rules, plans, an order)," and 「根据」 means "do or judge ... based on (data, facts, surveys)." 「根据」 can also be a noun meaning "basis, grounds." Both are prepositions placed before the verb or at the start of the sentence.

"According to" a source of information is 「根据」. To "follow" rules or procedures, use 「按照」. Remember: 「按照」 = rules, 「根据」 = data.

Example sentences

请按照顺序排队。
qǐng àn zhào shùn xù pái duì。
Please line up in order.
