「随着」: as ..., along with ...

HSK 4

随着 + 変化（名詞句），主語 + 変化

「随着」 means "as ..., along with ...," expressing a change that happens in step with another change. It is typically followed by a noun phrase expressing change, such as 「…的发展」, 「…的提高」 or 「时间的推移」. It usually goes at the start of the sentence, and the second half often contains 「越来越」, 「也」 and so on.

It must be followed by words expressing change. It's handy to learn it in the form noun + 「的」 + 「发展」/「提高」/「增加」.

Example sentences

随着经济的发展，人们的生活越来越好了。
suí zhe jīng jì de fā zhǎn， rén men de shēng huó yuè lái yuè hǎo le。
With economic development, people's lives have gotten better and better.
