「并」 + negative: not at all, not really

HSK 4

主語 + 并 + 不／没（有）+ 動詞・形容詞

「并不」 and 「并没有」 contradict the listener's assumption or a common expectation, emphasizing "actually not ..." or "it's not that ...." 「他并不是日本人」 means "(You may think so, but) he isn't Japanese." It is used only in negative sentences, never in affirmative ones.

「并」 is an adverb that strengthens a negative and must go directly before 「不」 or 「没」: ×「他不并知道」→ 「他并不知道」.

Example sentences

这个问题并不难。
zhè ge wèn tí bìng bù nán。
This problem isn't actually that hard.
