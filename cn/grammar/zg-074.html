「从来」: never (ever)

HSK 4

主語 + 从来 + 不／没（有）+ 動詞（+过）

「从来」 means "always, all along (up to now)," and in most cases it is used with a negative to mean "never once (up to now)." 「从来不」 is for habits and principles (never do ...), and 「从来没（有）…过」 is for experience (have never once done ...). With 「从来没…过」, 「过」 is normally added.

「从来不」 = principle or habit; 「从来没…过」 = no experience. ×「我从来不去过」→ 「我从来没去过」.

Example sentences

我从来不喝酒。
wǒ cóng lái bù hē jiǔ。
I never drink alcohol.
