「要是…（的话）」: if ... (colloquial)

HSK 4

要是／如果 + 条件 +（的话），…

「要是」 is the colloquial version of 「如果」: "if ...." 「的话」 can be added at the end of the condition to make the hypothesis clear, and 「的话」 alone can also express a condition (「明天下雨的话，我就不去了」). The second half often has 「就」.

「的话」 doesn't mean "the talk of ..." here; it's a marker of hypothesis. It can be paired as 「如果…的话」 or 「要是…的话」, or used alone as 「…的话」.

Example sentences

要是你不舒服，就别来了。
yào shi nǐ bù shū fu， jiù bié lái le。
If you're not feeling well, don't come.
