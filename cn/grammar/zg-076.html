「到底」: ... on earth? (pressing for an answer)

HSK 4

主語 + 到底 + 疑問 ／ 到底 + 主語 + 疑問

Used in questions to press strongly for an answer: "What on earth ...? / Just what ...?" It combines with question-word questions, affirmative-negative questions and 「还是」 choice questions, but not with 「吗」 questions. In statements it can also mean "after all, in the end," but learn "on earth" first.

×「你到底去吗？」→ 「到底」 doesn't go with 「吗」 questions. Combine it with an affirmative-negative question, as in 「你到底去不去？」, or with a question word.

Example sentences

你到底去不去？
nǐ dào dǐ qù bu qù？
So are you going or not?
