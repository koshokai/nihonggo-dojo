「竟然」: to one's surprise, actually

HSK 4

主語 + 竟然 + 動詞・形容詞

「竟然」 is an adverb expressing surprise at something unexpected: "actually, to my surprise, of all things." It goes after the subject and before the verb. 「居然」 means almost the same. It can be used for both good and bad things.

「竟然」 conveys the speaker's surprise; it isn't needed when simply stating a fact. The combination 「没想到…竟然…」 is very natural.

Example sentences

他竟然一个人去了南极。
tā jìng rán yí ge rén qù le nán jí。
He actually went to Antarctica by himself!
