「由于」: due to, owing to (cause)

HSK 4

由于 + 原因，（所以／因此）+ 結果

「由于」 expresses cause and leans toward written language, sounding more formal than 「因为」. In the second half it can pair with 「因此」 as well as 「所以」. Also, unlike 「因为」, it isn't normally used to add a reason afterward, as in 「…，由于…」.

「由于…原因」 is often heard in announcements and news. To add a reason afterward, as in 「…，是因为…」, use 「因为」, not 「由于」.

Example sentences

由于天气原因，航班推迟了。
yóu yú tiān qì yuán yīn， háng bān tuī chí le。
The flight was delayed due to the weather.
