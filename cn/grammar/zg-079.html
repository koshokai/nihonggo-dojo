「与其…不如…」: rather than ..., better to ...

HSK 5

与其 + A，（还）不如 + B

Compares two options, "rather than doing A, B would be better," with the speaker choosing B. Note that the option chosen is the one after 「不如」. It feels close to English "rather than A, I'd (sooner) B" or "instead of A, might as well B," and 「还」 is often added as 「还不如」.

The option chosen comes after 「不如」. On its own, 「A不如B」 means "A is not as good as B," so learning the two halves as a set keeps you from getting confused.

Example sentences

与其在家睡觉，不如出去走走。
yǔ qí zài jiā shuì jiào， bù rú chū qu zǒu zou。
Rather than sleeping at home, we'd be better off going out for a walk.
