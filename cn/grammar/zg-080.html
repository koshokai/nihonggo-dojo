「宁可…也不…」: would rather ... than ...

HSK 5

宁可 + A（我慢する選択），也不／也要 + B

Faced with options that are both undesirable, this expresses a firm resolve: "even if I have to pay the price of A, I will never do B." 「宁可」 can also be said as 「宁愿」. The first half contains the hardship or loss you're willing to put up with, and the second half the thing you absolutely don't want to do. There is also the pattern 「宁可…也要…」 ("even if it means ..., I want to ...").

After 「宁可」 comes the option you put up with and choose; after 「也不」 comes the option you would never choose. Since it is a statement of will, it sounds natural with an explicit subject, as in 「我宁可…」.

Example sentences

我宁可走路去，也不坐他的车。
wǒ nìng kě zǒu lù qù， yě bú zuò tā de chē。
I'd rather walk there than ride in his car.
