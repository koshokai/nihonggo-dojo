「何况」: let alone ..., much less ...

HSK 5

A（程度の高い例）都／尚且…，何况 + B（呢）？

An expression that lets the listener infer a conclusion about the second half from the example in the first half: "if even A is like this, then B is all the more so." The first half often uses 「连…都」 or 「尚且」. It is often said as a rhetorical question with 「呢」 at the end.

「何况」 can be followed by just a noun (「何况我呢？」). You can use it much like English "let alone" or "much less."

Example sentences

这个问题连老师都不会，何况我们呢？
zhè ge wèn tí lián lǎo shī dōu bú huì， hé kuàng wǒ men ne？
Even the teacher can't solve this problem, let alone us.
