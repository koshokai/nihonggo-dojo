「而」: written-style connective (but / and)

HSK 5

A + 而 + B（逆接・並列）／ 为 + 目的 + 而 + 動詞

「而」 is a connective used often in writing, and its meaning changes depending on how the two parts relate: contrast, "but / whereas" (「南方多雨，而北方干燥」); coordination, "and" (「聪明而善良」); and purpose, "do ... for the sake of ..." (「为…而…」). Contrastive 「而」 is lighter than 「但是」 and is handy for setting two things side by side.

「为…而…」 is a staple of speeches and essays, used as "purpose + 「而」 + action," as in 「为健康而运动」. In conversation 「而」 sounds stiff, so 「但是・可是」 are perfectly fine.

Example sentences

南方多雨，而北方比较干燥。
nán fāng duō yǔ， ér běi fāng bǐ jiào gān zào。
The south gets a lot of rain, whereas the north is relatively dry.
