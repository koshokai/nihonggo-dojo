「之所以…是因为…」: the reason ... is that ...

HSK 5

主語 + 之所以 + 結果，是因为 + 原因

A written-style expression that states the result first and explains the reason afterward (「我之所以选择这个工作，是因为…」 = "The reason I chose this job is that ..."). The order of cause and effect is the reverse of 「因为…所以…」. It is handy in speeches and essays when you want to state a reason clearly.

After 「之所以」 comes the result, and after 「是因为」 comes the reason. The order is the reverse of 「因为…所以…」, so don't mix them up as in ×「之所以…所以…」.

Example sentences

我之所以学汉语，是因为对中国文化感兴趣。
wǒ zhī suǒ yǐ xué hàn yǔ， shì yīn wèi duì zhōng guó wén huà gǎn xìng qù。
The reason I study Chinese is that I'm interested in Chinese culture.
