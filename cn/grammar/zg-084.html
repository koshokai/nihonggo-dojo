「非…不可」: absolutely must ...

HSK 5

主語 + 非 + 動詞句 + 不可／不行

A double negative expressing strong necessity or determination: "cannot not ...," "will ... no matter what" (「这件事非你去不可」). In speech, 「不行」 is also used instead of 「不可」. It can also express a confident prediction, "is bound to ..." (「不带伞非淋湿不可」 = "If you don't take an umbrella, you're bound to get wet").

「非」 and 「不可」 go together as a set. In casual speech people sometimes use 「非」 alone, as in 「我非去！」, but learn 「非…不可」 first.

Example sentences

这件事非你亲自去不可。
zhè jiàn shì fēi nǐ qīn zì qù bù kě。
You absolutely have to go in person for this matter.
