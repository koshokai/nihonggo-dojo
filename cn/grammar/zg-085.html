「以免」: so as not to ..., to avoid ...

HSK 5

A（対策），以免 + B（避けたい事態）

「以免」 means "so as not to ... / to avoid ...," showing that the action in the first half prevents the bad situation in the second half. The undesirable result is written as is after it (without a negative). It leans toward written style and is often used in notices and advice. In speech, 「免得」 and 「省得」 are also used.

×「以免不迟到」 → 「以免迟到」. 「以免」 already contains the meaning "so as not to," so the trick is not to add a separate negative for the English "not."

Example sentences

出门带把伞，以免淋湿。
chū mén dài bǎ sǎn， yǐ miǎn lín shī。
Take an umbrella when you go out so you don't get wet.
