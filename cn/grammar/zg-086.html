Rhetorical 「何必」: there's no need to ...

HSK 5

何必 + 動詞句（+呢）？

「何必」 is a rhetorical question, "why would you need to ...? (you don't)," used to calm someone down or talk them out of something: "you don't have to ..., do you?" 「呢」 is often added at the end. It is a rhetorical, slightly more emotional version of 「不必」 ("there's no need to").

「何必」 isn't really asking "why"; its message is "you don't need to." It's used to show concern for someone or to gently admonish them.

Example sentences

这么近，何必打车呢？
zhè me jìn， hé bì dǎ chē ne？
It's so close; why bother taking a taxi?
