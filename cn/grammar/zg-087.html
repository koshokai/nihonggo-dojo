「以…为…」: take ... as ...

HSK 5

以 + A + 为 + B

A written-style expression meaning "take A as B / regard A as B." There are many set combinations, such as 「以学生为主」 ("mainly for students"), 「以…为目标」 ("take ... as one's goal"), and 「以…为荣」 ("take pride in ..."). This 「为」 is read wéi.

Don't read 「为」 as wèi ("for"). In 「以A为B」 it's wéi ("to take as"). 「以…为主」 and 「以…为例」 are especially common.

Example sentences

这家公司以年轻人为主要顾客。
zhè jiā gōng sī yǐ nián qīng rén wéi zhǔ yào gù kè。
This company's main customers are young people.
