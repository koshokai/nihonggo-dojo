「拿…来说」: take ... for example

HSK 5

（就）拿 + 例 + 来说，…

An expression used to give a concrete example after talking about something in general. 「就」 is often added in front, as in 「就拿我来说」 ("take me, for example"). 「拿…来讲」 and 「以…为例」 mean the same thing. It helps make explanations and speeches easier to follow.

「拿」 here doesn't mean "to hold"; it means "to take up (as an example)." It sounds natural when used to give a specific example after a general statement.

Example sentences

现在很多人不看纸质书了，就拿我来说，一年只买一两本。
xiàn zài hěn duō rén bú kàn zhǐ zhì shū le， jiù ná wǒ lái shuō， yì nián zhǐ mǎi yì liǎng běn。
Nowadays lots of people don't read paper books anymore. Take me, for example: I only buy one or two a year.
