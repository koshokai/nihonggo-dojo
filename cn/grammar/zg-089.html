「未必・不见得」: not necessarily

HSK 5

主語 + 未必／不见得 + 動詞・形容詞

「未必」 and 「不见得」 mean "not necessarily," gently disagreeing with someone's opinion or a common assumption (「贵的未必好」 = "Expensive things aren't necessarily good"). They are softer than a flat 「不」, so you can push back in a discussion without causing friction. 「不见得」 is colloquial, while 「未必」 is somewhat more formal.

「未必」 = 「不一定」. Don't confuse it with 「一定不」 ("definitely not"). 「未必不好」 means "not necessarily bad."

Example sentences

贵的东西未必好。
guì de dōng xi wèi bì hǎo。
Expensive things aren't necessarily good.
