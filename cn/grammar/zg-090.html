「难免」: hard to avoid, bound to

HSK 5

（主語）+ 难免 +（会）+ 動詞句

「难免」 means "it's hard to avoid ... / one inevitably ...," used to acknowledge that it's natural for something undesirable to happen (「刚开始工作，难免会出错」). It is often followed by 「会」, and is also often used to console someone.

「难免」 doesn't mean "difficult"; it means "hard to avoid → tends to happen." It isn't used for good things, only for problems like mistakes, nervousness, or misunderstandings.

Example sentences

第一次做，难免会紧张。
dì yī cì zuò， nán miǎn huì jǐn zhāng。
It's only natural to be nervous the first time you do something.
