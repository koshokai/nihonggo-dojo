「哪怕…也…」: even if ... (strong resolve)

HSK 5

哪怕 + 極端な仮定，（主語）+ 也／都 + …

「哪怕」 means almost the same as 「即使」, "even if," but it is more colloquial and is often used to express strong resolve by citing an extreme situation (「哪怕只有一点儿希望，我也不放弃」). The second half pairs with 「也」 or 「都」.

The 「哪」 in 「哪怕」 doesn't mean "where" or "which." Remember 「哪怕」 = 「即使」 ("even if"), and always follow it with 「也／都」.

Example sentences

哪怕再忙，我也要陪孩子吃晚饭。
nǎ pà zài máng， wǒ yě yào péi hái zi chī wǎn fàn。
No matter how busy I am, I make a point of having dinner with my kids.
