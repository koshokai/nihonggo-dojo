「不是…而是…」: not ... but ...

HSK 5

不是 + A，而是 + B

"Not A, but B": used to correct a misunderstanding or give the real reason. The point is B. It is very often used to explain reasons, as in 「我不是不想去，而是没时间」 ("It's not that I don't want to go; I just don't have time"). Don't confuse it with 「不是A就是B」 ("if not A, then B").

「不是A就是B」 means "either A or B" (「周末他不是看书就是睡觉」). The meaning changes completely between 「而是」 and 「就是」, so be careful.

Example sentences

他不是日本人，而是韩国人。
tā bú shì rì běn rén， ér shì hán guó rén。
He's not Japanese; he's Korean.
