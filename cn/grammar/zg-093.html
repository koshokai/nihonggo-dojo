「是否」: whether or not (written style)

HSK 5

主語 + 是否 + 動詞・形容詞

「是否」 is the written-style version of 「是不是」 and means "whether (or not)." You often see it in notices, emails, the news, and so on. Sentences with 「是否」 don't take 「吗」. It goes before the verb, and a similar expression is 「能否」 ("whether ... can").

×「你是否去吗？」 → 「是否」 already carries the question, so 「吗」 isn't needed. In conversation, it sounds more natural to rephrase with 「是不是」 or 「V不V」.

Example sentences

请问您是否需要帮助？
qǐng wèn nín shì fǒu xū yào bāng zhù？
Excuse me, do you need any help?
