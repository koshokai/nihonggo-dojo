「至于」: as for ... / go so far as to ...

HSK 5

至于 + 話題，… ／（不）至于 + 動詞句

「至于」 has two uses. ① Changing the topic, "as for ...": brings up another topic related to the previous one (「至于价格，我们以后再谈」). ② Degree, "go so far as to ...": mainly in the negative 「不至于」, "not to the point of ..." (「不至于这么生气吧」). ① goes at the start of a sentence, and ② is used as the predicate.

It's easily confused with 「关于」. 「至于」 is used when switching topics, not for the first topic. To open a topic, use 「关于这个问题，…」.

Example sentences

我们先决定去哪儿，至于怎么去，以后再说。
wǒ men xiān jué dìng qù nǎr， zhì yú zěn me qù， yǐ hòu zài shuō。
Let's first decide where to go; as for how to get there, we can talk about that later.
