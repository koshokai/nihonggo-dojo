「一旦…就…」: once ...

HSK 5

一旦 + 条件（起きたら），（主語）+ 就 + 結果

「一旦」 means "once ... / if ever ...," showing the result that follows if a certain situation arises. The result after it is often serious or irreversible. While 「一…就…」 is an everyday "as soon as ...," 「一旦」 carries the hypothetical weight of "if it ever came to that."

「一旦」 does not mean "temporarily" or "for a moment." In Chinese, 「一旦」 is hypothetical: "once ... (happens)."

Example sentences

一旦决定了，就不要轻易改变。
yí dàn jué dìng le， jiù bú yào qīng yì gǎi biàn。
Once you've made a decision, don't change it lightly.
