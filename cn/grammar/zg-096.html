「反而」: on the contrary, instead

HSK 5

（不但没A，）主語 + 反而 + B（予想と逆の結果）

「反而」 is an adverb showing that the result was the opposite of what was expected or what common sense would suggest (「吃了药，病反而更重了」 = "I took the medicine, but instead the illness got worse"). It is often used in the pattern 「不但没A，反而B」, with 「不但没…」 in the first half: "far from A, it actually became B." It goes after the subject and before the verb.

「却」 is a simple "but," while 「反而」 means "the exact opposite of what was expected." 「不但没…反而…」 is a combination that comes up often on exams. Word order: ×「反而他…」 → 「他反而…」.

Example sentences

雨不但没停，反而越下越大了。
yǔ bú dàn méi tíng， fǎn ér yuè xià yuè dà le。
Far from stopping, the rain actually got heavier and heavier.
