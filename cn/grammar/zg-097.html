Written-style 「之」 (of)

HSK 6

A + 之 + B（＝A的B）／ 分母 + 分之 + 分子 ／ …之一

「之」 is a particle of Classical origin, used today in place of 「的」 in written style and set expressions (「原因之一」 = "one of the reasons"). It also appears in fractions, as in 「三分之一」 ("one third"), and in many words such as 「之前・之后・之间・总之」, making it essential for HSK 6 reading.

In fractions, unlike English, the denominator comes first: "one third" = 「三分之一」. "One of ..." is 「之一」, and it must always be preceded by a group of several (such as 「最…的作家」).

Example sentences

这是我最喜欢的城市之一。
zhè shì wǒ zuì xǐ huan de chéng shì zhī yī。
This is one of my favorite cities.
