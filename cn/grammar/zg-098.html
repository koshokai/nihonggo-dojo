Written-style 「以」 (with, by means of)

HSK 6

以 + 手段・方式 + 動詞 ／ …，以 + 目的

In written style, 「以」 means "using / with" (「以优惠的价格出售」 = "sold at a special price") or "by." When it follows a previous clause as 「…，以」 + verb, it expresses purpose, "in order to ...," and this is the same 「以」 as in 「以便」 and 「以免」. You see it all the time in ads and notices.

「八折」 means "80% of the list price = 20% off." Be careful not to read it as "80% off"! 「以」 + price/method + 「出售」 is a standard expression in ads.

Example sentences

我们以最快的速度赶到了现场。
wǒ men yǐ zuì kuài de sù dù gǎn dào le xiàn chǎng。
We rushed to the scene as fast as we could.
