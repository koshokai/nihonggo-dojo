「乃至」: and even, and by extension

HSK 6

A，B，乃至 + C（最も大きい範囲）

「乃至」 means "and even / and by extension." It goes at the end of a list whose scope gradually widens, introducing the largest item. It's more formal than 「甚至」 and suited to emphasizing how far the scope extends. Note that it does not mean "A or B" or "anywhere from A to B."

It does not mean "A or B." Chinese 「乃至」 means "and even," with the scope getting larger toward the end.

Example sentences

这个发现对中国乃至全世界都有重要意义。
zhè ge fā xiàn duì zhōng guó nǎi zhì quán shì jiè dōu yǒu zhòng yào yì yì。
This discovery is significant for China and even for the whole world.
