「鉴于」: in view of, considering

HSK 6

鉴于 + 状況・事実，… （判断・措置）

「鉴于」 is a stiff written-style expression meaning "considering the situation that ... / in view of ...," used in official documents, notices, business documents, and so on. The first half presents the situation, and the second half states the judgment or measure based on it. It can be used much like English "in view of" or "in light of."

「由于」 is simply a cause, while 「鉴于」 has the nuance of "making a judgment or responding based on that." It sounds natural with a response such as 「决定」 or 「需要」 in the second half.

Example sentences

鉴于天气原因，比赛推迟到下周举行。
jiàn yú tiān qì yuán yīn， bǐ sài tuī chí dào xià zhōu jǔ xíng。
Due to the weather, the match has been postponed until next week.
