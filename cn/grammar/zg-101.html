「况且」: besides, moreover (adding a reason)

HSK 6

理由A，况且 +（又／也／还）理由B

「况且」 means "besides / moreover," used to add another reason to one already given. It's often followed by 「又」, 「也」, or 「还」. In speech, 「再说」 does almost the same job. 「何况」 is mainly used for the inference "let alone ..."

To line up reasons that support a conclusion, use 「况且」. Keep it distinct from the rhetorical inference of 「何况…呢？」 ("let alone").

Example sentences

今天太晚了，况且外面还下着雨，明天再去吧。
jīn tiān tài wǎn le， kuàng qiě wài miàn hái xià zhe yǔ， míng tiān zài qù ba。
It's too late today, and besides, it's raining outside; let's go tomorrow.
