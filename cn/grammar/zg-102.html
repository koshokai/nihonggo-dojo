「固然…但…」: admittedly ..., but

HSK 6

A + 固然 + 〜，但（是）／可是／也 + …

「固然」 means "admittedly / it's true that ...," first acknowledging one fact and then presenting another point of view in the second half. 「固然…但是…」 concedes and then counters (「钱固然重要，但健康更重要」), while 「固然…也…」 accepts both (「去固然好，不去也行」). It's more argumentative than 「虽然」 and is often used in essays and opinion pieces.

「固然」 is an adverb, so it goes after the subject (×「固然钱重要」 → 「钱固然重要」). Note the difference from 「虽然」, which can also go before the subject.

Example sentences

钱固然重要，但健康更重要。
qián gù rán zhòng yào， dàn jiàn kāng gèng zhòng yào。
Money is certainly important, but health is more important.
