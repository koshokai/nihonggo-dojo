「倘若・假如」: if (written style)

HSK 6

倘若／假如 + 仮定，（主語）+ 就／便 + …

「倘若」 and 「假如」 both mean "if" and are written-style versions of 「如果」. 「倘若」 in particular appears in literature, opinion pieces, and formal documents. Besides 「就」, the second half may also pair with the written-style 「便」 or 「则」. 「假如」 is also often used for suppositions contrary to reality.

「倘若・假如・如果・要是」 differ only in formality and mean almost the same thing. Use 「要是／如果」 in conversation; using 「倘若／假如」 in essays and emails makes them crisper.

Example sentences

倘若明天天气不好，活动便改期举行。
tǎng ruò míng tiān tiān qì bù hǎo， huó dòng biàn gǎi qī jǔ xíng。
If the weather is bad tomorrow, the event will be rescheduled.
