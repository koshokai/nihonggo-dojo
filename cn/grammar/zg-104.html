「未免」: a bit too ...

HSK 6

主語 + 未免 +（太／有点儿／过于）+ 形容詞（+了）

「未免」 means "rather too ... / one can't help saying ...," giving a restrained negative evaluation of a situation (「你这么说未免太过分了」 = "Saying that is going a bit too far"). It is often used with 「太」, 「有点儿」, or 「过于」, and softens direct criticism.

「未免」 = gentle criticism, 「难免」 = unavoidable. They look similar but mean different things. Adding 「太・过于」 after 「未免」 sounds natural.

Example sentences

你这样做未免太不公平了。
nǐ zhè yàng zuò wèi miǎn tài bù gōng píng le。
Doing it this way is a bit too unfair.
