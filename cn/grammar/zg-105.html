Rhetorical 「何尝」: how could it not be ...?

HSK 6

主語 + 何尝 + 不／没 + 動詞 ／ 何尝 + 動詞

「何尝」 is a written-style rhetorical question: 「何尝不…」 emphasizes the positive, "how could I not ...? (I actually do)," and 「何尝…」 emphasizes the negative, "when did I ever ...? (I didn't)." 「我何尝不想去」 means "It's not that I don't want to go (I really do)." It's used to clear up a misunderstanding or appeal to someone's feelings.

「何尝不」 = actually positive; 「何尝」 (with no negative) = actually negative. If you remember that rhetorical questions flip positive and negative, you can sort out 「难道」 and 「何必」 at the same time.

Example sentences

我何尝不想休息，可是工作太多了。
wǒ hé cháng bù xiǎng xiū xi， kě shì gōng zuò tài duō le。
Don't you think I want to rest too? But there's just too much work.
