「务必」: be sure to ...

HSK 6

（请）+ 主語 + 务必 + 動詞

「务必」 is a written-style word that makes a strong request of someone: "be sure to / by all means ..." It's often used in notices and business emails in the form 「请务必…」 ("please be sure to ..."). It's similar to 「一定」, but 「务必」 is centered on making a request of someone and has no meaning of conjecture ("must surely be").

「务必」 can't be used for conjecture such as 「他一定知道」 ("He must know"). Remember it as reserved for requests and instructions to others.

Example sentences

请务必在周五之前提交报告。
qǐng wù bì zài zhōu wǔ zhī qián tí jiāo bào gào。
Please be sure to submit your report by Friday.
