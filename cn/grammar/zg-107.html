「从而」: thereby, as a result

HSK 6

原因・方法，从而 + 結果

「从而」 is a written-style conjunction meaning "thereby / as a result," introducing the result brought about by a previous action or method. The subject is basically the same in both halves, and the second half often has a positive result such as 「提高」, 「促进」, or 「实现」. While 「因此」 is simple cause and effect, 「从而」 emphasizes the link "method → achievement."

As a rule, no new subject is put after 「从而」. In essays, the pattern 「通过…，从而…」 ("through ..., thereby ...") is handy.

Example sentences

他改进了学习方法，从而提高了成绩。
tā gǎi jìn le xué xí fāng fǎ， cóng ér tí gāo le chéng jì。
He improved his study methods and thereby raised his grades.
