「以便」: so that, in order to

HSK 6

A（行動），以便 + B（実現したい目的）

「以便」 is written style meaning "so that ... can / in order to ...," stating the purpose of the earlier action in the second half. While 「以免」 means "to avoid a bad situation," 「以便」 means "to make a good goal easier to achieve." It's used following a previous clause and isn't placed at the start of a sentence.

「以便」 = a good purpose; 「以免」 = avoiding a bad result. 「以便迟到」 is × ("in order to be late"?) → 「以免迟到」. Choose based on whether what follows is good or bad.

Example sentences

请留下电话号码，以便我们联系您。
qǐng liú xià diàn huà hào mǎ， yǐ biàn wǒ men lián xì nín。
Please leave your phone number so that we can contact you.
