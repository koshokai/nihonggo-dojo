「与…相比」: compared with (written style)

HSK 6

与／跟 + A + 相比，B + …

「与…相比」 is a written-style comparison meaning "compared with ...," placed at the start of a sentence to show the basis of comparison (「与去年相比，…」). The second half often has 「更」, 「比较」, or words showing an increase or decrease. In speech, people say 「和／跟…比起来」. It's a standard expression in reports and the news.

The 「与其」 in 「与其他方法相比」 has nothing to do with 「与其…不如」; it's 「与」 + 「其他」 ("other"). Be careful not to misread where the words break.

Example sentences

与去年相比，今年的游客增加了百分之二十。
yǔ qù nián xiāng bǐ， jīn nián de yóu kè zēng jiā le bǎi fēn zhī èr shí。
Compared with last year, the number of tourists this year rose by 20%.
