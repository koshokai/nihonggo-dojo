「莫非」: could it be that ...?

HSK 6

莫非 + 推測（+不成／吗）？

「莫非」 is written style used to guess at an unexpected possibility: "could it be that ...?" It's similar to 「难道」, but while 「难道」 presses rhetorically, "surely it can't be ...," 「莫非」 has a stronger sense of guessing, "could it be ...?" 「不成」 or 「吗」 is sometimes added at the end.

「莫非」 = "could it be ...?" (a guess); 「难道」 = "surely you don't mean ...?" (rhetorical, pressing). They often appear in dialogue in novels and dramas.

Example sentences

他今天没来，莫非生病了？
tā jīn tiān méi lái， mò fēi shēng bìng le？
He didn't come today; could he be sick?
