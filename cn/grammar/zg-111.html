「可见」: it can be seen that ...

HSK 6

根拠・事実，（由此）可见 + 結論

「可见」 means "(from this) it can be seen that ...," drawing a conclusion from facts or examples mentioned before. In essays and opinion pieces, summing up with 「由此可见」 ("from this we can see") after concrete examples is standard. It is followed by the speaker's judgment or conclusion.

「可见」 doesn't mean "can be seen (with the eyes)"; it means "it's clear that ..." Ending an essay with 「由此可见，…」 really tightens the logic.

Example sentences

他每天都练习到很晚，可见他有多么认真。
tā měi tiān dōu liàn xí dào hěn wǎn， kě jiàn tā yǒu duō me rèn zhēn。
He practices until late every day, which shows how serious he is.
