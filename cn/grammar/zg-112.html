Numbers (「十」, 「百」, 「千」, 「零」)

HSK 1

数字 + 十／百／千／万 ／ 間の0は「零」

Chinese numbers are built very regularly, unit by unit (「三十五」 = three-ten-five, 「六百」, 「两千」). However, the "one" before 「百」 and 「千」 is not dropped: say 「一百」 and 「一千」. Also, when there is a zero in the middle, insert 「零」: 「一百零五」 (105). "200" is 「两百」 or 「二百」, and "2000" is usually 「两千」.

×「百块」→ 「一百块」. Just as English says "one hundred," not just "hundred," keep the 「一」; dropping it makes you hard to understand. "105" is 「一百零五」, not 「一百五」 (「一百五」 means 150).

Example sentences

我们班有三十五个学生。
wǒ men bān yǒu sān shí wǔ ge xué sheng。
There are 35 students in our class.
这本书一百块钱。
zhè běn shū yì bǎi kuài qián。
This book costs 100 yuan.
我的房间是一百零八号。
wǒ de fáng jiān shì yì bǎi líng bā hào。
My room is number 108.
学校里有两千多个学生。
xué xiào lǐ yǒu liǎng qiān duō ge xué sheng。
There are over 2,000 students at the school.
