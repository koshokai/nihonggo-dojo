Noun predicates (「今天星期三」, 「我二十岁」)

HSK 1

主語 + 名詞（日付・曜日・年齢・値段など）／ 否定：主語 + 不是 + 名詞

For dates, days of the week, times, ages, prices, hometowns and so on, a noun can serve as the predicate on its own, without 「是」 (「今天星期三」 = Today is Wednesday). It's as if English could simply say "Today Wednesday." The negative, however, needs 「是」: 「今天不是星期三」.

×「今天不星期五。」→ The negative must use 「不是」: 「今天不是星期五」. In the affirmative, 「是」 is optional (「今天是星期五」).

Example sentences

今天星期五。
jīn tiān xīng qī wǔ。
Today is Friday.
我女儿六岁。
wǒ nǚ ér liù suì。
My daughter is six.
现在八点。
xiàn zài bā diǎn。
It's eight o'clock now.
明天不是星期天。
míng tiān bú shì xīng qī tiān。
Tomorrow isn't Sunday.
