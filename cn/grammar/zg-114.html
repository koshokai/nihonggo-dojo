Dates and days of the week

HSK 1

年 + 月 + 号（日）+ 星期 + 数字 ／ 几月几号？星期几？

Dates go from the largest unit to the smallest: year, month, day (「三月五号」 = March 5). In speech, 「号」 is often used instead of 「日」 for the day of the month. Days of the week are 「星期」 + a number: Monday is 「星期一」 and Saturday 「星期六」; only Sunday is different, 「星期天」 or 「星期日」. To ask, replace the number with 「几」: 「几月几号？」, 「星期几？」.

There is no 「星期七」. Sunday is 「星期天」. Also note that the Japanese word 「月曜」 for "Monday" is not understood in Chinese.

Example sentences

今天几月几号？
jīn tiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào？
What's the date today?
我的生日是十月二十号。
wǒ de shēng rì shì shí yuè èr shí hào。
My birthday is October 20.
明天星期几？
míng tiān xīng qī jǐ？
What day of the week is it tomorrow?
我们星期六去看电影吧。
wǒ men xīng qī liù qù kàn diàn yǐng ba。
Let's go see a movie on Saturday.
