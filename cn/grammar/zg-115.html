Telling time (「点」, 「分」, 「半」)

HSK 1

数字 + 点（+ 数字 + 分）／ 数字 + 点半 ／ 差 + 数字 + 分 + 数字 + 点

Clock times are expressed as ...「点」 (o'clock) ...「分」 (minutes): 「八点十分」 = 8:10. "Half past" is 「半」, as in 「八点半」 (8:30), and 15 minutes can also be 「一刻」 (a quarter). "2 o'clock" is 「两点」. The rule is that times go before the verb: "I get up at 7" is 「我七点起床」 (×「我起床七点」).

×「二点」→ 「两点」. Only "2 o'clock" uses 「两」. Times go before the verb: ×「我睡觉十一点」→ 「我十一点睡觉」.

Example sentences

现在几点？
xiàn zài jǐ diǎn？
What time is it now?
我每天早上七点起床。
wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang qī diǎn qǐ chuáng。
I get up at 7 every morning.
电影两点半开始。
diàn yǐng liǎng diǎn bàn kāi shǐ。
The movie starts at 2:30.
我们十二点一刻吃饭。
wǒ men shí èr diǎn yí kè chī fàn。
We eat at a quarter past twelve.
