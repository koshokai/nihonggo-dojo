「太…了」: too ..., so ...

HSK 1

太 + 形容詞・心理動詞 + 了

「太…了」 expresses a very high degree and is used both for "too ..." (a complaint) and "so ..." (an exclamation): 「太贵了」 = too expensive, 「太好了」 = Great! Context tells you which meaning is intended. As a rule, the final 「了」 is not left out. The negative 「不太」 + adjective means "not very ...," a softened negative.

×「太贵。」→ It's natural to add 「了」: 「太贵了」. 「不太」 means "not very ...," which is different from 「太不」 (extremely not ...).

Example sentences

这件衣服太贵了。
zhè jiàn yī fu tài guì le。
This piece of clothing is too expensive.
太好了！明天不上课。
tài hǎo le！ míng tiān bú shàng kè。
Great! No class tomorrow.
今天太热了。
jīn tiān tài rè le。
It's too hot today.
我不太喜欢喝咖啡。
wǒ bú tài xǐ huan hē kā fēi。
I don't really like coffee.
