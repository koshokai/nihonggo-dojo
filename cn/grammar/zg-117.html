「和」: and (joining nouns)

HSK 1

A + 和 + B ／ 主語 + 和 + 人 + 一起 + 動詞

「和」 is a conjunction that joins nouns and pronouns, as in "A and B." With three or more items, put it between the last two (「苹果、香蕉和西瓜」). Unlike English "and," it basically can't join clauses or verb phrases. For "do ... and (also) ...," don't use 「和」; just put the phrases side by side or use words like 「还」 or 「也」.

×「我吃饭和看电视。」→ 「和」 doesn't readily join verb phrases, so say something like 「我吃饭，还看电视」. Learn "together with ..." as the set pattern 「和…一起」.

Example sentences

我和我朋友都是学生。
wǒ hé wǒ péng you dōu shì xué sheng。
My friend and I are both students.
我喜欢猫和狗。
wǒ xǐ huan māo hé gǒu。
I like cats and dogs.
桌子上有书、杯子和电脑。
zhuō zi shàng yǒu shū、 bēi zi hé diàn nǎo。
There's a book, a cup and a computer on the desk.
我和妈妈一起去商店。
wǒ hé mā ma yì qǐ qù shāng diàn。
I'm going to the store with my mom.
