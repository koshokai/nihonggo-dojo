「叫」: to be called (names)

HSK 1

主語 + 叫 + 名前 ／ 主語 + 姓 + 名字（姓）

「叫」 means "to be named ..." and is used for full names or given names. To give only your surname, use 「姓」: 「我姓王」 (My surname is Wang). To ask someone's name, say 「你叫什么名字？」; to ask for a surname politely, say 「您贵姓？」.

×「我姓田中明。」→ Full names take 「叫」; only surnames take 「姓」. Answering 「您贵姓？」 with 「我贵姓…」 is wrong; answer 「我姓…」.

Example sentences

你叫什么名字？
nǐ jiào shén me míng zi？
What's your name?
我叫田中明。
wǒ jiào tián zhōng míng。
My name is Tanaka Akira.
您贵姓？——我姓李。
nín guì xìng？—— wǒ xìng lǐ。
May I ask your surname? — It's Li.
这只小狗叫什么？
zhè zhī xiǎo gǒu jiào shén me？
What's this puppy's name?
