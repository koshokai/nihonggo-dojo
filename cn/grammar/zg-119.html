「请」: please (do ...)

HSK 1

请 +（相手）+ 動詞（+目的語）

「请」 goes at the start of a sentence and makes a polite request or invitation: "please (do ...)" (「请坐」 = Please have a seat). Like English "please," it comes before the verb. It is also a verb meaning "to treat (someone to), to invite," as in 「我请你吃饭」.

×「坐请。」→ 「请」 always goes before the verb. 「请问」 is a set phrase meaning "Excuse me, may I ask ...," used to introduce a question.

Example sentences

请进！
qǐng jìn！
Please come in!
请喝茶。
qǐng hē chá。
Please have some tea.
请你再说一遍。
qǐng nǐ zài shuō yí biàn。
Please say that again.
今天我请你吃饭。
jīn tiān wǒ qǐng nǐ chī fàn。
It's my treat today.
