「喜欢」: to like (something, doing something)

HSK 1

主語 + 喜欢 + 名詞 ／ 主語 + 喜欢 + 動詞（+目的語）

「喜欢」 is a verb meaning "to like," and it can be followed directly by a noun or a verb phrase (「喜欢猫」, 「喜欢看书」). Nothing needs to be added to the verb the way English adds "-ing" or "to" (like reading, like to read). To show degree, use 「很喜欢」 or 「非常喜欢」; the negative is 「不喜欢」.

×「我喜欢的看书。」→ Follow it directly with the verb: 「我喜欢看书」. "Like" is a verb, not an adjective, so 「是」 is not needed either (×「我是喜欢」).

Example sentences

我很喜欢中国菜。
wǒ hěn xǐ huan zhōng guó cài。
I really like Chinese food.
她喜欢看电影。
tā xǐ huan kàn diàn yǐng。
She likes watching movies.
你喜欢什么颜色？
nǐ xǐ huan shén me yán sè？
What color do you like?
我爸爸不喜欢喝茶。
wǒ bà ba bù xǐ huan hē chá。
My dad doesn't like drinking tea.
