「怎么」: how (method)

HSK 1

主語 + 怎么 + 動詞（+目的語）？

「怎么」 + verb asks how to do something (「怎么去」 = how do you get there?, 「怎么写」 = how do you write it?). It is a question word, so no 「吗」 goes at the end. 「怎么」 can also mean "why, how come" (expressing surprise or dissatisfaction), as in 「你怎么不来？」 (How come you're not coming?).

×「你怎么去吗？」→ There is a question word, so no 「吗」. Distinguish 「怎么」 + verb (method, "how to") from 「怎么样」 ("How is it?", asking for an impression).

Example sentences

去火车站怎么走？
qù huǒ chē zhàn zěn me zǒu？
How do I get to the train station?
这个字怎么写？
zhè ge zì zěn me xiě？
How do you write this character?
你每天怎么来学校？
nǐ měi tiān zěn me lái xué xiào？
How do you get to school every day?
你怎么还没睡觉？
nǐ zěn me hái méi shuì jiào？
How come you're still not asleep?
