「怎么样」: How is ...? How about ...?

HSK 1

名詞 + 怎么样？ ／ 提案の文，怎么样？

「怎么样」 asks "How is it?" about a state or someone's impression (「天气怎么样？」 = How's the weather?). Added after a suggestion, it asks for the other person's opinion: "How about ...?" (「我们去吃饭，怎么样？」). It doesn't take 「吗」. Typical answers are 「很好」, 「不错」 and 「不太好」.

「怎么」 asks about method (how to), 「怎么样」 about a state or impression (how is it?). Keep them apart: ×「怎么样去？」→ 「怎么去？」

Example sentences

今天天气怎么样？
jīn tiān tiān qì zěn me yàng？
How's the weather today?
你的新工作怎么样？
nǐ de xīn gōng zuò zěn me yàng？
How's your new job?
我们明天去爬山，怎么样？
wǒ men míng tiān qù pá shān， zěn me yàng？
Let's go hiking tomorrow. How about it?
这个菜怎么样？——很好吃。
zhè ge cài zěn me yàng？—— hěn hǎo chī。
How's this dish? — It's delicious.
