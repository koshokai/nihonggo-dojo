Location words (「上」, 「里」, 「下」, 「前」, 「后」)

HSK 1

名詞 + 上／里／下／前（边）／后（边）／旁边

To express a place such as "on the desk" or "in the bag," Chinese adds a location word (「上」, 「里」, 「下」, 「前边」, 「后边」, 「旁边」) after the noun. Nothing like English "on" or "in" goes before the noun, and no linking word is needed: 「桌子上」 (desk + on = on the desk), 「书包里」 (bag + in = in the bag). Note that a noun for an object, such as 「桌子」, can't be used as a place without a location word.

×「手机在桌子。」→ Nouns for objects need a location word: 「在桌子上」. However, nouns that are already places, such as 「北京」 and 「学校」, don't need one (「在北京」).

Example sentences

你的手机在桌子上。
nǐ de shǒu jī zài zhuō zi shàng。
Your phone is on the desk.
书包里有一本书。
shū bāo lǐ yǒu yì běn shū。
There's a book in the bag.
椅子下有一只猫。
yǐ zi xià yǒu yì zhī māo。
There's a cat under the chair.
学校前边有一个商店。
xué xiào qián biān yǒu yí ge shāng diàn。
There's a store in front of the school.
