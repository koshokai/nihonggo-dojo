「多」 + adjective: How ...?

HSK 1

主語 + 多 + 大／高／长／远 …？

「多」 + adjective asks about degree: "how (old, tall, far) ...?" Age is 「多大？」, height 「多高？」, distance 「多远？」, and length or duration 「多长（时间）？」. How you ask about age depends on the person: 「几岁？」 for children, 「多大？」 for people your own age and adults, and 「多大年纪？」 for elderly people.

Asking an adult 「几岁？」 treats them like a child and is rude. You can also add 「了」 after 「多大」: 「多大了？」.

Example sentences

你今年多大？
nǐ jīn nián duō dà？
How old are you?
你哥哥多高？
nǐ gē ge duō gāo？
How tall is your older brother?
你家离这儿多远？
nǐ jiā lí zhèr duō yuǎn？
How far is your home from here?
你学汉语多长时间了？
nǐ xué hàn yǔ duō cháng shí jiān le？
How long have you been studying Chinese?
