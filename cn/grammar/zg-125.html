Checking and suggesting: 「…，好吗？」, 「…，是不是？」

HSK 1

文，好吗？／ 文，对吗？／ 文，是不是？

Adding 「好吗？」 after a sentence makes a soft suggestion or request ("..., OK?" or "Let's ..., shall we?"), while adding 「对吗？」 or 「是不是？」 checks a fact ("..., right?"). They work much like English tag questions such as "..., right?" and "..., isn't it?" 「是不是」 can also go inside the sentence: 「你是不是累了？」 (You're tired, aren't you?).

「好吗？」 is for suggestions and requests, 「对吗？」 and 「是不是？」 for confirming facts. ×「你是日本人，好吗？」 sounds like "Would you be Japanese for me, please?"

Example sentences

我们一起去，好吗？
wǒ men yì qǐ qù， hǎo ma？
Let's go together, OK?
你是日本人，对吗？
nǐ shì rì běn rén， duì ma？
You're Japanese, right?
你明天来，是不是？
nǐ míng tiān lái， shì bu shì？
You're coming tomorrow, aren't you?
你是不是不舒服？
nǐ shì bu shì bù shū fu？
Aren't you feeling well?
