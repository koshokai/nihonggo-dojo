「已经…了」: already

HSK 2

主語 + 已经 + 動詞・形容詞 +（了）

「已经」 means "already," showing that an action has been completed or a change has already taken place. It usually comes with 「了」 at the end (「他已经走了」 = He's already left). It also combines with quantities and times: 「已经十点了」 (It's already 10 o'clock). The negative is 「还没（有）…（呢）」, without 「已经」.

×「我已经没吃。」→ "Haven't ... yet" is 「我还没吃呢」. Don't forget the final 「了」 in sentences with 「已经」.

Example sentences

我已经吃饭了。
wǒ yǐ jīng chī fàn le。
I've already eaten.
天已经黑了。
tiān yǐ jīng hēi le。
It's already dark.
已经十一点了，快睡觉吧。
yǐ jīng shí yī diǎn le， kuài shuì jiào ba。
It's already eleven. Hurry up and go to bed.
他已经在这儿工作三年了。
tā yǐ jīng zài zhèr gōng zuò sān nián le。
He's been working here for three years now.
