「还没（有）…呢」: haven't ... yet

HSK 2

主語 + 还没（有）+ 動詞（+目的語）+（呢）

「还没（有）」 expresses "haven't ... yet." Adding 「呢」 at the end conveys "not yet (but I will)" and sounds natural. Don't let English "not yet" tempt you into using 「不」. Since the action hasn't been completed, the final 「了」 isn't used either.

×「我还没吃了。」→ The action isn't complete, so no 「了」. ×「我还不吃」 means "I'm not going to eat yet," expressing intention.

Example sentences

我还没吃早饭呢。
wǒ hái méi chī zǎo fàn ne。
I haven't had breakfast yet.
他还没来呢。
tā hái méi lái ne。
He hasn't come yet.
作业你写完了吗？——还没有。
zuò yè nǐ xiě wán le ma？—— hái méi yǒu。
Have you finished your homework? — Not yet.
电影还没开始呢。
diàn yǐng hái méi kāi shǐ ne。
The movie hasn't started yet.
