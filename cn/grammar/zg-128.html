「别…（了）」/「不要…」: don't ... (prohibition)

HSK 2

别／不要 + 動詞（+目的語）（+了）

「别」 and 「不要」 go before a verb to forbid or stop an action: "don't ...." 「别」 is shorter and more colloquial and is common in everyday conversation. Adding 「了」 at the end, 「别…了」, means "stop (what you're doing now)" (「别哭了」 = Stop crying).

「别…了」 means "stop doing what you're already doing." For warnings about the future, there are set expressions without that meaning, such as 「别忘了带伞」 (Don't forget your umbrella).

Example sentences

别说话！
bié shuō huà！
Stop talking!
别哭了，没关系。
bié kū le， méi guān xi。
Don't cry, it's OK.
上课的时候不要玩手机。
shàng kè de shí hou bú yào wán shǒu jī。
Don't play on your phone in class.
太晚了，别看电视了。
tài wǎn le， bié kàn diàn shì le。
It's late. Stop watching TV.
