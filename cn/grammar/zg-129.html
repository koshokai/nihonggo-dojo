「给」: for, to (doing something for someone)

HSK 2

主語 + 给 + 人 + 動詞（+目的語）

As a preposition, 「给」 means "to, for," marking the person who receives the action (「给妈妈打电话」 = call Mom). In English the recipient comes after the verb ("call Mom," "write to a friend"), but in Chinese 「给」 + person goes before the verb. 「给」 is also a verb meaning "to give" (「我给你一本书」).

×「我打电话给你。」 is also said in some dialects, but learn the word order 「我给你打电话」 first. 「给」 + person normally goes before the verb.

Example sentences

我晚上给你打电话。
wǒ wǎn shang gěi nǐ dǎ diàn huà。
I'll call you tonight.
妈妈给我做了一个蛋糕。
mā ma gěi wǒ zuò le yí ge dàn gāo。
Mom made me a cake.
请给我看一下。
qǐng gěi wǒ kàn yí xià。
Please let me have a look.
我给他介绍了一个朋友。
wǒ gěi tā jiè shào le yí ge péng you。
I introduced a friend to him.
