「从…到…」: from ... to ...

HSK 2

从 + 起点（時間・場所）+ 到 + 終点（+ 動詞）

「从A到B」 means "from A to B," expressing a range of time or place (「从九点到五点」 = from 9 to 5). With a verb, the 「从…到…」 phrase normally goes before the verb. 「从」 alone can also mark a starting point, "from" (「我从日本来」).

×「我工作从九点到五点。」→ 「从…到…」 goes before the verb. "Come from ..." is 「从」 + place + 「来」; unlike English "come from Beijing," the place comes before the verb.

Example sentences

我从九点到五点工作。
wǒ cóng jiǔ diǎn dào wǔ diǎn gōng zuò。
I work from 9 to 5.
从这儿到车站要走十分钟。
cóng zhèr dào chē zhàn yào zǒu shí fēn zhōng。
It's a ten-minute walk from here to the station.
我们从星期一到星期五上课。
wǒ men cóng xīng qī yī dào xīng qī wǔ shàng kè。
We have classes from Monday to Friday.
他从北京来。
tā cóng běi jīng lái。
He's from Beijing.
