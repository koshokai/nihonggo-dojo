「离」: from (distance, time gap)

HSK 2

A + 离 + B + 远／近／（时间）

「离」 expresses the distance or time between two points, as in 「A离B很近」 (A is close to B). English uses "from" for both, but Chinese uses 「从」 for the starting point of movement and 「离」 for the reference point of distance. It also works for time, as in 「离考试还有三天」 (three more days until the exam).

×「我家从学校很近。」→ Distance takes 「离」. ×「我离日本来。」→ The starting point of movement takes 「从」: 「我从日本来」.

Example sentences

我家离学校很近。
wǒ jiā lí xué xiào hěn jìn。
My home is close to school.
机场离这儿远吗？
jī chǎng lí zhèr yuǎn ma？
Is the airport far from here?
离考试还有一个星期。
lí kǎo shì hái yǒu yí ge xīng qī。
There's one more week until the exam.
医院离公司不太远。
yī yuàn lí gōng sī bú tài yuǎn。
The hospital isn't very far from the office.
