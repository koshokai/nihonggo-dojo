「跟」/「和…一起」: together with ...

HSK 2

主語 + 跟／和 + 人 + 一起 + 動詞

"Do ... together with someone" is expressed in the order 「跟」 (or 「和」) + person + 「一起」 + verb. Unlike English, where "with someone" usually comes after the verb, "with whom" and "together" both go before the verb. 「跟」 is common in speech. When the subject is plural, as in 「我们一起去吧」, 「一起」 can be used alone without 「跟…」.

×「我一起跟朋友去。」→ 「跟」 + person comes first, then 「一起」. ×「我去一起」→ 「一起」 also goes before the verb.

Example sentences

我跟朋友一起去旅游。
wǒ gēn péng you yì qǐ qù lǚ yóu。
I'm going on a trip with friends.
你想跟我一起吃饭吗？
nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yì qǐ chī fàn ma？
Would you like to have dinner with me?
周末我和家人一起看电影。
zhōu mò wǒ hé jiā rén yì qǐ kàn diàn yǐng。
On weekends I watch movies with my family.
我们一起唱歌吧。
wǒ men yì qǐ chàng gē ba。
Let's sing together.
