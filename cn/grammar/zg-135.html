「每…都…」: every ..., each ...

HSK 2

每 +（数詞）+ 量詞 + 名詞 + 都 + 動詞

「每」 means "every, each" and must be followed by a measure word (「每个人」, 「每本书」). However, words like 「天」 and 「年」 attach directly: 「每天」, 「每年」. In 「每…」 sentences it is natural to put 「都」 before the verb, which reinforces the meaning "all, without exception."

×「每人学生」→ Insert the measure word: 「每个学生」. Leaving out 「都」 often sounds unnatural, so learn 「每…都」 as a set.

Example sentences

我每天都喝牛奶。
wǒ měi tiān dōu hē niú nǎi。
I drink milk every day.
每个人都有自己的爱好。
měi ge rén dōu yǒu zì jǐ de ài hào。
Everyone has their own hobbies.
他每个星期都去游泳。
tā měi ge xīng qī dōu qù yóu yǒng。
He goes swimming every week.
这里每本书都很有意思。
zhè lǐ měi běn shū dōu hěn yǒu yì si。
Every book here is interesting.
