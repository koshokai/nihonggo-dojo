「…的时候」: when ...

HSK 2

動詞句・文 + 的时候，…

「…的时候」 means "when (someone does/did) ..." and goes before the main clause (「我吃饭的时候」 = when I eat). Unlike English, where a "when" clause can come either before or after the main clause, it always comes first. The form is the same for past and future; the tense is judged from context or from 「了」.

×「当我吃饭时候」→ Don't forget 「的」: 「吃饭的时候」. The "when" part goes in the first half of the sentence; putting it at the end is unnatural.

Example sentences

我小的时候住在上海。
wǒ xiǎo de shí hou zhù zài shàng hǎi。
When I was little, I lived in Shanghai.
上课的时候请不要说话。
shàng kè de shí hou qǐng bú yào shuō huà。
Please don't talk during class.
你有时间的时候来我家玩儿吧。
nǐ yǒu shí jiān de shí hou lái wǒ jiā wánr ba。
Come over to my place when you have time.
我到家的时候，他已经睡了。
wǒ dào jiā de shí hou， tā yǐ jīng shuì le。
When I got home, he was already asleep.
