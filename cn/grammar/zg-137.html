「…以前」/「以后」: before ..., after ...

HSK 2

動詞句・時間 + 以前（之前）／以后（之后），…

「以前」 and 「以后」 go after a verb phrase or a time expression to mean "before ..." and "after ..." (「吃饭以前」 = before eating, 「三天以后」 = three days later). Unlike English "before" and "after," they come at the end of the phrase. Placed alone at the start of a sentence, they mean 「以前」 (in the past) and 「以后」 (from now on).

×「以前吃饭」→ "Before eating" is 「吃饭以前」, with 「以前」 placed after. 「以前」 at the start of a sentence means "in the past," so watch the word order.

Example sentences

吃饭以前要洗手。
chī fàn yǐ qián yào xǐ shǒu。
You should wash your hands before you eat.
下课以后我们去打球吧。
xià kè yǐ hòu wǒ men qù dǎ qiú ba。
Let's go play ball after class.
一个星期以后我回国。
yí ge xīng qī yǐ hòu wǒ huí guó。
I'm going back to my country in a week.
以前我不喜欢吃鱼。
yǐ qián wǒ bù xǐ huan chī yú。
I didn't use to like fish.
