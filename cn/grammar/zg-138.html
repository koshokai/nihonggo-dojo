「先…然后／再…」: first ..., then ...

HSK 3

主語 + 先 + 動詞A，然后／再 + 動詞B

To show the order of actions, combine 「先」 (first) with 「然后」 (then) or 「再」 (after that). 「先」 and 「再」 are adverbs, so they go after the subject and before the verb, while 「然后」 is a conjunction and goes at the start of the second clause. For three or more steps, you can continue with 「先…，然后…，最后…」.

×「我们然后去看电影。」→ 「然后」 only works when a previous action has been mentioned. ×「先我吃饭」→ 「先」 goes after the subject (「我先吃饭」).

Example sentences

我们先吃饭，然后去看电影。
wǒ men xiān chī fàn， rán hòu qù kàn diàn yǐng。
Let's eat first and then go see a movie.
