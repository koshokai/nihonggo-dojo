「一点儿也不…」: not ... at all

HSK 3

一点儿 + 也／都 + 不／没 + 形容詞・動詞

「一点儿也不…」 is a strong negative: "not ... at all, not the least bit ..." (「一点儿也不累」 = not tired at all). 「都」 can be used instead of 「也」. For past actions, use 「一点儿也没…」. It is close to English "not one bit" and is common in conversation.

×「一点儿不累」→ Don't forget 「也」 or 「都」. Its meaning is the opposite of 「有点儿」 (a bit ..., expressing dissatisfaction), so don't confuse the two.

Example sentences

这个菜一点儿也不辣。
zhè ge cài yì diǎnr yě bú là。
This dish isn't spicy at all.
