「地」: adverbial modifier (-ly)

HSK 3

形容詞・副詞的な語 + 地 + 動詞

「地」 (「de」) follows a word that modifies a verb, meaning "do ... in a ... way" (「认真地学习」 = study seriously). It works much like English "-ly." 「的」, pronounced the same, goes before nouns, and 「得」 goes after verbs (degree complement); they are distinguished by position. It is often attached to two-syllable adjectives and reduplicated forms.

「的」 + noun, 「地」 + verb, verb + 「得」. ×「认真的学习」 is often considered wrong in writing, so remember: before a verb, use 「地」.

Example sentences

他认真地听老师讲课。
tā rèn zhēn de tīng lǎo shī jiǎng kè。
He's listening attentively to the teacher's lecture.
