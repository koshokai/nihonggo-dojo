「又A又B」: both A and B

HSK 3

又 + 形容詞・動詞A + 又 + 形容詞・動詞B

「又A又B」 expresses two qualities or states existing at the same time (「又便宜又好吃」 = cheap and tasty). As a rule, A and B point in the same direction, both positive or both negative. It is close to English "both ... and ...."

Pairing a positive with a negative, as in ×「又便宜又难吃」, is unnatural. Opposing qualities are expressed with 「虽然便宜，但是不好吃」.

Example sentences

这家饭馆的菜又便宜又好吃。
zhè jiā fàn guǎn de cài yòu pián yi yòu hǎo chī。
The food at this restaurant is cheap and tasty.
