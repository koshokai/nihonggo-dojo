「像…一样」: like ..., as ... as ...

HSK 3

A + 像 + B + 一样（+形容詞）／ 像 + B + 一样 + 動詞

「像…一样」 expresses figurative comparison or similarity: "A is like B," "do ... like B" (「像花一样漂亮」 = as beautiful as a flower). While 「跟…一样」 is a simple comparison of sameness, 「像」 carries the nuance of resemblance, "if you were to compare it to something." The negative is 「不像」.

「似的」 can be used instead of 「一样」 (「像孩子似的」). ×「他一样像他爸爸」→ B is sandwiched inside 「像…一样」.

Example sentences

她的脸红得像苹果一样。
tā de liǎn hóng de xiàng píng guǒ yí yàng。
Her face turned as red as an apple.
