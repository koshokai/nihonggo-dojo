「对…感兴趣」: be interested in ...

HSK 3

主語 + 对 + 対象 + 感兴趣 ／ 对…有兴趣

"Be interested in ..." is 「对」 + object of interest + 「感兴趣」, with the object brought forward by 「对」. Don't follow English "interested in ..." and put the object after 「感兴趣」. 「对…有兴趣」 and 「对…没（有）兴趣」 have the same meaning, and degree is expressed as 「很感兴趣」 or 「非常感兴趣」.

×「我感兴趣中国文化。」→ Bring the object forward with 「对」: 「我对中国文化感兴趣」. The negative is 「不感兴趣」 or 「没有兴趣」.

Example sentences

我对中国历史很感兴趣。
wǒ duì zhōng guó lì shǐ hěn gǎn xìng qù。
I'm very interested in Chinese history.
