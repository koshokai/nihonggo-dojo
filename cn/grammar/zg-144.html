「还是…吧」: had better ..., ... after all

HSK 3

（主語）+ 还是 + 動詞句 + 吧

「还是…吧」 suggests or decides, after weighing the options, that "(after all) it's better to ..." (「还是坐地铁吧」 = Let's take the subway after all). This is a different use from the question 「A还是B」 (A or B). It is similar to English "Let's ... after all" or "We'd better ...."

In a question, 「还是」 means "or"; in a statement with 「吧」, it means "(after all) it's better to ...." The 「吧」 at the end is the key.

Example sentences

外边下雨了，我们还是在家吧。
wài biān xià yǔ le， wǒ men hái shì zài jiā ba。
It's raining outside, so let's just stay home.
