「刚」 and 「刚才」: just, a moment ago

HSK 3

主語 + 刚 + 動詞（+時間）／ 刚才 + 主語 + 動詞（+了）

Both mean "just now," but 「刚」 is an adverb that can only go before a verb and expresses the speaker's sense of "have just done ..." (「我刚到」 = I just arrived). 「刚才」 is a time noun: it can go before the subject and can modify nouns, as in 「刚才的事」. Sentences with 「刚」 usually don't take 「了」 at the end, but 「刚才」 can be used with 「了」.

×「我刚到了。」→ 「刚」 sentences usually don't take 「了」 (「我刚到」). ×「刚的事」→ 「刚才的事」. 「刚」 can also be used for longer periods, as in "I came here just a year ago."

Example sentences

我刚到家。
wǒ gāng dào jiā。
I just got home.
