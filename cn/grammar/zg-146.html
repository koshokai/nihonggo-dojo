「尽管…还是／但是…」: despite ..., even though ...

HSK 4

尽管 + 事実，（但是／可是）+ 主語 + 还是／也／却 + …

「尽管」 means "despite ..., even though ...": it acknowledges something that is already true and says the result in the second half happens anyway. It is almost the same as 「虽然」, but somewhat more literary, with a stronger sense of concession. Unlike the hypothetical 「即使」 (even if), what follows 「尽管」 is an actual fact.

「尽管」 = fact, 「即使」 = hypothesis. ×「尽管明天下雨，我也去」 (tomorrow's rain isn't a fact yet) → 「即使明天下雨，我也去」.

Example sentences

尽管下着大雨，他还是来了。
jǐn guǎn xià zhe dà yǔ， tā hái shì lái le。
Even though it was pouring, he still came.
