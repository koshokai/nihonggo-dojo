「于是」: so, and then

HSK 4

出来事A，于是 +（主語）+ 出来事B

「于是」 expresses that a next action or result followed, prompted by the previous event: "so, and then." It is often used in stories and accounts of events, and the second half is a past fact. While 「所以」 shows a logical link between cause and effect, 「于是」 shows a connection that follows the flow of time: "and so, following that." It isn't used for commands or future plans.

×「明天下雨，于是你别去了。」→ For the future or commands, use 「所以」 or 「那就」. 「于是」 means "so (actually) did ...."

Example sentences

天突然下雨了，于是我们回家了。
tiān tū rán xià yǔ le， yú shì wǒ men huí jiā le。
It suddenly started raining, so we went home.
