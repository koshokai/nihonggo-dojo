「既…又／也…」: both ... and ...

HSK 4

主語 + 既 + A + 又／也 + B

「既A又B」 expresses having two qualities or circumstances at the same time (「既聪明又努力」 = both smart and hardworking). It is more literary than 「又A又B」, and A and B should point in the same direction (both positive, for example). With 「既A也B」, the sense of adding B, "A, and B as well," is stronger.

This is a different word from 「既然」 (since). 「既」 is picked up with 「又」 or 「也」. Opposites aren't paired, as in ×「既漂亮但是贵」.

Example sentences

他既是我的老师，又是我的朋友。
tā jì shì wǒ de lǎo shī， yòu shì wǒ de péng you。
He's both my teacher and my friend.
