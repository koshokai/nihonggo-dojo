「不是A就是B」: if not A, then B (either A or B)

HSK 4

不是 + A，就是 + B

「不是A就是B」 means "if it's not A, it's B": one or the other of two things. It is often used with a note of complaint, "always doing either A or B" (「他周末不是睡觉就是玩游戏」). Be careful: it means something entirely different from 「不是A而是B」 (not A but B).

「不是A就是B」 = either A or B; 「不是A而是B」 = not A but B (a correction). The single character 「就」 or 「而」 changes the meaning.

Example sentences

他周末不是睡觉就是玩游戏。
tā zhōu mò bú shì shuì jiào jiù shì wán yóu xì。
On weekends he's either sleeping or playing video games.
