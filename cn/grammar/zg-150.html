「以为」 and 「认为」: (wrongly) assumed, think

HSK 4

主語 + 以为 + 内容（事実と違う）／ 主語 + 认为 + 意見

Both mean "think ...," but 「以为」 has a strong nuance of "(wrongly) assumed ...," and is often used when it later turns out to be a misunderstanding (「我以为你走了」 = I thought you'd left). 「认为」 means "consider, think," stating a reasoned opinion or judgment. English "I thought ...," when it turned out to be wrong, usually corresponds to 「以为」.

When giving your opinion at a meeting, saying 「我以为…」 tends to sound like "I had (perhaps wrongly) assumed ...." For opinions, use 「我认为…」 or 「我觉得…」.

Example sentences

我以为今天是星期五，原来是星期四。
wǒ yǐ wéi jīn tiān shì xīng qī wǔ， yuán lái shì xīng qī sì。
I thought today was Friday, but it turns out it's Thursday.
