「对…来说」: for ..., to ...

HSK 4

对 + 人・物 + 来说，…

「对…来说」 means "for ..., as far as ... is concerned," stating a judgment or assessment from a certain standpoint (「对我来说，汉语很难」 = For me, Chinese is difficult). It normally goes at the start of the sentence. It is used much like English "for me" or "to me," and can also be 「对于…来说」.

×「对我说，…」→ Without 「来」, it means "say to me." Be sure to say the whole thing: 「对…来说」.

Example sentences

对我来说，周末是最快乐的时间。
duì wǒ lái shuō， zhōu mò shì zuì kuài lè de shí jiān。
For me, the weekend is the happiest time.
