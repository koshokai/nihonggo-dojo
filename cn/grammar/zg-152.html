「千万」: be sure to, whatever you do (don't)

HSK 4

千万 + 要／别／不要 + 動詞

「千万」 is an adverb that strengthens a request or warning to someone: "be sure to ...," "whatever you do, don't ...." It is mainly used as 「千万要…」 or 「千万别…」 and is limited to commands and requests. It can't be used to describe your own actions (×「我千万去了」).

「千万」 also has the numerical meaning "ten million," but as an adverb it only strengthens requests and prohibitions. To emphasize a statement, use 「一定」 or 「绝对」.

Example sentences

路上千万要小心。
lù shàng qiān wàn yào xiǎo xīn。
Be very careful on the way.
