「首先…其次…最后…」: first, second, finally

HSK 4

首先 + A，其次 + B，（然后 + C，）最后 + D

「首先…其次…最后…」 is used to present steps, reasons or points in order. It is useful for organizing and conveying your ideas in speeches and essays. 「首先」 goes at the start of the sentence but can also go after the subject. In conversation, 「第一…第二…」 and 「先…然后…」 are also used.

「其次」 means "second, next (in importance)." For "then" in a sequence of steps, 「然后」 is also natural. These words are handy for opening paragraphs in essays.

Example sentences

首先，我要感谢大家的帮助。
shǒu xiān， wǒ yào gǎn xiè dà jiā de bāng zhù。
First of all, I'd like to thank everyone for their help.
