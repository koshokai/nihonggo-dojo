「总之」: in short, anyway

HSK 5

具体的な内容…，总之 + まとめ・結論

「总之」 sums up several points made before and states a conclusion: "in short / anyway." It is often used to wrap up essays and speeches. In conversation, it is also used to set the details aside and state something flatly: "anyway, ..." (「总之，你别担心」).

「总之」 sums things up, so it needs specific content before it. In writing, 「总而言之」 is also used.

Example sentences

有的说好，有的说不好，总之，意见很不一样。
yǒu de shuō hǎo， yǒu de shuō bù hǎo， zǒng zhī， yì jiàn hěn bù yí yàng。
Some say it's good, some say it's not; in short, opinions vary widely.
