「毕竟」: after all

HSK 5

（主語）+ 毕竟 + 事実・本質

「毕竟」 emphasizes the essential nature of something or an undeniable fact, "after all, ... (is the case)," asking the listener to understand or accept it (「他毕竟是孩子」 = "He's still a child, after all"). It is often accompanied by a judgment such as "you should understand" or "it can't be helped."

「毕竟」 is an adverb for giving a reason, used in situations close to English "after all" or "at the end of the day." Its meaning differs from 「终于」 ("finally").

Example sentences

别怪他了，他毕竟还是个孩子。
bié guài tā le， tā bì jìng hái shì ge hái zi。
Don't blame him; he's still a child, after all.
