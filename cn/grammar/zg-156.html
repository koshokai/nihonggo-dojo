「幸亏」: luckily, thanks to

HSK 5

幸亏 + 好条件，（要不然／不然／否则 + 悪い結果）

「幸亏」 means "luckily, ... (so a bad outcome was avoided)." The second half is often followed by 「要不然・不然・否则」 ("otherwise") to show the bad outcome that was avoided. It corresponds to English "luckily," "it's a good thing ...," or "thank goodness ..."

「幸亏」 is used for a fortunate chance or condition that has already happened. Using 「才」 in the second half gives "it was only because ... that ... was possible."

Example sentences

幸亏你提醒我，要不然我就忘了。
xìng kuī nǐ tí xǐng wǒ， yào bù rán wǒ jiù wàng le。
Good thing you reminded me; otherwise I would have forgotten.
