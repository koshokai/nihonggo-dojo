「难怪」: no wonder

HSK 5

难怪 + 結果（+ 原来 + 理由）／ 理由，难怪 + 結果

「难怪」 expresses the feeling of understanding something once you know the reason: "no wonder ..." It is often used together with 「原来」 ("so that's why / so it was ..."), which gives the reason. In speech, 「怪不得」 means almost the same thing.

「难怪」 means "understanding once you know the reason." For simple surprise, use 「竟然」. Learn 「难怪」 and 「原来」 as a set.

Example sentences

难怪他这么高兴，原来考上大学了。
nán guài tā zhè me gāo xìng， yuán lái kǎo shàng dà xué le。
No wonder he's so happy; it turns out he got into college.
