「要不是」: if it weren't for ...

HSK 5

要不是 + 実際の事実，（主語）+ 就／早就 + 起こり得た結果

「要不是」 means "if it weren't (for) ...," hypothetically negating something that actually happened and describing the different outcome that could have happened (「要不是你帮忙，我就完不成了」). What follows is the actual fact, so it is stated in the affirmative, not with 「不」. It often expresses gratitude or regret.

「要是…」 ("if ...") is a simple hypothetical, while 「要不是…」 flips a fact. Be careful not to make a double negative like ×「要不是你不帮忙」.

Example sentences

要不是你帮忙，我一个人肯定完不成。
yào bú shì nǐ bāng máng， wǒ yí ge rén kěn dìng wán bù chéng。
If you hadn't helped, I definitely couldn't have finished on my own.
