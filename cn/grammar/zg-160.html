「一方面…另一方面…」: on the one hand ..., on the other hand ...

HSK 5

一方面 + A，另一方面 +（也／又）B

「一方面…另一方面…」 lays out two aspects of something, or two reasons, side by side. It is handy in essays and speeches for organizing reasons or contrasting pros and cons. The second half is often accompanied by 「也」 or 「又」.

The proper form for the second half is 「另一方面」, not a repeat of 「一方面」. In sentences giving reasons, combining it with 「是因为」 makes things neat.

Example sentences

我学汉语，一方面是因为工作需要，另一方面是因为喜欢中国文化。
wǒ xué hàn yǔ， yì fāng miàn shì yīn wèi gōng zuò xū yào， lìng yì fāng miàn shì yīn wèi xǐ huan zhōng guó wén huà。
I study Chinese partly because I need it for work and partly because I like Chinese culture.
