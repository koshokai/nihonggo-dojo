「A不如B」: A is not as good as B

HSK 5

A + 不如 + B（+ 形容詞）

「A不如B」 is a comparison meaning "A is not as good as B / B is better." With an adjective after it, it means "A is not as ... as B" (「坐公交不如坐地铁快」). The adjective is basically one with a positive meaning, and it's more formal than 「没有…那么」. It's the same 「不如」 as in 「与其…不如…」.

Avoid negative adjectives, as in ×「今天不如昨天冷」; 「今天没有昨天冷」 is natural. 「不如」 is usually used with positive words such as 「好・快・方便」.

Example sentences

我的汉语不如他好。
wǒ de hàn yǔ bù rú tā hǎo。
My Chinese isn't as good as his.
