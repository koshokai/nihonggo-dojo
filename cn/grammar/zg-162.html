「简直」: simply, practically (exaggeration)

HSK 5

主語 + 简直 +（是／像／太）+ 誇張した表現

「简直」 is an adverb that emphasizes degree with some exaggeration: "simply / practically / you could almost say ..." In patterns such as 「简直是…」, 「简直像…一样」, and 「简直太…了」, it expresses surprise, admiration, or annoyance. It feels close to English "simply," "practically," or "unbelievably."

「简直」 has the sense of saying more than the plain facts. If you just mean "very," 「非常」 is enough. Be careful, since it looks similar to 「简单」 ("simple").

Example sentences

这里的风景简直太美了！
zhè lǐ de fēng jǐng jiǎn zhí tài měi le！
The scenery here is simply gorgeous!
