「趁」: while ..., take advantage of ...

HSK 5

趁 +（着）+ 好機・条件 + 動詞

「趁」 means "while ... / taking advantage of the opportunity of ...," showing that you make good use of a condition or timing to do something (「趁热吃」 = "eat it while it's hot"). It can be followed by a noun, an adjective, or a verb phrase. It corresponds well to English "while (it's still) ..." and can also be said as 「趁着」.

What follows 「趁」 is a good opportunity or condition. "Before ... happens" can also be expressed with 「趁」 + negative (「趁他还没来」).

Example sentences

饺子趁热吃吧。
jiǎo zi chèn rè chī ba。
Eat the dumplings while they're hot.
