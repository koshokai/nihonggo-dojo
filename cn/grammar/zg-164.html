「从…来看」: judging from ...

HSK 5

从 + 観点・資料 + 来看／看来，…

「从…来看」 means "judging from (a viewpoint or data) ...," showing the basis or standpoint for a judgment. It's used as in 「从目前的情况来看」 ("judging from the current situation") and 「从这一点来看」 ("from this point of view"), followed by a judgment or conclusion. 「看来」 and 「来说」 are also used, but 「来看」 is the usual choice for stating a basis.

「对…来说」 ("for ...") is about standpoint, while 「从…来看」 ("judging from ...") is about basis or viewpoint. They're similar, but use them appropriately.

Example sentences

从目前的情况来看，问题不太严重。
cóng mù qián de qíng kuàng lái kàn， wèn tí bú tài yán zhòng。
Judging from the current situation, the problem isn't very serious.
