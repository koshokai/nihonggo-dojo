「偏偏」: of all times, just has to

HSK 5

主語 + 偏偏 + 動詞（期待と反対の事態・行動）

「偏偏」 means "unfortunately / of all times," expressing frustration that something happened contrary to one's hopes or convenience (「我要出门，偏偏下雨了」). It also means a person deliberately goes against something, "insists on ... / just has to ..." (「不让他去，他偏偏要去」). Both carry the speaker's dissatisfaction or irritation.

「偏偏」 carries the speaker's dissatisfaction, so it isn't used for happy coincidences. For a happy coincidence, use 「正好・恰好」 ("just right / as it happens").

Example sentences

我正要出门，偏偏下起雨来了。
wǒ zhèng yào chū mén， piān piān xià qǐ yǔ lái le。
Just as I was about to go out, it had to start raining.
