「不料」: unexpectedly

HSK 6

前提・予想，不料 + 予想外の結果

「不料」 is an adverb leaning toward written style, expressing that something happened contrary to one's expectations or hopes: "but unexpectedly ..." Typically the first half states a plan or expectation, and 「不料」 comes at the start of the second half. It's close to English "but unexpectedly" or "to one's surprise," and is mostly used for unwelcome developments. In speech, 「没想到」 is more common.

「不料」 basically goes in the position that links two clauses (the start of the second half). In conversation, it sounds natural to replace it with 「没想到」.

Example sentences

本来打算周末去爬山，不料下了一整天的雨。
běn lái dǎ suan zhōu mò qù pá shān， bú liào xià le yì zhěng tiān de yǔ。
I'd planned to go hiking on the weekend, but unexpectedly it rained all day.
