「势必」: is bound to

HSK 6

条件・状況，（主語）+ 势必 + 動詞句（会／要 + …）

「势必」 is a written-style adverb that logically predicts an unavoidable result from a situation: "given how things are going, ... is bound to happen." It's a stiff expression close to English "inevitably" or "is bound to," often used in opinion pieces and reports. It describes the result of circumstances, not the speaker's will, so be careful not to confuse it with 「必须」 ("must").

「势必」 means "will inevitably happen given how things are going," while 「必须」 means "must, as an obligation." ×「我势必完成作业」 → 「我必须完成作业」.

Example sentences

如果不及时处理，问题势必越来越严重。
rú guǒ bù jí shí chǔ lǐ， wèn tí shì bì yuè lái yuè yán zhòng。
If it isn't dealt with promptly, the problem is bound to get more and more serious.
