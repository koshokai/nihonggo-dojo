「动不动就…」: at the slightest thing

HSK 6

主語 + 动不动（就）+ 動詞句（好ましくない行為）

「动不动就…」 is a colloquial expression saying that an action happens frequently, "at the slightest thing ...," with a feeling of complaint or exasperation. It's close to English "at the drop of a hat" or "always ... at the slightest thing," and tends to go with undesirable actions such as 「发脾气・哭・请假」. Note that it's almost never used in praise.

「动不动就」 carries a strong tone of criticism or complaint. ×「他动不动就帮助别人」 (in a context of praise) is unnatural; say 「他经常帮助别人」.

Example sentences

他脾气不好，动不动就发火。
tā pí qi bù hǎo， dòng bu dòng jiù fā huǒ。
He has a bad temper and blows up at the slightest thing.
