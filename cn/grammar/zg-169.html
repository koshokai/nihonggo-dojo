「以至（于）」: so much so that, to the point that

HSK 6

原因・程度，以至（于）+ 行き着いた結果

「以至（于）」 is a written-style conjunction showing the result reached because the earlier situation was so extreme: "to the point that ..." It's close to English "so ... that ..." or "to the point that ..." The result is usually unwelcome. It is also written 「以致」, but that form is limited to even worse results. Don't confuse it with HSK 5's 「（不）至于」 ("not to the point of ...").

With 「以至于」, the result arises from the degree in the first half. It sounds natural when the first half has an intensifier such as 「太・很・非常」.

Example sentences

他工作太投入，以至于忘了吃饭。
tā gōng zuò tài tóu rù， yǐ zhì yú wàng le chī fàn。
He was so absorbed in his work that he forgot to eat.
