「不免」: can't help but ...

HSK 6

原因・状況，（主語）+ 不免 +（有些／会）+ 動詞・形容詞

「不免」 is an adverb showing that, given the preceding situation, "one naturally ends up ... / can't help but ..." Its meaning is close to HSK 5's 「难免」, but because 「不免」 is an adverb, it must always go before a verb or adjective; it can't go at the end of a sentence or be wrapped in 「是…的」. It's close to English "can't help ..." or "naturally end up ...," and is often used in sentences describing changes in feelings or state of mind.

×「这是不免的」 → 「这是难免的」. 「不免」 is adverb-only; only 「难免」 can be used as a predicate.

Example sentences

第一次在这么多人面前讲话，不免有些紧张。
dì yī cì zài zhè me duō rén miàn qián jiǎng huà， bù miǎn yǒu xiē jǐn zhāng。
Speaking in front of so many people for the first time, I can't help feeling a little nervous.
