「进而」: and then go on to, and further

HSK 6

A（第一段階），进而 + B（さらに進んだ段階）

「进而」 is a written-style conjunction expressing step-by-step development: "having achieved A, go a step further and do B." It's close to English "and then go on to" or "and further," and is favored in proposals and opinion pieces. It differs from 「从而」 ("as a result"), which expresses a result, in that 「进而」 expresses moving forward to the next stage.

「进而」 gives the image of stepping up from A to B. For a simple result, use 「从而」; for a purpose, use 「以便」.

Example sentences

我们先要了解顾客的需要，进而提高服务质量。
wǒ men xiān yào liǎo jiě gù kè de xū yào， jìn ér tí gāo fú wù zhì liàng。
We first need to understand our customers' needs, and then go on to improve the quality of our service.
