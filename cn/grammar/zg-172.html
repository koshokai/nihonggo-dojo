「不惜」: not hesitate to, even at the cost of

HSK 6

主語 + 不惜 + 代償（花／牺牲／代价…）+（也要）+ 目的

「不惜」 means "not hesitating to pay the price of ...," expressing a strong determination to accept a big sacrifice or burden for a goal. It is typically followed by words describing the price, such as 「花大量时间・一切代价・牺牲休息」, and then the goal with 「也要／来」. It's a somewhat stiff expression corresponding to English "not hesitate to ..." or "even at the cost of ..."

What follows 「不惜」 is the price to be paid. ×「不惜成功」 (putting the goal there) is wrong. Remember the order 「不惜」 + price + 「也要」 + goal.

Example sentences

为了治好孩子的病，父母不惜花光所有积蓄。
wèi le zhì hǎo hái zi de bìng， fù mǔ bù xī huā guāng suǒ yǒu jī xù。
To cure their child's illness, the parents didn't hesitate to spend all their savings.
