「无非」: nothing more than, simply

HSK 6

主語 + 无非（是）+ 内容（+ 而已／罢了）

「无非」 is an adverb meaning "nothing other than ..., merely ...," used to play something down as no big deal. It's often used in the pattern 「无非是…而已／罢了」 and feels close to English "it's just ..." or "nothing more than ..." It's used to dispel someone's worries or to state flatly what something really comes down to.

「无非」 has a nuance of limiting and downplaying. Combining it with 「而已／罢了」 makes "nothing more than" even clearer.

Example sentences

他这么说，无非是想让你高兴罢了。
tā zhè me shuō， wú fēi shì xiǎng ràng nǐ gāo xìng bà le。
He only said that because he wanted to make you happy.
