Rhetorical 「岂不（是）…吗」: wouldn't that ...?

HSK 6

（如果…，）岂不（是）+ 結論 +（吗）？

「岂不（是）…吗」 is a written-style expression that strongly asserts a conclusion as a rhetorical question: "wouldn't that mean ...?" 「岂」 is a Classical Chinese word meaning "how could," so it helps to think of English rhetorical questions like "Wouldn't that mean ...?" or "Isn't it ...?" It's typically used after a supposition such as 「如果・这样」 to point out how absurd or how obvious the result would be.

The form is negative, but the meaning is a positive assertion. 「岂不是白费了」 = "it will all have been wasted." In conversation, 「那不就…了吗？」 is a similar way to say it.

Example sentences

如果现在放弃，以前的努力岂不是白费了吗？
rú guǒ xiàn zài fàng qì， yǐ qián de nǔ lì qǐ bú shì bái fèi le ma？
If you give up now, wouldn't all your past efforts have been wasted?
