「就…而言」: as far as ... is concerned

HSK 6

就 + 対象・観点 + 而言，…

「就…而言」 is a written-style expression that limits the scope of a topic or evaluation: "speaking only in terms of / within the scope of ..." It corresponds to English "as far as ... is concerned" or "in terms of ...," and is often used in papers and speeches. In speech, 「拿…来说」 and 「对…来说」 are similar, but 「就…而言」 sounds more objective and formal. 「而言」 isn't dropped; the two parts are used as a set.

Don't mix them, as in ×「就价格来说而言」. Use either 「就…而言」 or 「就…来说」.

Example sentences

就价格而言，这款手机很有优势。
jiù jià gé ér yán， zhè kuǎn shǒu jī hěn yǒu yōu shì。
In terms of price, this phone has a real advantage.
