「凡是…都…」: all ..., any ...

HSK 6

凡是 + 範囲（名詞句），都 + 述語

「凡是」 refers emphatically to everything that falls within a certain scope: "anything that is ..., without exception." It is basically always followed up by 「都」, and is close to English "anyone who ..." / "anything that ..." or "all ..." It's often used in sentences stating rules or principles, and compared with 「所有的…都」, it draws more attention to whether something meets the condition.

As a rule, 「凡是」 goes before the subject, and the 「都」 that follows isn't left out. ×「凡是学生可以参加」 → 「凡是学生都可以参加」.

Example sentences

凡是参加比赛的人都可以得到一份纪念品。
fán shì cān jiā bǐ sài de rén dōu kě yǐ dé dào yí fèn jì niàn pǐn。
Anyone who takes part in the competition will receive a souvenir.
