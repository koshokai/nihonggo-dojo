「并非」: by no means

HSK 6

主語 + 并非 + 名詞・動詞句（，而是…）

「并非」 is the written-style version of 「并不是」, used to clearly deny someone's assumption or a common view: "it's by no means the case that ..." It's often followed by 「而是」 ("rather, ...") to give the correct version. It's stronger than English "not necessarily" and closer to "by no means" or "not at all."

「并非」 already contains 「是」, so as a rule you don't say ×「并非是」 (you do see it in speech, but avoid it in writing).

Example sentences

成功并非一件容易的事。
chéng gōng bìng fēi yí jiàn róng yì de shì。
Success is by no means easy.
