「不妨」: might as well, there's no harm in

HSK 6

（主語）+ 不妨 + 動詞（+ 一下／试试）

「不妨」 is an adverb meaning "there's no harm in ... / why not ...?," used to make a casual suggestion to someone or to express a willingness to try something yourself. It's close to English "you might as well ..." or "why not ...?," making a soft recommendation that isn't pushy. It's not as strong as 「应该」, and expresses a suggestion more clearly than 「可以」. It's often followed by 「试试・一下」.

「不妨」 is a positive suggestion. Even though it has 「不」, it isn't negative, so don't misread it as "better not to ..."

Example sentences

你不妨先跟他谈谈，看看他的想法。
nǐ bù fáng xiān gēn tā tán tan， kàn kan tā de xiǎng fǎ。
Why not talk to him first and see what he thinks?
