「索性」: might as well just, simply

HSK 6

（状況），主語 + 索性 + 動詞句

「索性」 is an adverb expressing a decisive choice in response to a situation that isn't going as hoped or is left half-done: "might as well just ..." It corresponds closely to English "might as well just" or "simply go ahead and," and in speech 「干脆」 is also used. The situation is often described first with 「既然・反正」 etc., and it expresses an on-the-spot decision to cut one's losses rather than a planned one.

「索性」 is almost the same as 「干脆」, but 「干脆」 can also be an adjective ("straightforward, decisive"). 「索性」 is adverb-only.

Example sentences

雨一直不停，我们索性不出去了。
yǔ yì zhí bù tíng， wǒ men suǒ xìng bù chū qu le。
The rain just wouldn't stop, so we decided not to go out at all.
