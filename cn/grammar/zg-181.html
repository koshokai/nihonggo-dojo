「几」 and 「多少」: how many, how much

HSK 1

几 + 量詞 + 名詞（10未満の予想）／ 多少（+ 量詞）+ 名詞（数の大小を問わない）

There are two words for asking about numbers. 「几」 is used when you expect the answer to be under about 10, and it always takes a measure word (「几个人？」). 「多少」 works for any number, and the measure word can be omitted (「多少学生？」). For prices, phone numbers, room numbers and other large numbers or strings of digits, use 「多少」. Be careful not to use 「几」 for every "how many."

×「几学生？」→ 「几」 always needs a measure word (「几个学生」). To ask a child's age, 「你几岁了？」 is natural; for an adult, 「你多大了？」.

Example sentences

你家有几口人？
nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén？
How many people are there in your family?
你今天上几节课？
nǐ jīn tiān shàng jǐ jié kè？
How many classes do you have today?
你们学校有多少学生？
nǐ men xué xiào yǒu duō shao xué sheng？
How many students are there at your school?
你的电话号码是多少？
nǐ de diàn huà hào mǎ shì duō shao？
What's your phone number?
