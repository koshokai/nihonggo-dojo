Affirmative-negative questions: 「V不V」, 「A不A」

HSK 1

動詞／形容詞 + 不 + 動詞／形容詞 ？（文末に「吗」は付けない）

Putting the affirmative and negative forms of a verb or adjective side by side makes a question asking whether something is or isn't the case (「你去不去？」). It means almost the same as a 「吗」 question, but sounds as if you want a clear answer. It is a bit like English "Are you going or not?" Adding 「吗」 at the end would make it a double question, so that is wrong. Adverbs such as 「很」 can't be inserted either.

×「你去不去吗？」→ No 「吗」. ×「你很忙不忙？」→ Don't insert adverbs: 「你忙不忙？」. With 「有」, the form is 「有没有」.

Example sentences

你明天来不来？
nǐ míng tiān lái bu lái？
Are you coming tomorrow?
这个菜好吃不好吃？
zhè ge cài hǎo chī bù hǎo chī？
Is this dish good?
你是不是中国人？
nǐ shì bu shì zhōng guó rén？
Are you Chinese?
你喜欢不喜欢喝茶？
nǐ xǐ huan bù xǐ huan hē chá？
Do you like drinking tea?
