Dropping the noun after 「的」 (「我的」, 「红的」): mine, the red one

HSK 1

名詞／代名詞／形容詞 + 的（+ 名詞の省略）

When the context makes clear what is meant, the noun after 「的」 can be dropped, giving the meaning "...'s" or "the ... one" (「这是我的」 = This is mine). It works much like English "mine" or "the red one." It can also follow adjectives and verb phrases (「红的」 = the red one, 「我买的」 = what I bought). Note that 「老师的」 means "the teacher's (thing)"; it doesn't refer to the person.

Just as English says "mine," not "me," use 「的」 here: "This is mine" is 「这是我的」. ×「这本书是我。」→ Without 「的」, it would mean "This book is me."

Example sentences

这本书是我的。
zhè běn shū shì wǒ de。
This book is mine.
那个杯子是谁的？
nà ge bēi zi shì shéi de？
Whose cup is that?
我不喜欢红的，我要白的。
wǒ bù xǐ huan hóng de， wǒ yào bái de。
I don't like the red one. I want the white one.
这些菜都是妈妈做的。
zhè xiē cài dōu shì mā ma zuò de。
All these dishes were made by Mom.
