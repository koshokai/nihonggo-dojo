「们」: plural (「我们」, 「朋友们」)

HSK 1

人称代名詞／人を表す名詞 + 们

「们」 attaches to words for people to show the plural (「我们」, 「你们」, 「他们」, 「同学们」). It is a bit like the English plural "-s," but its use is much narrower: it isn't normally attached to things or animals (×「书们」). It also isn't added when the number is already clear from a numeral or a word like 「很多」 (×「三个学生们」). The colloquial 「咱们」 is "we" including the listener.

×「很多学生们」→ 「很多学生」. Quantity words and 「们」 aren't used together. For things, the plural is shown with 「些」, as in 「这些书」.

Example sentences

我们是好朋友。
wǒ men shì hǎo péng you。
We're good friends.
他们都是我的同学。
tā men dōu shì wǒ de tóng xué。
They're all my classmates.
同学们，下午好！
tóng xué men， xià wǔ hǎo！
Good afternoon, everyone!
你们明天去哪儿？
nǐ men míng tiān qù nǎr？
Where are you all going tomorrow?
