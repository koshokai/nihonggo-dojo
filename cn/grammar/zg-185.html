「这些」, 「那些」, 「一些」: these, those, some

HSK 1

这／那／哪 + 些 + 名詞 ／ 一些 + 名詞

「些」 is a measure word for an unspecified plural, used as 「这些」 (these), 「那些」 (those) and 「哪些」 (which ones). While 「这个」 points to one thing, 「这些」 points to two or more. 「一些」 means "some, a few, a little." It works for both things and people and doesn't need to be combined with another measure word such as 「个」 (「这些个书」 sounds unnatural).

Learn them as a pair: 「这个」 = this (one), 「这些」 = these (plural). A number can't follow 「这些」 (×「这些三本书」→ 「这三本书」).

Example sentences

这些水果很新鲜。
zhè xiē shuǐ guǒ hěn xīn xiān。
This fruit is very fresh.
那些人是谁？
nà xiē rén shì shéi？
Who are those people?
我买了一些东西。
wǒ mǎi le yì xiē dōng xi。
I bought some things.
你想看哪些书？
nǐ xiǎng kàn nǎ xiē shū？
Which books do you want to read?
