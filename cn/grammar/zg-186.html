「坐」/「开」 + vehicle (means of transportation)

HSK 1

坐 + 乗り物 + 去／来 + 場所 ／ 开车 + 去 + 場所

To say "go by ...," put 「坐」 + vehicle before the verb (「坐飞机去北京」). The key point is that no preposition like English "by" is used; the means is expressed with the verb 「坐」 (to ride). If you drive yourself, use 「开车」; for things you straddle, such as bicycles and motorcycles, use 「骑」. Note that the means comes before the action, the reverse of English "go to Beijing by plane."

×「我去学校坐车。」→ The means goes before the verb: 「我坐车去学校」. Expressing the means with 「用」, as in 「用飞机去」, is also unnatural.

Example sentences

我每天坐公共汽车去学校。
wǒ měi tiān zuò gōng gòng qì chē qù xué xiào。
I take the bus to school every day.
他们坐飞机去北京。
tā men zuò fēi jī qù běi jīng。
They're flying to Beijing.
爸爸开车去公司。
bà ba kāi chē qù gōng sī。
Dad drives to work.
你怎么来的？——我坐出租车来的。
nǐ zěn me lái de？—— wǒ zuò chū zū chē lái de。
How did you get here? — I came by taxi.
