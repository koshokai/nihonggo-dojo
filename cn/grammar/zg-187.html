Time words 「上个」, 「这个」, 「下个」: last week, this week, next week

HSK 1

上个／这个／下个 + 星期／月 ／ 去年・今年・明年

"Last ...," "this ..." and "next ..." are expressed with 「上」, 「这」 and 「下」. Weeks and months take 「个」: 「上个星期」 (last week), 「下个月」 (next month). Years, however, are an exception: they are 「去年」, 「今年」 and 「明年」, never 「上个年」. Days are likewise 「昨天」, 「今天」 and 「明天」. Time words go before or after the subject, but before the verb.

×「下个年」→ 「明年」, ×「上个年」→ 「去年」. 「年」 doesn't take 「个」 and has its own special words. Saying 「来星期」 for "next week" is also wrong.

Example sentences

我上个星期很忙。
wǒ shàng ge xīng qī hěn máng。
I was very busy last week.
下个月我们去中国。
xià ge yuè wǒ men qù zhōng guó。
We're going to China next month.
这个星期天你做什么？
zhè ge xīng qī tiān nǐ zuò shén me？
What are you doing this Sunday?
他明年想去北京学习。
tā míng nián xiǎng qù běi jīng xué xí。
He wants to go to Beijing to study next year.
