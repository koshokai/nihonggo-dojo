「这儿」, 「那儿」, 「哪儿」: here, there, where

HSK 1

这儿／那儿（＝这里／那里）+ 有／是 ／ 在 + 这儿／那儿

The demonstratives for places are 「这儿」 (here), 「那儿」 (there) and 「哪儿」 (where). 「这儿」 and 「那儿」 are common in the north, while 「这里」 and 「那里」 are used more in the south and in writing; the meaning is the same. As with English "here" and "there," there are only two: everything that isn't close by is 「那儿」. Keep 「这个」 (things) and 「这儿」 (places) apart.

×「这个有一个商店。」→ For a place, say 「这儿有一个商店」. 「哪儿」 and 「那儿」 differ in tone (「nǎr」 vs. 「nàr」), so watch your pronunciation.

Example sentences

这儿有很多饭馆。
zhèr yǒu hěn duō fàn guǎn。
There are lots of restaurants here.
我的书在那儿。
wǒ de shū zài nàr。
My book is over there.
你在哪儿工作？
nǐ zài nǎr gōng zuò？
Where do you work?
那儿是我们的学校。
nàr shì wǒ men de xué xiào。
That's our school over there.
