Answering "yes" and "no"

HSK 1

是／对／動詞・形容詞で答える ／ 不（是）／ 没（有）

Chinese has no all-purpose "yes"; the basic way to answer is to repeat the verb or adjective used in the question (「你去吗？」—「去。」). 「是」 or 「对」 means "That's right," used when what the other person said is correct. "No" is likewise not just 「不」: you answer by negating the verb, as in 「不去」 or 「没有」. Answering 「是」 to everything, as if it were English "yes," sounds unnatural.

Answering 「你喜欢吗？」 with 「是」 is wrong. Answer with the verb: 「喜欢」 or 「不喜欢」. "No" to a question with 「有」 is 「没有」.

Example sentences

你喝咖啡吗？——喝，谢谢。
nǐ hē kā fēi ma？—— hē， xiè xie。
Would you like some coffee? — Yes, thanks.
你是老师吗？——是，我是老师。
nǐ shì lǎo shī ma？—— shì， wǒ shì lǎo shī。
Are you a teacher? — Yes, I am.
你忙吗？——不忙。
nǐ máng ma？—— bù máng。
Are you busy? — No, I'm not.
你有哥哥吗？——没有。
nǐ yǒu gē ge ma？—— méi yǒu。
Do you have an older brother? — No, I don't.
