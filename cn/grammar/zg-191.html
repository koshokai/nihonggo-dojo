「真」 + adjective: really ... (exclamation)

HSK 2

真 + 形容詞／心理動詞（+ 啊）

「真」 is an adverb expressing the speaker's exclamation: "It's really ...!" (「今天真冷！」). While 「很」 can also be used for objective description, 「真」 is used for heartfelt impressions. For this reason it can't be used to modify a noun: you can't say ×「真好的人」 (→ 「很好的人」). Adding 「啊」 at the end makes the exclamation stronger.

×「他是一个真好的老师。」→ To modify a noun, use 「很」: 「很好的老师」. Remember that 「真」 is only for exclamations in the predicate.

Example sentences

今天天气真好！
jīn tiān tiān qì zhēn hǎo！
The weather is really nice today!
你的汉语说得真好。
nǐ de hàn yǔ shuō de zhēn hǎo。
You speak Chinese really well.
这个菜真好吃啊！
zhè ge cài zhēn hǎo chī a！
This dish is really delicious!
见到你真高兴。
jiàn dào nǐ zhēn gāo xìng。
I'm really happy to see you.
