「会」 and 「可能」: will probably, might (conjecture)

HSK 2

会 + 動詞（〜するだろう）／ 可能 + 動詞（〜かもしれない）

Besides "can," 「会」 is used for future likelihood: "will (probably), should" (「明天会下雨」). The speaker is fairly confident. 「可能」, on the other hand, means "might, may," with somewhat less confidence. The combination 「可能会」 is also common. The negatives are 「不会」 (probably won't) and 「不可能」 (impossible).

Adding 「的」 at the end, 「会…的」, makes it more certain (「他会来的」). 「不会吧？」 is also common in conversation, meaning "No way!"

Example sentences

明天会下雨吗？
míng tiān huì xià yǔ ma？
Will it rain tomorrow?
别担心，他会来的。
bié dān xīn， tā huì lái de。
Don't worry, he'll come.
他可能生病了。
tā kě néng shēng bìng le。
He might be sick.
这么晚了，商店可能已经关门了。
zhè me wǎn le， shāng diàn kě néng yǐ jīng guān mén le。
It's so late, the stores might already be closed.
