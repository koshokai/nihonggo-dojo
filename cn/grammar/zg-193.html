「往」: toward, in the direction of

HSK 2

往 + 方向／場所 + 動詞（走・开・看 など）

「往」 is a preposition indicating the direction of movement, often used in giving directions, as in 「往前走」 (go straight ahead) and 「往左拐」 (turn left). It is close to English "toward": it doesn't mean arriving at a destination, only heading in that direction. It is followed by a location word or place, and the verb is mainly one of motion, such as 「走」, 「开」, 「看」 or 「跑」.

×「我往学校。」→ 「往」 alone isn't a verb, so a verb is needed: 「往学校走」. Distinguish 「去」 (go to a destination) from 「往」 (shows direction).

Example sentences

一直往前走，就到了。
yì zhí wǎng qián zǒu， jiù dào le。
Go straight ahead and you'll get there.
到了路口往右拐。
dào le lù kǒu wǎng yòu guǎi。
Turn right when you get to the intersection.
请往这边看。
qǐng wǎng zhè biān kàn。
Please look this way.
这个火车往北京开。
zhè ge huǒ chē wǎng běi jīng kāi。
This train is bound for Beijing.
