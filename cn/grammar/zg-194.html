Person/thing + 「这儿」/「那儿」: someone's place

HSK 2

人称代名詞／人・物の名詞 + 这儿／那儿（場所化）

In Chinese, nouns for people and things can't be places on their own. "Come to my place" is 「来我这儿」: you add 「这儿」 or 「那儿」 after the noun to turn it into a place. The idea is like English "my place" or "the teacher's office," but no 「的」 is inserted. This is especially important after words that need a place, such as 「在」, 「来」, 「去」 and 「到」.

×「你来我。」 ×「我去老师。」→ People aren't places, so say 「我这儿」 and 「老师那儿」. Don't insert 「的」, as in ×「我的这儿」.

Example sentences

你的手机在我这儿。
nǐ de shǒu jī zài wǒ zhèr。
Your phone is here with me.
明天到我那儿去玩儿吧。
míng tiān dào wǒ nàr qù wánr ba。
Come over to my place tomorrow.
我去老师那儿问问。
wǒ qù lǎo shī nàr wèn wen。
I'll go to the teacher and ask.
钥匙在桌子那儿。
yào shi zài zhuō zi nàr。
The keys are over by the desk.
