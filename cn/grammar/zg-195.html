「第」: ordinal numbers (first, second ...)

HSK 2

第 + 数詞（+ 量詞 + 名詞）

「第」 before a number shows order: "-th" (「第一」, 「第三个人」). It is used constantly in everyday conversation, and expressions such as 「第一次」 (the first time) and 「第二天」 (the next day) are important. Measure words go after 「第」 and the number. Some ordinal expressions don't use 「第」, such as 「一楼」 (first floor) and 「三号」 (the 3rd of the month).

"The first time" is 「第一次」; 「一次」 means "once." 「第二天」 means "the second day, the next day," which is different from 「二天」 (two days).

Example sentences

这是我第一次来中国。
zhè shì wǒ dì yī cì lái zhōng guó。
This is my first time in China.
他是我们班第一个到学校的。
tā shì wǒ men bān dì yī ge dào xué xiào de。
He was the first one in our class to get to school.
第二天早上，我们去爬山了。
dì èr tiān zǎo shang， wǒ men qù pá shān le。
The next morning, we went hiking.
请看第三十页。
qǐng kàn dì sān shí yè。
Please look at page 30.
