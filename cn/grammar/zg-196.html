Approximate numbers with 「多」 and 「左右」: over ..., about ...

HSK 2

数詞 + 多 + 量詞 ／ 数詞 + 量詞 + 多 ／ 数量 + 左右

"Over ..., ... or more" is expressed with 「多」. With round numbers such as 10 or 20, it goes before the measure word, as in 「十多个人」; when there is a remainder of 1 to 9, it goes after the measure word, as in 「一个多月」 (over a month). "About ..." is 「左右」 placed after the quantity (「三点左右」). The meaning changes with the position, so don't let English word order mislead you.

「十多个人」 means "over 10 people" (11 to 19), and 「十个多人」 is wrong. 「一个多小时」 means "over an hour." The position depends on the size of the number.

Example sentences

教室里有二十多个学生。
jiào shì lǐ yǒu èr shí duō ge xué sheng。
There are over 20 students in the classroom.
我学了一年多汉语。
wǒ xué le yì nián duō hàn yǔ。
I studied Chinese for over a year.
他三点左右来。
tā sān diǎn zuǒ yòu lái。
He'll come around 3 o'clock.
这个西瓜五斤多。
zhè ge xī guā wǔ jīn duō。
This watermelon weighs over 5 jin.
