「知道」 and 「认识」: to know

HSK 2

知道 + 事柄・情報 ／ 认识 + 人・字・道

English uses "know" for both, but 「知道」 means knowing information or facts (「知道他的名字」), while 「认识」 means being acquainted with a person, or being able to recognize characters or a route (「认识他」, 「认识这个字」). "I know him" is 「认识他」 if you've met him, and 「知道他」 if you've only heard his name or heard of him.

The greeting on first meeting, "Nice to meet you," is 「认识你很高兴」. ×「知道你很高兴」 is unnatural.

Example sentences

你认识那个人吗？
nǐ rèn shi nà ge rén ma？
Do you know that person?
我知道他的电话号码。
wǒ zhī dao tā de diàn huà hào mǎ。
I know his phone number.
这个字我不认识。
zhè ge zì wǒ bú rèn shi。
I can't read this character.
你知道明天几点开会吗？
nǐ zhī dao míng tiān jǐ diǎn kāi huì ma？
Do you know what time the meeting is tomorrow?
