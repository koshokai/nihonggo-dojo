「怎么」: how come (surprise, dissatisfaction)

HSK 2

怎么 + 動詞句／没・不 + 動詞 ？

Besides asking about method, 「怎么」 can ask for a reason: "How come ...?" While 「为什么」 asks for the reason itself in a neutral way, 「怎么」 carries surprise, a sense that something is unexpected, strange or annoying (「你怎么没来？」). It is also often used when talking to oneself without expecting an answer, or for mild criticism. It is close to English "How come ...?"

「怎么去？」 asks about method (how), while 「怎么不去？」 asks for a reason (why aren't you going?). When a negative or 「了」 follows, it tends to mean "why."

Example sentences

你昨天怎么没来上课？
nǐ zuó tiān zěn me méi lái shàng kè？
Why didn't you come to class yesterday?
他怎么还不来？
tā zěn me hái bù lái？
How come he's still not here?
你怎么哭了？
nǐ zěn me kū le？
Why are you crying?
怎么这么贵啊？
zěn me zhè me guì a？
How come it's so expensive?
