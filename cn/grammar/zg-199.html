「不用」: don't need to

HSK 2

不用 + 動詞（必要がない）

「不用」 means "there's no need to, don't have to" and is often used to lightly decline someone's politeness or offer (「不用谢」 = No need to thank me). While 「别」/「不要」 are prohibitions, "don't ...," 「不用」 only says something isn't necessary, so it sounds much softer. Also learn it as the negative of 「要」 in the sense of "have to."

「不要去」 means "Don't go," while 「不用去」 means "You don't need to go." Be careful that a considerate remark doesn't come out as a prohibition.

Example sentences

不用谢。
bú yòng xiè。
You're welcome.
明天不用上班。
míng tiān bú yòng shàng bān。
You don't have to go to work tomorrow.
你不用担心，我能做好。
nǐ bú yòng dān xīn， wǒ néng zuò hǎo。
You don't need to worry. I can handle it.
这些菜够了，不用再买了。
zhè xiē cài gòu le， bú yòng zài mǎi le。
We have enough food. There's no need to buy any more.
