「还」: still, also (continuation and addition)

HSK 2

还 + 動詞／形容詞（継続）／ 还 + 動詞（追加：ほかに〜）

「还」 has two main meanings. One is continuation, "still doing ..." (「他还在睡觉」); the other is addition, "also, else, in addition" (「我还想买一本书」). A shop clerk's "Anything else?" is 「还要什么？」. In both meanings it goes before the verb and can't go before the subject. It is a handy adverb covering both English "still" and "also, else."

×「还我想去。」→ 「还」 goes after the subject and before the verb: 「我还想去」. To add a noun, use 「还有」 (「还有一个问题」 = I have one more question).

Example sentences

他还在图书馆学习。
tā hái zài tú shū guǎn xué xí。
He's still studying at the library.
你还要别的吗？
nǐ hái yào bié de ma？
Do you need anything else?
我会说汉语，还会说英语。
wǒ huì shuō hàn yǔ， hái huì shuō yīng yǔ。
I can speak Chinese, and I can speak English too.
十点了，你还不睡觉？
shí diǎn le， nǐ hái bú shuì jiào？
It's ten o'clock and you're still not going to bed?
