「都…了」: already ... (and yet)

HSK 3

都 + 時間・数量・状態 + 了

「都…了」 means "it's already ...," showing that the speaker strongly feels that more time has passed or things have gone further than expected (「都十二点了」 = It's already 12!). While 「已经…了」 states the fact plainly, 「都…了」 carries emotions such as surprise, dissatisfaction or urging. A typical pattern is to follow it with 「还…」: "it's already ..., and still ...."

This 「都」 means "already," not "all." It is usually followed by a time or quantity, and the final 「了」 is almost always required.

Example sentences

都十二点了，快睡觉吧。
dōu shí èr diǎn le， kuài shuì jiào ba。
It's already twelve. Hurry up and go to bed.
