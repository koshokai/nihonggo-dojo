「才」: only, just (quantity)

HSK 3

才 + 数量／時間（少ない・早いと感じる）

Besides meaning "not until" with times, 「才」 placed before a quantity expresses smallness: "only, just" (「他才五岁」 = He's only five). It shows that the speaker feels the amount is small, young or early. Conversely, when a quantity feels large, use 「都…了」. In this use, too, 「才」 sentences usually don't take 「了」 at the end.

With the same 「五岁」, 「才五岁」 (only five) and 「都五岁了」 (already five) express opposite feelings. Learn them as a pair.

Example sentences

他才五岁，就会写很多字。
tā cái wǔ suì， jiù huì xiě hěn duō zì。
He's only five, but he can already write lots of characters.
