「只」: only

HSK 3

只 + 動詞（+ 数量 + 名詞）

「只」 is an adverb that limits scope: "only, just." English "only" can attach to almost anything ("only one cup"), but Chinese 「只」 must go before the verb (「我只喝了一杯」 = I only drank one cup). 「只有」 means "there is only ..." and is handy for limiting nouns. In speech, 「就」 is also used with the same meaning.

×「我喝一杯只。」→ 「只」 goes before the verb. "Only you" (as in "I told only you") is likewise not ×「告诉你只」 but 「只告诉了你」. Be careful not to put it after the noun.

Example sentences

我只会说一点儿汉语。
wǒ zhǐ huì shuō yì diǎnr hàn yǔ。
I can only speak a little Chinese.
