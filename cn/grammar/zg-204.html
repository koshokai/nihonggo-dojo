「其实」: actually, in fact

HSK 3

其实 + 主語 + 述語（前の内容・思い込みを訂正）

「其实」 is used to reveal the truth when it differs from appearances or assumptions: "Actually, ..." (「其实我不喜欢吃鱼」). The typical flow is that a common view or the listener's misunderstanding comes first, and 「其实」 corrects or adds to it. It can go at the start of the sentence or after the subject. Like English "actually," it also serves as a cushion for bringing up something awkward.

Use 「其实」 to state "the truth of the matter" when it conflicts with what came before. If you simply want to emphasize "really," use 「真」 or 「确实」.

Example sentences

他看起来很严肃，其实人很好。
tā kàn qǐ lai hěn yán sù， qí shí rén hěn hǎo。
He looks stern, but he's actually a really nice person.
