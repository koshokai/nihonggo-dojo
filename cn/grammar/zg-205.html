「终于」: finally, at last

HSK 3

（長い時間・努力の後）+ 终于 + 動詞（+ 了）

「终于」 expresses that something hoped for has finally come true after a long wait or much effort (「我终于找到工作了」). It corresponds to English "finally, at last" and is mostly used for good outcomes. 「了」 is often added at the end. Unlike 「才」, which expresses lateness, the key point of 「终于」 is a sense of achievement: the long-awaited result has finally come.

「终于」 normally goes after the subject and before the verb (「他终于来了」). For a bad outcome, "ended up ...," 「最后」 or a similar word is more natural.

Example sentences

等了一个小时，公共汽车终于来了。
děng le yí ge xiǎo shí， gōng gòng qì chē zhōng yú lái le。
After waiting an hour, the bus finally came.
