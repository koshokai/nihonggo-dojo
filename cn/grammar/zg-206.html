「一直」: all along, straight

HSK 3

一直 + 動詞（継続）／ 一直 + 往 + 方向 + 走（まっすぐ）

「一直」 expresses the continuation of an action or state: "have been doing ... all along" (from some point until now, or over a period) (「我一直在等你」). In giving directions, it also means the direction doesn't change: 「一直往前走」 (go straight ahead). It can be used much like English "all along, the whole time," and in the negative, 「一直没…」 or 「一直不…」 means "haven't ... the whole time."

For "much ...-er" in comparisons, use 「得多」, not 「一直」 (「他比我高得多」). Don't confuse continuation over time with a difference in degree.

Example sentences

我一直在等你。
wǒ yì zhí zài děng nǐ。
I've been waiting for you this whole time.
