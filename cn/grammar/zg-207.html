「好像」: seem, look like

HSK 3

好像 + 文／動詞句（+ 似的）

「好像」 expresses an uncertain judgment based on what you've seen or felt: "it seems ..., it looks like ..." (「他好像生病了」). It is close to English "seem" or "look like." While 「像…一样」 compares something to what it resembles, "like ...," 「好像」 is mainly about guessing. It can go before or after the subject.

「他好像他爸爸」 can also mean "He looks like his father," leaning toward resemblance. For guesses, 「好像」 is normally followed by a verb or a clause.

Example sentences

外面好像下雨了。
wài miàn hǎo xiàng xià yǔ le。
It looks like it's started raining outside.
