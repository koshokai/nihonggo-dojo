「差不多」: almost, about the same

HSK 3

差不多 + 数量・動詞（ほぼ）／ A 和 B 差不多（ほぼ同じ）

「差不多」 literally means "the difference is not much." It is used in two ways: as an adverb meaning "almost, about" (「差不多十点了」), and as an adjective meaning "about the same" (「我们俩差不多高」). From "not much different," it also comes to mean "so-so, good enough." Before a quantity it means "about ...," and as a predicate it means "pretty much the same."

「差不多」 means "almost," so it implies something isn't fully complete. "All finished" is 「都做完了」; "almost finished" is 「差不多做完了」.

Example sentences

作业差不多做完了。
zuò yè chà bu duō zuò wán le。
I'm almost done with my homework.
