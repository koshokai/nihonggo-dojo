「一定」 and 「不一定」: definitely, not necessarily

HSK 3

一定 + 動詞（意志・確信）／ 不一定（必ずしも〜ない）

「一定」 expresses strong will, "will definitely ...," or conviction, "must be ..., surely ..." (「我一定去」, 「他一定很累」). Its negative 「不一定」 is a partial negation: "not necessarily." 「一定不…」 is a total negation, "definitely won't ...," which changes the meaning greatly. Watch the word order.

「不一定去」 = won't necessarily go; 「一定不去」 = definitely won't go. The position of the negative reverses the meaning, so be careful.

Example sentences

明天我一定早点儿来。
míng tiān wǒ yí dìng zǎo diǎnr lái。
I'll definitely come a little early tomorrow.
