「向」: to, from, toward (target, direction)

HSK 3

向 + 人 + 動詞（学习・借・问・介绍 など）／ 向 + 方向 + 動詞

「向」 is a preposition indicating the person or direction an action is aimed at. In cases like 「向老师学习」 (learn from the teacher) and 「向他借书」 (borrow a book from him), where English might use "to" or "from," 「向」 alone covers both. For direction it is close to 「往」, but what sets 「向」 apart is that it can also be used for abstract actions directed at people.

For directions, "turn left" can be either 「往左拐」 or 「向左拐」, but when a person is the target, as in 「向他学习」, 「向」 can't be replaced with 「往」.

Example sentences

我们要向他学习。
wǒ men yào xiàng tā xué xí。
We should learn from his example.
