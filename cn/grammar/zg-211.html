「并且」: and, moreover

HSK 4

動詞句A，并且 + 動詞句B ／ 形容詞A + 并且 + 形容詞B

「并且」 is a conjunction that lines up two actions or qualities, adding the second: "and, moreover." It often joins verb phrases or adjectives with the same subject. It is almost the same as 「而且」 but leans a little toward written language, and it is also often used to add a further step: "and at the same time, and then also ...." It can't join nouns; for that, use 「和」.

Joining nouns, as in ×「我和他并且…」, is wrong. Remember: nouns take 「和」; verb phrases and adjectives take 「并且」.

Example sentences

他通过了考试，并且拿到了第一名。
tā tōng guò le kǎo shì， bìng qiě ná dào le dì yī míng。
He passed the exam and came in first, too.
