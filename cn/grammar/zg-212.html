「再…也…」: no matter how ...

HSK 4

再 + 形容詞・動詞，也 + 不／没 + …

「再…也…」 means "no matter how ..., still ...," expressing that however high the degree, the result doesn't change. The typical form puts an adjective or verb after 「再」 and picks it up with 「也」 in the second half, followed by a negative or an unchanged result. It corresponds to English "no matter how ..." and is more colloquial and emotional than 「即使…也」.

Here 「再」 means "no matter how," not "again." 「也」 is always required in the second half (×「再忙要吃饭」).

Example sentences

再忙也要吃早饭。
zài máng yě yào chī zǎo fàn。
No matter how busy you are, you should eat breakfast.
