「说不定」: maybe, might

HSK 4

说不定 + 推測 ／ 主語 + 说不定 + 動詞句

「说不定」 is a colloquial adverb expressing a guess: from "can't say for sure" comes "maybe, it might be that ...." It can go at the start of the sentence or after the subject. It is close to English "maybe" or "for all I know," and more colloquial than 「也许」 or 「可能」. It can also be used on its own as a predicate meaning "it's not certain yet" (「这事还说不定」).

Adding 「会」 after 「说不定」 makes a guess about the future sound more natural (「说不定会下雨」).

Example sentences

他还没来，说不定路上堵车了。
tā hái méi lái， shuō bu dìng lù shàng dǔ chē le。
He's still not here. Maybe he got stuck in traffic.
