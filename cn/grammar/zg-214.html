「果然」: sure enough, just as expected

HSK 4

予想・予告，（主語）+ 果然 + 予想どおりの結果

「果然」 is an adverb meaning "sure enough, just as expected," showing that things turned out as predicted or as someone said. It is close to English "sure enough," and it is always used when there was a prediction or statement beforehand that turned out to be right. It can be used for both good and bad outcomes. For unexpected results, by contrast, use 「竟然」, so it's handy to learn the two as a pair.

As expected → 「果然」; unexpected → 「竟然」. 「果然」 goes after the subject or at the start of the sentence.

Example sentences

天气预报说今天有雨，果然下雨了。
tiān qì yù bào shuō jīn tiān yǒu yǔ， guǒ rán xià yǔ le。
The weather forecast said it would rain today, and sure enough, it did.
