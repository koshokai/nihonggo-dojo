「以来」: since ...

HSK 4

時点・出来事 + 以来，… ／ 自从 + … + 以来

「以来」 expresses a period from a point in time up to the present. It goes after a word for a point in time or an event, and the form 「自从…以来」 is also common. It is much like English "since ...," and what follows 「以来」 is often a state or change that is still continuing. Note that it can't be used with a starting point in the future.

「以来」 covers the period from the past up to the present. For a starting point in the future, use 「从…起」 or 「从…开始」 (×「明天以来」).

Example sentences

开学以来，他每天都很早来学校。
kāi xué yǐ lái， tā měi tiān dōu hěn zǎo lái xué xiào。
Since the semester started, he's been coming to school early every day.
