「一向」: always, consistently

HSK 4

主語 + 一向 + 動詞・形容詞（習慣・性質）

「一向」 is an adverb expressing an unchanging habit or trait: "has always been ... (from before until now)." It is close to English "has always been" or "by nature," and is often used about people's personalities or attitudes. While 「一直」 expresses continuation over a specific period, 「一向」 focuses on long-term tendencies or character and sounds somewhat formal.

「一向」 doesn't go well with a specific length of time (×「我一向等了三个小时」→ 「我一直等了三个小时」).

Example sentences

他一向很守时。
tā yí xiàng hěn shǒu shí。
He's always punctual.
