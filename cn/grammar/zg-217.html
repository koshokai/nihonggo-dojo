「反正」: anyway, in any case

HSK 4

（状況・条件），反正 + 変わらない結論

「反正」 is a colloquial adverb used to state a conclusion flatly, regardless of the other person's circumstances or the conditions: the conclusion is the same whatever the situation. It is close to English "anyway" or "in any case." It is also often used to give a reason, showing the basis for the suggestion or judgment that follows, as in 「反正有时间」 (we have time anyway).

「不管…，反正…」 is a standard combination. It isn't used only with a resigned, negative nuance; it also works for giving a casual reason.

Example sentences

不管你信不信，反正我没骗你。
bù guǎn nǐ xìn bu xìn， fǎn zhèng wǒ méi piàn nǐ。
Whether you believe me or not, I didn't lie to you.
