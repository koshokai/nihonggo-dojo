「总算」: finally, at long last

HSK 4

主語 + 总算 + 動詞（+ 了）

「总算」 is an adverb expressing that something hoped for has finally come true after a long time or much trouble, with a feeling of relief. It is close to English "finally, at long last" and almost the same as 「终于」, but 「总算」 can also mean "it can more or less be called ..., though not perfect" (「这次考得总算还可以」).

Unlike 「终于」, 「总算」 also has a compromising sense: "it can more or less be called ...."

Example sentences

等了一个小时，车总算来了。
děng le yí ge xiǎo shí， chē zǒng suàn lái le。
We waited an hour, and the bus finally showed up.
