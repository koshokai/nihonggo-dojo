「差点儿」: almost, nearly

HSK 4

主語 + 差点儿 +（没）+ 動詞

「差点儿」 means "almost did ... (but actually didn't)." For undesirable things, 「差点儿摔倒」 and 「差点儿没摔倒」 both mean "almost fell"; they have the same meaning. For desirable things, however, the presence or absence of 「没」 changes the meaning, as in 「差点儿没赶上」 (almost didn't make it = made it), so be careful.

Undesirable things: 「差点儿」 = 「差点儿没」 (it didn't happen). Desirable things: 「差点儿」... = narrowly failed to; 「差点儿没」... = just barely managed to.

Example sentences

路太滑了，我差点儿摔倒。
lù tài huá le， wǒ chà diǎnr shuāi dǎo。
The road was so slippery that I almost fell.
