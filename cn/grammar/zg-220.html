「本来」: originally, by rights

HSK 4

主語 + 本来 + 動詞句（，可是／后来…）／ 本来 + 就 + 応該…

「本来」 has two uses: one describes a former state, "originally it was ... (but now it's different)," and the other expresses what is right or natural, "by rights, ... should / of course." It is close to English "originally" and "by rights / ought to have." The first use is often followed by 「后来」, 「可是」, etc. to describe the change, and the second is often used in the pattern 「本来就应该…」.

「本来」 means "originally," while 「原来」 means "so it was ... (all along)" (a realization). 「原来是你！」 is ○ and 「本来是你！」 is ×.

Example sentences

我本来想去旅行，可是后来生病了。
wǒ běn lái xiǎng qù lǚ xíng， kě shì hòu lái shēng bìng le。
I had originally wanted to go traveling, but then I got sick.
