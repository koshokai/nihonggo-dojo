「恨不得」: be dying to ...

HSK 5

主語 + 恨不得 + 動詞句（実現できない強い願望）

「恨不得」 expresses a strong wish: "it's frustrating that I can't ... → I'm dying to ..." It is mostly used for exaggerated wishes that can't actually come true (「恨不得马上飞回家」). It is close to English "I wish I could ... right now," "be dying to ...," or "can't wait to ..." There is no negative form, and note that despite the character 「恨」, it carries no meaning of anger.

「恨不得」 is often followed by words meaning "right away," such as 「马上・立刻・一下子」. Understand 「恨」 here not as "hate / resent" but as "frustrated (at being unable to)."

Example sentences

听到这个好消息，他恨不得马上告诉所有人。
tīng dào zhè ge hǎo xiāo xi， tā hèn bu de mǎ shàng gào su suǒ yǒu rén。
When he heard the good news, he couldn't wait to tell everyone right away.
