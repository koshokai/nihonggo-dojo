「有的是」: plenty of

HSK 5

主語 + 有的是 + 名詞 ／ 名詞 + 有的是

「有的是」 is a colloquial expression stressing that there is more than enough of something: "there's plenty / no end of ..." The 「的是」 part is for emphasis, and note that it has a completely different meaning from 「有些是」 ("some are ..."). It's close to English "there's plenty of ..." or "there's no shortage of ...," and is often used to reassure someone or tell them not to hold back.

「有的是」 is read as one word, 「yǒudeshì」, and means "a lot." Don't confuse it with 「有的…有的…」 ("some ..., others ...").

Example sentences

别着急，我们有的是时间。
bié zháo jí， wǒ men yǒu de shì shí jiān。
Don't worry, we've got plenty of time.
