「依然・仍然」: still, as before

HSK 5

主語 + 依然／仍然 + 動詞・形容詞（+ 状態）

「依然」 and 「仍然」 are adverbs showing that "even though circumstances have changed, the same state as before continues." They are written-style versions of 「还是」 and correspond to English "still" or "as ever." They are often preceded by a contrastive word such as 「虽然」 or 「尽管」, emphasizing the contrast that nothing changed even though something else did.

「依然」 can go before an adjective without 「是」 (「依然很美」). 「仍然」 is often used before verbs.

Example sentences

虽然过了很多年，这里依然很安静。
suī rán guò le hěn duō nián， zhè lǐ yī rán hěn ān jìng。
Although many years have passed, it's still very quiet here.
