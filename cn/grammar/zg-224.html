「再…不过了」: couldn't be more ...

HSK 5

再 + 形容詞 + 不过了

「再…不过了」 is an emphatic superlative meaning "nothing could be more ... = as ... as it gets." The adjective is sandwiched between 「再」 and 「不过了」, as in 「再好不过了」 and 「再合适不过了」. It corresponds to English "couldn't be better / couldn't be more ..." and is mostly used for positive evaluations. It's often used in everyday conversation to say "that would be perfect."

The adjective goes between 「再」 and 「不过」 (×「再不过好了」). The 「了」 at the end is also part of the set.

Example sentences

你能来帮忙，那就再好不过了。
nǐ néng lái bāng máng， nà jiù zài hǎo bú guò le。
If you can come and help, that would be perfect.
