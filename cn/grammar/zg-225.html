「明明」: clearly ..., and yet

HSK 5

主語 + 明明 + 事実，（可是／却／为什么）+ 矛盾する内容

「明明」 is an adverb that points to a clear fact, "clearly ... (and yet)," expressing dissatisfaction or puzzlement at words, actions, or results that contradict it. It is typically followed by 「可是」, 「却」, 「怎么」, 「为什么」, etc. It's close to English "I definitely ..., so why ..." or "it's obvious that ..., but ...," and is often used to blame someone or express that you can't accept something.

The second half of a 「明明」 sentence often has a contrast (「却／可是」) or a question (「怎么／为什么」). If you just want to say "it's obvious," use 「很明显」.

Example sentences

我明明把钥匙放在桌子上了，怎么找不到了？
wǒ míng míng bǎ yào shi fàng zài zhuō zi shàng le， zěn me zhǎo bú dào le？
I definitely put the keys on the table, so why can't I find them?
