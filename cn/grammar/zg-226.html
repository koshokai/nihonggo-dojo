「好在」: fortunately

HSK 5

悪い状況，好在 + 救いとなる条件（+ 所以…）

「好在」 is an adverb meaning "in a bad situation, fortunately there is the favorable condition that ..." Typically the first half describes the trouble, and 「好在」 comes at the beginning of the second half. It's similar to 「幸亏」, but while 「幸亏」 focuses on the result, "thanks to ..., a bad outcome was avoided," 「好在」 points to the saving condition itself.

「好在」 goes at the beginning of the second half. 「幸亏」 is often used in the pattern 「幸亏…，不然…」, but 「好在」 doesn't take such a fixed follow-up.

Example sentences

雨下得很大，好在我带了伞。
yǔ xià de hěn dà， hǎo zài wǒ dài le sǎn。
It was raining hard, but fortunately I had brought an umbrella.
