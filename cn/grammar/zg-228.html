「再说」: besides, what's more

HSK 5

理由A，再说 + 理由B（+ 結論）

「再说」 is a colloquial conjunction that adds another reason after one has been given: "besides, ... too." It's close to English "besides" or "what's more," and is often used when turning down a suggestion or reinforcing an opinion. As a verb it also means "think about it later / put it off" (「以后再说」), so tell the two apart by context.

「再说」 adds a reason. 「以后再说／回头再说」 is a set phrase for putting something off: "let's think about it later."

Example sentences

今天太晚了，再说我也累了，明天再去吧。
jīn tiān tài wǎn le， zài shuō wǒ yě lèi le， míng tiān zài qù ba。
It's too late today, and besides, I'm tired; let's go tomorrow.
