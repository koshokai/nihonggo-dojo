「不至于」: not to the point of ..., surely won't go so far as to ...

HSK 5

主語 + 不至于 + 動詞句（極端な結果）

「不至于」 means "doesn't reach a certain degree," that is, a negative guess about an extreme outcome: "surely it won't get to the point of ..." It's close to English "it won't come to that" or "surely ... won't go so far as to ..." The affirmative 「至于」 is rarely used on its own in this sense; it appears in rhetorical questions such as 「至于吗？」 ("Is that really necessary?").

「不至于」 is a negative guess about degree. It's a different use from topic-changing 「至于」 ("as for ..."), so keep them apart.

Example sentences

他虽然生气了，但不至于不理你。
tā suī rán shēng qì le， dàn bú zhì yú bù lǐ nǐ。
He got angry, but not to the point of ignoring you.
