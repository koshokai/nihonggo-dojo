「好像…似的」: just like, as if

HSK 5

主語 + 好像／像 + 名詞・動詞句 + 似的

「似的」 (shìde) is a particle placed at the end of a phrase to express a comparison or guess, "it's like ...," and is often combined with 「好像」 or 「像」 in the pattern 「好像…似的」. It corresponds to English "just like ..." or "as if ..." It means almost the same as 「像…一样」, but 「似的」 is more colloquial and can wrap a whole verb phrase as is, so it's often used to describe how someone looks or behaves.

「似的」 is read 「shìde」, not 「sìde」. 「好像」 alone gets the meaning across, but adding 「似的」 strengthens the sense of comparison.

Example sentences

他高兴得好像孩子似的。
tā gāo xìng de hǎo xiàng hái zi shì de。
He's as happy as a child.
