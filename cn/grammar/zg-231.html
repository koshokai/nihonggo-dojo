「尚且…何况…」: even ..., let alone ...

HSK 6

A + 尚且 + 述語，（更）何况 + B（呢）？

「尚且」 means "even ...," citing an example at a high (or low) extreme, and 「何况」 in the second half draws the conclusion "let alone B." It's a written-style expression corresponding to English "if even ... can't ..., let alone ..." or "even ..., to say nothing of ..." It's more formal than 「连…都」 and is often used in opinion pieces. The first half has the extreme example, and the second half has the case for which the conclusion naturally follows.

「尚且」 goes after the subject and before the predicate. The second half can be followed up with 「更不用说」 as well as 「何况」.

Example sentences

大人尚且搬不动，何况孩子呢？
dà rén shàng qiě bān bu dòng， hé kuàng hái zi ne？
Even adults can't move it, let alone children.
