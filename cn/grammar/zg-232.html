「不禁」: can't help but ...

HSK 6

（状況・刺激），主語 + 不禁 + 動作・感情（笑／流泪／想起…）

「不禁」 is a written-style adverb meaning "unable to hold back, can't help ...," expressing that an emotion or reaction wells up naturally from some stimulus. It is followed by verbs expressing emotions or physical reactions, such as 「笑了起来」, 「流下眼泪」, and 「想起」. It's close to English "can't help ..." or "couldn't stop oneself from ...," and in speech 「忍不住」 is commonly used.

「不禁」 isn't used for intentional actions (×「不禁去买东西」). In speech, it can be replaced with 「忍不住」.

Example sentences

看到这张老照片，她不禁想起了童年。
kàn dào zhè zhāng lǎo zhào piàn， tā bù jīn xiǎng qǐ le tóng nián。
Seeing this old photo, she couldn't help thinking back to her childhood.
