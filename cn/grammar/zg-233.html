「足以」: enough to, sufficient to

HSK 6

主語 + 足以 + 動詞句 ／ 不足以 + 動詞句

「足以」 is a written-style expression meaning "sufficient to ...," showing that the evidence or conditions are enough to lead to a conclusion or effect. The negative 「不足以」 means "not enough to ..." It corresponds to English "enough to ..." or "sufficient to ..." It is often followed by verbs such as 「说明」, 「证明」, 「表明」, and 「解决」.

「足以」 is followed by a verb phrase, not a noun (×「足以钱」). To say there's a sufficient amount of a noun, use 「足够」.

Example sentences

这些证据足以说明问题。
zhè xiē zhèng jù zú yǐ shuō míng wèn tí。
This evidence is enough to make the point.
