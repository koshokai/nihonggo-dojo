「难以」: hard to ...

HSK 6

主語 + 难以 + 二音節動詞（想象／接受／理解／忘记…）

「难以」 is a written-style expression meaning "hard to ...," mainly followed by a two-syllable verb. There are many fixed combinations such as 「难以想象」 ("hard to imagine"), 「难以接受」 ("hard to accept"), and 「难以忘记」 ("hard to forget"). It's close to English "hard to ..." or adjectives like "unimaginable" and "unforgettable," and sounds more formal than colloquial 「很难」 + verb.

「难以」 is basically followed by a two-syllable verb (×「难以吃」). With a one-syllable verb, say something like 「很难吃」 or 「不好吃」.

Example sentences

那次旅行给我留下了难以忘记的回忆。
nà cì lǚ xíng gěi wǒ liú xià le nán yǐ wàng jì de huí yì。
That trip left me with unforgettable memories.
