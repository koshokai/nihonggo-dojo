「一味」: blindly, single-mindedly

HSK 6

主語 + 一味 + 動詞（地 + 動詞）（，而不／却不…）

「一味」 is an adverb meaning "keep doing just one thing, ignoring everything else," usually with a critical nuance. It's close to English "blindly" or "do nothing but ..." It is often followed by verbs such as 「追求」, 「强调」, 「模仿」, and 「迁就」, and typically the second half points out the problem with 「而不…」 ("and not ...").

「一味」 doesn't suit the positive sense of "single-mindedly" (working wholeheartedly). In that case, use 「一心」 or 「专心」.

Example sentences

不能一味追求速度，而忽视了质量。
bù néng yí wèi zhuī qiú sù dù， ér hū shì le zhì liàng。
You can't just blindly pursue speed and neglect quality.
