「得以」: be able to (once conditions allow)

HSK 6

条件・助け，主語 + 得以 + 動詞句（实现／解决／完成…）

「得以」 is a written-style expression meaning "thanks to some condition or help, was able to ...," showing that something became possible to achieve. Unlike 「能」, which simply expresses ability, the first half states a cause or condition, and 「得以」 shows what was achieved as a result. It's close to English "was able to ... thanks to ..." or "made it possible to ...," and is often used in reports and opinion pieces.

「得以」 almost never takes a negative form (×「不得以」). Don't confuse it with 「不得已」 ("having no choice").

Example sentences

在大家的帮助下，这个计划得以顺利完成。
zài dà jiā de bāng zhù xià， zhè ge jì huà dé yǐ shùn lì wán chéng。
With everyone's help, this plan was completed smoothly.
