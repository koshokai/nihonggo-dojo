「在于」: lie in (essence, cause)

HSK 6

問題・関鍵・目的 + 在于 + 内容（名詞句・動詞句）

「在于」 is a written-style verb pointing to the heart of something: "the essence / cause / key point of ... lies in ..." The subject is often an abstract noun such as 「问题」, 「关键」, 「原因」, 「目的」, or 「区别」. It's close to English "... lies in ..." or "... depends on ...," and is handy for stating a conclusion clearly in opinion pieces and speeches.

The pattern 「不在于A，而在于B」 ("lies not in A but in B") is extremely common in opinion pieces.

Example sentences

成功的关键在于坚持。
chéng gōng de guān jiàn zài yú jiān chí。
The key to success lies in perseverance.
