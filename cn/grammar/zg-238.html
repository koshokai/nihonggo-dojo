「与其说A，不如说B」: not so much A as B

HSK 6

与其说 + A，不如说 + B

「与其说A，不如说B」 means "rather than calling it A, it would be more accurate to call it B," presenting a more fitting way of seeing something. It's 「与其…不如…」 ("rather than doing ..., better to ..."), which is about choosing an action, with 「说」 added, and it's used to rephrase an evaluation or judgment. It corresponds to English "not so much A as B" or "less A than B," and is often used in opinion pieces and reviews.

「与其…不如…」 is about choosing an action, while 「与其说…不如说…」 is about rephrasing a perception or evaluation. The presence or absence of 「说」 changes its role.

Example sentences

与其说他是我的老师，不如说是我的朋友。
yǔ qí shuō tā shì wǒ de lǎo shī， bù rú shuō shì wǒ de péng you。
He's not so much my teacher as my friend.
