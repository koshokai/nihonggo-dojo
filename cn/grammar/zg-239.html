「历来」: always, traditionally

HSK 6

主語 + 历来 + 動詞・形容詞（+ 都／就）

「历来」 is a written-style adverb meaning "always, from long ago until now," expressing a tendency, tradition, or policy that has remained unchanged for a long time. It's similar to 「一向」, but while 「一向」 is often used for an individual's personality or habits, 「历来」 is often used to talk about larger scopes or long histories, such as countries, regions, organizations, or fields. It's close to English "has always" or "traditionally."

「历来」 emphasizes the continuation of a long history or tradition. For an individual's recent habits, 「一向」 or 「一直」 is more natural.

Example sentences

这个地方历来以美食出名。
zhè ge dì fang lì lái yǐ měi shí chū míng。
This place has long been famous for its food.
