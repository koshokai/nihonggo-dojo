「诸如…之类」: such as ...

HSK 6

诸如 + 例A、例B（+ 等／之类的 + 名詞）

「诸如」 is a written-style expression for giving several examples, "such as ...," typically rounded off with 「等」 or 「之类」 after it to mean "... and the like." It's more formal than 「比如」 and is often used in reports and explanatory writing. It corresponds to English "such as ..." or "... and the like." Listing several examples is more natural than giving just one.

「诸如此类」 ("things of this kind / and so on") is also often used on its own as a four-character idiom.

Example sentences

诸如此类的问题，我们以后再讨论。
zhū rú cǐ lèi de wèn tí， wǒ men yǐ hòu zài tǎo lùn。
Issues like these we'll discuss later.
