一样 (yī yàng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 一樣

yī yàng the same; as ... as; like

Used in the pattern 「跟／和…一样」.

Example sentences

我和你一样大。
wǒ hé nǐ yí yàng dà。
You and I are the same age.
