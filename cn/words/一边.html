一边 (yī biān)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 一邊

yī biān (adverb) while (「一边…一边…」: doing one thing while doing another)

Example sentences

他一边吃饭一边看电视。
tā yì biān chī fàn yì biān kàn diàn shì。
He's watching TV while he eats.
