丈夫 (zhàng fu)

HSK 2

zhàng fu husband

Note: it means "husband"; it has nothing to do with being strong or sturdy.

Example sentences

她丈夫是医生。
tā zhàng fu shì yī shēng。
Her husband is a doctor.
