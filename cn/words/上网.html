上网 (shàng wǎng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 上網

shàng wǎng to go online; to use the internet

Example sentences

我晚上常上网看电影。
wǒ wǎn shang cháng shàng wǎng kàn diàn yǐng。
I often watch movies online in the evening.
