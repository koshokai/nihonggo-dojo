东西 (dōng xī)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 東西

dōng xī thing; things; stuff

With 「xī」 read in the neutral tone it means "thing"; with the first tone it means "east and west."

Example sentences

我去超市买东西。
wǒ qù chāo shì mǎi dōng xi。
I'm going to the supermarket to buy some things.
