个 (gè)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 個

gè (measure word) general measure word for people and things

An all-purpose measure word you can use when you don't know the specific one.

Example sentences

我买了三个苹果。
wǒ mǎi le sān ge píng guǒ。
I bought three apples.
