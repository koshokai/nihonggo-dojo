为了 (wèi le)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 為了

wèi le (preposition) for; in order to (purpose)

Example sentences

为了健康，我每天跑步。
wèi le jiàn kāng， wǒ měi tiān pǎo bù。
I run every day for my health.
