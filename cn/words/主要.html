主要 (zhǔ yào)

HSK 3

zhǔ yào main; major; mainly

Example sentences

我来北京主要是学习汉语。
wǒ lái běi jīng zhǔ yào shì xué xí hàn yǔ。
The main reason I came to Beijing is to study Chinese.
