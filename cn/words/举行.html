举行 (jǔ xíng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 舉行

jǔ xíng to hold (a meeting, event, etc.)

Example sentences

运动会下周举行。
yùn dòng huì xià zhōu jǔ xíng。
The sports day will be held next week.
