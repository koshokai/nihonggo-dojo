买 (mǎi)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 買

mǎi to buy

It is distinguished from 「卖 mài」 (to sell) only by tone.

Example sentences

我想买一件衣服。
wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí jiàn yī fu。
I want to buy a piece of clothing.
