了 (le)

HSK 1

le (particle) marks a completed action or a change of situation

Right after a verb it marks completion; at the end of a sentence it marks a change ("has become ...").

Example sentences

天冷了，我买了一件毛衣。
tiān lěng le， wǒ mǎi le yí jiàn máo yī。
It got cold, so I bought a sweater.
