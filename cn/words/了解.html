了解 (liǎo jiě)

HSK 3

liǎo jiě to understand; to know well; to find out

Note: it means "to know well, understand deeply"; it is not used as a reply meaning "OK, got it."

Example sentences

我很了解他。
wǒ hěn liǎo jiě tā。
I know him very well.
