以为 (yǐ wéi)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 以為

yǐ wéi to think, assume (wrongly)

Often used for a belief that turned out to be wrong.

Example sentences

我以为你是中国人。
wǒ yǐ wéi nǐ shì zhōng guó rén。
I thought you were Chinese.
