以后 (yǐ hòu)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 以後

yǐ hòu after; afterward; from now on; in the future

Example sentences

下课以后我们去吃饭吧。
xià kè yǐ hòu wǒ men qù chī fàn ba。
Let's go eat after class.
