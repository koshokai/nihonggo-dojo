会 (huì)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 會

huì (auxiliary verb) can; know how to (a learned skill); will; be likely to

Use 「会」 for skills acquired through practice.

Example sentences

我会说一点儿汉语。
wǒ huì shuō yì diǎnr hàn yǔ。
I can speak a little Chinese.
