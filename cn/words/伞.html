伞 (sǎn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 傘

sǎn umbrella

The measure word is 「把」.

Example sentences

外面下雨了，带把伞吧。
wài miàn xià yǔ le， dài bǎ sǎn ba。
It's raining out. Take an umbrella.
