但是 (dàn shì)

HSK 2

dàn shì (conjunction) but; however

Example sentences

这件衣服很好看，但是太贵了。
zhè jiàn yī fu hěn hǎo kàn， dàn shì tài guì le。
This outfit looks nice, but it's too expensive.
