位 (wèi)

HSK 3

wèi (measure word) for people (polite)

「几位？」 is how restaurant staff ask how many are in your party.

Example sentences

那位老师是我们的校长。
nà wèi lǎo shī shì wǒ men de xiào zhǎng。
That teacher is our principal.
