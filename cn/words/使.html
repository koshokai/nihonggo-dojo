使 (shǐ)

HSK 3

shǐ to make; to cause; to use

More literary than 「让」.

Example sentences

这个消息使大家很高兴。
zhè ge xiāo xi shǐ dà jiā hěn gāo xìng。
The news made everyone very happy.
