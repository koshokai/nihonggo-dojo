便宜 (pián yi)

HSK 2

pián yi cheap; inexpensive

Note: as an everyday word it means "cheap," not "convenience" or "benefit."

Example sentences

这里的水果很便宜。
zhè lǐ de shuǐ guǒ hěn pián yi。
Fruit is cheap here.
