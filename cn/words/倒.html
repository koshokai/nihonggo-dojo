倒 (dǎo)

HSK 5

dǎo (dǎo) to fall; (dào) to pour; to turn upside down; (adverb) on the contrary; unexpectedly

The tones differ: 「dǎo」 "to fall" versus 「dào」 "to pour; reverse".
