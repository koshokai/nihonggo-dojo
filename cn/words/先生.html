先生 (xiān sheng)

HSK 1

xiān sheng Mr. (title of respect for a man); husband

Note: it does not mean "teacher." A teacher is 「老师」.

Example sentences

王先生，您好！
wáng xiān sheng， nín hǎo！
Hello, Mr. Wang!
