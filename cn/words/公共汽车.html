公共汽车 (gōng gòng qì chē)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 公共汽車

gōng gòng qì chē bus

Note: 「汽车」 means "car" (automobile), not "train." In everyday speech a bus is also called 「公交车」.

Example sentences

我坐公共汽车去学校。
wǒ zuò gōng gòng qì chē qù xué xiào。
I take the bus to school.
