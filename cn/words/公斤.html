公斤 (gōng jīn)

HSK 2

gōng jīn kilogram

One 「斤」 is 500 grams.

Example sentences

我买了两公斤苹果。
wǒ mǎi le liǎng gōng jīn píng guǒ。
I bought two kilos of apples.
