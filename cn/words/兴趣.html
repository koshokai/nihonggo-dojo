兴趣 (xìng qù)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 興趣

xìng qù interest

Often used in the pattern 「对…感兴趣」.

Example sentences

我对做菜很有兴趣。
wǒ duì zuò cài hěn yǒu xìng qù。
I'm really interested in cooking.
