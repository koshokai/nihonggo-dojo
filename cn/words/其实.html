其实 (qí shí)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 其實

qí shí (adverb) actually; in fact

Example sentences

其实我不太喜欢吃辣的。
qí shí wǒ bú tài xǐ huan chī là de。
Actually, I don't really like spicy food.
