几乎 (jī hū)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 幾乎

jī hū (adverb) almost; nearly

Example sentences

我几乎每天都喝咖啡。
wǒ jī hū měi tiān dōu hē kā fēi。
I drink coffee almost every day.
