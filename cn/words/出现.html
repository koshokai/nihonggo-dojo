出现 (chū xiàn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 出現

chū xiàn to appear; to emerge

Example sentences

天上出现了一道彩虹。
tiān shàng chū xiàn le yí dào cǎi hóng。
A rainbow appeared in the sky.
