分 (fēn)

HSK 3

fēn to divide; to separate; minute (of time); point (score)

Also used for the money unit 「分」 (one hundredth of a yuan).

Example sentences

现在是八点十分。
xiàn zài shì bā diǎn shí fēn。
It's 8:10 now.
