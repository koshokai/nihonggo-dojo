刷 (shuā)

HSK 3

shuā to brush; to scrub; to swipe (a card)

「刷卡」 means to pay by card.

Example sentences

我每天刷两次牙。
wǒ měi tiān shuā liǎng cì yá。
I brush my teeth twice a day.
