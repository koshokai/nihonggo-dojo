副 (fù)

HSK 6

fù deputy; vice-; assistant; (measure word) for pairs or sets of things, and for facial expressions

As a measure word: 「眼镜」 (glasses), 「手套」 (gloves), 「对联」 (couplets), 「笑脸」 (a smiling face), etc.
