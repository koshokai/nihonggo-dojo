办法 (bàn fǎ)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 辦法

bàn fǎ way; method; means

「没办法」 means "There's nothing we can do."

Example sentences

我们一起想想办法吧。
wǒ men yì qǐ xiǎng xiang bàn fǎ ba。
Let's think of a solution together.
