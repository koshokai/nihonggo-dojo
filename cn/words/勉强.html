勉强 (miǎn qiǎng)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 勉強

miǎn qiǎng to force oneself; reluctantly; to force (someone); barely

Does not mean "to study": it means "to force oneself" or "reluctantly." "To study" is 「学习」.
