包 (bāo)

HSK 3

bāo bag; to wrap; (measure word) pack; package

「包饺子」 means to make (wrap) dumplings.

Example sentences

我的包里有一本书。
wǒ de bāo lǐ yǒu yì běn shū。
There's a book in my bag.
