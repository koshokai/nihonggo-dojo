医院 (yī yuàn)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 醫院

yī yuàn hospital

Note: this is the general word for any hospital, including large ones, not just a small clinic.

Example sentences

我去医院看病。
wǒ qù yī yuàn kàn bìng。
I'm going to the hospital to see a doctor.
