参加 (cān jiā)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 參加

cān jiā to take part (in); to participate; to attend

Example sentences

你参加明天的会议吗？
nǐ cān jiā míng tiān de huì yì ma？
Are you going to tomorrow's meeting?
