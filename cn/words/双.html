双 (shuāng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 雙

shuāng (measure word) pair (shoes, chopsticks, etc.)

Example sentences

我买了一双新鞋。
wǒ mǎi le yì shuāng xīn xié。
I bought a new pair of shoes.
