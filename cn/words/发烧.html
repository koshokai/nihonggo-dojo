发烧 (fā shāo)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 發燒

fā shāo to have a fever

Example sentences

孩子发烧了，要去医院。
hái zi fā shāo le， yào qù yī yuàn。
My child has a fever; we need to go to the hospital.
