发现 (fā xiàn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 發現

fā xiàn to discover; to find; to notice

Example sentences

我发现他今天很高兴。
wǒ fā xiàn tā jīn tiān hěn gāo xìng。
I noticed that he was very happy today.
