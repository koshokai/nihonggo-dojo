叔叔 (shū shu)

HSK 3

shū shu uncle (father's younger brother); (used to address an adult man)

Also used by children to address adult men.

Example sentences

叔叔，请问去车站怎么走？
shū shu， qǐng wèn qù chē zhàn zěn me zǒu？
Excuse me, sir, how do I get to the station?
