只 (zhǐ)

HSK 3

zhǐ (adverb) only; just; (measure word) for animals and other things

As a measure word it is read 「zhī」: 「一只猫」.

Example sentences

我只有一百块钱。
wǒ zhǐ yǒu yì bǎi kuài qián。
I only have a hundred yuan.
