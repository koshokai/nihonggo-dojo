告诉 (gào su)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 告訴

gào su to tell; to let (someone) know

Example sentences

请告诉我你的电话号码。
qǐng gào su wǒ nǐ de diàn huà hào mǎ。
Please tell me your phone number.
