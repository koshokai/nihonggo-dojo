和 (hé)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 咊

hé (conjunction) and; (preposition) with

It joins nouns; it is not used to join clauses or sentences.

Example sentences

我和朋友去吃饭。
wǒ hé péng you qù chī fàn。
I'm going out to eat with a friend.
