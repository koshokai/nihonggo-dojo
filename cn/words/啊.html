啊 (a)

HSK 3

a (particle) at the end of a sentence, shows exclamation or emphasis; ah; oh

Depending on the preceding sound, it may change to 「呀」, 「哪」, and so on.

Example sentences

今天的天气真好啊！
jīn tiān de tiān qì zhēn hǎo a！
What great weather today!
