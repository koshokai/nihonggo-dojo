因为 (yīn wèi)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 因為

yīn wèi (conjunction) because

Often used in the pattern 「因为…所以…」.

Example sentences

因为下雨，我没去。
yīn wèi xià yǔ， wǒ méi qù。
It was raining, so I didn't go.
