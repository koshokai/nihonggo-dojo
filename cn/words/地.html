地 (de)

HSK 3

de (particle) forms an adverbial phrase that modifies a verb

Read 「de」. In the sense "land, ground," it is read 「dì」.

Example sentences

他高兴地笑了。
tā gāo xìng de xiào le。
He smiled happily.
