地方 (dì fang)

HSK 3

dì fang place

Note: 「地方」 means "place"; the sense "the provinces, outlying areas" is less common.

Example sentences

这个地方很漂亮。
zhè ge dì fang hěn piào liang。
This place is beautiful.
