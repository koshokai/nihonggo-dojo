复习 (fù xí)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 復習

fù xí to review

To prepare a lesson in advance is 「预习」.

Example sentences

考试前要好好儿复习。
kǎo shì qián yào hǎo hǎor fù xí。
Review thoroughly before the exam.
