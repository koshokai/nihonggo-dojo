奶奶 (nǎi nai)

HSK 3

nǎi nai grandma (father's mother)

A mother's mother is 「姥姥」 or 「外婆」.

Example sentences

我奶奶今年八十岁了。
wǒ nǎi nai jīn nián bā shí suì le。
My grandma is eighty this year.
