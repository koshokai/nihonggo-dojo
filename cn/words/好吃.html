好吃 (hǎo chī)

HSK 2

hǎo chī delicious; tasty

For drinks, use 「好喝」.

Example sentences

妈妈做的菜很好吃。
mā ma zuò de cài hěn hǎo chī。
The food Mom makes is delicious.
