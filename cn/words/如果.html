如果 (rú guǒ)

HSK 3

rú guǒ (conjunction) if

Often paired with 「就」 in the following clause.

Example sentences

如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。
rú guǒ míng tiān xià yǔ， wǒ men jiù bú qù le。
If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
