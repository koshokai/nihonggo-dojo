姓 (xìng)

HSK 2

xìng to be surnamed; surname

To ask someone's surname politely, say 「您贵姓？」.

Example sentences

您贵姓？——我姓田中。
nín guì xìng？—— wǒ xìng tián zhōng。
"May I ask your surname?" "Tanaka."
