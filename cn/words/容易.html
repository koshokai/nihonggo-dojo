容易 (róng yì)

HSK 3

róng yì easy; likely to; apt to

Note: it is an everyday word, not a formal one, and it also means "likely to; apt to."

Example sentences

这个问题很容易。
zhè ge wèn tí hěn róng yì。
This question is easy.
