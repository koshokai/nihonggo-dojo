宾馆 (bīn guǎn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 賓館

bīn guǎn hotel

Example sentences

我们住在火车站附近的宾馆。
wǒ men zhù zài huǒ chē zhàn fù jìn de bīn guǎn。
We're staying at a hotel near the train station.
