小姐 (xiǎo jie)

HSK 1

xiǎo jie Miss (when addressing a young woman); young lady

To call a waiter or waitress, 「服务员」 is the safer choice today.

Example sentences

李小姐，你好！
lǐ xiǎo jie， nǐ hǎo！
Hello, Miss Li!
