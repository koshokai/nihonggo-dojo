就 (jiù)

HSK 2

jiù (adverb) right away; already; exactly; then (in that case)

Conveys a sense of "early" or "soon"; it is the counterpart of 「才」.

Example sentences

我马上就来。
wǒ mǎ shàng jiù lái。
I'll be right there.
