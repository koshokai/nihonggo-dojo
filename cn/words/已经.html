已经 (yǐ jīng)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 已經

yǐ jīng (adverb) already

Often used together with 「了」 at the end of the sentence.

Example sentences

我已经吃饭了。
wǒ yǐ jīng chī fàn le。
I've already eaten.
