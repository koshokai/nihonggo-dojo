帮忙 (bāng máng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 幫忙

bāng máng to help; to do a favor

It is a separable verb, so you don't say 「帮忙我」; say 「帮我个忙」.

Example sentences

你能帮个忙吗？
nǐ néng bāng ge máng ma？
Could you do me a favor?
