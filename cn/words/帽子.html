帽子 (mào zi)

HSK 3

mào zi hat; cap

To put on or wear a hat is 「戴」.

Example sentences

外面太阳很大，戴上帽子吧。
wài miàn tài yang hěn dà， dài shàng mào zi ba。
The sun is really strong out there. Put on a hat.
