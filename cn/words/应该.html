应该 (yīng gāi)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 應該

yīng gāi (auxiliary verb) should; ought to; must (be)

Example sentences

你应该早点儿休息。
nǐ yīng gāi zǎo diǎnr xiū xi。
You should get some rest early.
