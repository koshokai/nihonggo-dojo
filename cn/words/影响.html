影响 (yǐng xiǎng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 影響

yǐng xiǎng influence; effect; to influence; to affect

Example sentences

天气不好会影响心情。
tiān qì bù hǎo huì yǐng xiǎng xīn qíng。
Bad weather can affect your mood.
