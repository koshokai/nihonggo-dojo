很 (hěn)

HSK 1

hěn (adverb) very

Before an adjective used as a predicate, it is often added just to make the sentence sound natural rather than for emphasis.

Example sentences

这个菜很好吃。
zhè ge cài hěn hǎo chī。
This dish is really good.
