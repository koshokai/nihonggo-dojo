得 (de)

HSK 2

de (particle) used after a verb to introduce its degree or result

Read 「de」. In the sense "must," it is read 「děi」.

Example sentences

他汉语说得很好。
tā hàn yǔ shuō de hěn hǎo。
He speaks Chinese very well.
