心眼儿 (xīn yǎnr)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 心眼兒

xīn yǎnr mind; intentions; consideration; wits; tolerance

「心眼儿小」 means "narrow-minded"; 「缺心眼儿」 means "dim-witted" or "a bit slow."
