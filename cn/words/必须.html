必须 (bì xū)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 必須

bì xū (adverb) must; have to

The negative is 「不必」 or 「不用」.

Example sentences

明天你必须早点儿来。
míng tiān nǐ bì xū zǎo diǎnr lái。
You must come a bit early tomorrow.
