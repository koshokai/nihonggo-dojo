感冒 (gǎn mào)

HSK 3

gǎn mào cold (illness); to catch a cold

Example sentences

我感冒了，今天想在家休息。
wǒ gǎn mào le， jīn tiān xiǎng zài jiā xiū xi。
I've caught a cold, so I want to stay home and rest today.
