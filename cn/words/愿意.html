愿意 (yuàn yì)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 願意

yuàn yì to be willing to; to be glad to; to want to

Example sentences

我很愿意帮助你。
wǒ hěn yuàn yì bāng zhù nǐ。
I'm glad to help you.
