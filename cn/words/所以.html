所以 (suǒ yǐ)

HSK 2

suǒ yǐ (conjunction) so; therefore

Example sentences

我感冒了，所以没去上课。
wǒ gǎn mào le， suǒ yǐ méi qù shàng kè。
I had a cold, so I didn't go to class.
