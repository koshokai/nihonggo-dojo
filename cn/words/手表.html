手表 (shǒu biǎo)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 手錶

shǒu biǎo wristwatch; watch

The measure word is 「块」.

Example sentences

这块手表是我爸爸送的。
zhè kuài shǒu biǎo shì wǒ bà ba sòng de。
My dad gave me this watch.
