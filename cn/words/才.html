才 (cái)

HSK 3

cái (adverb) only then; not until; just (a moment ago)

Conveys a sense of "late" or "finally"; it is the counterpart of 「就」.

Example sentences

他十点才起床。
tā shí diǎn cái qǐ chuáng。
He didn't get up until ten.
