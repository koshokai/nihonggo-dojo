打扫 (dǎ sǎo)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 打掃

dǎ sǎo to clean; to sweep

Example sentences

周末我打扫房间。
zhōu mò wǒ dǎ sǎo fáng jiān。
I clean my room on weekends.
