打篮球 (dǎ lán qiú)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 打籃球

dǎ lán qiú to play basketball

Ball games take 「打」; soccer takes 「踢」.

Example sentences

下课后我们去打篮球吧。
xià kè hòu wǒ men qù dǎ lán qiú ba。
Let's go play basketball after class.
