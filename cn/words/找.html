找 (zhǎo)

HSK 2

zhǎo to look for; to visit (someone); to give (change)

「找你五块」 means "Here's five yuan change."

Example sentences

我在找我的钥匙。
wǒ zài zhǎo wǒ de yào shi。
I'm looking for my keys.
