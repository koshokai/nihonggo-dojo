把 (bǎ)

HSK 3

bǎ (preposition) marks the object, moving it before the verb; (measure word) for things with a handle

In a 「把」 sentence, the verb must be followed by something more, such as a result or direction.

Example sentences

请把门关上。
qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng。
Please close the door.
