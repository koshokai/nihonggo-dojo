报纸 (bào zhǐ)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 報紙

bào zhǐ newspaper

Note: "newspaper" is 「报纸」; 「新闻」 means "news."

Example sentences

爸爸每天早上看报纸。
bà ba měi tiān zǎo shang kàn bào zhǐ。
Dad reads the newspaper every morning.
