拔苗助长 (bá miáo zhù zhǎng)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 拔苗助長

bá miáo zhù zhǎng to pull up seedlings to help them grow; to spoil things through impatience

The source of the word 「助长」 ("to encourage; to foster," usually something bad). Also 「揠苗助长」.
