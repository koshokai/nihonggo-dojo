拿 (ná)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 㧱

ná to hold; to take; to pick up

Example sentences

请帮我拿一下这个包。
qǐng bāng wǒ ná yí xià zhè ge bāo。
Could you hold this bag for me for a sec?
