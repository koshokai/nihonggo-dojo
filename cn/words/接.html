接 (jiē)

HSK 3

jiē to pick up; to meet (someone); to receive; to answer (the phone)

「接电话」 means to answer the phone.

Example sentences

我去机场接朋友。
wǒ qù jī chǎng jiē péng you。
I'm going to the airport to pick up a friend.
