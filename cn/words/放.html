放 (fàng)

HSK 3

fàng to put; to place; to release; to let go; to start (a break or holiday)

「放假」 means to have a break or holiday from school or work.

Example sentences

把书放在桌子上吧。
bǎ shū fàng zài zhuō zi shàng ba。
Put the books on the desk.
