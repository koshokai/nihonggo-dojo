放心 (fàng xīn)

HSK 3

fàng xīn to be at ease; to stop worrying

Example sentences

放心吧，我会照顾好自己的。
fàng xīn ba， wǒ huì zhào gu hǎo zì jǐ de。
Don't worry. I'll take good care of myself.
