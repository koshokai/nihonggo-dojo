故事 (gù shì)

HSK 3

gù shì story; tale

Note: it is a general word for "story," not specifically the tale behind an idiom or saying.

Example sentences

这个故事很有意思。
zhè ge gù shì hěn yǒu yì si。
This story is really interesting.
