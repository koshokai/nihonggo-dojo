新闻 (xīn wén)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 新聞

xīn wén news

Note: it means "news," not "newspaper." A newspaper is 「报纸」.

Example sentences

我每天晚上看新闻。
wǒ měi tiān wǎn shang kàn xīn wén。
I watch the news every evening.
