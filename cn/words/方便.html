方便 (fāng biàn)

HSK 3

fāng biàn convenient

「方便的时候」 means "whenever it's convenient for you."

Example sentences

这里交通很方便。
zhè lǐ jiāo tōng hěn fāng biàn。
Getting around is very convenient here.
