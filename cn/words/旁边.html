旁边 (páng biān)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 旁邊

páng biān side; next to; beside

Example sentences

银行旁边有一家咖啡店。
yín háng páng biān yǒu yì jiā kā fēi diàn。
There's a café next to the bank.
