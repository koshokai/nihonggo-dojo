服务员 (fú wù yuán)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 服務員

fú wù yuán waiter; waitress; attendant; server

Also used to call a server or attendant in a restaurant or shop.

Example sentences

服务员，请给我菜单。
fú wù yuán， qǐng gěi wǒ cài dān。
Excuse me, could I have the menu, please?
