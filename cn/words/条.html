条 (tiáo)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 條

tiáo (measure word) for long, narrow things (roads, rivers, fish, pants, etc.)

Example sentences

我买了一条裤子。
wǒ mǎi le yì tiáo kù zi。
I bought a pair of pants.
