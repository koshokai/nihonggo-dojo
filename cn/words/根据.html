根据 (gēn jù)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 根據

gēn jù according to; based on; basis

Example sentences

根据天气预报，明天有雨。
gēn jù tiān qì yù bào， míng tiān yǒu yǔ。
According to the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow.
