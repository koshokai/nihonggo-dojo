检查 (jiǎn chá)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 檢查

jiǎn chá to examine; to check; to inspect

Example sentences

你去医院检查一下身体吧。
nǐ qù yī yuàn jiǎn chá yí xià shēn tǐ ba。
Why don't you go to the hospital for a checkup?
