检讨 (jiǎn tǎo)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 檢討

jiǎn tǎo to make a self-criticism; to reflect on one's mistakes; written self-criticism

Does not mean "to review" or "to consider": it means to admit one's mistakes and criticize oneself. To discuss or consider something is 「讨论」 or 「研究」.
