比 (bǐ)

HSK 2

bǐ (preposition) than (comparison)

A 「比」 B + adjective. Don't add 「很」.

Example sentences

今天比昨天冷。
jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān lěng。
Today is colder than yesterday.
