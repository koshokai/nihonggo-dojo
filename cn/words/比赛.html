比赛 (bǐ sài)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 比賽

bǐ sài match; competition; to compete

Example sentences

今天晚上有足球比赛。
jīn tiān wǎn shang yǒu zú qiú bǐ sài。
There's a soccer game tonight.
