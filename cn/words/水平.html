水平 (shuǐ píng)

HSK 3

shuǐ píng level; standard

Note: it mainly means "level" or "standard," not "horizontal."

Example sentences

他的汉语水平很高。
tā de hàn yǔ shuǐ píng hěn gāo。
His Chinese is at a high level.
