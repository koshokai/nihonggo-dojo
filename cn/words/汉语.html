汉语 (hàn yǔ)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 漢語

hàn yǔ Chinese (language)

In everyday speech, 「中文」 is also common.

Example sentences

你的汉语说得很好。
nǐ de hàn yǔ shuō de hěn hǎo。
You speak Chinese really well.
