没关系 (méi guān xi)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 沒關係

méi guān xi it's OK; it doesn't matter; never mind

Used as a reply to 「对不起」.

Example sentences

没关系，不用谢。
méi guān xi， bú yòng xiè。
It's OK, no need to thank me.
