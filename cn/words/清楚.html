清楚 (qīng chu)

HSK 3

qīng chu clear; distinct; to know well

Example sentences

我听不清楚，请再说一遍。
wǒ tīng bù qīng chu， qǐng zài shuō yí biàn。
I can't hear you clearly. Please say that again.
