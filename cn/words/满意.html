满意 (mǎn yì)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 滿意

mǎn yì satisfied; pleased

Example sentences

我对这个房间很满意。
wǒ duì zhè ge fáng jiān hěn mǎn yì。
I'm very happy with this room.
