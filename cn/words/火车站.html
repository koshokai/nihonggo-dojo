火车站 (huǒ chē zhàn)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 火車站

huǒ chē zhàn train station

A subway station is 「地铁站」.

Example sentences

火车站离这儿远吗？
huǒ chē zhàn lí zhèr yuǎn ma？
Is the train station far from here?
