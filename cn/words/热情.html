热情 (rè qíng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 熱情

rè qíng warm; friendly; enthusiastic; enthusiasm

Often used for a warm, kind attitude toward people.

Example sentences

中国朋友对我很热情。
zhōng guó péng you duì wǒ hěn rè qíng。
My Chinese friends are very warm and kind to me.
