照顾 (zhào gu)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 照顧

zhào gu to take care of; to look after

Example sentences

谢谢你照顾我的猫。
xiè xie nǐ zhào gu wǒ de māo。
Thanks for taking care of my cat.
