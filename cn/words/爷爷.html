爷爷 (yé ye)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 爺爺

yé ye grandpa (father's father)

A mother's father is 「姥爷」 or 「外公」.

Example sentences

我爷爷每天早上去公园散步。
wǒ yé ye měi tiān zǎo shang qù gōng yuán sàn bù。
My grandpa takes a walk in the park every morning.
