生气 (shēng qì)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 生氣

shēng qì to get angry; to be angry

Note: in everyday use it means "to get angry," not "vitality."

Example sentences

你别生气了。
nǐ bié shēng qì le。
Please don't be angry anymore.
