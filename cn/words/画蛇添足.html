画蛇添足 (huà shé tiān zú)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 畫蛇添足

huà shé tiān zú to draw legs on a snake; to ruin something by adding unnecessary details

The source of the expression 「蛇足」 ("snake legs"), meaning something superfluous.
