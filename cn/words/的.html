的 (de)

HSK 1

de (particle) of; 's; links a modifier to a noun

It is often omitted before family members or groups one belongs to, as in 「我妈妈」 and 「我们学校」.

Example sentences

这是我的书。
zhè shì wǒ de shū。
This is my book.
