看见 (kàn jiàn)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 看見

kàn jiàn to see; to catch sight of

「看」 is the act of looking; 「看见」 expresses the result: actually seeing something.

Example sentences

我看见他了。
wǒ kàn jiàn tā le。
I saw him.
