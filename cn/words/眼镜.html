眼镜 (yǎn jìng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 眼鏡

yǎn jìng glasses; eyeglasses

Example sentences

我看书的时候戴眼镜。
wǒ kàn shū de shí hou dài yǎn jìng。
I wear glasses when I read.
