着 (zhe)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 著

zhe (particle) marks a continuing action or state

Its basic use is to show a continuing state rather than an action in progress.

Example sentences

门一直开着呢。
mén yì zhí kāi zhe ne。
The door has been open the whole time.
