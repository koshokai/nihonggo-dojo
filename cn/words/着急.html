着急 (zháo jí)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 著急

zháo jí to be anxious; to be impatient; to be in a hurry

Example sentences

别着急，慢慢说。
bié zháo jí， màn màn shuō。
Don't panic. Say it slowly.
