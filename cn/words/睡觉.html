睡觉 (shuì jiào)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 睡覺

shuì jiào to sleep; to go to bed

Example sentences

我每天十一点睡觉。
wǒ měi tiān shí yī diǎn shuì jiào。
I go to bed at eleven every day.
