知道 (zhī dào)

HSK 2

zhī dào to know; to be aware of

For knowing a person you have met, use 「认识」.

Example sentences

我不知道他的电话号码。
wǒ bù zhī dao tā de diàn huà hào mǎ。
I don't know his phone number.
