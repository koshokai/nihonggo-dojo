碗 (wǎn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 㼝

wǎn bowl; (measure word) bowl (of)

Example sentences

我吃了两碗米饭。
wǒ chī le liǎng wǎn mǐ fàn。
I ate two bowls of rice.
