礼物 (lǐ wù)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 禮物

lǐ wù gift; present

Example sentences

这是送给你的生日礼物。
zhè shì sòng gěi nǐ de shēng rì lǐ wù。
This is a birthday present for you.
