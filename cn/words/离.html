离 (lí)

HSK 2

lí (preposition) from (distance or separation)

Unlike 「从」, which marks a starting point, it expresses the distance between two points.

Example sentences

学校离我家很近。
xué xiào lí wǒ jiā hěn jìn。
The school is close to my home.
