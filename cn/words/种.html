种 (zhǒng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 種

zhǒng (measure word) kind; type; sort

Example sentences

这种水果很好吃。
zhè zhǒng shuǐ guǒ hěn hǎo chī。
This kind of fruit is delicious.
