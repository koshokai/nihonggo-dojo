空调 (kōng tiáo)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 空調

kōng tiáo air conditioner; air conditioning

Example sentences

房间里太热了，开空调吧。
fáng jiān lǐ tài rè le， kāi kōng tiáo ba。
It's too hot in here. Let's turn on the AC.
