穿 (chuān)

HSK 2

chuān to wear; to put on

Used for clothes, shoes, and socks alike.

Example sentences

今天很冷，多穿点儿衣服。
jīn tiān hěn lěng， duō chuān diǎnr yī fu。
It's cold today, so put on some extra layers.
