端午节 (duān wǔ jié)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 端午節

duān wǔ jié Dragon Boat Festival (5th day of the 5th lunar month)

It follows the lunar calendar, so it does not fall on May 5 of the Western calendar (the fixed date of the related festival in Japan).
