素质 (sù zhì)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 素質

sù zhì quality; (personal) caliber; manners; cultivation

Does not mean "natural aptitude": it refers to a person's caliber, manners, and cultivation. 「没素质」 = to have bad manners.
