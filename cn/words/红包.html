红包 (hóng bāo)

HSK 6 · Traditional: 紅包

hóng bāo red envelope (of money given at New Year or celebrations); (illicit) payment; kickback

Today, digital red envelopes such as 「微信红包」 (WeChat red envelopes) are also common.
