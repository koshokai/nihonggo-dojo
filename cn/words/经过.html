经过 (jīng guò)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 經過

jīng guò to pass (by or through); to go through; process; course (of events)

Example sentences

我每天上班都经过这家书店。
wǒ měi tiān shàng bān dōu jīng guò zhè jiā shū diàn。
I pass this bookstore every day on my way to work.
