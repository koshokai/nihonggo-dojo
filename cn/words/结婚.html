结婚 (jié hūn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 結婚

jié hūn to get married; to marry

「跟…结婚」. You can't say 「结婚他」.

Example sentences

他们明年结婚。
tā men míng nián jié hūn。
They're getting married next year.
