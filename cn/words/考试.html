考试 (kǎo shì)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 考試

kǎo shì exam; test; to take an exam

Example sentences

明天有汉语考试。
míng tiān yǒu hàn yǔ kǎo shì。
There's a Chinese exam tomorrow.
