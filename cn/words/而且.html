而且 (ér qiě)

HSK 3

ér qiě (conjunction) moreover; and also; besides

Often used in the pattern 「不但…而且…」.

Example sentences

这家店很便宜，而且很好吃。
zhè jiā diàn hěn pián yi， ér qiě hěn hǎo chī。
This place is cheap, and the food is good too.
