自行车 (zì xíng chē)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 自行車

zì xíng chē bicycle; bike

Note: 「自行车」 is a bicycle, not a motor vehicle. To ride one is 「骑」.

Example sentences

我骑自行车上班。
wǒ qí zì xíng chē shàng bān。
I ride my bike to work.
