舒服 (shū fu)

HSK 3

shū fu comfortable; feeling good

「不舒服」 means "not feeling well."

Example sentences

洗个热水澡，真舒服。
xǐ ge rè shuǐ zǎo， zhēn shū fu。
A hot bath feels so good.
