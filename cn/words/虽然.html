虽然 (suī rán)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 雖然

suī rán (conjunction) although; though

Used in the pattern 「虽然…但是…」.

Example sentences

虽然很累，但是很开心。
suī rán hěn lèi， dàn shì hěn kāi xīn。
I was tired, but I had a great time.
