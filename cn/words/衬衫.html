衬衫 (chèn shān)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 襯衫

chèn shān shirt; dress shirt

Example sentences

他穿着一件白衬衫。
tā chuān zhe yí jiàn bái chèn shān。
He's wearing a white shirt.
