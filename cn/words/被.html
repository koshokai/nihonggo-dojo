被 (bèi)

HSK 3

bèi (preposition) by (marks the passive)

Mostly used for unwelcome events.

Example sentences

我的蛋糕被弟弟吃了。
wǒ de dàn gāo bèi dì di chī le。
My cake got eaten by my younger brother.
