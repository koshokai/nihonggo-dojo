要求 (yāo qiú)

HSK 3

yāo qiú to require; to demand; to ask; requirement

Example sentences

老师要求我们每天写日记。
lǎo shī yāo qiú wǒ men měi tiān xiě rì jì。
Our teacher requires us to keep a diary every day.
