见面 (jiàn miàn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 見面

jiàn miàn to meet; to see each other

A separable verb. Say 「跟／和」 + person + 「见面」, not 「见面他」.

Example sentences

我们明天在车站见面吧。
wǒ men míng tiān zài chē zhàn jiàn miàn ba。
Let's meet at the station tomorrow.
