角 (jiǎo)

HSK 3

jiǎo corner; angle; jiao (money unit: one tenth of a yuan)

In speech, 「毛」 is used.

Example sentences

一斤苹果五块五角。
yì jīn píng guǒ wǔ kuài wǔ jiǎo。
Apples are five yuan five jiao per jin.
