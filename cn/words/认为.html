认为 (rèn wéi)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 認為

rèn wéi to think; to believe; to consider

More formal than 「觉得」.

Example sentences

我认为这个办法很好。
wǒ rèn wéi zhè ge bàn fǎ hěn hǎo。
I think this is a good method.
