认识 (rèn shi)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 認識

rèn shi to get to know; to know (a person); to recognize

For knowing a person you have met, use 「认识」, not 「知道」.

Example sentences

很高兴认识你。
hěn gāo xìng rèn shi nǐ。
Nice to meet you.
