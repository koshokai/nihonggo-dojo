讲 (jiǎng)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 講

jiǎng to speak; to explain; to tell (a story)

Example sentences

奶奶给我讲了一个故事。
nǎi nai gěi wǒ jiǎng le yí ge gù shì。
Grandma told me a story.
