请 (qǐng)

HSK 1 · Traditional: 請

qǐng please (do ...); to treat (someone to a meal); to invite

「我请你」 means "It's my treat."

Example sentences

请坐，请喝茶。
qǐng zuò， qǐng hē chá。
Please have a seat, and have some tea.
