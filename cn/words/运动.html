运动 (yùn dòng)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 運動

yùn dòng exercise; sports; to exercise

Example sentences

我每天都运动一个小时。
wǒ měi tiān dōu yùn dòng yí ge xiǎo shí。
I exercise for an hour every day.
