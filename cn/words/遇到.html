遇到 (yù dào)

HSK 3

yù dào to run into; to meet; to encounter (a problem, etc.)

Example sentences

我在路上遇到了老同学。
wǒ zài lù shàng yù dào le lǎo tóng xué。
I ran into an old classmate on the street.
