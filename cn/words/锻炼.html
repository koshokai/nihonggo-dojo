锻炼 (duàn liàn)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 鍛鍊

duàn liàn to work out; to exercise; to train

Example sentences

爸爸每天早上锻炼身体。
bà ba měi tiān zǎo shang duàn liàn shēn tǐ。
Dad works out every morning.
