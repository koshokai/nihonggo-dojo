非常 (fēi cháng)

HSK 2

fēi cháng (adverb) extremely; very

Note: it is an adverb that intensifies degree; it does not mean "emergency."

Example sentences

这里的风景非常美。
zhè lǐ de fēng jǐng fēi cháng měi。
The scenery here is really beautiful.
