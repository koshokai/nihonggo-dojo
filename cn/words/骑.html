骑 (qí)

HSK 3 · Traditional: 騎

qí to ride (astride)

Used for bicycles, motorcycles, and horses. For cars, use 「坐」 or 「开」.

Example sentences

我骑自行车去学校。
wǒ qí zì xíng chē qù xué xiào。
I ride my bike to school.
