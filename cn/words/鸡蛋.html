鸡蛋 (jī dàn)

HSK 2 · Traditional: 雞蛋

jī dàn egg (chicken egg)

Example sentences

我早上吃了两个鸡蛋。
wǒ zǎo shang chī le liǎng ge jī dàn。
I ate two eggs this morning.
