英语每日一句 · 11月30日 · 季节

Winter is just around the corner.

冬天马上就要来了。

around the corner 表示“即将到来”，时间上很近。也可以说 The holidays are just around the corner.

例句

The trees are bare now. Winter is just around the corner.
树叶都掉光了，冬天马上就要来了。
