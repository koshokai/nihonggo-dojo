both

/boʊθ/ · CEFR A1

adv（both…and）既…又…；两者都

det两个都；双方的

pron两者；两个都

例句

She is both smart and kind.
她既聪明又善良。
Both my parents are teachers at my school.
我父母都是我们学校的老师。
I have two brothers, and both of them play soccer.
我有两个哥哥，他们俩都踢足球。
