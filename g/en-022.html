动词 + to do（不定式）

想/决定/希望做…… · 基础

want、hope、decide、plan、agree、learn、would like 等动词后接 to + 动词原形；否定形式是 not to do。

例句

I want to be a teacher in the future.
我将来想当老师。
They decided not to go out because of the rain.
因为下雨，他们决定不出去了。
Would you like to join us for dinner?
你愿意和我们一起吃晚饭吗？
