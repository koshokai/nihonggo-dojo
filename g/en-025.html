so … that / such … that

如此……以至于…… · 基础

so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句；such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + that 从句。

例句

I was so tired that I fell asleep on the sofa.
我累得在沙发上睡着了。
It was such a nice day that we went for a picnic.
天气那么好，我们就去野餐了。
She spoke so fast that nobody could follow her.
她说得太快，谁都跟不上。
