no matter what / whatever 等让步从句

无论…… · 四级

no matter what / who / how / when / where 引导让步状语从句，相当于 whatever / whoever / however / whenever / wherever；however 后常接形容词或副词。

例句

No matter what happens, I'll support you.
无论发生什么，我都会支持你。
However hard he tried, he couldn't open the jar.
不管他怎么用力，就是打不开那个罐子。
You can sit wherever you like.
你想坐哪儿都行。
