although / even though 与 despite / in spite of

尽管…… · 四级

although / though / even though 是连词，后接句子；despite、in spite of 是介词，后接名词、代词或动名词。although 不能和 but 同时使用。

例句

Although it was raining, the match went on.
尽管下着雨，比赛还是继续进行。
Despite the rain, the match went on.
尽管下雨，比赛还是照常进行。
She went to work in spite of feeling ill.
尽管不舒服，她还是去上班了。
