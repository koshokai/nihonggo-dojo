No sooner … than / Hardly … when

一……就…… · 六级

主句常用过去完成时，从句用一般过去时；no sooner、hardly、scarcely 放在句首时，主句要部分倒装。注意搭配：no sooner…than，hardly / scarcely…when。

例句

No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang.
我刚坐下电话就响了。
Hardly had we arrived when it started to pour.
我们刚到就下起了倾盆大雨。
I had no sooner left the office than I remembered my keys.
我刚离开办公室就想起钥匙没拿。
