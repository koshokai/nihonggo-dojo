倍数表达 … times as … as / … times + 比较级 + than

是……的几倍 · 六级

常用结构：A is three times as large as B；A is three times larger than B；A is three times the size of B。half、twice 也可以这样用。

例句

The new stadium is twice as big as the old one.
新体育场是旧体育场的两倍大。
This city's population is three times that of my hometown.
这座城市的人口是我家乡的三倍。
The price of houses here is five times higher than it was twenty years ago.
这里的房价是二十年前的五倍。
