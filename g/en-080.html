cannot help doing / cannot but do / cannot … too …

忍不住…… / 不得不…… / 再……也不为过 · 六级

can't help doing 表示“忍不住做”；cannot (help) but do 表示“只能、不得不”；cannot + 动词 + too + 形容词/副词表示“越……越好，再……也不为过”。

例句

I couldn't help laughing when I saw his face.
看到他的表情我忍不住笑了。
Faced with such evidence, he could not but admit his mistake.
面对这样的证据，他只好承认了错误。
You can't be too careful when crossing the road.
过马路时越小心越好。
