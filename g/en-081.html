lest / for fear that / in case

以免……；万一…… · 六级

lest 和 for fear that 引导的从句多用 (should) + 动词原形，书面语色彩较重；in case 后多用一般现在时或 should do，口语常用。

例句

Take an umbrella in case it rains.
带把伞吧，以防下雨。
He spoke quietly lest he (should) wake the baby.
他小声说话，以免吵醒宝宝。
She wrote the number down for fear that she might forget it.
她把号码写了下来，怕自己忘记。
