不可数名词与量词 a piece of / a cup of

一条（消息）/ 一杯（茶）…… · 基础

water, bread, advice, information, news, furniture 等不可数名词不能直接加 a 或 -s，要借助 a piece of, a cup of, a bottle of, a slice of 等量词表示数量；复数变化在量词上。

例句

Can I have a cup of tea and two slices of bread?
能给我一杯茶和两片面包吗？
My teacher gave me a useful piece of advice.
老师给了我一条有用的建议。
We need to buy some new furniture for the living room.
我们得给客厅买些新家具。
