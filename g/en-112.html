would like / want 表示愿望

想要 · 基础

would like（'d like）比 want 更礼貌：would like + 名词 / to do；Would you like …? 用于邀请或提供东西，回答 Yes, please / I'd love to。

例句

I'd like a glass of orange juice, please.
请给我来一杯橙汁。
Would you like to come to my party?
你愿意来参加我的聚会吗？
She wants to be a doctor in the future.
她将来想当医生。
