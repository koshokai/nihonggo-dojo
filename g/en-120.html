try / mean / regret / go on doing 与 to do

动词接 doing 和 to do 意义不同 · 四级

try to do 努力做，try doing 尝试着做（看效果）；mean to do 打算做，mean doing 意味着；regret to do 遗憾地要（告知），regret doing 后悔做过；go on to do 接着做另一件事，go on doing 继续做同一件事。

例句

If the light doesn't work, try changing the bulb.
灯不亮的话，试试换个灯泡。
Missing this train means waiting another hour.
错过这班车就意味着再等一个小时。
We regret to inform you that your application was unsuccessful.
我们遗憾地通知您，您的申请未获通过。
