日语道场 · 语法索引

ask / tell / want / encourage sb to do

要求 / 告诉 / 想要 / 鼓励某人做某事 · 四级

ask, tell, want, invite, advise, encourage, allow, expect, warn 等动词后接“宾语 + to do”作宾补；否定形式是 sb not to do。

例句

My parents encouraged me to study abroad.
父母鼓励我出国留学。
The teacher warned us not to cheat in the exam.
老师警告我们考试不要作弊。
I want you to be honest with me.
我希望你对我坦诚。
