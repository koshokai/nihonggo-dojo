形容词 + 介词搭配 be good at / be afraid of / be responsible for

擅长 / 害怕 / 对……负责 · 四级

形容词后接固定介词：be good at, be interested in, be afraid of, be proud of, be famous for, be responsible for, be similar to, be different from；介词后接名词或动名词。

例句

She is good at solving problems.
她擅长解决问题。
Hangzhou is famous for its West Lake.
杭州以西湖闻名。
Who is responsible for this project?
谁负责这个项目？
