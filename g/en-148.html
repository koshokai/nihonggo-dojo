The reason (why) … is that …

……的原因是…… · 六级

reason 作主语时，表语从句应该用 that 引导，而不用 because（The reason … is because … 在正式写作中被视为冗余）；reason 后的定语从句可以用 why / that 或省略。

例句

The reason why he was late is that his car broke down.
他迟到的原因是车坏了。
The main reason for the delay was that the data were incomplete.
延误的主要原因是数据不完整。
The reason I called is that I need your advice.
我打电话是因为需要你的建议。
