递进标记 moreover / furthermore / in addition / what's more

此外，而且 · 六级

用于引出补充的、通常更有说服力的论点；moreover / furthermore 较正式，what's more 偏口语；in addition 后接名词时用 in addition to sth / doing。它们都是副词性成分，不能单独连接两个分句。

例句

The apartment is close to the subway. Moreover, the rent is reasonable.
这套公寓离地铁近，而且房租合理。
In addition to teaching, she writes novels.
除了教书，她还写小说。
The plan is too costly. Furthermore, it would take years to complete.
这个计划成本太高，此外还需要好几年才能完成。
