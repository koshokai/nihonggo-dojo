名词化：用名词短语代替动词 make a decision

（正式文体的名词化表达） · 六级

学术和正式写作中常把动词、形容词转换成名词短语，使表达更简洁客观：decide → make a decision，improve → bring about an improvement，analyze → carry out an analysis，be able → have the ability。

例句

The committee will make a final decision next week.
委员会将于下周作出最终决定。
The new policy brought about a significant improvement in air quality.
新政策使空气质量显著改善。
Researchers carried out a detailed analysis of the data.
研究人员对数据进行了详细分析。
