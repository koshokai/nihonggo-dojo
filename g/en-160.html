副词 + 形容词的强调搭配 highly / deeply / bitterly

极其……（习惯搭配） · 六级

英语中程度副词与形容词有固定搭配，不能随意替换：highly likely / recommended / successful，deeply concerned / moved，bitterly disappointed / cold，fully aware，utterly ridiculous，widely accepted。

例句

It is highly likely that prices will rise again.
价格很可能会再次上涨。
We are deeply concerned about the situation.
我们对局势深感关切。
The fans were bitterly disappointed by the defeat.
球迷们对这场失利极为失望。
