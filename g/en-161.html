名词 + 介词搭配 impact on / threat to / solution to

对……的影响 / 威胁 / 解决办法 · 六级

抽象名词有固定介词搭配：impact / effect / influence on，threat / solution / key / access / approach to，demand / need / respect for，increase / decline in（某方面的增减）。

例句

Social media has a huge impact on young people.
社交媒体对年轻人影响巨大。
Plastic waste poses a serious threat to marine life.
塑料垃圾对海洋生物构成严重威胁。
There has been a sharp increase in online shopping.
网购大幅增加。
