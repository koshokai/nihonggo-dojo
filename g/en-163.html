复合介词 in terms of / with regard to / in light of

就……而言 / 关于 / 鉴于 · 六级

正式文体常用复合介词：in terms of 从……方面来说；with regard to / regarding 关于；in light of / in view of 鉴于，考虑到；on behalf of 代表；in accordance with 按照。后面接名词或动名词。

例句

In terms of price, this model is the best choice.
就价格而言，这款是最佳选择。
With regard to your request, we will reply within a week.
关于您的请求，我们将在一周内答复。
In light of the new evidence, the case was reopened.
鉴于新的证据，此案重新审理。
