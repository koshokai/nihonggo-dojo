独立成分 to be honest / generally speaking / judging from

说实话 / 一般来说 / 从……判断 · 六级

一些不定式、现在分词短语作插入语，表达说话人的态度，其逻辑主语不必与句子主语一致：to be honest, to tell the truth, to begin with, so to speak；generally / frankly / strictly speaking, judging from / by, considering。

例句

To be honest, I didn't enjoy the film.
说实话，我不喜欢那部电影。
Generally speaking, women live longer than men.
一般来说，女性比男性寿命长。
Judging from his accent, he must be from Scotland.
从口音判断，他一定是苏格兰人。
