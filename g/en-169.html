prefer to do rather than do / prefer A to B

宁愿……而不…… · 六级

prefer doing A to doing B 或 prefer A to B（to 是介词）；表示具体一次的偏好时常用 prefer to do A rather than (do) B。注意不要把 to 和 than 混用。

例句

I prefer tea to coffee.
比起咖啡我更喜欢茶。
She prefers walking to driving.
她更喜欢走路而不是开车。
I'd prefer to stay in rather than go out tonight.
今晚我宁愿待在家里也不想出去。
