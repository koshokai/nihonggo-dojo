be about to do / be on the point of doing

正要……，即将…… · 六级

be about to do 表示“马上就要做”，不与具体的将来时间状语连用，常与 when（这时）连用；be on the point of doing 意义相同，更正式。

例句

We were about to leave when it started to rain.
我们正要离开，这时开始下雨了。
The film is about to start. Turn off your phone.
电影快开始了，把手机关掉。
The company was on the point of closing down when a buyer appeared.
公司正要倒闭时出现了一个买家。
