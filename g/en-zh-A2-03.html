be going to：计划和预测

CEFR A2

be (am / is / are) going to + 动词原形

be going to 表示说话之前就已经决定好的计划或打算，如 I'm going to visit my aunt this weekend. 也可以根据眼前的迹象做预测：Look at those clouds. It's going to rain. 和 will 不同，说话时当场做的决定一般用 will。

别漏掉 be 动词：✗ I going to call him. 应该是 I'm going to call him。to 后面要用动词原形，不能说 ✗ going to visited。

例句

I'm going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
这个周末我打算去看爷爷奶奶。
What are you going to do after graduation?
你毕业以后打算做什么？
Look at those dark clouds. It's going to rain.
看那些乌云，要下雨了。
She isn't going to take the new job.
她不打算接受那份新工作。
