what 引导的名词性从句（= the thing that）

CEFR B2

what + 主语 + 动词（= the thing(s) that ...）

what 本身就包含了先行词，相当于 the thing that / the things that，它引导的从句可以作主语、宾语或表语：What she said was true. / I didn't understand what he meant. 如果前面已经有名词（先行词），就不能再用 what，要用 that 或 which。

中文“他说的话”容易写成 the thing what he said；“我需要的一切”写成 all what I need。前面有先行词（the thing、all、everything）时用 that，不用 what。

例句

I can't believe what he just told me.
我不敢相信他刚才跟我说的话。
Show me what you bought at the market.
给我看看你在市场买了什么。
What happened yesterday was nobody's fault.
昨天发生的事不是任何人的错。
This neighborhood is very different from what it was ten years ago.
这一带和十年前很不一样了。
