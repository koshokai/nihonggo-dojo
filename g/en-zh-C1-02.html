虚拟语气：suggest / insist that he be

CEFR C1

suggest / recommend / insist / demand + that + 主语 + 动词原形；It is essential / vital that + 主语 + 动词原形

表示建议、要求、坚持的动词（suggest, recommend, insist, demand, request）以及 It is essential / important / vital that 后面的 that 从句里，动词用原形，与主语人称和主句时态无关：She insisted that he be there。否定是 not + 原形：that he not be late。美式英语几乎都用原形；英式英语也常用 should + 原形，意思相同。

中文动词没有变化，学习者容易顺手写成 he goes / he is，这里要写 he go / he be。但 insist、suggest 表示“坚持说、表明（某事是事实）”时用正常时态：The data suggests that prices are rising.

例句

The doctor recommended that he get more sleep.
医生建议他多睡一些。
She insisted that the report be finished by Friday.
她坚持要求报告在周五前完成。
It is essential that every passenger wear a seat belt.
每位乘客都必须系好安全带。
We suggested that the meeting not start before ten.
我们建议会议不要在十点前开始。
