模糊限制语：seem to / appear to / tend to / be likely to

CEFR C1

主语 + seem / appear / tend + to do；主语 + be likely / unlikely + to do；It seems / appears that + 从句

在学术写作和正式场合中，常用这些表达避免把话说得太绝对（hedging）。seem / appear to 表示“看起来、似乎”，基于观察；tend to 表示“往往、通常”；be likely to 表示“很可能”。对过去的推测用完成式 seem to have done。There 句型也可以用：There appears to be a problem.

中文“他好像很累”常被写成 ×He seems that he is tired。正确的是 He seems to be tired 或 It seems that he is tired。另外 likely 是形容词，be 不能省：×He likely to come。

例句

The new policy seems to have reduced waiting times.
新政策似乎缩短了等待时间。
Younger users tend to prefer shorter videos.
年轻用户往往更喜欢短视频。
The meeting is likely to run late again.
会议很可能又要拖延。
There appears to be a problem with your account.
您的账户似乎出了点问题。
