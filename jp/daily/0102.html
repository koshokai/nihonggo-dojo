Japanese phrase of the day · January 2 · Season

初夢

はつゆめ

first dream of the New Year

The first dream of the new year, generally the one you have between the night of New Year's Day and the night of January 2. Tradition says the luckiest things to dream of are, in order, Mt. Fuji (富士), a hawk (鷹), and an eggplant (茄子).

Example

今年の初夢は富士山の夢だったから、いい年になりそうだ。
ことしの はつゆめわ ふじさんの ゆめだったから、 いい としに なり そうだ。
My first dream of the year was about Mt. Fuji, so it looks like it's going to be a good year.
