Japanese phrase of the day · January 5 · Season

小寒

しょうかん

Lesser Cold (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms of the traditional calendar, falling around January 5. It marks 「寒の入り」, the start of the coldest period of the year.

Example

今日は小寒で、いよいよ寒の入りだね。
きょうわ しょうかんで、 いよいよ かんの いりだね。
Today is Lesser Cold, so the coldest season is finally here.
