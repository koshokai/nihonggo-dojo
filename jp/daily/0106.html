Japanese phrase of the day · January 6 · Four-character idiom

無理難題

むりなんだい

unreasonable demand; impossible request

A demand that is unreasonable and simply cannot be met, often used in 「無理難題を押し付ける」 (to force an impossible demand on someone). It is stronger than just 'a difficult problem': the point is that the request itself is unfair.

Example

上司に無理難題を押し付けられて困っている。
じょうしに むりなんだいお おしつけられて こまっている。
My boss keeps forcing impossible demands on me, and I don't know what to do.
