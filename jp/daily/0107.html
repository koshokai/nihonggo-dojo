Japanese phrase of the day · January 7 · Season

七草粥

ななくさがゆ

seven-herb rice porridge

Rice porridge eaten on the morning of January 7, cooked with the 「春の七草」 (seven herbs of spring) such as water dropwort (芹) and shepherd's purse (薺). It is a wish for a year free of illness, and it also gives a stomach worn out by New Year's feasting a rest.

Example

お正月で疲れた胃に、七草粥がやさしい。
おしょうがつで つかれた いに、 ななくさがゆが やさしい。
After all the New Year's food, seven-herb porridge is gentle on a tired stomach.
