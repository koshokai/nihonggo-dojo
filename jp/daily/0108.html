Japanese phrase of the day · January 8 · Idiom

手も足も出ない

てもあしもでない

to be helpless; to not stand a chance

Literally 'can't put out a hand or a foot,' it means being completely powerless. It's used when an opponent or a problem is simply too much for you.

Example

相手が強すぎて、手も足も出なかった。
あいてが つよ すぎて、 ても あしも でなかった。
The other team was too strong; we didn't stand a chance.
