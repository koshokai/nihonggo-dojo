Japanese phrase of the day · January 9 · Kind words

自分のペースでいいよ

じぶんのぺーすでいいよ

Go at your own pace

Tells someone they don't have to compare themselves with others and can move forward at their own pace. 「いいよ」 gives it a gentle, reassuring tone.

Example

人と比べなくていい。自分のペースでいいよ。
ひとと くらべなくていい。 じぶんの ペースでいいよ。
You don't have to compare yourself with other people. Just go at your own pace.
