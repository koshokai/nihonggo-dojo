Japanese phrase of the day · January 10 · Proverb

出る杭は打たれる

でるくいはうたれる

The nail that sticks out gets hammered down

Literally 'the stake that sticks out gets hit,' it means people who stand out too much tend to attract envy and get pushed back down. It's close to the idea of 'tall poppy syndrome' in English.

Example

出る杭は打たれるというけど、自分の意見ははっきり言いたい。
でる くいわ うたれると いうけど、 じぶんの いけんわ はっきり いいたい。
They say the nail that sticks out gets hammered down, but I still want to state my opinions clearly.
