Japanese phrase of the day · January 11 · Season

鏡開き

かがみびらき

kagami-biraki (breaking open the New Year's rice cake)

The custom of breaking up and eating the round rice cakes (鏡餅) offered over the New Year, usually on January 11. Because 「切る」 (to cut) and 「割る」 (to split) are considered unlucky words, the auspicious 「開く」 (to open) is used instead.

Example

鏡開きのお餅でおしるこを作った。
かがみびらきの おもちで おしるこお つくった。
I made oshiruko (sweet red bean soup) with the rice cake from kagami-biraki.
