Japanese phrase of the day · January 12 · Four-character idiom

無我夢中

むがむちゅう

totally absorbed; lost in the moment

Describes being so focused that you forget yourself, often while doing something with all your might. In English you might say 'lose yourself in something' or 'be in the zone.'

Example

無我夢中で走って、気がついたらゴールしていた。
むが むちゅうで はしって、 きが ついたら ゴール していた。
I ran with everything I had, and the next thing I knew I'd crossed the finish line.
