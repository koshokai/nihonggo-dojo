Japanese phrase of the day · January 14 · Kind words

お疲れ様

おつかれさま

Thanks for your hard work; good work

The most common phrase of appreciation in Japan, said when leaving work, finishing a task, or simply passing a coworker. To superiors, use the more polite 「お疲れ様です」 or 「お疲れ様でした」.

Example

今日も一日お疲れ様。ゆっくり休んでね。
きょうも いちにち おつかれさま。 ゆっくり やすんでね。
Thanks for all your hard work today. Get some good rest.
