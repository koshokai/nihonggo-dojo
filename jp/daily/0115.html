Japanese phrase of the day · January 15 · Proverb

一を聞いて十を知る

いちをきいてじゅうをしる

Hear one, understand ten; to grasp things instantly

From the Analects of Confucius, it describes a very bright person who understands things quickly. Hearing just one part, they grasp the whole.

Example

彼女は一を聞いて十を知るタイプで、説明がすぐ伝わる。
かのじょわ いちお きいて じゅうお しる タイプで、 せつめいが すぐ つたわる。
She's the type who hears one thing and understands ten, so explanations get through to her right away.
