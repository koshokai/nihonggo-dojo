Japanese phrase of the day · January 16 · Four-character idiom

一騎当千

いっきとうせん

a match for a thousand

Literally 'one horseman can hold off a thousand.' It describes someone whose strength or skill far surpasses others.

Example

彼らは一騎当千のベテラン選手ばかりだ。
かれらわ いっきとうせんの ベテランせんしゅばかりだ。
They're all veteran players, each one a match for a thousand.
