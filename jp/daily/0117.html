Japanese phrase of the day · January 17 · Idiom

頭が固い

あたまがかたい

stubborn; inflexible

Literally 'one's head is hard,' it describes someone who is rigid and can't adapt their thinking. It's similar to English 'set in one's ways.'

Example

祖父は頭が固くて、新しいことを受け入れない。
そふわ あたまが かたくて、 あたらしい ことお うけいれない。
My grandfather is set in his ways and won't accept anything new.
