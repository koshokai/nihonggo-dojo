Japanese phrase of the day · January 18 · Kind words

えらいね

Good for you! Well done!

Praise for someone who has done something commendable. It's often used to praise children, but friends also say it to give each other credit.

Example

毎朝早起きして勉強してるの？えらいね。
まいあさはやおき して べんきょう してるの？ えらいね。
You get up early every morning to study? Good for you!
