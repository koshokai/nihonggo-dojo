Japanese phrase of the day · January 19 · Proverb

壁に耳あり障子に目あり

かべにみみありしょうじにめあり

The walls have ears

Literally 'walls have ears, and paper screens have eyes,' it means secrets leak easily, so be careful what you say. English has the same saying: 'The walls have ears.'

Example

壁に耳あり障子に目ありだから、ここで秘密の話はやめよう。
かべに みみありしょうじに めありだから、 ここで ひみつの はなしわ やめよう。
The walls have ears, so let's not talk about secrets here.
