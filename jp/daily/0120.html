Japanese phrase of the day · January 20 · Season

大寒

だいかん

Greater Cold (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around January 20, and the coldest time of the year. Japanese people traditionally use this time for 「寒稽古」 (midwinter training), brewing sake, and making miso.

Example

大寒の朝は、窓に霜がびっしりついていた。
だいかんの あさわ、 まどに しもが びっしり ついていた。
On the morning of Greater Cold, the windows were completely covered in frost.
