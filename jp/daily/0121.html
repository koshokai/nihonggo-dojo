Japanese phrase of the day · January 21 · Four-character idiom

頭寒足熱

ずかんそくねつ

cool head, warm feet (for good health)

A traditional Japanese health saying: keeping your head cool and your feet warm is good for your health and sleep. The four characters literally mean 'head cold, feet hot.'

Example

頭寒足熱を心がけて、寝る前に足湯をしている。
ずかんそくねつお こころがけて、 ねる まえに あしゆお している。
I try to keep my head cool and my feet warm, so I soak my feet in hot water before bed.
