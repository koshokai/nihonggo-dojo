Japanese phrase of the day · January 22 · Idiom

棚に上げる

たなにあげる

to ignore one's own faults (while criticizing others)

Literally 'to put something up on a shelf,' it means conveniently keeping quiet about your own shortcomings while criticizing others. It often appears as 「自分のことは棚に上げて」.

Example

自分のことは棚に上げて、人の文句ばかり言う。
じぶんの ことわ たなに あげて、 ひとの もんくばかり ゆう。
He ignores his own faults and does nothing but complain about other people.
