Japanese phrase of the day · January 23 · Kind words

おかえりなさい

Welcome home

What you say to welcome a family member home; the person coming home says 「ただいま」. It can also be used to welcome back a friend who has been away for a long time.

Example

おかえりなさい。ご飯できてるよ。
おかえりなさい。 ごはん できてるよ。
Welcome home. Dinner's ready.
