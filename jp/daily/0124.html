Japanese phrase of the day · January 24 · Proverb

言うは易く行うは難し

いうはやすくおこなうはかたし

Easier said than done

It means something is easy to say but hard to actually do. It matches the English proverb 'Easier said than done' exactly.

Example

毎日勉強すると決めたけど、言うは易く行うは難しだ。
まいにちべんきょう すると きめたけど、 ゆうわ やすく おこなうわ かたしだ。
I decided to study every day, but it's easier said than done.
