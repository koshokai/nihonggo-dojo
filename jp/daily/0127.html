Japanese phrase of the day · January 27 · Kind words

お元気で

おげんきで

Take care; all the best

A parting wish used when saying goodbye to someone for a long time, hoping they stay healthy and well. It's like English 'Take care' or 'All the best.'

Example

向こうでもお元気で。また会いましょう。
むこうでも おげんきで。 また あいましょう。
Take care over there. Let's meet again.
