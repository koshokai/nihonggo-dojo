Japanese phrase of the day · January 28 · Proverb

百聞は一見に如かず

ひゃくぶんはいっけんにしかず

Seeing is believing

It means seeing something once with your own eyes is better than hearing about it a hundred times. It comes from the ancient Chinese history Book of Han, and English expresses the same idea with 'Seeing is believing.'

Example

百聞は一見に如かず。一度京都に行ってみたら？
ひゃくぶんわ いっけんに しかず。 いちどきょうとに いってみたら？
Seeing is believing. Why not visit Kyoto and see it for yourself?
