Japanese phrase of the day · January 29 · Four-character idiom

油断大敵

ゆだんたいてき

Carelessness is your worst enemy

「油断」 is a Japanese word meaning carelessness or letting your guard down; despite its kanji ('oil' + 'cut off'), it has nothing to do with oil. Close English equivalents are 'Never let your guard down' and 'Pride comes before a fall.'

Example

相手は弱いチームだけど、油断大敵だぞ。
あいてわ よわい チームだけど、 ゆだんたいてきだぞ。
They may be a weak team, but don't let your guard down!
