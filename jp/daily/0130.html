Japanese phrase of the day · January 30 · Idiom

顔に泥を塗る

かおにどろをぬる

to bring shame on someone; to make someone lose face

Literally 'to smear mud on someone's face,' it means causing someone, especially a parent or superior, to lose face. English has a similar image in 'to drag someone's name through the mud.'

Example

親の顔に泥を塗るようなことはするな。
おやの かおに どろお ぬる ような ことわ するな。
Don't do anything that would bring shame on your parents.
