Japanese phrase of the day · January 31 · Kind words

頑張りすぎないで

がんばりすぎないで

Don't push yourself too hard

A caring thing to say to someone who is already working very hard. It's gentler than just saying 「頑張って」 and reminds them to take care of themselves.

Example

もう十分頑張ってるんだから、頑張りすぎないでね。
もう じゅうぶん がんばってるんだから、 がんばりすぎないでね。
You're already working hard enough, so don't overdo it, OK?
