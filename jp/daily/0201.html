Japanese phrase of the day · February 1 · Proverb

下手な鉄砲も数撃ちゃ当たる

へたなてっぽうもかずうちゃあたる

Fire enough shots and one is bound to hit

Literally 'even a poor marksman will hit something if he fires enough shots,' it means that if you keep trying, you'll succeed sooner or later. It's similar to English 'If at first you don't succeed, try, try again.'

Example

下手な鉄砲も数撃ちゃ当たるで、いろいろな会社に応募してみた。
へたな てっぽうも かず うちゃ あたるで、 いろいろな かいしゃに おうぼ してみた。
Even a bad shot hits the target if he fires enough, so I applied to all kinds of companies.
