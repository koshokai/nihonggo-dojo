Japanese phrase of the day · February 2 · Four-character idiom

意気投合

いきとうごう

to hit it off; to click

Describes people whose feelings and ideas match so they get along great, often right from the first meeting. The English equivalent is 'to hit it off.'

Example

初対面なのに、二人はすぐに意気投合した。
しょたいめんなのに、 ふたりわ すぐに いきとうごう した。
Even though it was the first time they'd met, the two of them hit it off right away.
