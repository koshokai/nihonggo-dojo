Japanese phrase of the day · February 4 · Season

立春

りっしゅん

Beginning of Spring (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around February 4. On the calendar, spring begins on this day, although in reality it's still quite cold.

Example

暦の上では立春だけど、まだまだ寒い日が続くね。
こよみの うえでわ りっしゅんだけど、 まだまだ さむい ひが つづくね。
According to the calendar it's the beginning of spring, but the cold days just keep on coming.
