Japanese phrase of the day · February 5 · Idiom

目を通す

めをとおす

to look over; to skim through

Means to read through documents or materials quickly to get the gist. It's a very common expression at work.

Example

会議の前に資料に目を通しておいてください。
かいぎの まえに しりょうに めお とおしておいてください。
Please look over the materials before the meeting.
