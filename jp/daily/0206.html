Japanese phrase of the day · February 6 · Kind words

いつもありがとう

Thanks for everything; thank you, as always

Thanks for the everyday things your family, friends, or coworkers do for you. People don't often say it out loud, so hearing it can really move someone.

Example

お母さん、いつもありがとう。
おははさん、 いつも ありがとう。
Mom, thanks for everything you always do.
