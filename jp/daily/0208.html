Japanese phrase of the day · February 8 · Proverb

一寸の虫にも五分の魂

いっすんのむしにもごぶのたましい

Even the smallest creature has its pride

Literally 'even a one-inch bug has a half-inch soul,' it means that even small, weak people have their own spirit and dignity and shouldn't be looked down on. Compare English 'Even a worm will turn.'

Example

一寸の虫にも五分の魂。新人だからって馬鹿にしないでほしい。
いっすんの むしにも ごぶの たましい。 しんじんだからって ばかに しないでほしい。
Even the smallest bug has a soul. I'd appreciate it if you didn't look down on me just because I'm new.
