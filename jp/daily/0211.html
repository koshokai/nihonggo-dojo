Japanese phrase of the day · February 11 · Idiom

油を売る

あぶらをうる

to goof off; to slack off chatting

It's said that in the Edo period, hair-oil sellers chatted with customers while slowly pouring out the oil. From this, it came to mean idling and chatting during work time.

Example

どこで油を売っていたんだ。
どこで あぶらお うっていたんだ。
Where have you been goofing off?
