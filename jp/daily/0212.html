Japanese phrase of the day · February 12 · Kind words

ちゃんと見てるよ

ちゃんとみてるよ

I see how hard you're trying

Lets someone know their efforts have been noticed and recognized, which is very reassuring. 「ちゃんと」 means 'properly' or 'really,' as in 'I really am watching.'

Example

君が毎日頑張ってること、ちゃんと見てるよ。
きみが まいにち がんばってる こと、 ちゃんと みてるよ。
I see how hard you work every single day.
