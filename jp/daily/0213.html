Japanese phrase of the day · February 13 · Proverb

人の振り見て我が振り直せ

ひとのふりみてわがふりなおせ

Learn from others' faults and correct your own

It means watching how others behave and using it to reflect on and correct your own conduct. It's close to a line from the Analects of Confucius: 'When you see someone unworthy, look inward and examine yourself.'

Example

電車で騒ぐ人を見て、人の振り見て我が振り直せと思った。
でんしゃで さわぐ ひとお みて、 ひとの ふり みて わが ふり なおせと おもった。
Seeing people making a racket on the train, I thought to myself: learn from others' faults and correct your own.
