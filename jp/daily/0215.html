Japanese phrase of the day · February 15 · Four-character idiom

不言実行

ふげんじっこう

actions, not words

A four-character idiom coined in Japan, meaning to quietly get things done without a lot of talk. The English equivalent is 'Actions speak louder than words.'

Example

父は不言実行タイプで、文句一つ言わずに働く。
ちちわ ふげんじっこうタイプで、 もんくひとつ いわずに はたらく。
My father is a man of action, not words; he works without a single complaint.
