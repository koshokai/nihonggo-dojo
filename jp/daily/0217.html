Japanese phrase of the day · February 17 · Kind words

一緒に頑張ろう

いっしょにがんばろう

Let's do our best together

Means you'll work hard side by side with the other person. It feels more supportive than just saying 「頑張って」, because you're in it together.

Example

試験まであと一か月、一緒に頑張ろう。
しけんまで あといちかげつ、 いっしょに がんばろう。
There's one month left until the exam. Let's do our best together.
