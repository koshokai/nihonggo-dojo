Japanese phrase of the day · February 18 · Proverb

井の中の蛙大海を知らず

いのなかのかわずたいかいをしらず

A frog in a well knows nothing of the great ocean

It describes someone with a narrow outlook who doesn't know the wider world. It comes from the ancient Chinese classic Zhuangzi.

Example

留学して、自分が井の中の蛙大海を知らずだったと気づいた。
りゅうがく して、 じぶんが いの なかの かわずたいかいお しらずだったと きづいた。
Studying abroad made me realize I had been a frog in a well that knew nothing of the ocean.
