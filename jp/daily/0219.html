Japanese phrase of the day · February 19 · Season

雨水

うすい

Rain Water (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around February 19, when snow gradually turns to rain and ice begins to melt. Traditionally it's considered a good time to set out the dolls for the Girls' Festival.

Example

雨水を過ぎて、雪が雨に変わってきた。
うすいお すぎて、 ゆきが あめに かわってきた。
Now that Rain Water has passed, the snow has started turning to rain.
