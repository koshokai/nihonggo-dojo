Japanese phrase of the day · February 22 · Kind words

成長したね

せいちょうしたね

You've really grown

Expresses how much someone has improved or matured compared with before. It's a way of recognizing the effort they've put in.

Example

前より堂々と話せるようになったね。成長したね。
まえより どうどうと はなせる ように なったね。 せいちょう したね。
You speak much more confidently than before. You've really grown.
