Japanese phrase of the day · February 24 · Season

春一番

はるいちばん

the first strong southerly wind of spring

The first strong south wind to blow between 立春 and 春分. It's announced by the Japan Meteorological Agency and means spring is on its way.

Example

今日は春一番が吹いて、一気に暖かくなった。
きょうわ はるいちばんが ふいて、 いっきに あたたかく なった。
The first spring gale blew today, and it suddenly got much warmer.
