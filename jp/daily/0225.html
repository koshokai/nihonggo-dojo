Japanese phrase of the day · February 25 · Four-character idiom

七転八倒

しちてんばっとう

writhing in agony

Describes being in extreme pain and twisting and turning in agony. Don't confuse it with the similar-looking 七転八起, which means 'fall down seven times, get up eight.'

Example

夜中にひどい腹痛で七転八倒した。
よなかに ひどい はらいたで しちてんばっとう した。
In the middle of the night, I was writhing in agony with a terrible stomachache.
