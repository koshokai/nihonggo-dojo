Japanese phrase of the day · February 26 · Idiom

息が合う

いきがあう

to be in sync; to work well together

Literally 'breaths match,' it means doing something together in perfect harmony and timing. In English we'd say people are 'in sync.'

Example

二人のダンスは息がぴったり合っている。
ふたりの ダンスわ いきが ぴったり あっている。
The two of them are perfectly in sync when they dance.
