Japanese phrase of the day · February 27 · Kind words

元気をもらった

げんきをもらった

You gave me energy; you lifted my spirits

Literally 'I received energy,' it means you were encouraged by someone's words, smile, or performance. It's a very common way for Japanese people to say thank you.

Example

あなたの笑顔に、たくさん元気をもらったよ。
あなたの えがおに、 たくさん げんきお もらったよ。
Your smile really lifted my spirits.
