Japanese phrase of the day · February 28 · Proverb

過ぎたるは猶及ばざるが如し

すぎたるはなおおよばざるがごとし

Too much is as bad as too little

From the Analects of Confucius, it means overdoing something is just as bad as not doing enough. English has a similar saying: 'Everything in moderation.'

Example

勉強しすぎて体を壊しては意味がない。過ぎたるは猶及ばざるが如しだ。
べんきょう しすぎて からだお こわしてわ いみが ない。 すぎたるわ なお およばざるがごとしだ。
There's no point studying so hard that you wreck your health. Too much is as bad as too little.
