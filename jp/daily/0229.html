Japanese phrase of the day · February 29 · Four-character idiom

日進月歩

にっしんげっぽ

rapid, constant progress

Literally 'advancing by the day, stepping forward by the month.' It describes steady, rapid progress and is often used about science and technology.

Example

ＩＴ技術は日進月歩だ。
あいてぃーぎじゅつわ にっしんげっぽだ。
IT is advancing by leaps and bounds.
