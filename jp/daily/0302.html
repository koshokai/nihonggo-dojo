Japanese phrase of the day · March 2 · Kind words

一人じゃないよ

ひとりじゃないよ

You're not alone

Said to someone who feels lonely or is going through a hard time. It reminds them that you and others are right there with them.

Example

困ったときは頼ってね。一人じゃないよ。
こまった ときわ たよってね。 ひとりじゃ ないよ。
Lean on me when things get tough. You're not alone.
