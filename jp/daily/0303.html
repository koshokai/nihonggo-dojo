Japanese phrase of the day · March 3 · Season

ひな祭り

ひなまつり

Hinamatsuri, the Girls' Festival

A festival on March 3 to pray for girls' healthy growth. Families display hina dolls and enjoy chirashi sushi, diamond-shaped rice cakes (菱餅), and 白酒 (a sweet rice drink); it's also called 「桃の節句」, the Peach Festival.

Example

ひな祭りに、娘のためにちらし寿司を作った。
ひなまつりに、 むすめの ために ちらしずしお つくった。
For Hinamatsuri, I made chirashi sushi for my daughter.
