Japanese phrase of the day · March 4 · Proverb

若い時の苦労は買ってでもせよ

わかいときのくろうはかってでもせよ

Hardship in youth is worth paying for

It means the hardships you go through when young become a treasure later in life, so they're worth seeking out even if you had to pay for them. Literally 'buy hardship in your youth if you have to.'

Example

若い時の苦労は買ってでもせよというから、アルバイトも頑張るよ。
わかい ときの くろうわ かってでも せよと いうから、 アルバイトも がんばるよ。
They say hardship in your youth is worth paying for, so I'm going to give my part-time job my all too.
