Japanese phrase of the day · March 6 · Four-character idiom

一喜一憂

いっきいちゆう

to swing between joy and worry

Describes being happy one moment and anxious the next as a situation changes. It's often used to advise people not to get too caught up in short-term results.

Example

テストの点数に一喜一憂しないで、続けることが大切だ。
テストの てんすうに いっきいちゆう しないで、 つづける ことが たいせつだ。
Don't get too up or down about your test scores; what matters is sticking with it.
