Japanese phrase of the day · March 10 · Four-character idiom

適材適所

てきざいてきしょ

the right person in the right place

Means assigning people to the positions that best suit their abilities. The English equivalent is 'the right person for the right job.'

Example

適材適所の人事で、チームの成績が上がった。
てきざいてきしょの じんじで、 チームの せいせきが あがった。
Putting the right people in the right roles improved the team's results.
