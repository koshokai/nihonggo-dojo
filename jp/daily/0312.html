Japanese phrase of the day · March 12 · Kind words

なんくるないさ

It'll all work out

An Okinawan dialect phrase with a meaning close to 「なんとかなる」. It's often cited as an expression of the optimistic, easygoing outlook of Okinawan people.

Example

心配しすぎだよ。なんくるないさ。
しんぱい しすぎだよ。 なんくるないさ。
You worry too much. It'll all work out.
