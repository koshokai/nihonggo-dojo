Japanese phrase of the day · March 16 · Idiom

足を洗う

あしをあらう

to wash one's hands of something; to go straight

Literally 'to wash one's feet,' it means quitting a shady line of work or leaving a bad crowd. English says 'to wash one's hands of something,' but Japanese washes the feet.

Example

彼は悪い仲間から足を洗った。
かれわ わるい なかまから あしお あらった。
He cut ties with his bad friends and went straight.
