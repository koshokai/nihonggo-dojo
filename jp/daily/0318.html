Japanese phrase of the day · March 18 · Season

お彼岸

おひがん

Ohigan (the equinox weeks for visiting graves)

The seven days centered on the spring and autumn equinoxes. During this time Japanese people visit family graves and offer 「ぼたもち」 (also called 「おはぎ」, rice balls coated in sweet red bean paste) to their ancestors.

Example

お彼岸なので、家族でお墓参りに行った。
おひがんなので、 かぞくで おはかまいりに いった。
Since it's Ohigan, the whole family went to visit the family grave.
