Japanese phrase of the day · March 21 · Four-character idiom

老若男女

ろうにゃくなんにょ

people of all ages and both sexes

The reading is irregular: 若 is read にゃく and 女 is read にょ. Note the order, too: 'old, young, men, women,' whereas English usually says 'men and women, young and old.'

Example

この祭りには老若男女が集まる。
この まつりにわ ろうにゃくなんにょが あつまる。
People of all ages, young and old, men and women, come to this festival.
