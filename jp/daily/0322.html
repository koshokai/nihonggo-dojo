Japanese phrase of the day · March 22 · Idiom

気が利く

きがきく

thoughtful; attentive

Describes someone who notices little things and thinks of others' needs before being asked. It's a compliment you'll often hear about coworkers or friends.

Example

彼は気が利くので、職場で人気がある。
かれわ きが きくので、 しょくばで にんきが ある。
He's very attentive, so he's popular at work.
