Japanese phrase of the day · March 23 · Kind words

いい一日を

いいいちにちを

Have a nice day

A parting wish for when you say goodbye in the morning or leave someone a message. It's short for 「いい一日をお過ごしください」, just like English 'Have a nice day.'

Example

今日は晴れて気持ちいいね。いい一日を！
きょうわ はれて きもち いいね。 いい いちにちお！
It's a beautiful sunny day. Have a good one!
