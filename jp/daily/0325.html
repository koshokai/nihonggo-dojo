Japanese phrase of the day · March 25 · Proverb

習うより慣れろ

ならうよりなれろ

Practice makes perfect

Literally 'get used to it rather than learn it,' it means repeated hands-on practice teaches you more than listening to explanations. English says 'Practice makes perfect.'

Example

会話は習うより慣れろだから、どんどん日本人と話してみて。
かいわわ ならうより なれろだから、 どんどん にほんじんと はなしてみて。
With conversation, practice makes perfect, so talk with Japanese people as much as you can.
