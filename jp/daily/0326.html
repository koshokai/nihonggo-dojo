Japanese phrase of the day · March 26 · Four-character idiom

粉骨砕身

ふんこつさいしん

to work oneself to the bone; to give one's all

Literally 'grinding one's bones and crushing one's body,' it's used in Japanese to express determination, for example in a speech when starting a new job. Despite the violent image, it isn't about sacrifice or a terrible fate; it simply means 'I'll give it everything I've got.'

Example

新しい職場で粉骨砕身努力いたします。
あたらしい しょくばで ふんこつさいしんどりょく いたします。
I will devote myself body and soul to my new workplace.
