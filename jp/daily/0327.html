Japanese phrase of the day · March 27 · Idiom

歯が立たない

はがたたない

to be no match for; to be out of one's depth

Literally 'one's teeth can't bite into it,' it means an opponent is too strong or a problem too hard to deal with. Compare English 'a tough nut to crack.'

Example

この問題は難しすぎて、全然歯が立たない。
この もんだいわ むずかし すぎて、 ぜんぜん はが たたない。
This problem is way too hard. I can't make a dent in it.
