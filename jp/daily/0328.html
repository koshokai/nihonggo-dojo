Japanese phrase of the day · March 28 · Season

桜前線

さくらぜんせん

the cherry blossom front

A line connecting places where the cherry trees bloom on the same day. Like a weather front on a map, it moves from south to north.

Example

桜前線が北上して、東京でも桜が咲き始めた。
さくらぜんせんが ほくじょう して、 とうきょうでも さくらが さきはじめた。
The cherry blossom front has moved north, and the cherry trees have started blooming in Tokyo too.
