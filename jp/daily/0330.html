Japanese phrase of the day · March 30 · Proverb

実るほど頭を垂れる稲穂かな

みのるほどこうべをたれるいなほかな

The fuller the ear of rice, the lower it bows

Just as ripe ears of rice bend low, the more learned and cultivated a person is, the more humble they become. It's used to praise people who stay modest despite their achievements.

Example

あの教授は偉いのにとても謙虚だ。実るほど頭を垂れる稲穂かなだね。
あの きょうじゅわ えらいのに とても けんきょだ。 みのるほど こうべお たれる いなほかなだね。
That professor is so distinguished, yet so humble. The fuller the ear of rice, the lower it bows.
