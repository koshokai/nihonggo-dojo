Japanese phrase of the day · March 31 · Four-character idiom

文武両道

ぶんぶりょうどう

excelling in both academics and sports

Originally it meant mastery of both scholarship and the martial arts. In modern Japanese it usually describes someone who is good at both studying and sports.

Example

彼は文武両道で、成績もよくサッカー部のエースだ。
かれわ ぶんぶりょうどうで、 せいせきも よく サッカーぶの エースだ。
He excels at both academics and sports: he gets good grades and is the ace of the soccer team.
