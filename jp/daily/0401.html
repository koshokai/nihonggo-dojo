Japanese phrase of the day · April 1 · Season

新年度

しんねんど

the new fiscal or school year

In Japan, most schools, companies, and government offices start their year on April 1. That's why school entrance, new jobs, and staff transfers all happen at this time.

Example

新年度が始まって、職場に新しい人が入ってきた。
しんねんどが はじまって、 しょくばに あたらしい ひとが はいってきた。
The new fiscal year has started, and some new people have joined our workplace.
