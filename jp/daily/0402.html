Japanese phrase of the day · April 2 · Idiom

腰が低い

こしがひくい

humble; modest

Literally 'one's waist is low,' as in bowing deeply, it describes someone who is modest and doesn't act self-important. It's a compliment.

Example

社長なのに、とても腰が低い人だ。
しゃちょうなのに、 とても こしが ひくい ひとだ。
He's the company president, yet he's a very humble person.
