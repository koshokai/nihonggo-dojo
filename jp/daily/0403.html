Japanese phrase of the day · April 3 · Kind words

ドンマイ

どんまい

Don't worry about it; never mind

Japanese-made English from 'don't mind,' often said to a teammate who makes a mistake in sports. Today it's also very common in everyday life.

Example

ドンマイ、次がんばろう！
ドンマイ、 つぎ がんばろう！
Don't worry about it. We'll get it next time!
