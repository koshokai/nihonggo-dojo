Japanese phrase of the day · April 4 · Season

清明

せいめい

Clear and Bright (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around April 4. Its name comes from 「清浄明潔」 (pure and bright), as everything feels fresh and clear. Unlike China, where this is the tomb-sweeping festival, Japan generally has no custom of visiting graves at this time.

Example

清明のころは、空も花も明るくて気持ちがいい。
せいめいの ころわ、 そらも はなも あかるくて きもちが いい。
Around Clear and Bright, the sky and the flowers are so bright that it really lifts your mood.
