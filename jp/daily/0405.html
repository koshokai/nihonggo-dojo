Japanese phrase of the day · April 5 · Season

花見

はなみ

hanami, cherry blossom viewing

People spread out mats under the cherry trees and eat, drink, and chat with family, friends, or coworkers while admiring the blossoms. It's one of the most important spring events in Japan.

Example

週末、会社の仲間と上野公園へ花見に行く。
しゅうまつ、 かいしゃの なかまと うえのこうえんえ はなみに いく。
This weekend I'm going cherry blossom viewing in Ueno Park with my coworkers.
