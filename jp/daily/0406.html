Japanese phrase of the day · April 6 · Proverb

学問に王道なし

がくもんにおうどうなし

There is no royal road to learning

From the Western proverb 'There is no royal road to learning.' It means there are no easy shortcuts to knowledge; you simply have to put in steady effort.

Example

学問に王道なし。一つずつ覚えていくしかない。
がくもんに おうどうなし。 ひとつずつ おぼえていくしか ない。
There's no royal road to learning. You just have to memorize things one at a time.
