Japanese phrase of the day · April 7 · Season

ご入学おめでとうございます

ごにゅうがくおめでとうございます

Congratulations on starting school

Entrance ceremonies in Japan are mostly held in early April. This is the standard phrase for congratulating someone, or their child, on starting school.

Example

お子さん、ご入学おめでとうございます。ランドセル姿がかわいいですね。
おこさん、 ごにゅうがく おめでとう ございます。 ランドセルすがたが かわいいですね。
Congratulations on your child starting school. They look adorable with their new school backpack!
