Japanese phrase of the day · April 8 · Four-character idiom

千載一遇

せんざいいちぐう

a once-in-a-lifetime chance

Literally 'a meeting that comes once in a thousand years.' It refers to a golden opportunity that hardly ever comes along.

Example

これは千載一遇のチャンスだ。
これわ せんざいいちぐうの チャンスだ。
This is the chance of a lifetime.
