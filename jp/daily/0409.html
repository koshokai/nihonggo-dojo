Japanese phrase of the day · April 9 · Idiom

耳にたこができる

みみにたこができる

to be sick of hearing something

Here 「たこ」 means a callus, not an octopus. It describes hearing the same thing so many times that you're fed up with it, as if your ears had grown calluses.

Example

母の小言は耳にたこができるほど聞いた。
ははの こごとわ みみに たこが できるほど きいた。
I've heard my mom's nagging so many times I'm sick of it.
