Japanese phrase of the day · April 10 · Season

花冷え

はなびえ

a cold snap during cherry blossom season

A sudden return of cold weather while the cherry trees are in full bloom. It's a seasonal word with a very Japanese feel.

Example

花冷えの日が続くので、まだコートが手放せない。
はなびえの ひが つづくので、 まだ コートが てばなせない。
These chilly blossom-season days keep coming, so I still can't put my coat away.
