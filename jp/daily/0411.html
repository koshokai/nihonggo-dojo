Japanese phrase of the day · April 11 · Kind words

楽しみにしてるね

たのしみにしてるね

I'm looking forward to it

Shows that you're looking forward to seeing someone or to what they'll do. Hearing it makes people feel valued.

Example

来月会えるの、楽しみにしてるね。
らいげつ あえるの、 たのしみに してるね。
I'm really looking forward to seeing you next month.
