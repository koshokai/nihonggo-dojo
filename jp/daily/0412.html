Japanese phrase of the day · April 12 · Proverb

楽あれば苦あり

らくあればくあり

No pleasure without pain

It means that life brings pain along with pleasure, and the two always take turns. It's often paired with 「苦あれば楽あり」 (after hardship comes ease).

Example

楽あれば苦ありで、旅行の後は宿題が山積みだ。
らく あれば く ありで、 りょこうの あとわ しゅくだいが やまづみだ。
No pleasure without pain: after my trip, my homework has piled up like a mountain.
