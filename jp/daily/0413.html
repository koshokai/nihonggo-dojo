Japanese phrase of the day · April 13 · Four-character idiom

一念発起

いちねんほっき

to resolve to do something; to turn over a new leaf

Originally a Buddhist term for resolving to devote oneself to the Buddhist path. Now it means making up your mind to start something new.

Example

一念発起して、毎朝ジョギングを始めた。
いちねんほっき して、 まいあさジョギングお はじめた。
I made up my mind and started jogging every morning.
