Japanese phrase of the day · April 15 · Idiom

根掘り葉掘り

ねほりはほり

to pry into every detail

Literally 'digging up the roots and the leaves.' It describes questioning someone about every last detail, usually to an annoying degree.

Example

恋人のことを根掘り葉掘り聞かれた。
こいびとの ことお ねほりはほり きかれた。
I got grilled about every little detail of my love life.
