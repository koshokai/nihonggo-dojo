Japanese phrase of the day · April 17 · Proverb

石の上にも三年

いしのうえにもさんねん

Perseverance pays off

Literally 'even a cold stone gets warm if you sit on it for three years,' it means that if you endure and keep at it, you'll succeed in the end. Compare English 'Rome wasn't built in a day.'

Example

新しい仕事は大変だけど、石の上にも三年だと思って頑張るよ。
あたらしい しごとわ たいへんだけど、 いしの うえにも さんねんだと おもって がんばるよ。
My new job is tough, but I'll stick with it, telling myself that perseverance pays off.
