Japanese phrase of the day · April 18 · Four-character idiom

我田引水

がでんいんすい

self-serving; drawing water to one's own field

Literally 'drawing water into one's own rice field,' it means thinking only of your own benefit or twisting facts to suit yourself. It's a four-character idiom created in Japan.

Example

彼の意見は我田引水で、全体のことを考えていない。
かれの いけんわ がでんいんすいで、 ぜんたいの ことお かんがえていない。
His opinion is completely self-serving; he isn't thinking about the big picture.
