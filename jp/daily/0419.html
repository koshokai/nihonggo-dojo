Japanese phrase of the day · April 19 · Idiom

頭に来る

あたまにくる

to get mad; to tick someone off

A casual way of saying you're really angry, as if the anger has rushed to your head. It's similar to English 'to blow one's top.'

Example

何度言っても聞かないので、頭に来た。
なんど いっても きかないので、 あたまに きた。
No matter how many times I told him, he wouldn't listen, so I got really mad.
