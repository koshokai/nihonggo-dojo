Japanese phrase of the day · April 20 · Season

穀雨

こくう

Grain Rain (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around April 20, when spring rains nourish all the grains. It's the last solar term of spring.

Example

穀雨のころの雨は、田畑にとって恵みの雨だ。
こくうの ころの あめわ、 たはたに とって めぐみの あめだ。
The rain around Grain Rain is a blessing for the fields.
