Japanese phrase of the day · April 21 · Kind words

ひと休みしよう

ひとやすみしよう

Let's take a break

A suggestion to stop for a short rest, often used in the middle of work or studying. 「ひと」 means 'one' here, so it's literally 'let's have one rest.'

Example

ずっと座りっぱなしだね。ひと休みしよう。
ずっと すわりっぱなしだね。 ひとやすみ しよう。
We've been sitting forever. Let's take a break.
