Japanese phrase of the day · April 22 · Proverb

果報は寝て待て

かほうはねてまて

Good things come to those who wait

It means you can't force good fortune; once you've done your best, wait calmly for the result. Literally 'wait for good luck while sleeping.'

Example

やるだけやったから、果報は寝て待てだ。
やるだけやったから、 かほうわ ねて まてだ。
I've done all I can, so now I'll just sit back and wait for good news.
