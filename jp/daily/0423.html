Japanese phrase of the day · April 23 · Four-character idiom

和洋折衷

わようせっちゅう

a blend of Japanese and Western styles

Describes a fusion of Japanese and Western styles. It's often used for architecture, food, and clothing.

Example

この旅館は和洋折衷の造りで、とても落ち着く。
この りょかんわ わようせっちゅうの つくりで、 とても おちつく。
This inn is built in a mix of Japanese and Western styles, and it's very relaxing.
