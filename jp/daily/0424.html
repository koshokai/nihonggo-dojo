Japanese phrase of the day · April 24 · Idiom

口が軽い

くちがかるい

loose-lipped; can't keep a secret

Literally 'one's mouth is light,' it describes someone who can't keep a secret. The opposite is 「口が堅い」.

Example

口が軽い人には秘密を話さないほうがいい。
くちが かるい ひとにわ ひみつお はなさない ほうが いい。
You'd better not tell your secrets to people with loose lips.
