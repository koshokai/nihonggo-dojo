Japanese phrase of the day · April 25 · Kind words

深呼吸しよう

しんこきゅうしよう

Let's take a deep breath

Said to calm yourself or someone else down when nervous, flustered, or angry. It's a simple way to help someone get back to normal.

Example

緊張してる？まず深呼吸しよう。
きんちょう してる？ まず しんこきゅう しよう。
Nervous? First, take a deep breath.
