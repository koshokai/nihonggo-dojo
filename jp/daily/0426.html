Japanese phrase of the day · April 26 · Proverb

後悔先に立たず

こうかいさきにたたず

There's no use regretting it afterward

Literally 'regret never comes first,' it means that regretting things after the fact does no good, so think carefully beforehand. English has a similar warning: 'Don't shut the barn door after the horse has bolted.'

Example

後悔先に立たず。試験前にちゃんと復習しておこう。
こうかいさきに たたず。 しけんまえに ちゃんと ふくしゅう しておこう。
Regret always comes too late. Let's review properly before the exam.
