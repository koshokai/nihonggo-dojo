Japanese phrase of the day · April 29 · Kind words

いただきます

Itadakimasu (said before eating)

Said before a meal, often with palms pressed together. It expresses gratitude to the food, to the person who made it, and to nature's bounty.

Example

みんなそろったね。それじゃあ、いただきます。
みんな そろったね。 それじゃあ、 いただきます。
Is everyone here? All right then, let's eat!
