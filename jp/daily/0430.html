Japanese phrase of the day · April 30 · Proverb

柳に雪折れなし

やなぎにゆきおれなし

Supple willows don't break under snow

Literally 'willow branches don't snap under snow,' it means that soft, flexible things can withstand hardship better than rigid ones. English has a similar proverb: 'Better to bend than to break.'

Example

柳に雪折れなしで、柔軟に考えれば困難も乗り越えられる。
やなぎに ゆきおれなしで、 じゅうなんに かんがえれば こんなんも のりこえられる。
Like the willow that never snaps under the snow, if you think flexibly you can get through hard times.
