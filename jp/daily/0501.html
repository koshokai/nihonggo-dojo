Japanese phrase of the day · May 1 · Four-character idiom

針小棒大

しんしょうぼうだい

making a mountain out of a molehill

Literally 'turning something as small as a needle into something as big as a pole,' it means wildly exaggerating. It's a four-character idiom created in Japan, and the English equivalent is 'making a mountain out of a molehill.'

Example

彼女の話はいつも針小棒大だから、半分に聞いておこう。
かのじょの はなしわ いつも しんしょうぼうだいだから、 はんぶんに きいておこう。
She always blows things out of proportion, so let's only believe half of what she says.
