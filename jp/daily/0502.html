Japanese phrase of the day · May 2 · Season

八十八夜

はちじゅうはちや

the 88th night (tea-picking season)

The 88th day counted from 立春, usually around May 2. It's the season for picking the year's first tea, and folk belief says tea picked on this day brings good health.

Example

八十八夜に摘んだ新茶をいただいた。
はちじゅうはちやに つんだ しんちゃお いただいた。
I was given some new tea picked on the 88th night.
