Japanese phrase of the day · May 3 · Idiom

気が短い

きがみじかい

short-tempered; impatient

Literally 'one's temper is short,' it describes someone who gets irritated or angry easily. English likewise says 'short-tempered.'

Example

父は気が短いので、待たせると怒る。
ちちわ きが みじかいので、 またせると おこる。
My dad is short-tempered, so he gets angry if you keep him waiting.
