Japanese phrase of the day · May 6 · Season

立夏

りっか

Beginning of Summer (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around May 5. Summer officially begins on the calendar, and fresh green leaves are at their most beautiful.

Example

立夏を迎えて、日差しが少しずつ強くなってきた。
りっかお むかえて、 ひざしが すこしずつ つよく なってきた。
Now that summer has begun, the sun is getting stronger little by little.
