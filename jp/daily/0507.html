Japanese phrase of the day · May 7 · Proverb

腐っても鯛

くさってもたい

Quality endures, even past its prime

Literally 'even a rotten sea bream is still a sea bream,' it means something truly fine keeps its value even after it's past its best. The sea bream is a prized, celebratory fish in Japan.

Example

古いけど、腐っても鯛で、このブランドの時計はまだ高く売れる。
ふるいけど、 くさっても たいで、 この ブランドの とけいわ まだ たかく うれる。
It's old, but quality is quality: a watch from this brand still sells for a good price.
