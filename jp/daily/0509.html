Japanese phrase of the day · May 9 · Idiom

手が離せない

てがはなせない

to be tied up; can't get away

Literally 'can't take one's hands off.' It means you're busy with something and can't break away to do anything else.

Example

今、料理中で手が離せないから、後で電話するね。
いま、 りょうりちゅうで てが はなせないから、 あとで でんわ するね。
I'm in the middle of cooking and can't get away right now, so I'll call you later.
