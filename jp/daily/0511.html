Japanese phrase of the day · May 11 · Proverb

人は見かけによらぬもの

ひとはみかけによらぬもの

Don't judge a book by its cover

It means you can't judge a person's true character or ability by appearance alone. The English equivalent is 'Don't judge a book by its cover.'

Example

怖そうな先輩が実はとても優しい。人は見かけによらぬものだ。
こわ そうな せんぱいが じつわ とても やさしい。 ひとわ みかけに よらぬ ものだ。
That senpai who looks so scary is actually really kind. You can't judge people by their looks.
