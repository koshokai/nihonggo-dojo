Japanese phrase of the day · May 12 · Season

五月晴れ

さつきばれ

clear May weather

Today it usually means a fresh, sunny day in May. Originally it referred to a rare sunny day in the fifth month of the old lunar calendar, which falls in the rainy season.

Example

五月晴れの空の下、家族でピクニックに出かけた。
さつきばれの そらの もと、 かぞくで ピクニックに でかけた。
Under the clear May sky, our family went out for a picnic.
