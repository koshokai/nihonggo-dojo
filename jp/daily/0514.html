Japanese phrase of the day · May 14 · Idiom

足が出る

あしがでる

to go over budget

Literally 'one's feet stick out,' it means spending more than you had planned. It's often used as 「予算から足が出る」.

Example

旅行で使いすぎて、予算から足が出た。
りょこうで つかいすぎて、 よさんから あしが でた。
I spent too much on the trip and went over budget.
