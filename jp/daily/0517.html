Japanese phrase of the day · May 17 · Season

新緑

しんりょく

fresh green leaves

The bright, tender new leaves that trees put out in early summer. The word evokes a fresh, vivid green.

Example

新緑の季節は、山を歩くだけで元気が出る。
しんりょくの きせつわ、 やまお あるくだけで げんきが でる。
In the season of fresh greenery, just walking in the mountains gives me energy.
