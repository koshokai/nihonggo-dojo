Japanese phrase of the day · May 18 · Four-character idiom

一汁一菜

いちじゅういっさい

one soup, one side dish; a simple meal

A simple meal of rice with just one bowl of soup and one side dish. It reflects a traditional Japanese approach to eating.

Example

忙しい日は一汁一菜で十分だ。
いそがしい ひわ いちじゅういっさいで じゅうぶんだ。
On busy days, one soup and one side dish is plenty.
