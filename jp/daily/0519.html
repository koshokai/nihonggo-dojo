Japanese phrase of the day · May 19 · Idiom

首をかしげる

くびをかしげる

to tilt one's head in puzzlement

Describes tilting your head to one side because you don't understand something or have doubts. English might say 'to scratch one's head' for the same feeling.

Example

彼の説明に、みんな首をかしげた。
かれの せつめいに、 みんなくびお かしげた。
His explanation left everyone scratching their heads.
