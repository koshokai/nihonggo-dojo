Japanese phrase of the day · May 20 · Kind words

焦らなくていいよ

あせらなくていいよ

There's no need to rush

Comforts someone who feels rushed or anxious, telling them it's fine to take their time. 「焦る」 means to be impatient or flustered.

Example

答えはすぐに出さなくてもいい。焦らなくていいよ。
こたえわ すぐに ださなくても いい。 あせらなくていいよ。
You don't have to give me an answer right away. There's no rush.
