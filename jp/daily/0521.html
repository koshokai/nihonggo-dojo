Japanese phrase of the day · May 21 · Proverb

笑う門には福来る

わらうかどにはふくきたる

Fortune comes to those who smile

It means good fortune naturally comes to a home that is always full of laughter. Literally 'luck comes to the gate where people laugh.'

Example

落ち込まないで。笑う門には福来るだよ。
おちこまないで。 わらう かどにわ ふく きたるだよ。
Don't be down. Good luck comes to those who smile!
