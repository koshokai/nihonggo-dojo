Japanese phrase of the day · May 22 · Season

風薫る

かぜかおる

fragrant breeze of early summer

Describes the fresh early-summer breeze blowing through new green leaves, as if it carried a fragrance. Letters often open with 「風薫る季節となりました」 (the season of fragrant breezes has arrived).

Example

風薫る五月、いかがお過ごしでしょうか。
かぜ かおる ごがつ、 いかが おすごしでしょうか。
How have you been in this May of fragrant breezes?
