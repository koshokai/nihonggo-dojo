Japanese phrase of the day · May 25 · Kind words

いつでも話聞くよ

いつでもはなしきくよ

I'm always here to listen

Tells someone who's struggling that they can come and talk to you any time. In casual speech, the particle 「を」 after 「話」 is dropped.

Example

一人で抱え込まないで。いつでも話聞くよ。
ひとりで かかえこまないで。 いつでも はなし きくよ。
Don't keep it all bottled up. I'm always here to listen.
