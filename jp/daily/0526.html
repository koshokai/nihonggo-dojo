Japanese phrase of the day · May 26 · Proverb

好きこそものの上手なれ

すきこそもののじょうずなれ

You get good at what you love

It means that when you love something, you throw yourself into it and naturally get better and better. In other words, passion is the best teacher.

Example

アニメが好きで日本語が上手になったなんて、好きこそものの上手なれだね。
アニメが すきで にほんごが じょうずに なったなんて、 すきこそ ものの じょうずなれだね。
Your Japanese got this good because you love anime? That proves you get good at what you love.
