Japanese phrase of the day · May 27 · Season

木漏れ日

こもれび

sunlight filtering through the leaves

Sunlight streaming down through the gaps between leaves on a tree. It's often cited as a beautiful Japanese word that's hard to translate directly into other languages.

Example

木漏れ日の中を歩くと、心が落ち着く。
こもれびの なかお あるくと、 こころが おちつく。
Walking through the sunlight filtering down through the trees calms my mind.
