Japanese phrase of the day · May 28 · Four-character idiom

自業自得

じごうじとく

You reap what you sow; it serves you right

From Buddhism: you yourself bear the consequences of your own actions. In Japanese it's used almost exclusively for bad outcomes.

Example

徹夜でゲームして寝坊したのは自業自得だ。
てつやで ゲーム して ねぼう したのわ じごうじとくだ。
You stayed up all night gaming and overslept, so you've only got yourself to blame.
