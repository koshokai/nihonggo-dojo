Japanese phrase of the day · May 30 · Kind words

上を向いて歩こう

うえをむいてあるこう

Let's look up as we walk

A famous song by Kyu Sakamoto (lyrics by Rokusuke Ei); the lyrics continue 「涙がこぼれないように」 (so the tears won't fall). Overseas, it became a huge hit under the title 'Sukiyaki.'

Example

つらいときこそ、上を向いて歩こう。
つらい ときこそ、 うえお むいて あるこう。
When things are hard is exactly when you should hold your head up and keep walking.
