Japanese phrase of the day · May 31 · Proverb

木を見て森を見ず

きをみてもりをみず

can't see the forest for the trees

It means focusing so much on details that you miss the big picture. It's exactly like English 'can't see the forest for the trees.'

Example

単語ばかり覚えて文章を読まないのは、木を見て森を見ずだよ。
たんごばかり おぼえて ぶんしょうお よまないのわ、 きお みて もりお みずだよ。
Memorizing only vocabulary without ever reading texts is seeing the trees but not the forest.
