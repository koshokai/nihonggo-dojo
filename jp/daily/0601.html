Japanese phrase of the day · June 1 · Season

衣替え

ころもがえ

seasonal change of clothes

Changing your wardrobe with the seasons. Traditionally, Japanese schools and companies switch to summer clothes on June 1 and to winter clothes on October 1.

Example

今日から衣替えで、制服が半袖になった。
きょうから ころもがえで、 せいふくが はんそでに なった。
Today's the seasonal changeover, so our uniforms are short-sleeved now.
