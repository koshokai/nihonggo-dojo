Japanese phrase of the day · June 2 · Four-character idiom

八方美人

はっぽうびじん

someone who tries to please everyone

Literally 'a beauty from all eight directions,' but in Japanese it's mostly negative, describing someone who tries to please everyone and has no principles. Despite 「美人」 (beautiful person), it has nothing to do with looks.

Example

八方美人では、結局誰からも信頼されないよ。
はっぽうびじんでわ、 けっきょく だれからも しんらい されないよ。
If you try to please everyone, in the end nobody will really trust you.
