Japanese phrase of the day · June 3 · Idiom

腹が立つ

はらがたつ

to get angry

Japanese uses 「腹」 (belly) to talk about emotions, so 「腹が立つ」, literally 'one's belly stands up,' means getting angry. It's a very common everyday expression.

Example

約束を破られて、本当に腹が立った。
やくそくお やぶられて、 ほんとうに はらが たった。
They broke their promise, and I was really angry.
