Japanese phrase of the day · June 5 · Proverb

雨降って地固まる

あめふってじかたまる

After the rain, the ground grows firm

Literally 'after it rains, the ground hardens,' it means relationships or situations often become stronger after a conflict or hardship. Compare English 'Every cloud has a silver lining.'

Example

大げんかしたけど、雨降って地固まるで、今は前より仲がいい。
だいげんか したけど、 あめ ふって じ かたまるで、 いまわ まえより なかが いい。
We had a huge fight, but the ground is firmer after the rain: we get along better now than before.
