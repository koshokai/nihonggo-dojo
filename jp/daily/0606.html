Japanese phrase of the day · June 6 · Season

芒種

ぼうしゅ

Grain in Ear (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around June 6. It's the time for planting grains with awns, such as rice and wheat.

Example

芒種のころ、田んぼでは田植えが盛んだ。
ぼうしゅの ころ、 たんぼでわ たうえが さかんだ。
Around Grain in Ear, rice planting is in full swing in the paddies.
