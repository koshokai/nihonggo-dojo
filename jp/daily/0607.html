Japanese phrase of the day · June 7 · Four-character idiom

試行錯誤

しこうさくご

trial and error

A translation of the English 'trial and error,' it means trying things over and over and improving through failure. English speakers will find it easy to remember.

Example

試行錯誤を重ねて、ようやく新しいレシピが完成した。
しこうさくごお かさねて、 ようやく あたらしい レシピが かんせい した。
After a lot of trial and error, I finally perfected my new recipe.
