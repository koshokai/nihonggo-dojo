Japanese phrase of the day · June 8 · Season

梅雨入り

つゆいり

start of the rainy season

The beginning of the rainy season, officially announced by the Japan Meteorological Agency. In the Kanto region it usually starts in early June and brings more than a month of cloudy, rainy weather.

Example

関東地方も梅雨入りして、毎日傘が手放せない。
かんとうちほうも つゆいり して、 まいにちかさが てばなせない。
The rainy season has started in Kanto too, and I can't go anywhere without an umbrella.
