Japanese phrase of the day · June 9 · Idiom

耳を傾ける

みみをかたむける

to listen carefully; to lend an ear

Literally 'to tilt one's ear,' it means listening attentively to what others say or think. English says 'to lend an ear.'

Example

リーダーは部下の意見に耳を傾けるべきだ。
リーダーわ ぶかの いけんに みみお かたむけるべきだ。
A leader should listen to the opinions of their team.
