Japanese phrase of the day · June 11 · Proverb

遠くの親類より近くの他人

とおくのしんるいよりちかくのたにん

A good neighbor is better than a distant relative

It means that in an emergency, relatives who live far away are less help than the neighbors or friends close by. English has a similar proverb: 'Better a neighbor near than a brother far off.'

Example

遠くの親類より近くの他人。お隣さんとは仲良くしておこう。
とおくの しんるいより ちかくの たにん。 おとなりさんとわ なかよく しておこう。
A neighbor nearby is worth more than a relative far away. Let's stay on good terms with the people next door.
