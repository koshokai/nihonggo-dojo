Japanese phrase of the day · June 12 · Season

紫陽花

あじさい

hydrangea

The signature flower of the rainy season. Its color changes depending on the acidity of the soil, and the hydrangeas of places like Kamakura are famous.

Example

雨にぬれた紫陽花が、いちばんきれいに見える。
あめに ぬれた あじさいが、 いちばん きれいに みえる。
Hydrangeas look their most beautiful when they're wet with rain.
