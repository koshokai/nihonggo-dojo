Japanese phrase of the day · June 13 · Four-character idiom

心機一転

しんきいってん

a fresh start; turning over a new leaf

Means completely changing your outlook because of some event, often when moving, changing jobs, or at the New Year. It's a very common expression in Japanese.

Example

引っ越しを機に、心機一転して勉強を頑張ろう。
ひっこしお きに、 しんきいってん して べんきょうお がんばろう。
Let's use the move as a fresh start and really buckle down on studying.
