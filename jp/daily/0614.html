Japanese phrase of the day · June 14 · Idiom

猫の手も借りたい

ねこのてもかりたい

so busy you'd take help from anyone

Literally 'I'd even like to borrow a cat's paws,' it describes being incredibly busy and short-handed. It's often used as 「猫の手も借りたいほど忙しい」.

Example

年末は猫の手も借りたいほど忙しい。
ねんまつわ ねこの ても かりたいほど いそがしい。
The end of the year is so busy I'd take help from anyone, even the cat.
