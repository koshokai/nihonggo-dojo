Japanese phrase of the day · June 15 · Kind words

おかげさまで

Thanks to you; I'm doing well, thank you

A modest reply when someone asks how you are, meaning things are going well thanks to everyone's support. You can use it even if the person didn't directly help you.

Example

「お元気ですか。」「はい、おかげさまで元気にしています。」
「 おげんきですか。 」「 はい、 おかげさまで げんきに しています。 」
"How are you?" "I'm doing well, thank you."
