Japanese phrase of the day · June 16 · Season

五月雨

さみだれ

early summer rain (the rainy season)

Rain in the fifth month of the old lunar calendar, in other words the rainy season. The phrase 「五月雨式に」 also describes things that happen in scattered, on-and-off bits.

Example

五月雨が降り続いて、洗濯物がなかなか乾かない。
さみだれが ふりつづいて、 せんたくものが なかなか かわかない。
The early summer rain keeps falling, and the laundry just won't dry.
