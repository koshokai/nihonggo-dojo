Japanese phrase of the day · June 17 · Proverb

猫に小判

ねこにこばん

pearls before swine

Literally 'gold coins to a cat,' it means giving something valuable to someone who can't appreciate it is pointless. The English equivalent is 'casting pearls before swine.'

Example

料理をしない僕に高い包丁なんて、猫に小判だよ。
りょうりお しない ぼくに たかい ほうちょうなんて、 ねこに こばんだよ。
An expensive kitchen knife for me, when I never cook? That's pearls before swine.
