Japanese phrase of the day · June 18 · Four-character idiom

才色兼備

さいしょくけんび

both brains and beauty

Describes a woman who has both talent and good looks. It's generally used only for women.

Example

彼女は才色兼備で、みんなの憧れだ。
かのじょわ さいしょくけんびで、 みんなの あこがれだ。
She has both brains and beauty, and everyone looks up to her.
