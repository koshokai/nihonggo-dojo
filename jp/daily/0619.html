Japanese phrase of the day · June 19 · Idiom

猫をかぶる

ねこをかぶる

to put on an innocent act

Literally 'to wear a cat,' it means hiding your true nature and acting meek and well-behaved in front of others. English speakers might say someone is 'on their best behavior.'

Example

彼は初対面では猫をかぶっているが、本当はうるさい。
かれわ はつたいめんでわ ねこお かぶっているが、 ほんとうわ うるさい。
He acts all quiet and polite when you first meet him, but he's actually really loud.
