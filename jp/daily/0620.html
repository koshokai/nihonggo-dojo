Japanese phrase of the day · June 20 · Kind words

また明日

またあした

See you tomorrow

An everyday phrase for parting. It carries a reassuring sense that you'll see each other again tomorrow.

Example

今日は楽しかったね。また明日！
きょうわ たのしかったね。 また あした！
Today was so much fun. See you tomorrow!
