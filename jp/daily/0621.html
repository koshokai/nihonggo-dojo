Japanese phrase of the day · June 21 · Season

夏至

げし

Summer Solstice (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around June 21, the day with the most daylight of the year. In Japan it falls during the rainy season, so the sun often isn't even visible.

Example

夏至の日は、夜七時を過ぎてもまだ明るい。
げしの ひわ、 よるしちじお すぎても まだ あかるい。
On the summer solstice, it's still light out even after seven in the evening.
