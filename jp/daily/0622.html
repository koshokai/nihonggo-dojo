Japanese phrase of the day · June 22 · Proverb

沈黙は金、雄弁は銀

ちんもくはきん、ゆうべんはぎん

Silence is golden

From the Western proverb 'Speech is silver, silence is golden.' It means that being eloquent is good, but knowing when to keep quiet is even more valuable.

Example

会議で余計なことを言わなかったのは正解だ。沈黙は金、雄弁は銀だよ。
かいぎで よけいな ことお いわなかったのわ せいかいだ。 ちんもくわ きん、 ゆうべんわ ぎんだよ。
You were right not to say anything unnecessary at the meeting. Silence is golden, speech is silver.
