Japanese phrase of the day · June 23 · Four-character idiom

無病息災

むびょうそくさい

good health and safety

Means staying healthy and free from misfortune. It's often used when praying at shrines.

Example

家族の無病息災を神社で祈った。
かぞくの むびょうそくさいお じんじゃで いのった。
I prayed at the shrine for my family's good health.
