Japanese phrase of the day · June 24 · Idiom

手を抜く

てをぬく

to cut corners; to slack off

Literally 'to pull out one's hand,' it means not putting proper effort into work you're supposed to do. English says 'to cut corners.'

Example

掃除の手を抜くと、すぐばれるよ。
そうじの てお ぬくと、 すぐ ばれるよ。
If you cut corners on the cleaning, it'll be obvious right away.
