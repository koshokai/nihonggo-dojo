Japanese phrase of the day · June 25 · Season

梅雨の晴れ間

つゆのはれま

a break in the rainy season

A brief sunny spell during the rainy season. People make the most of it to air out their futons and do the laundry.

Example

梅雨の晴れ間に、布団を干しておこう。
つゆの はれまに、 ふとんお ほしておこう。
Let's air out the futons while there's a break in the rain.
