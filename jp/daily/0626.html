Japanese phrase of the day · June 26 · Kind words

一歩ずつでいい

いっぽずつでいい

One step at a time is fine

Comforts someone in a hurry, telling them they don't have to be perfect all at once and can move forward slowly. It's like English 'Take it one step at a time.'

Example

焦らなくていいよ。一歩ずつでいいんだから。
あせらなくていいよ。 いっぽずつでいいんだから。
Don't rush. One step at a time is all it takes.
