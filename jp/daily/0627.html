Japanese phrase of the day · June 27 · Proverb

孝行したい時に親はなし

こうこうしたいときにおやはなし

By the time you want to repay your parents, they're gone

It means that by the time you want to show your parents gratitude, they may no longer be alive. It urges people to be good to their parents while they still can.

Example

孝行したい時に親はなしというから、たまには実家に帰ろう。
こうこう したい ときに おやわ なしと いうから、 たまにわ じっかに かえろう。
They say that by the time you want to repay your parents, they're gone, so I should visit home once in a while.
