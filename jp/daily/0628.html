Japanese phrase of the day · June 28 · Four-character idiom

付和雷同

ふわらいどう

blindly following others

Describes someone with no opinion of their own who just goes along with whatever others say. English calls this 'following the herd.'

Example

付和雷同せず、自分の意見を持ちなさい。
ふわらいどう せず、 じぶんの いけんお もちなさい。
Don't just follow the crowd. Have opinions of your own.
