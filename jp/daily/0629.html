Japanese phrase of the day · June 29 · Idiom

鯖を読む

さばをよむ

to fudge the numbers (often one's age)

Mackerel spoils quickly, so fishmongers are said to have counted them in a hurry and often got the numbers wrong. It came to mean deliberately misstating figures, especially shaving years off one's age.

Example

彼女は年を五歳も鯖を読んでいた。
かのじょわ としお ごさいも さばお よんでいた。
She had been knocking a full five years off her age.
