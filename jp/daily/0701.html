Japanese phrase of the day · July 1 · Season

山開き

やまびらき

the opening of the climbing season

The day the climbing season officially opens and a mountain may be climbed. Mt. Fuji traditionally opens on July 1 (on the Yamanashi side).

Example

富士山の山開きを待って、友だちと登る計画を立てた。
ふじさんの やまびらきお まって、 ともだちと のぼる けいかくお たてた。
My friends and I are waiting for Mt. Fuji to open for the season, and we've made plans to climb it.
