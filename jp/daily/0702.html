Japanese phrase of the day · July 2 · Proverb

隣の芝生は青い

となりのしばふはあおい

The grass is always greener on the other side

From the English proverb, it means other people's things always look better than your own. Literally 'the neighbor's lawn is green.'

Example

隣の芝生は青いというけど、自分の会社もそんなに悪くない。
となりの しばふわ あおいと いうけど、 じぶんの かいしゃも そんなに わるく ない。
They say the grass is always greener on the other side, but my own company isn't so bad after all.
