Japanese phrase of the day · July 4 · Idiom

胸がいっぱいになる

むねがいっぱいになる

to be overwhelmed with emotion

Describes feelings such as being moved, joy, or sadness filling your chest so much that you can't speak. English similarly says 'my heart was full.'

Example

卒業式で、胸がいっぱいになった。
そつぎょうしきで、 むねが いっぱいに なった。
At the graduation ceremony, my heart was full.
