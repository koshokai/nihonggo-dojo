Japanese phrase of the day · July 6 · Proverb

捨てる神あれば拾う神あり

すてるかみあればひろうかみあり

When one door closes, another opens

Literally 'if there's a god who throws you away, there's also a god who picks you up,' it means that if someone rejects you, someone else will help you, so don't despair. The English equivalent is 'When one door closes, another opens.'

Example

一社落ちても、捨てる神あれば拾う神ありだよ。
いっしゃ おちても、 すてる かみ あれば ひろう かみありだよ。
So one company turned you down. When one door closes, another one opens.
