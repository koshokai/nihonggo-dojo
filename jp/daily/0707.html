Japanese phrase of the day · July 7 · Season

七夕

たなばた

Tanabata, the Star Festival

In Japan, Tanabata is usually celebrated on July 7 of the modern calendar. People write their wishes on colorful paper strips called 「短冊」 and hang them on bamboo branches.

Example

七夕の短冊に「日本語が上手になりますように」と書いた。
たなばたの たんざくに「 にほんごが じょうずに なります ように」と かいた。
On my Tanabata wish strip, I wrote, 'I hope my Japanese gets better.'
