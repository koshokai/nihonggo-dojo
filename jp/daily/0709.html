Japanese phrase of the day · July 9 · Idiom

胸を打つ

むねをうつ

to touch one's heart

Literally 'to strike the chest,' it means to move people deeply. English says something 'touches your heart.'

Example

彼のスピーチは多くの人の胸を打った。
かれの スピーチわ おおくの ひとの むねお うった。
His speech touched the hearts of many people.
