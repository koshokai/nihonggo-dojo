Japanese phrase of the day · July 10 · Kind words

よかったね

That's great! I'm so happy for you

Said when you're genuinely glad about someone's good news. It shows that you share in their joy.

Example

「第一志望に受かったよ！」「本当によかったね！」
「 だいいちしぼうに うかったよ！」「 ほんとうに よかったね！」
"I got into my first-choice school!" "That's wonderful!"
