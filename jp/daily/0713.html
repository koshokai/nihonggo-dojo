Japanese phrase of the day · July 13 · Idiom

尻に敷く

しりにしく

to have one's husband under one's thumb

Literally 'to sit on someone,' it means the wife wears the pants in the family. A husband who gets bossed around is said to be 「尻に敷かれる」, like English 'henpecked.'

Example

彼は奥さんの尻に敷かれている。
かれわ おくさんの しりに しかれている。
He's completely under his wife's thumb.
