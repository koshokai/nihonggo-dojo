Japanese phrase of the day · July 15 · Kind words

雨ニモマケズ

あめにもまけず

Unbeaten by the rain

The opening of a poem by Kenji Miyazawa, followed by 「風ニモマケズ」 (unbeaten by the wind). The original is written in katakana and describes an ideal person who is strong, simple, and thinks of others.

Example

雨ニモマケズ、風ニモマケズ、毎日コツコツ続けよう。
あめニモマケズ、 かぜニモマケズ、 まいにち コツコツ つづけよう。
Unbeaten by rain, unbeaten by wind, let's keep at it steadily every day.
