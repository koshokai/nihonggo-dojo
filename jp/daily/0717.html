Japanese phrase of the day · July 17 · Four-character idiom

言語道断

ごんごどうだん

outrageous; inexcusable

Originally a Buddhist term meaning that profound truth cannot be expressed in words. In Japanese it shifted to mean 'so bad there are no words for it.'

Example

飲酒運転なんて言語道断だ。
いんしゅうんてんなんて げんごどうだんだ。
Drunk driving is absolutely inexcusable.
