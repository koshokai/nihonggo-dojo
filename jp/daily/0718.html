Japanese phrase of the day · July 18 · Idiom

肩を持つ

かたをもつ

to take someone's side

Literally 'to hold someone's shoulder,' it means backing one side in an argument. It often implies favoritism.

Example

母はいつも妹の肩を持つ。
ははわ いつも いもうとの かたお もつ。
My mom always takes my little sister's side.
