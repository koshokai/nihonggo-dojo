Japanese phrase of the day · July 20 · Kind words

無理しないでね

むりしないでね

Don't push yourself too hard

Tells someone you care about not to overdo it or force themselves; it's common between friends and family. To someone older or higher in status, say 「ご無理なさらないでください」.

Example

忙しいと思うけど、無理しないでね。
いそがしいと おもうけど、 むり しないでね。
I know you're busy, but don't push yourself too hard, OK?
