Japanese phrase of the day · July 22 · Proverb

情けは人のためならず

なさけはひとのためならず

Kindness comes back to you

It means that being kind to others eventually comes back to benefit you. It's often misunderstood as 'being kind to people only spoils them,' but that's incorrect.

Example

困っている人を助けよう。情けは人のためならずだよ。
こまっている ひとお たすけよう。 なさけわ ひとの ため ならずだよ。
Let's help people who are in trouble. Kindness always comes back around.
