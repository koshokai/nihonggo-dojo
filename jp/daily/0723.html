Japanese phrase of the day · July 23 · Season

大暑

たいしょ

Greater Heat (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, falling around July 23. It marks the hottest time of the year.

Example

大暑の今日は、外に出るだけで汗が止まらない。
たいしょの きょうわ、 そとに でるだけで あせが とまらない。
Today is Greater Heat, and just stepping outside makes me sweat buckets.
