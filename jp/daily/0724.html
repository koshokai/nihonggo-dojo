Japanese phrase of the day · July 24 · Four-character idiom

一生懸命

いっしょうけんめい

with all one's might; as hard as one can

Originally 「一所懸命」, describing samurai risking their lives to defend their one plot of land; it later came to mean 'trying as hard as you can.' It's one of the most commonly used words in Japanese.

Example

彼は毎日一生懸命日本語を勉強している。
かれわ まいにちいっしょう けんめい にほんごお べんきょう している。
He studies Japanese as hard as he can every day.
