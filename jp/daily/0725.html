Japanese phrase of the day · July 25 · Idiom

舌を巻く

したをまく

to be amazed; to be deeply impressed

Literally 'to curl one's tongue,' it describes being astonished by and full of admiration for someone's ability. It's close to English 'to be blown away.'

Example

彼の日本語の上手さには舌を巻いた。
かれの にほんごの じょうずさにわ したお まいた。
I was blown away by how good his Japanese is.
