Japanese phrase of the day · July 26 · Kind words

会えてうれしい

あえてうれしい

I'm so happy to see you

Expresses joy at meeting a friend or seeing someone after a long time. It sounds more heartfelt than a polite formula.

Example

久しぶりだね。会えてうれしいよ。
ひさしぶりだね。 あえて うれしいよ。
It's been so long! I'm so happy to see you.
