Japanese phrase of the day · July 27 · Proverb

親の心子知らず

おやのこころこしらず

Children never understand their parents' love

It means children don't realize how much their parents care and worry about them. Literally 'the child doesn't know the parent's heart.'

Example

毎日電話してくる母がうるさいなんて、親の心子知らずだね。
まいにちでんわ してくる ははが うるさいなんて、 おやの こころ こ しらずだね。
Calling your mom annoying for phoning every day? Children never understand how much their parents care.
