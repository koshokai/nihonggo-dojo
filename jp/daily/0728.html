Japanese phrase of the day · July 28 · Season

花火大会

はなびたいかい

fireworks festival

Large fireworks displays held all over Japan in summer. Many people go in yukata, and Tokyo's Sumida River Fireworks Festival is especially famous.

Example

浴衣を着て、友だちと花火大会に行った。
ゆかたお きて、 ともだちと はなびたいかいに いった。
I put on a yukata and went to the fireworks festival with my friends.
