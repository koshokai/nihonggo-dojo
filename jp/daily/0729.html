Japanese phrase of the day · July 29 · Four-character idiom

絶体絶命

ぜったいぜつめい

a desperate situation; with one's back to the wall

Describes being in a crisis with no way out. Note that it's written 「絶体」, not 「絶対」, a mistake even Japanese people often make.

Example

九回裏ツーアウト、チームは絶体絶命のピンチだ。
きゅうかいうらツーアウト、 チームわ ぜったいぜつめいの ピンチだ。
Bottom of the ninth, two outs, and the team's back is against the wall.
