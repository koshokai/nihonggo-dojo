Japanese phrase of the day · July 31 · Kind words

元気出して

げんきだして

Cheer up!

Comforts someone who is down or disappointed and encourages them to pick themselves up. 「元気」 means energy or spirits, so it's literally 'bring out your energy.'

Example

一回負けたくらいで落ち込まないで。元気出して！
いっかい まけたくらいで おちこまないで。 げんき だして！
Don't get discouraged just because you lost once. Cheer up!
