Japanese phrase of the day · August 1 · Proverb

覆水盆に返らず

ふくすいぼんにかえらず

What's done cannot be undone

Literally 'spilled water won't return to the tray.' From an ancient Chinese story about the statesman Jiang Ziya and his wife, it means that what's done cannot be undone; English says 'There's no use crying over spilled milk.'

Example

覆水盆に返らずだから、言葉には気をつけて。
ふくすいぼんに かえらずだから、 ことばにわ きお つけて。
Spilled water can't be put back, so be careful what you say.
