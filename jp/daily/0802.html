Japanese phrase of the day · August 2 · Four-character idiom

竜頭蛇尾

りゅうとうだび

starting strong but fizzling out

Literally 'dragon's head, snake's tail,' it describes something that begins impressively but ends weakly. In English you'd say it 'started with a bang and ended with a whimper.'

Example

その計画は竜頭蛇尾に終わった。
その けいかくわ りゅうずだびに おわった。
That plan started with a bang and ended with a whimper.
