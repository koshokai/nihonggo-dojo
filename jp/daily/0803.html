Japanese phrase of the day · August 3 · Season

夏バテ

なつばて

summer fatigue

Exhaustion and loss of appetite caused by the summer heat. To fight it off, Japanese people traditionally eat eel on 「土用の丑の日」, a 'Day of the Ox' in midsummer.

Example

夏バテで食欲がないけど、ちゃんと食べなきゃ。
なつばてで しょくよくが ないけど、 ちゃんと たべなきゃ。
The heat has killed my appetite, but I have to eat properly.
