Japanese phrase of the day · August 4 · Idiom

腹が黒い

はらがくろい

scheming; black-hearted

Literally 'one's belly is black,' it describes someone who seems nice on the surface but is scheming inside. English similarly says 'black-hearted.'

Example

優しそうに見えるが、実は腹が黒い人だ。
やさし そうに みえるが、 じつわ はらが くろい ひとだ。
He looks kind, but he's actually quite a schemer.
