Japanese phrase of the day · August 5 · Kind words

ただいま

I'm home

What you say when you get home; your family answers 「おかえり」. It's one of the warmest phrases in everyday Japanese life.

Example

ただいま。あー、やっぱり家が一番落ち着く。
ただいま。 あー、 やっぱり いえが いちばん おちつく。
I'm home. Ah, there really is no place like home.
