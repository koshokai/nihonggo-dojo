Japanese phrase of the day · August 6 · Proverb

雨垂れ石を穿つ

あまだれいしをうがつ

Constant dripping wears away the stone

Raindrops falling from the eaves can bore through stone over many years, meaning that persistence pays off in the end. English has exactly the same proverb: 'Constant dripping wears away the stone.'

Example

雨垂れ石を穿つで、毎日の少しの努力が大きな力になる。
あまだれいしお うがつで、 まいにちの すこしの どりょくが おおきな ちからに なる。
Constant dripping wears away the stone: a little effort every day adds up to great strength.
