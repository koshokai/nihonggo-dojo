Japanese phrase of the day · August 7 · Season

立秋

りっしゅう

Beginning of Autumn (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around August 7. Autumn begins on the calendar, but in reality the heat is still intense.

Example

立秋とはいえ、まだまだ暑さが厳しい。
りっしゅうとわ いえ、 まだまだ あつさが きびしい。
It may be the beginning of autumn, but the heat is still brutal.
