Japanese phrase of the day · August 9 · Four-character idiom

一石二鳥

いっせきにちょう

killing two birds with one stone

Literally 'one stone, two birds,' it comes from the English 'kill two birds with one stone.' So English speakers will find it easy to remember.

Example

自転車通勤は運動にもなるし節約にもなるし、一石二鳥だ。
じてんしゃ つうきんわ うんどうにも なるし せつやくにも なるし、 いっせきにちょうだ。
Biking to work is exercise and saves money too: two birds with one stone.
