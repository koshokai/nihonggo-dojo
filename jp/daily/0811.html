Japanese phrase of the day · August 11 · Kind words

笑顔が一番

えがおがいちばん

A smile is what matters most

Encourages people to keep smiling, because a smile beats everything else. 「一番」 means 'number one' or 'the best.'

Example

いろいろあったけど、やっぱり笑顔が一番だね。
いろいろ あったけど、 やっぱり えがおが いちばんだね。
A lot has happened, but in the end, a smile is what matters most.
