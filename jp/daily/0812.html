Japanese phrase of the day · August 12 · Season

帰省

きせい

going back to one's hometown

Returning to your hometown to visit family when you live somewhere else. Obon and New Year's are the two big times for this in Japan, and the resulting travel rush is called 「帰省ラッシュ」.

Example

お盆休みに、三年ぶりに実家へ帰省した。
おぼんやすみに、 さんねんぶりに じっかえ きせい した。
During the Obon holidays, I went back to my parents' home for the first time in three years.
