Japanese phrase of the day · August 13 · Season

お盆

おぼん

Obon (festival of the ancestors)

A festival for welcoming the spirits of ancestors back home, usually held from August 13 to 16. Many companies give 「お盆休み」 (Obon holidays), and people return to their hometowns to visit graves and join in Bon dancing.

Example

お盆には家族が集まって、ご先祖様をお迎えする。
おぼんにわ かぞくが あつまって、 ごせんぞさまお おむかえする。
At Obon, the family gathers to welcome our ancestors home.
