Japanese phrase of the day · August 15 · Four-character idiom

良妻賢母

りょうさいけんぼ

a good wife and wise mother

Literally 'good wife, wise mother' (良 means good and 賢 means wise). From the late nineteenth century onward, it served as a slogan for women's education in Japan.

Example

祖母は良妻賢母として知られていた。
そぼわ りょうさいけんぼと して しられていた。
My grandmother was known as a good wife and a wise mother.
