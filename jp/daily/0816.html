Japanese phrase of the day · August 16 · Season

送り火

おくりび

send-off fires for ancestral spirits

Fires lit on the last day of Obon to send the ancestors' spirits back. The most famous is Kyoto's 「五山送り火」 on August 16.

Example

京都で五山の送り火を見て、夏の終わりを感じた。
きょうとで ござんの おくりびお みて、 なつの おわりお かんじた。
Watching the Gozan send-off fires in Kyoto, I felt that summer was coming to an end.
