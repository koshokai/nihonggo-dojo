Japanese phrase of the day · August 17 · Idiom

道草を食う

みちくさをくう

to dawdle on the way

Literally 'to eat the grass by the road,' like a horse stopping to graze along the way. It means wasting time on other things when you're supposed to be going somewhere.

Example

学校の帰りに道草を食って、母に叱られた。
がっこうの かえりに みちくさお くって、 ははに しかられた。
I dawdled on my way home from school and got scolded by my mom.
