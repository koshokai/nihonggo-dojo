Japanese phrase of the day · August 19 · Proverb

旅は道連れ世は情け

たびはみちづれよはなさけ

On a journey, companions; in life, kindness

Literally 'on a journey, a companion; in this world, compassion.' It means a traveling companion gives you peace of mind on the road, and getting through life takes kindness and helping one another.

Example

旅は道連れ世は情けで、旅先で出会った人に助けてもらった。
たびわ みちづれよわ なさけで、 たびさきで であった ひとに たすけてもらった。
Journeys need companions and life needs kindness: some people I met on my trip helped me out.
