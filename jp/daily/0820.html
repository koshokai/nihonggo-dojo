Japanese phrase of the day · August 20 · Four-character idiom

臨機応変

りんきおうへん

adapting to the situation

Means handling things flexibly according to the circumstances. English might say 'to play it by ear.'

Example

マニュアルどおりではなく、臨機応変に対応してください。
マニュアルどおりでわ なく、 りんきおうへんに たいおう してください。
Please don't just go by the manual; deal with things flexibly as they come up.
