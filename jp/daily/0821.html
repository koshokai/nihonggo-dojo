Japanese phrase of the day · August 21 · Idiom

足を引っ張る

あしをひっぱる

to hold someone back; to drag someone down

Literally 'to pull someone's leg,' but it has nothing to do with teasing! It means hindering others or being a burden on the team, like English 'to drag someone down.'

Example

チームの足を引っ張らないように頑張る。
チームの あしお ひっぱらない ように がんばる。
I'll do my best not to drag the team down.
