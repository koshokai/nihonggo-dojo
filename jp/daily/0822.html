Japanese phrase of the day · August 22 · Kind words

ゆっくり休んでね

ゆっくりやすんでね

Get some good rest

Caring words for someone who is tired, sick, or has just finished a busy stretch. 「ゆっくり」 means slowly, without hurrying.

Example

今日は早く寝て、ゆっくり休んでね。
きょうわ はやく ねて、 ゆっくり やすんでね。
Go to bed early tonight and get some good rest.
