Japanese phrase of the day · August 23 · Season

処暑

しょしょ

End of Heat (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around August 23. 「処」 here means 'to stop,' as the summer heat gradually eases.

Example

処暑を過ぎて、朝晩は少し涼しくなってきた。
しょしょお すぎて、 あさばんわ すこし すずしく なってきた。
Now that the End of Heat has passed, the mornings and evenings have gotten a little cooler.
