Japanese phrase of the day · August 24 · Proverb

歳月人を待たず

さいげつひとをまたず

Time waits for no one

From a line by the Chinese poet Tao Yuanming, 'Strive while you can; the years wait for no one,' it means time never comes back, so make the most of it. English says 'Time and tide wait for no man.'

Example

歳月人を待たず。やりたいことは今やろう。
さいげつ ひとお またず。 やりたい ことわ いま やろう。
Time waits for no one. Let's do what we want to do now.
