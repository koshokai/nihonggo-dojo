Japanese phrase of the day · August 25 · Four-character idiom

有言実行

ゆうげんじっこう

walking the talk; doing what you say

A Japanese coinage modeled on 「不言実行」, it means announcing a goal publicly and then achieving it. The English equivalent is 'to walk the talk.'

Example

彼は有言実行の人で、宣言どおり大会で優勝した。
かれわ ゆうげんじっこうの ひとで、 せんげんどおりたいかいで ゆうしょう した。
He's a man of his word: he won the tournament, just as he said he would.
