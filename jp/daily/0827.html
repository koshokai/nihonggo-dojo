Japanese phrase of the day · August 27 · Kind words

よく頑張ったね

よくがんばったね

You did a great job

Recognizes the effort someone has made, whatever the result. It's very warm praise.

Example

結果はどうであれ、よく頑張ったね。
けっかわ どうであれ、 よく がんばったね。
Whatever the result, you did a great job.
