Japanese phrase of the day · August 28 · Proverb

負けるが勝ち

まけるがかち

Losing is winning

It means that giving in or admitting defeat for now can work to your advantage in the long run. English has a related idea in 'Lose the battle, win the war.'

Example

兄弟げんかは負けるが勝ちだよ。先に謝ったほうがいい。
きょうだいげんかわ まけるが かちだよ。 さきに あやまった ほうが いい。
In fights between brothers, the one who gives in wins. It's better to apologize first.
