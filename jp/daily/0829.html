Japanese phrase of the day · August 29 · Four-character idiom

正真正銘

しょうしんしょうめい

the real deal; genuine

Means absolutely nothing fake about it: the genuine article. It's often used to stress that something is authentic.

Example

これは正真正銘のピカソの絵だ。
これわ しょうしんしょうめいの ピカソの えだ。
This is a genuine Picasso painting.
