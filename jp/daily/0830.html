Japanese phrase of the day · August 30 · Idiom

骨が折れる

ほねがおれる

to be hard work; to take a lot of effort

Literally 'bones break,' but as an idiom it only means that something takes a lot of effort, not that anyone actually breaks a bone. English has a similar image in 'back-breaking work.'

Example

この資料をまとめるのは骨が折れる。
この しりょうお まとめるのわ ほねが おれる。
Putting these materials together is hard work.
