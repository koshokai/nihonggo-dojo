Japanese phrase of the day · September 1 · Season

防災の日

ぼうさいのひ

Disaster Prevention Day

Established in memory of the Great Kanto Earthquake of September 1, 1923. On this day, disaster drills are held across Japan and people check their emergency supplies.

Example

防災の日なので、家の非常用持ち出し袋を点検した。
ぼうさいの ひなので、 いえの ひじょうよう もちだしふくろお てんけん した。
Since it's Disaster Prevention Day, I checked our emergency grab bag at home.
