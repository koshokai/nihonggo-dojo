Japanese phrase of the day · September 2 · Proverb

読書百遍義自ずから見る

どくしょひゃっぺんぎおのずからあらわる

Read a book a hundred times and its meaning becomes clear

It comes from the commentary on the Chinese history Records of the Three Kingdoms. It means that if you read a difficult book over and over, you will naturally come to understand it.

Example

難しい文章も、読書百遍義自ずから見るで、繰り返し読めばわかる。
むずかしい ぶんしょうも、 どくしょひゃっぺん ぎ おのずから あらわるで、 くりかえし よめば わかる。
Even a difficult text will make sense if you read it again and again: read it a hundred times and the meaning reveals itself.
