Japanese phrase of the day · September 3 · Four-character idiom

晴耕雨読

せいこううどく

farming on sunny days, reading on rainy days

Literally 'plow when it's sunny, read when it rains,' it describes a leisurely country life. It's often used to describe an ideal retirement.

Example

退職後は田舎で晴耕雨読の生活を送りたい。
たいしょくごわ いなかで せいこううどくの せいかつお おくりたい。
After I retire, I want to live in the countryside, farming when it's sunny and reading when it rains.
