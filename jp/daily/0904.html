Japanese phrase of the day · September 4 · Idiom

腹を割る

はらをわる

to open up; to speak frankly

Literally 'to split open one's belly,' it means saying what you really think without hiding anything. English might say 'to have a heart-to-heart.'

Example

一度腹を割って話し合おう。
いちどはらお わって はなしあおう。
Let's sit down and have a heart-to-heart talk.
