Japanese phrase of the day · September 5 · Kind words

気にしないで

きにしないで

Don't worry about it

Reassures someone who has apologized or feels bad, telling them not to let it bother them. 「気にする」 means to mind or worry about something.

Example

ちょっと遅れたくらい、気にしないで。
ちょっと おくれたくらい、 きに しないで。
Being a little late is nothing. Don't worry about it.
