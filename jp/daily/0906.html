Japanese phrase of the day · September 6 · Proverb

立つ鳥跡を濁さず

たつとりあとをにごさず

Leave things as you found them

Literally 'a bird that flies off doesn't muddy the water behind it.' It means that when you leave a place, you should tidy up and settle everything properly.

Example

立つ鳥跡を濁さずで、引っ越す前に部屋をきれいに掃除した。
たつ とりあとお にごさずで、 ひっこす まえに へやお きれいに そうじ した。
Like a bird that leaves the water clear, I cleaned my room thoroughly before moving out.
