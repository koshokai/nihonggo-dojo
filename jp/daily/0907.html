Japanese phrase of the day · September 7 · Four-character idiom

真剣勝負

しんけんしょうぶ

a serious contest; playing for keeps

Originally a duel fought with real swords instead of wooden ones. It now means an all-out contest where both sides are completely serious.

Example

練習試合でも真剣勝負で臨むつもりだ。
れんしゅうしあいでも しんけんしょうぶで のぞむ つもりだ。
Even if it's just a practice match, I intend to go all out.
