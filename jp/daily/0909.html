Japanese phrase of the day · September 9 · Season

重陽の節句

ちょうようのせっく

Chrysanthemum Festival (the Double Ninth)

September 9, one of the five seasonal festivals, also called 「菊の節句」 (the Chrysanthemum Festival). Customs include admiring chrysanthemums and drinking chrysanthemum sake to pray for long life.

Example

重陽の節句には、菊の花を飾って長寿を願う。
ちょうようの せっくにわ、 きくの はなお かざって ちょうじゅお ねがう。
For the Chrysanthemum Festival, we display chrysanthemums and pray for long life.
