Japanese phrase of the day · September 10 · Idiom

目に余る

めにあまる

too much to overlook; intolerable

Describes words or actions so excessive that you can't just stand by and watch. Literally 'too much for the eyes.'

Example

彼の最近の態度は目に余る。
かれの さいきんの たいどわ めに あまる。
His attitude lately is more than I can put up with.
