Japanese phrase of the day · September 11 · Kind words

待ってるね

まってるね

I'll be waiting

Shows that you're looking forward to someone coming or coming back. It has a friendly, affectionate tone.

Example

駅の前で待ってるね。ゆっくり来ていいよ。
えきの まえで まってるね。 ゆっくり きていいよ。
I'll be waiting in front of the station. Take your time.
