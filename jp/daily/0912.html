Japanese phrase of the day · September 12 · Proverb

仏の顔も三度まで

ほとけのかおもさんどまで

Even a saint's patience has limits

Literally 'even the Buddha's face (can be stroked) only three times,' it means that even the gentlest person will get angry if wronged again and again. English might say 'Three strikes and you're out.'

Example

遅刻はこれで三回目だよ。仏の顔も三度までだからね。
ちこくわ これで さんかいめだよ。 ほとけの かおも さんどまでだからね。
This is the third time you've been late. Even my patience has limits, you know.
