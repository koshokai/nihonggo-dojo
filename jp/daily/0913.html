Japanese phrase of the day · September 13 · Four-character idiom

四苦八苦

しくはっく

struggling desperately

Originally a Buddhist term for all the sufferings of life, such as birth, aging, sickness, and death. In everyday Japanese it means having a really hard time with something.

Example

慣れない敬語に四苦八苦している。
なれない けいごに しくはっく している。
I'm having a terrible time with honorific language, since I'm not used to it.
