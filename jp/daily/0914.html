Japanese phrase of the day · September 14 · Idiom

鼻が高い

はながたかい

to be proud

Literally 'one's nose is high.' It means feeling proud because of your own or a family member's achievements, and unlike English 'nose in the air,' it isn't negative.

Example

息子が賞をもらって、親として鼻が高い。
むすこが しょうお もらって、 おやと して はなが たかい。
My son won an award, and as his parent I'm very proud.
