Japanese phrase of the day · September 15 · Season

秋の夜長

あきのよなが

the long autumn nights

A seasonal phrase for the lengthening nights of autumn. It suggests a time well suited to reading, listening to music, and quiet evenings.

Example

秋の夜長に、ゆっくり小説を読むのが好きだ。
あきの よながに、 ゆっくり しょうせつお よむのが すきだ。
I love reading novels at a leisurely pace on long autumn nights.
