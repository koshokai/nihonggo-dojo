Japanese phrase of the day · September 16 · Kind words

大丈夫、なんとかなる

だいじょうぶ、なんとかなる

It's okay, it'll work out somehow

「なんとかなる」 means 'somehow it'll work out' or 'we'll manage.' It's often used to cheer up yourself or others with an optimistic tone.

Example

失敗しても大丈夫、なんとかなるよ。
しっぱい しても だいじょうぶ、 なんとか なるよ。
Even if you fail, it's okay. Things will work out somehow.
