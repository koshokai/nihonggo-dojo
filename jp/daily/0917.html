Japanese phrase of the day · September 17 · Proverb

親しき仲にも礼儀あり

したしきなかにもれいぎあり

Good manners even among close friends

It means that no matter how close you are, you should still keep proper courtesy and respect boundaries. Literally 'even in close relationships there is etiquette.'

Example

親しき仲にも礼儀あり。借りたものはちゃんと返そう。
したしき なかにも れいぎあり。 かりた ものわ ちゃんと かえそう。
Good manners matter even between close friends. Return the things you borrow.
