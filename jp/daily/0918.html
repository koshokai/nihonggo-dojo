Japanese phrase of the day · September 18 · Four-character idiom

唯我独尊

ゆいがどくそん

self-centered arrogance

From words the Buddha is said to have spoken at birth: 'In heaven and on earth, I alone am honored.' In everyday Japanese it's mostly negative, describing someone conceited and arrogant.

Example

彼の唯我独尊な態度に、みんな困っている。
かれの ゆいがどくそんな たいどに、 みんな こまっている。
Everyone is fed up with his arrogant, me-first attitude.
