Japanese phrase of the day · September 20 · Season

彼岸花

ひがんばな

red spider lily

A red flower that blooms around the autumn equinox, named after the autumn 「彼岸」 period when it appears. It's also called 「曼珠沙華」.

Example

田んぼのあぜ道に、真っ赤な彼岸花が咲いていた。
たんぼの あぜみちに、 まっかな ひがんばなが さいていた。
Bright red spider lilies were blooming along the paths between the rice paddies.
