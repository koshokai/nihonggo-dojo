Japanese phrase of the day · September 21 · Kind words

気持ちだけでうれしい

きもちだけでうれしい

The thought alone makes me happy

Said when someone offers to help or to give you something, meaning you appreciate the thought and they don't need to go to any trouble. English says 'It's the thought that counts.'

Example

気持ちだけでうれしいよ。本当にありがとう。
きもちだけで うれしいよ。 ほんとうに ありがとう。
Just the thought makes me happy. Thank you so much.
