Japanese phrase of the day · September 22 · Proverb

百里を行く者は九十を半ばとす

ひゃくりをゆくものはくじゅうをなかばとす

Ninety miles is only halfway on a hundred-mile journey

From the ancient Chinese text Strategies of the Warring States, it means that the closer you are to finishing, the more careful you must be, because the last stretch is the hardest. Don't let up.

Example

卒論はあと少しだけど、百里を行く者は九十を半ばとすだ。
そつろんわ あと すこしだけど、 ひゃくりお いく ものわ きゅうじゅうお なかばと すだ。
My graduation thesis is almost done, but ninety miles is only halfway on a hundred-mile road.
