Japanese phrase of the day · September 24 · Four-character idiom

門外不出

もんがいふしゅつ

closely guarded; never shown to outsiders

Describes a precious object or skill that is never taken out of the house or revealed to outsiders. It's often used for secret recipes and family treasures.

Example

この店のたれは門外不出の秘伝だ。
この みせの たれわ もんがいふしゅつの ひでんだ。
This restaurant's sauce is made from a closely guarded secret recipe.
