Japanese phrase of the day · September 25 · Season

十五夜

じゅうごや

the night of the harvest moon

The moonlit night of the 15th day of the eighth lunar month. People view the moon with offerings of dumplings and pampas grass, which is called 「お月見」; the date changes every year but usually falls in mid-to-late September.

Example

十五夜にお月見団子を食べながら、きれいな満月を眺めた。
じゅうごやに おつきみだんごお たべながら、 きれいな まんげつお ながめた。
On the night of the harvest moon, we ate moon-viewing dumplings and gazed at the beautiful full moon.
