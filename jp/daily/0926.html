Japanese phrase of the day · September 26 · Idiom

足が棒になる

あしがぼうになる

legs as stiff as sticks

Literally 'one's legs turn into sticks.' It describes legs that are stiff and aching from walking or standing too long.

Example

一日中歩き回って、足が棒になった。
いちにちじゅう あるきまわって、 あしが ぼうに なった。
After walking around all day, my legs were stiff as boards.
