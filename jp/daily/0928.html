Japanese phrase of the day · September 28 · Proverb

玉磨かざれば光なし

たまみがかざればひかりなし

An unpolished gem won't shine

It means that even the most talented person can't shine without study and training. It's close to a line in the Chinese classic Book of Rites: 'Jade that is not carved will not become a vessel.'

Example

才能があっても、玉磨かざれば光なしだよ。毎日練習しよう。
さいのうが あっても、 たま みがかざれば ひかりなしだよ。 まいにちれんしゅう しよう。
Even if you have talent, an unpolished gem won't shine. Let's practice every day.
