Japanese phrase of the day · September 30 · Idiom

お茶を濁す

おちゃをにごす

to gloss over; to fudge

Literally 'to muddy the tea'; it's said that people who didn't know the tea ceremony would just stir the tea until it was cloudy to fake it. It means giving a vague answer to get by.

Example

質問に答えられず、適当なことを言ってお茶を濁した。
しつもんに こたえられず、 てきとうな ことお いって おちゃお にごした。
I couldn't answer the question, so I said something vague to gloss it over.
