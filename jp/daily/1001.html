Japanese phrase of the day · October 1 · Season

鰯雲

いわしぐも

mackerel sky; sardine clouds

Small patches of high cloud packed together like a school of sardines. It's a seasonal word (季語) for autumn; English calls this a 'mackerel sky.'

Example

空を見上げると、鰯雲が広がっていた。
そらお みあげると、 いわしぐもが ひろがっていた。
When I looked up, a mackerel sky was spreading across the heavens.
