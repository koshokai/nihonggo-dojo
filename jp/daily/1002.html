Japanese phrase of the day · October 2 · Kind words

ありがとうを言いたくて

ありがとうをいいたくて

I just wanted to say thank you

Here 「ありがとう」 is used as a noun, meaning you've reached out specifically to say thanks. It often opens a message, letter, or phone call, and sounds sincere and warm.

Example

急に電話してごめんね。ただ、ありがとうを言いたくて。
きゅうに でんわ して ごめんね。 ただ、 ありがとうお いいたくて。
Sorry for calling out of the blue. I just wanted to say thank you.
