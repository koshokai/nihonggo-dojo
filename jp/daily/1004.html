Japanese phrase of the day · October 4 · Four-character idiom

一心不乱

いっしんふらん

wholeheartedly; with undivided attention

Means doing something with complete focus, without letting anything distract you. Literally 'one heart, not disturbed.'

Example

子どもたちは一心不乱に絵を描いている。
こどもたちわ いっしんふらんに えお えがいている。
The children are completely absorbed in their drawings.
