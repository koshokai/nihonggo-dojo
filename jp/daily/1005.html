Japanese phrase of the day · October 5 · Season

食欲の秋

しょくよくのあき

autumn, the season of appetite

Autumn is harvest season, with plenty of delicious food such as new rice, Pacific saury, chestnuts, and matsutake mushrooms. That's why it's called 'the autumn of appetite.'

Example

食欲の秋で、ついつい食べすぎてしまう。
しょくよくの あきで、 ついつい たべすぎてしまう。
It's the season of good food, and I keep eating too much.
