Japanese phrase of the day · October 6 · Idiom

口に合う

くちにあう

to suit one's taste

Means food agrees with your palate. When serving guests, people often say 「お口に合うかどうか」 (I'm not sure if it will suit your taste).

Example

お口に合うかどうか分かりませんが、どうぞ。
おくちに あうか どうか わかりませんが、 どうぞ。
I'm not sure whether it will suit your taste, but please help yourself.
