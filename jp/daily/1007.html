Japanese phrase of the day · October 7 · Kind words

さすが

As expected of you! Impressive!

Praises someone for living up to their reputation and being just as good as you expected. It's often used as 「さすがですね」 or 「さすが田中さん」.

Example

もう終わったの？さすがだね。
もう おわったの？ さすがだね。
You're done already? I'd expect nothing less from you.
