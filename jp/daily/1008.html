Japanese phrase of the day · October 8 · Season

寒露

かんろ

Cold Dew (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around October 8. The dew grows colder, and autumn is well advanced.

Example

寒露を過ぎて、朝晩はすっかり冷え込むようになった。
かんろお すぎて、 あさばんわ すっかり ひえこむ ように なった。
Since Cold Dew, the mornings and evenings have gotten really chilly.
