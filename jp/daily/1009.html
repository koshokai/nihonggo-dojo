Japanese phrase of the day · October 9 · Proverb

石橋を叩いて渡る

いしばしをたたいてわたる

to look before you leap; to be extremely cautious

Literally 'to tap a stone bridge before crossing it' to make sure it's solid, it describes someone who is very careful. English says 'Look before you leap.'

Example

彼は石橋を叩いて渡る性格だから、契約書を三回も読んだ。
かれわ いしばしお たたいてわたる せいかくだから、 けいやくしょお さんかいも よんだ。
He's the type who taps a stone bridge before crossing it, so he read the contract three times.
