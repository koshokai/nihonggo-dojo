Japanese phrase of the day · October 10 · Four-character idiom

他力本願

たりきほんがん

relying on others

Originally a term from Pure Land Buddhism, meaning rebirth in paradise through the power of Amida Buddha's vow. In modern Japanese it has taken on a negative meaning: depending on others for everything.

Example

他力本願では、いつまでたっても成長できないよ。
たりきほんがんでわ、 いつまで たっても せいちょう できないよ。
If you keep counting on other people, you'll never grow.
