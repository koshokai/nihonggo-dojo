Japanese phrase of the day · October 11 · Idiom

手が届く

てがとどく

within reach; affordable; nearing an age

Literally 'one's hand reaches,' it can mean a price is affordable. It can also describe approaching an age, as in 「四十に手が届く」 (to be pushing forty).

Example

この値段なら、私にも手が届く。
この ねだんなら、 わたしにも てが とどく。
At this price, even I can afford it.
