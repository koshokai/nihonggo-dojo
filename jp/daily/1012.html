Japanese phrase of the day · October 12 · Season

読書の秋

どくしょのあき

autumn, the season for reading

Autumn's cool weather and long nights make it the perfect time for reading. It's mentioned together with 「食欲の秋」 and 「スポーツの秋」.

Example

読書の秋だから、毎晩少しずつ本を読もう。
どくしょの あきだから、 まいばん すこしずつ ほんお よもう。
It's the season for reading, so I'll read a little every night.
