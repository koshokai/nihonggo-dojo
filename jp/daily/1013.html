Japanese phrase of the day · October 13 · Kind words

泣いてもいいんだよ

ないてもいいんだよ

It's okay to cry

Tells someone who's hurting that they don't have to hold it in and can let their feelings out. It's a very gentle way to comfort someone.

Example

つらいときは、泣いてもいいんだよ。
つらい ときわ、 ないても いいんだよ。
When things are hard, it's okay to cry.
