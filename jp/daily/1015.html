Japanese phrase of the day · October 15 · Four-character idiom

傍若無人

ぼうじゃくぶじん

acting as if no one else were around; inconsiderate

Literally 'as if there were no one beside you.' In Japanese it's clearly negative, describing someone who ignores the people around them and does as they please.

Example

電車で大声で話すなんて、傍若無人な態度だ。
でんしゃで おおごえで はなすなんて、 ぼうじゃくぶじんな たいどだ。
Talking loudly on the train is so inconsiderate.
