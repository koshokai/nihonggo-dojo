Japanese phrase of the day · October 16 · Idiom

目がない

めがない

to have a weakness for; to be crazy about

Literally 'to have no eyes,' 「〜に目がない」 means loving something so much that you lose all judgment about it. It can also mean having no eye for something, that is, poor judgment.

Example

姉は甘いものに目がない。
あねわ あまい ものに めが ない。
My big sister has a real weakness for sweets.
