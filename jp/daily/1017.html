Japanese phrase of the day · October 17 · Kind words

気をつけてね

きをつけてね

Take care; be careful

A common phrase when seeing someone off or reminding them to stay safe. It's like English 'Take care' or 'Be safe.'

Example

夜道は暗いから、気をつけてね。
よみちわ くらいから、 きお つけてね。
The streets are dark at night, so be careful.
