Japanese phrase of the day · October 18 · Season

秋晴れ

あきばれ

clear autumn weather

Fine autumn weather, when the air is crisp and the sky is high and deep blue. It has a fresh, clean feel.

Example

秋晴れの気持ちいい日に、運動会が開かれた。
あきばれの きもち いい ひに、 うんどうかいが ひらかれた。
The sports day was held on a lovely, clear autumn day.
