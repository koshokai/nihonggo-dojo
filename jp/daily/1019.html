Japanese phrase of the day · October 19 · Proverb

海老で鯛を釣る

えびでたいをつる

to throw a sprat to catch a mackerel

Literally 'to catch a sea bream with a shrimp,' it means getting a big return for a small investment. English has a near-identical proverb: 'Throw a sprat to catch a mackerel.'

Example

お土産をあげたらご飯をおごってもらった。海老で鯛を釣るだね。
おみやげお あげたら ごはんお おごってもらった。 えびで たいお つるだね。
I gave them a little souvenir and they treated me to dinner. Talk about catching a sea bream with a shrimp!
