Japanese phrase of the day · October 21 · Idiom

耳が痛い

みみがいたい

to hit a nerve; to strike close to home

It doesn't mean your ears actually hurt; it means someone has pointed out your faults and it's painful to hear. It's similar to English 'The truth hurts.'

Example

先生の注意は耳が痛いが、正しい。
せんせいの ちゅういわ みみが いたいが、 ただしい。
My teacher's criticism stings, but it's right.
