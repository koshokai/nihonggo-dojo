Japanese phrase of the day · October 22 · Kind words

生きてるだけで丸もうけ

いきてるだけでまるもうけ

Just being alive is a win

The motto of comedian Sanma Akashiya, meaning that simply being alive is already pure profit and everything else is a bonus. His daughter's stage name, IMALU, also comes from this phrase.

Example

失敗なんて気にしない。生きてるだけで丸もうけだよ。
しっぱいなんて きに しない。 いきてるだけで まるもうけだよ。
I don't worry about failing. Just being alive is a win.
