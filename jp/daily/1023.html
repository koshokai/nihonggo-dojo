Japanese phrase of the day · October 23 · Season

霜降

そうこう

Frost's Descent (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around October 23. Frost begins to form, and the leaves gradually turn red.

Example

霜降を迎えて、山の紅葉が色づき始めた。
そうこうお むかえて、 やまの こうようが いろづきはじめた。
With Frost's Descent here, the leaves in the mountains have started to change color.
