Japanese phrase of the day · October 24 · Proverb

論より証拠

ろんよりしょうこ

The proof is in the pudding

It means that rather than arguing, you should show the evidence, because facts are the most convincing. English says 'The proof of the pudding is in the eating.'

Example

論より証拠、実際に作ってみせよう。
ろんより しょうこ、 じっさいに つくってみせよう。
Proof beats argument. Let me actually make it and show you.
