Japanese phrase of the day · October 26 · Idiom

頭が上がらない

あたまがあがらない

to be forever in someone's debt

Literally 'can't raise one's head.' It means being unable to stand up to someone because you owe them a great deal or are in the wrong.

Example

世話になった先輩には頭が上がらない。
せわに なった せんぱいにわ あたまが あがらない。
I'm forever indebted to the senpai who looked after me.
