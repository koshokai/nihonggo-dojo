Japanese phrase of the day · October 27 · Kind words

明日があるさ

あしたがあるさ

There's always tomorrow

The title of a song sung by Kyu Sakamoto (lyrics by Yukio Aoshima). It means it's fine if today didn't go well, because tomorrow brings another chance.

Example

今日はうまくいかなかったけど、明日があるさ。
きょうわ うまく いかなかったけど、 あしたが あるさ。
Today didn't go well, but there's always tomorrow.
