Japanese phrase of the day · October 29 · Four-character idiom

抱腹絶倒

ほうふくぜっとう

to double over with laughter

Literally 'clutching one's belly and falling over.' It describes laughing extremely hard at something hilarious, like English 'to be in stitches.'

Example

彼の漫談に、観客は抱腹絶倒した。
かれの まんだんに、 かんきゃくわ ほうふくぜっとう した。
The audience was in stitches at his stand-up routine.
