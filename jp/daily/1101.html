Japanese phrase of the day · November 1 · Season

木枯らし

こがらし

cold winter wind

The strong north wind that blows from late autumn into early winter, stripping the leaves from the trees. The Japan Meteorological Agency announces the year's first one as 「木枯らし1号」.

Example

木枯らしが吹いて、街路樹の葉が一気に散った。
こがらしが ふいて、 がいろじゅの はが いっきに ちった。
The cold wind blew, and the leaves on the street trees all fell at once.
