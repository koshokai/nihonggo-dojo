Japanese phrase of the day · November 4 · Proverb

七転び八起き

ななころびやおき

Fall seven times, get up eight

Literally 'fall down seven times, get up eight,' it means never giving up and always bouncing back, no matter how many times you fail. English speakers often quote it directly as 'Fall seven times, stand up eight.'

Example

試験に落ちても、七転び八起きの精神でまた挑戦します。
しけんに おちても、 ななころびはちおきの せいしんで また ちょうせん します。
Even if I fail the exam, I'll take it again in the spirit of 'fall seven times, get up eight.'
