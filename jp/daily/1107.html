Japanese phrase of the day · November 7 · Season

立冬

りっとう

Beginning of Winter (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around November 7. Winter begins on the calendar from this day.

Example

立冬を過ぎて、そろそろこたつを出そうかな。
りっとうお すぎて、 そろそろ こたつお だそうかな。
Now that winter has begun, maybe it's about time to bring out the kotatsu.
