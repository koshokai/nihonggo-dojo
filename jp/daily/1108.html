Japanese phrase of the day · November 8 · Kind words

お大事に

おだいじに

Get well soon; take care of yourself

Caring words for someone who is sick or injured. Staff at hospitals and pharmacies also say this to patients.

Example

風邪ですか。どうぞお大事に。
かぜですか。 どうぞ おだいじに。
You have a cold? Please take care of yourself.
