Japanese phrase of the day · November 9 · Proverb

鬼に金棒

おににかなぼう

unbeatable; even stronger than before

Literally 'an iron club for an ogre (鬼),' a creature that is already strong. It means someone strong gets an extra boost and becomes even stronger.

Example

英語が得意な彼が日本語も話せたら、鬼に金棒だ。
えいごが とくいな かれが にほんごも はなせたら、 おにに かなぼうだ。
He's already great at English; if he could speak Japanese too, he'd be unstoppable.
