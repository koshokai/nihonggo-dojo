Japanese phrase of the day · November 10 · Four-character idiom

自画自賛

じがじさん

blowing one's own horn

Originally writing words of praise on your own painting, it came to mean praising yourself. English says 'to blow one's own horn.'

Example

自画自賛だけど、今日の料理はよくできた。
じがじさんだけど、 きょうの りょうりわ よく できた。
I know I'm blowing my own horn, but today's dinner turned out great.
