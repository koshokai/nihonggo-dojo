Japanese phrase of the day · November 11 · Idiom

口を出す

くちをだす

to butt in; to meddle

Means giving your opinion on, or interfering in, something that's none of your business. Literally 'to put out one's mouth.'

Example

関係ない人は口を出さないでください。
かんけい ない ひとわ くちお ださないでください。
If this doesn't concern you, please don't butt in.
