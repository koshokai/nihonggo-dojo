Japanese phrase of the day · November 12 · Season

紅葉狩り

もみじがり

autumn leaf viewing

Going to the mountains or to gardens in autumn to admire the red and yellow leaves. 「狩り」 normally means 'hunting,' but here it means going out to seek and enjoy something.

Example

週末に、京都へ紅葉狩りに出かけた。
しゅうまつに、 きょうとえ もみじがりに でかけた。
This weekend I went to Kyoto to see the autumn leaves.
