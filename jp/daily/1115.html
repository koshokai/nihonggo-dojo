Japanese phrase of the day · November 15 · Season

七五三

しちごさん

Shichi-Go-San (Seven-Five-Three Festival)

November 15, a festival to pray for the healthy growth of boys aged 3 and 5 and girls aged 3 and 7. Children visit shrines in kimono and get 「千歳飴」 (long, thin 'thousand-year candy').

Example

七五三のお参りに、着物を着た娘と神社へ行った。
しちごさんの おまいりに、 きものお きた むすめと じんじゃえ いった。
For Shichi-Go-San, I took my daughter, dressed in a kimono, to visit the shrine.
