Japanese phrase of the day · November 16 · Idiom

揚げ足を取る

あげあしをとる

to nitpick; to jump on someone's slip

Originally a term from sumo and similar sports for grabbing an opponent's raised leg to throw them. It came to mean seizing on someone's slip of the tongue or small mistake and making a big deal of it.

Example

人の揚げ足を取ってばかりいると嫌われるよ。
ひとの あげあしお とってばかり いると きらわれるよ。
If you're always nitpicking what people say, they'll start to dislike you.
