Japanese phrase of the day · November 17 · Proverb

転ばぬ先の杖

ころばぬさきのつえ

Better safe than sorry

Literally 'a cane before you fall,' it means preparing in advance so you don't fail. English says 'Better safe than sorry' or 'An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure.'

Example

転ばぬ先の杖で、旅行保険に入っておいた。
ころばぬ さきの つえで、 りょこうほけんに はいっておいた。
Better safe than sorry, so I took out travel insurance.
