Japanese phrase of the day · November 19 · Idiom

匙を投げる

さじをなげる

to give up on; to throw in the towel

Literally 'to throw away the medicine spoon,' it originally described a doctor giving up on a patient who couldn't be cured. Now it means giving up on something completely, like English 'to throw in the towel.'

Example

先生もあの生徒には匙を投げた。
せんせいも あの せいとにわ さじお なげた。
Even the teacher has given up on that student.
