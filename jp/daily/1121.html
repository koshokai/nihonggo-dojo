Japanese phrase of the day · November 21 · Proverb

三人寄れば文殊の知恵

さんにんよればもんじゅのちえ

Two heads are better than one

Monju (Manjushri) is the bodhisattva of wisdom, and the saying means that even ordinary people can come up with great ideas when three of them put their heads together. English says 'Two heads are better than one.'

Example

一人で悩まないで、みんなで考えよう。三人寄れば文殊の知恵だよ。
ひとりで なやまないで、 みんなで かんがえよう。 さんにん よれば もんじゅの ちえだよ。
Don't agonize over it by yourself; let's think it through together. Three heads together are as wise as Monju, after all.
