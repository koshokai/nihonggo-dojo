Japanese phrase of the day · November 22 · Four-character idiom

一部始終

いちぶしじゅう

the whole story from start to finish

「一部」 originally meant one complete book, so the phrase means 'from beginning to end.' It refers to the entire course of events, not just one part.

Example

事故の一部始終を警察に話した。
じこの いちぶしじゅうお けいさつに はなした。
I told the police everything about the accident, from start to finish.
