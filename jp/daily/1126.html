Japanese phrase of the day · November 26 · Four-character idiom

十人十色

じゅうにんといろ

To each their own

Literally 'ten people, ten colors,' it means everyone has different tastes and ideas. Note that 「十色」 is read といろ; English equivalents are 'To each their own' and 'Different strokes for different folks.'

Example

好みは十人十色だから、押し付けないほうがいい。
このみわ じゅうにん といろだから、 おしつけない ほうが いい。
Everyone's tastes are different, so it's better not to force yours on others.
