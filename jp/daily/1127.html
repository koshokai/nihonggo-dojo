Japanese phrase of the day · November 27 · Idiom

肩の荷が下りる

かたのにがおりる

a weight off one's shoulders

Literally 'the load comes off one's shoulders,' it describes the relief when a responsibility or burden is lifted. English uses the same image: 'a weight off my shoulders.'

Example

発表が終わって、やっと肩の荷が下りた。
はっぴょうが おわって、 やっと かたの にが おりた。
Now that my presentation is over, it's finally a weight off my shoulders.
