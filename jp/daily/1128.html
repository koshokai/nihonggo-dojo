Japanese phrase of the day · November 28 · Proverb

栴檀は双葉より芳し

せんだんはふたばよりかんばし

Genius shows itself early

「栴檀」 here means sandalwood, which is fragrant even as a tiny sprout. It means that outstanding people stand out even as young children.

Example

五歳で詩を書いたなんて、栴檀は双葉より芳しだね。
ごさいで しお かいたなんて、 せんだんわ ふたばより かんばしだね。
Writing poems at five years old? Sandalwood is fragrant from its very first leaves.
