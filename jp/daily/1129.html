Japanese phrase of the day · November 29 · Four-character idiom

右往左往

うおうさおう

running around in confusion

Literally 'going right, going left,' it describes rushing about in a panic, not knowing what to do. English says 'running around like a headless chicken.'

Example

突然の停電で、駅の乗客は右往左往していた。
とつぜんの ていでんで、 えきの じょうきゃくわ うおうさおう していた。
When the power suddenly went out, the passengers at the station were running around in confusion.
