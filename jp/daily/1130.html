Japanese phrase of the day · November 30 · Idiom

目が回る

めがまわる

to be dizzy; to be swamped

Literally 'one's eyes spin.' It can mean feeling dizzy, but it's often used to describe being incredibly busy.

Example

今週は目が回るほど忙しかった。
こんしゅうわ めが まわるほど いそがしかった。
This week I was so busy my head was spinning.
