Japanese phrase of the day · December 1 · Season

師走

しわす

Shiwasu (December)

An old name for the twelfth month of the lunar calendar, now also used for December. Folk etymology says that at the end of the year even teachers (師) are so busy they run (走) around.

Example

師走に入って、何かと忙しくなってきた。
しわすに はいって、 なにかと いそがしく なってきた。
Now that December is here, everything's getting busy.
