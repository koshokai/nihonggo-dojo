Japanese phrase of the day · December 2 · Proverb

鳶が鷹を生む

とびがたかをうむ

an extraordinary child from ordinary parents

Literally 'a kite gives birth to a hawk': the kite is a common bird, while the hawk is prized. It means that ordinary parents have produced an exceptional child.

Example

うちの子が東大に合格するなんて、鳶が鷹を生んだね。
うちの こが とうだいに ごうかく するなんて、 とびが たかお うんだね。
Our kid got into the University of Tokyo? A kite really did give birth to a hawk!
