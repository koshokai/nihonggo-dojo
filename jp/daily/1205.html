Japanese phrase of the day · December 5 · Proverb

三度目の正直

さんどめのしょうじき

Third time's the charm

It means that after two failed attempts, the third will surely succeed, and it's used to encourage people to try again. The English equivalent is 'Third time's the charm.'

Example

二回落ちたけど、三度目の正直で合格したい。
にかい おちたけど、 さんどめの しょうじきで ごうかく したい。
I've failed twice, but I hope the third time's the charm and I pass.
