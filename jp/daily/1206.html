Japanese phrase of the day · December 6 · Four-character idiom

三日坊主

みっかぼうず

someone who never sticks with anything

Literally 'a three-day monk,' it describes someone who lacks staying power and quits quickly. It's a very Japanese expression.

Example

日記を始めたけど、また三日坊主で終わった。
にっきお はじめたけど、 また みっか ぼうずでおわった。
I started keeping a diary, but once again I quit after three days.
