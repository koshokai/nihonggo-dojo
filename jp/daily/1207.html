Japanese phrase of the day · December 7 · Idiom

眉をひそめる

まゆをひそめる

to frown; to knit one's brows

Means frowning because you're displeased with or worried about someone's words or actions. Literally 'to draw one's eyebrows together.'

Example

彼の乱暴な言葉に、周りの人は眉をひそめた。
かれの らんぼうな ことばに、 まわりの ひとわ まゆお ひそめた。
His rude language made the people around him frown.
