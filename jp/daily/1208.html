Japanese phrase of the day · December 8 · Proverb

灯台下暗し

とうだいもとくらし

It's darkest under the lamp

Literally 'it's dark at the foot of the lamp stand'; here 「灯台」 means an old-fashioned candle stand, not a lighthouse. It means we tend to overlook what's right under our noses.

Example

探していた眼鏡が頭の上にあった。灯台下暗しだ。
さがしていた めがねが あたまの うえに あった。 とうだいもとくらしだ。
The glasses I'd been looking for were on top of my head. It's always darkest right under the lamp.
