Japanese phrase of the day · December 10 · Season

忘年会

ぼうねんかい

year-end party

A party at the end of the year where coworkers or friends eat and drink together, forgetting the year's troubles and getting ready for the new year. Literally 'forget-the-year gathering.'

Example

今週の金曜日は、会社の忘年会がある。
こんしゅうの きんようびわ、 かいしゃの ぼうねんかいが ある。
Our company's year-end party is this Friday.
