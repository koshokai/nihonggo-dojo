Japanese phrase of the day · December 11 · Idiom

顔が広い

かおがひろい

well-connected; to know a lot of people

Literally 'one's face is wide.' It describes someone who knows a lot of people and has a wide network.

Example

田中さんは顔が広いから、誰か紹介してくれるよ。
たなかさんわ かおが ひろいから、 だれか しょうかい してくれるよ。
Tanaka knows everyone, so he'll introduce you to someone.
