Japanese phrase of the day · December 12 · Proverb

蒔かぬ種は生えぬ

まかぬたねははえぬ

No pain, no gain

Literally 'seeds you don't sow won't sprout,' it means you won't get good results without making an effort. English says 'No pain, no gain' or 'You reap what you sow.'

Example

蒔かぬ種は生えぬ。勉強しないで合格するはずがない。
まかぬ たねわ はえぬ。 べんきょう しないで ごうかく する はずが ない。
Seeds you don't sow won't sprout. There's no way you'll pass without studying.
