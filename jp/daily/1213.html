Japanese phrase of the day · December 13 · Four-character idiom

一期一会

いちごいちえ

once in a lifetime; treasure every encounter

From the tea ceremony: every meeting may be the only one in your lifetime, so treat it with sincerity. English has no set phrase for this, so it's often rendered 'treasure every encounter.'

Example

一期一会の気持ちで、お客様をおもてなししています。
いちごいちえの きもちで、 おきゃくさまお おもてなししています。
We welcome each guest as though it were a once-in-a-lifetime meeting.
