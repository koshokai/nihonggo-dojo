Japanese phrase of the day · December 18 · Proverb

馬の耳に念仏

うまのみみにねんぶつ

like talking to a brick wall

Literally 'chanting Buddhist prayers into a horse's ear,' it means that no matter how much you advise someone, it doesn't sink in. English says 'like talking to a brick wall.'

Example

何度注意しても聞かないなんて、馬の耳に念仏だ。
なんどちゅうい しても きかないなんて、 うまの みみに ねんぶつだ。
I've warned him so many times and he still won't listen. It's like talking to a brick wall.
