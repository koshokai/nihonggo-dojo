Japanese phrase of the day · December 20 · Proverb

餅は餅屋

もちはもちや

Leave it to the experts

Literally 'for rice cakes, go to the rice cake shop.' It means that every job is best left to the professionals.

Example

餅は餅屋だから、パソコンの修理はプロに頼もう。
もちわ もちやだから、 パソコンの しゅうりわ プロに たのもう。
Leave it to the experts: let's have a professional fix the computer.
