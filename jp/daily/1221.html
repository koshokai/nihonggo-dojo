Japanese phrase of the day · December 21 · Proverb

門前の小僧習わぬ経を読む

もんぜんのこぞうならわぬきょうをよむ

You naturally pick things up from your surroundings

Literally 'the boy at the temple gate can recite sutras he was never taught.' It means your environment has a big influence, and you naturally learn what you're constantly exposed to.

Example

門前の小僧習わぬ経を読むで、日本のドラマを毎日見ていたら日本語が少しわかるようになった。
もんぜんの こぞう ならわぬ きょうお よむで、 にほんの ドラマお まいにち みていたら にほんごが すこし わかる ように なった。
Like the temple boy who recites sutras he was never taught, I started understanding a bit of Japanese just from watching Japanese dramas every day.
