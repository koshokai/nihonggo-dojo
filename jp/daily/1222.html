Japanese phrase of the day · December 22 · Season

冬至

とうじ

Winter Solstice (one of the 24 solar terms)

One of the 24 solar terms, around December 22, the shortest day of the year. Japanese customs include eating pumpkin and taking a yuzu citron bath (ゆず湯).

Example

冬至の夜は、ゆず湯に入って温まった。
とうじの よるわ、 ゆずゆに はいって あたたまった。
On the night of the winter solstice, I warmed up in a yuzu bath.
