Japanese phrase of the day · December 23 · Proverb

住めば都

すめばみやこ

Anywhere feels like home once you live there

It means that wherever you live, once you get used to it, it feels as comfortable as the capital. Literally 'live there, and it becomes the capital.'

Example

最初は田舎が不安だったけど、住めば都で今はとても気に入っている。
さいしょわ いなかが ふあんだったけど、 すめば みやこで いまわ とても きに いっている。
At first I was uneasy about the countryside, but anywhere feels like home once you live there, and now I really love it.
