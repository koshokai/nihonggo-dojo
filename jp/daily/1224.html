Japanese phrase of the day · December 24 · Season

クリスマスイブ

くりすますいぶ

Christmas Eve

In Japan it's a day to spend with a romantic partner or family. Eating fried chicken and strawberry shortcake is a uniquely Japanese tradition.

Example

クリスマスイブに、家族でケーキを囲んだ。
クリスマスイブに、 かぞくで ケーキお かこんだ。
On Christmas Eve, our family gathered around a cake.
