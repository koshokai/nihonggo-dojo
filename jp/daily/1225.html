Japanese phrase of the day · December 25 · Proverb

病は気から

やまいはきから

Illness starts in the mind

It means illness is often tied to your state of mind, and a positive mood can help your body get better. Literally 'sickness comes from the spirit.'

Example

病は気からだよ。元気を出して。
やまいわ きからだよ。 げんきお だして。
Sickness starts in the mind, you know. Cheer up.
