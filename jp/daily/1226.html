Japanese phrase of the day · December 26 · Proverb

三つ子の魂百まで

みつごのたましいひゃくまで

The child is father of the man

It means a child's character at age three stays the same until they're a hundred. It's close to English 'The child is father of the man' or 'As the twig is bent, so grows the tree.'

Example

弟は小さい頃から負けず嫌いだった。三つ子の魂百までだね。
おとうとわ ちいさい ころから まけず きらいだった。 みつごの たましいひゃくまでだね。
My little brother has hated losing ever since he was small. The soul of a three-year-old lasts until a hundred.
