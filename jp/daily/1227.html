Japanese phrase of the day · December 27 · Proverb

犬も歩けば棒に当たる

いぬもあるけばぼうにあたる

Get out there and you'll stumble onto something

Literally 'a dog that walks around will run into a stick,' it originally meant that meddling leads to trouble. Today it's mostly used to mean that getting out and about can bring unexpected good luck.

Example

犬も歩けば棒に当たるで、散歩中に素敵なカフェを見つけた。
いぬも あるけば ぼうに あたるで、 さんぽちゅうに すてきな カフェお みつけた。
Go out and you'll run into something good: I found a lovely café while out on a walk.
