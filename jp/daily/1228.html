Japanese phrase of the day · December 28 · Season

仕事納め

しごとおさめ

the last workday of the year

The last working day of the year, which for government offices is usually December 28. The first workday of the new year is called 「仕事始め」.

Example

今日で仕事納めです。一年間お世話になりました。
きょうで しごとおさめです。 いちねんかん おせわに なりました。
Today is our last day of work for the year. Thank you for all your help this past year.
