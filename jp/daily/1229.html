Japanese phrase of the day · December 29 · Proverb

備えあれば憂いなし

そなえあればうれいなし

Better safe than sorry; forewarned is forearmed

From the Chinese classic Book of Documents, it means that if you prepare in advance, you have nothing to worry about. English says 'Forewarned is forearmed.'

Example

備えあれば憂いなし。地震に備えて水を買っておこう。
そなえ あれば うれい なし。 じしんに そなえて みずお かっておこう。
If you're prepared, there's nothing to worry about. Let's buy some water in case of an earthquake.
