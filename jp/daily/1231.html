Japanese phrase of the day · December 31 · Season

大晦日

おおみそか

New Year's Eve

The last day of the year. Japanese people eat 「年越しそば」 (year-crossing soba), which symbolizes long life and cutting off the year's bad luck, and listen to temple bells ring 108 times.

Example

大晦日の夜は、年越しそばを食べながら除夜の鐘を聞く。
おおみそかの よるわ、 としこしそばお たべながら じょやの かねお きく。
On New Year's Eve, we eat year-crossing soba while listening to the temple bells ring out the old year.
