～にしたがって

as; in accordance with · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun / verb dictionary form + にしたがって. (1) Shows that as one thing changes, another changes along with it; (2) after a noun, it can also mean following instructions or rules (in this case it cannot be replaced with 「につれて」). Note that the second part is a natural change, not a one-time action.
