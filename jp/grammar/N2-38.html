～につれて

as (one thing changes, so does another) · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun / verb dictionary form + につれて. Shows that a change in the first part brings about a gradual change in the second; both parts must be changes. It cannot mean "following instructions", which is how it differs from 「にしたがって」.
