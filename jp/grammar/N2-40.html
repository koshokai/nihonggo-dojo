～とともに

together with; as; at the same time as · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun / verb dictionary form + とともに. (1) "together with" (more formal than 「と一緒に」); (2) "as (one changes, so does the other)"; (3) "at the same time as". Strongly written in tone.
