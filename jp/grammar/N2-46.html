～をめぐって

over, surrounding (an issue) · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun + をめぐって (to modify a noun, 「をめぐる」). Expresses that debate, conflict, discussion, etc. arise around an issue; the second part usually has words like 「議論」, 「対立」, or 「争う」.
