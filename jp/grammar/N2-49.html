～向け

intended for, aimed at · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun + 向け (だ / の / に). Means made or designed specifically for a certain target. Note the difference from 「向き」: 「向け」 means deliberately made for a target, while 「向き」 means naturally suited to it.
