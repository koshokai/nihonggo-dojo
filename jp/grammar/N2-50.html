～を込めて

filled with, putting (feeling) into · JLPT N2

How to use it: Noun + を込めて (to modify a noun, 「を込めた」). Usually follows nouns for feelings such as 「心」, 「愛」, 「感謝」, or 「願い」, meaning doing something while pouring that feeling into it.
