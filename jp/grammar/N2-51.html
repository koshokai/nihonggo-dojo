～次第

as soon as · JLPT N2

How to use it: Verb ます-stem / noun (expressing an action) + 次第. Means that as soon as the first part happens, the second will be done right away; the second part is usually the speaker's intention or a request and cannot be a past fact, so you cannot say 「着き次第電話した」.
