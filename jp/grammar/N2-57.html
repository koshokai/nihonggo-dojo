～にしては

for (a ...), surprisingly · JLPT N2

How to use it: Plain form (nouns and な-adjective stems attach directly) + にしては. Means that judged by the standard of the first part, the second part is unexpected. Close to 「わりには」, but with 「にしては」 the first part is usually a specific fact.
