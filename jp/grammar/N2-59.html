～だけあって

as might be expected of; precisely because (naturally) · JLPT N2

How to use it: Plain form (nouns and な-adjective stems attach directly) + だけあって. Expresses a good result that matches the first part (status, effort, etc.); usually a positive evaluation that shows admiration.
