～だけに

all the more because · JLPT N2

How to use it: Plain form (nouns and な-adjective stems attach directly) + だけに. Because of the special circumstances in the first part, the second part is all the greater in degree; the second part can be good or bad (often used as in "all the more disappointing because expectations were high").
