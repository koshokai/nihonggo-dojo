～ないことはない

it's not that (one) can't / doesn't · JLPT N2

How to use it: Verb ない-form / い-adjective minus い + くない / な-adjective or noun + でない + ことはない. A double negative expressing a reluctant affirmative: it is possible, but without much enthusiasm or only under certain conditions.
