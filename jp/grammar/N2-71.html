～おそれがある

there is a risk that, might (something bad) · JLPT N2

How to use it: Verb dictionary form / Noun + の + おそれがある. Expresses the possibility that something bad will happen; mostly used in formal contexts such as the news and weather forecasts, and only for bad things.
