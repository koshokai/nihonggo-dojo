～がちだ

tend to, be apt to · JLPT N2

How to use it: Verb ます-stem / noun + がちだ (to modify a noun, 「がちな」). Expresses a tendency for something (usually undesirable) to happen, though not necessarily often. Note: not used for positive tendencies.
