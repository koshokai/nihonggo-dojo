～くせに

even though, and yet (critical) · JLPT N3

How to use it: Verb / い-adjective plain form, な-adjective + な, noun + の + くせに. Carries a tone of blame or contempt; both parts have the same subject. Stronger in feeling than 「のに」, and generally not used about oneself.
