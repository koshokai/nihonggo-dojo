～させられる (causative-passive)

be made to, be forced to (causative-passive) · JLPT N3

How to use it: Group 1 (godan) verbs: ない-form stem + される (or せられる); Group 2 (ichidan) verbs: drop る + させられる; する becomes させられる; 来る becomes 来させられる. Expresses being made by someone else to do something unwillingly.
