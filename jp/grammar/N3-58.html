～こそ

precisely, it is ... (and nothing else) · JLPT N3

How to use it: Noun (or 「～からこそ」, etc.) + こそ emphasizes the preceding word, meaning "this and nothing else". Common in 「今度こそ」 and 「こちらこそ」.
