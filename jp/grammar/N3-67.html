～ということだ (hearsay)

I hear that, they say (hearsay) · JLPT N3

How to use it: Plain form + ということだ (casual: 「～って」). Used to report information one has heard or read; puts more emphasis on quoting than 「そうだ」. It cannot be put in the past tense to express hearsay.
