～なくてもいい

don't have to; it's all right not to · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb ない-form with the final い removed + くてもいい.

Example sentences

明日あしたは来こなくてもいいです。
You don't have to come tomorrow.
全部ぜんぶ食たべなくてもいいですよ。
You don't have to eat it all.
急いそがなくてもいいです。
You don't have to hurry.
靴くつを脱ぬがなくてもいいです。
You don't have to take off your shoes.
