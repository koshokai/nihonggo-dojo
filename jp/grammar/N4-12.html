～そうだ (looks like)

looks, seems (appearance) · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb ます-stem / adjective stem + そうだ.

Example sentences

このケーキはおいしそうですね。
This cake looks delicious.
雨あめが降ふりそうです。
It looks like it's going to rain.
彼かれはとても元気げんきそうです。
He looks very well.
この箱はこは重おもそうですね。
This box looks heavy.
