～ていく／～てくる

go / come (after doing); go on ... / come to ... (over time) · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb て-form + いく / くる. (1) Space: go or come after doing something (take an umbrella with you, buy something and bring it back). (2) Time: くる shows a change continuing from the past up to now; いく shows something continuing from now into the future. Note that the て-form is required (wrong: 持つ行く; right: 持っていく).

Example sentences

傘かさを持もっていきます。
I'll take an umbrella with me.
だんだん寒さむくなってきました。
It has gradually gotten colder.
これからも日本にほん語ごを勉強べんきょうしていきます。
I'll keep on studying Japanese from now on.
駅えきでパンを買かってきました。
I bought some bread at the station and brought it back.
