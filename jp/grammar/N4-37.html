～なさい

do ...! (command) · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb ます-stem + なさい. A gentle command from someone above to someone below, such as a parent to a child or a teacher to a student; it is also common in test instructions (「答えなさい」). It cannot be used toward elders or superiors.

Example sentences

早はやく寝ねなさい。
Go to bed now!
ご飯はんの前まえに手てを洗あらいなさい。
Wash your hands before eating.
次つぎの質問しつもんに答こたえなさい。
Answer the following questions.
ちゃんと野菜やさいも食たべなさい。
Eat your vegetables properly, too.
