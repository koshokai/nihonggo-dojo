～終わる

finish doing · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb ます-stem + 終わる. Expresses that an action is finished, like English "finish writing", "finish eating", "finish reading". Note that 終わる cannot simply be put after a verb in its dictionary form; it attaches to the ます-stem (書き終わる, not 書く終わる).

Example sentences

レポートを書かき終おわりました。
I finished writing the report.
この本ほんを読よみ終おわったら、貸かしてください。
When you finish reading this book, please lend it to me.
ご飯はんを食たべ終おわってから、テレビを見みます。
After I finish eating, I'll watch TV.
宿題しゅくだいをやり終おわった人ひとは帰かえってもいいです。
Those who have finished their homework may go home.
