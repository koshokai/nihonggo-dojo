～なくて (reason)

because (something) doesn't / didn't happen · JLPT N4

How to use it: Verb ない-form with ない changed to なくて; い-adjective minus い + くなくて; noun / な-adjective + じゃなくて. Expresses a cause; the second part is usually a feeling or an uncontrollable result, and cannot be a command, request, or statement of intention.

Example sentences

宿題しゅくだいが終おわらなくて、困こまっています。
I can't finish my homework, and I don't know what to do.
試験しけんに合格ごうかくできなくて、残念ざんねんでした。
I was disappointed that I couldn't pass the exam.
バスが来こなくて、遅刻ちこくしました。
The bus didn't come, so I was late.
友達ともだちから連絡れんらくがなくて、心配しんぱいしています。
I haven't heard from my friend, so I'm worried.
