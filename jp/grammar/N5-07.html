～へ

to, toward (direction) · JLPT N5

How to use it: Place + へ + verb of movement. Pronounced え (e); it can also be replaced with に.

Example sentences

来週らいしゅう、京都きょうとへ行いきます。
I'm going to Kyoto next week.
国くにへ帰かえります。
I'm going back to my home country.
こちらへどうぞ。
This way, please.
どこへ行いきますか。
Where are you going?
