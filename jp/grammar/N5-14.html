～ね／～よ

..., isn't it? (confirming) / ..., you know (informing) · JLPT N5

How to use it: Sentence + ね seeks the listener's agreement; sentence + よ tells the listener something they don't know.

Example sentences

今日きょうは暑あついですね。
It's hot today, isn't it?
この料理りょうりはおいしいですよ。
This dish is delicious, you know.
あの店みせは高たかいですよ。
That shop is expensive, you know.
きれいな花はなですね。
That's a beautiful flower, isn't it?
