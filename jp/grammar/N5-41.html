～や～など

A, B, and so on · JLPT N5

How to use it: Noun + や + noun (+ など) gives a few examples and implies there are others; 「と」, by contrast, lists everything.

Example sentences

冷蔵れいぞう庫こに牛乳ぎゅうにゅうや卵たまごなどがあります。
There's milk, eggs, and so on in the fridge.
休やすみの日ひは掃除そうじや洗濯せんたくをします。
On my days off, I do things like cleaning and laundry.
机つくえの上うえに本ほんやノートがあります。
There are books, notebooks, and other things on the desk.
デパートで服ふくや靴くつを買かいました。
I bought clothes, shoes, and so on at the department store.
