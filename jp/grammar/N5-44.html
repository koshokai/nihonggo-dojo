～ましょうか

shall I ...? (offering to help) · JLPT N5

How to use it: Verb ます-stem + ましょうか offers to do something for the listener; it can also be used to suggest something together ("shall we ...?").

Example sentences

重おもいですね。荷物にもつを持もちましょうか。
That's heavy, isn't it? Shall I carry your bags?
暑あついですね。窓まどを開あけましょうか。
It's hot, isn't it? Shall I open the window?
駅えきまで送おくりましょうか。
Shall I take you to the station?
写真しゃしんを撮とりましょうか。
Shall I take a picture for you?
