～くて／～で (joining adjectives)

both ... and ...; ... and also ... (linking adjectives) · JLPT N5

How to use it: い-adjective minus い + くて; な-adjective / noun + で. Adjectives cannot be linked with 「と」; 「いい」 becomes 「よくて」.

Example sentences

この部屋へやは広ひろくて、明あかるいです。
This room is spacious and bright.
あの店みせは安やすくておいしいです。
That restaurant is cheap and good.
彼女かのじょはきれいで、やさしいです。
She is pretty and kind.
兄あには医者いしゃで、姉あねは先生せんせいです。
My older brother is a doctor, and my older sister is a teacher.
