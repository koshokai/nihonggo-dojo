～て、～ (sequence of actions)

... and then ... (sequence of actions) · JLPT N5

How to use it: The verb て-form links several actions in the order they happen; the tense of the whole sentence is determined by the last verb.

Example sentences

朝あさ起おきて、顔かおを洗あらいます。
In the morning, I get up and wash my face.
デパートへ行いって、買かい物ものをしました。
I went to the department store and did some shopping.
家いえに帰かえって、ご飯はんを食たべます。
I go home and eat dinner.
シャワーを浴あびて、寝ねました。
I took a shower and went to bed.
