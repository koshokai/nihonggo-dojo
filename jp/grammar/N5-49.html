～ごろ

around, about (a point in time) · JLPT N5

How to use it: Point in time + ごろ (に) expresses an approximate point in time; for a length of time, use 「ぐらい」.

Example sentences

毎朝まいあさ七時しちじごろ起おきます。
I get up around seven every morning.
夜よる十じゅう一いち時じごろ寝ねます。
I go to bed around eleven at night.
三さん時じごろ友達ともだちが来きます。
My friend is coming around three.
来月らいげつの十日とおかごろ国くにへ帰かえります。
I'm going back to my home country around the tenth of next month.
