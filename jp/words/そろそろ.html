そろそろ

soon, it's about time (to) · JLPT N4

Example sentences

そろそろ帰かえりましょう。
Let's head home soon.
そろそろ桜さくらが咲さくころです。
It's almost time for the cherry blossoms to bloom.
もう十じゅう時じだから、そろそろ寝ねます。
It's already ten o'clock, so I'll go to bed soon.
