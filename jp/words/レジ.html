レジ

cash register, checkout · JLPT N4

Example sentences

レジでお金かねを払はらってください。
Please pay at the register.
スーパーのレジに人ひとがたくさん並ならんでいます。
There are a lot of people lined up at the supermarket checkout.

My older sister works at the register at a convenience store.
