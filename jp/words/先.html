先 (さき)

ahead, front; first (in order); before, previously · JLPT N5

Example sentences

お先さきに失礼しつれいします。
Excuse me for leaving ahead of you.
先さきに食たべてください。
Please go ahead and eat first.
この先さきに駅えきがあります。
There's a station up ahead.
