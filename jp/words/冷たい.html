冷たい (つめたい)

cold, chilly (to the touch); cold, unfriendly (attitude) · JLPT N5

Example sentences

冷つめたい水みずが飲のみたいです。
I want to drink some cold water.
今朝けさは風かぜが冷つめたいです。
The wind is cold this morning.
彼かれは冷つめたい人ひとです。
He's a cold person.
