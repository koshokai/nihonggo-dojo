出る (でる)

to go out, to leave; to appear; to attend · JLPT N5

Example sentences

毎朝まいあさ七時しちじに家いえを出でます。
I leave home at seven every morning.
部屋へやから出でてください。
Please get out of the room.
会議かいぎに出でます。
I'll attend the meeting.
