喫茶店 (きっさてん)

coffee shop, cafe · JLPT N5

Example sentences

I had coffee at a coffee shop.
駅えきの前まえに喫茶きっさ店てんがあります。
There's a coffee shop in front of the station.
喫茶きっさ店てんで友達ともだちを待まちます。
I'll wait for my friend at the coffee shop.
