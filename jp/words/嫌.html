嫌 (いや)

unpleasant, disliked, unwilling · JLPT N5

Example sentences

雨あめの日ひは嫌いやです。
I don't like rainy days.
嫌いやなことは忘わすれましょう。
Let's forget about unpleasant things.
一人ひとりで行いくのは嫌いやです。
I don't want to go alone.
