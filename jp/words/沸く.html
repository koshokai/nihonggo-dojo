沸く (わく)

to boil, to come to a boil · JLPT N4

Example sentences

お湯ゆが沸わいたら、お茶ちゃを入いれましょう。
When the water boils, let's make some tea.
お風呂ふろが沸わきましたよ。
The bath is heated and ready.
やかんのお湯ゆがもう沸わいています。
The water in the kettle is already boiling.
