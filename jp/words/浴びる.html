浴びる (あびる)

to pour over oneself, to bathe (as in taking a shower) · JLPT N5

Example sentences

毎朝まいあさシャワーを浴あびます。
I take a shower every morning.
帰かえってすぐシャワーを浴あびました。
I took a shower as soon as I got home.
日光にっこうを浴あびるのは体からだにいいです。
Getting some sunlight is good for your health.
