済む (すむ)

to be finished, to end; to be settled, to be resolved · JLPT N4

Example sentences

宿題しゅくだいが済すんだら、遊あそびに行いってもいいです。
Once you've finished your homework, you can go out and play.
用事ようじが早はやく済すみました。
My errand was finished early.
謝あやまって済すむ問題もんだいではありません。
This isn't a problem that can be settled just by apologizing.
